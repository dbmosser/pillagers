## Details

### 1. Embark PvE survey and test (confirmed)
Source read: [arcraiders.com PvE toggle beta test, Sep 24, 2026](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-beta-test). Page states 37% friendly co-op, 27% mixed, 34% pure PvP; "51% of the surveyed playerbase has expressed an interest in a dedicated PvE option"; over 70% of friendly and 40% of PvP players want fewer hostile encounters; test runs October 13 to 20, 2026; Night Raids, Hidden Bunker, Locked Gate and Frigate excluded; loot value lowered; zero player-to-player damage; solos, duos and trios share sessions; ARC may re-acquire a downed player who moves and "will ignore you if you stay still"; "It's possible that this will not work"; original concept was a raid-based PvE co-op game. Small wording note: the 51% is "expressed an interest in", not "want".

### 2. 60% slowdown (confirmed)
Source read: [TheGamer via OpenCritic, Mar 13, 2026](https://opencritic.com/news/27964/the-first-thing-embark-did-after-making-arc-raiders-an-extraction-shooter-was-making-it-slower). Caio Braga, production director, at GDC 2026: biggest change was "reducing the speed of everything" by "around 60 percent". Note: the stamina bar line ("keeps you from zooming across the map") is the article author's wording, not a Braga quote; the notes put it in quote marks as if it were his.

### 3. ARC machine reworks (confirmed)
Source read: [Shazoo, Mar 14, 2026](https://shazoo.ru/2026/03/14/180994/pervaia-versiia-vragov-v-arc-raiders-byla-sliskom-xardkornoi) (Russian). Braga: the Sentry had no aiming cone and shot in any direction (article frames it as an aimbot with 360 degree vision); Hornet armour "just insane" and not understood; Fireball had no weak point opening during its attack, now core to fighting it; suicide bots cut as they did not fit; Rocketeer reworked to be more aggressive; core problem was unclear counters and balance; sentiment improved sharply after reworks. Quotes are translations of a Russian rendering, so treat exact English wording as approximate.

### 4. ARC downed players extract from the elevator (confirmed, sub-claims unverified)
Source read: [allthings.how extraction guide](https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-extraction-elevators-hatches-and-timers-explained/). Downed players near the doors "can crawl inside", can use the interior console while downed, and extraction "still completes if you're inside when it departs". Not supported by that page: the 90 second auto-depart figure, the two minute bleed-out, and hatches opening "with no wait" (page says hatches need a single-use key, open silently, have no shutdown timer and close again after a short window). Elevator timers stay unconfirmed.

### 5. Escape from Duckov sales, peak, price (confirmed)
Sources read: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escape_from_Duckov) (1M Oct 22, 2M Oct 28, 3M Nov 8, 2025; peak over 300,000 Steam concurrent; Metacritic 77; OpenCritic 67% recommend; single-player); [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3167020/Escape_from_Duckov/) ($17.99; 93% of 10,727 English reviews; recent 89% of 540). Search results put the peak at about 301,300 on Oct 27, 2025. The 3M figure is a developer announcement.

### 6. "Both top-down hits are mouse-only" (corrected)
Duckov part holds: [Worthplaying, Dec 17, 2025](https://worthplaying.com/article/2025/12/17/reviews/148515-pc-review-escape-from-duckov/) says it "is solely a keyboard/mouse endeavor" and Team Soda said controller support would come eventually; the Steam store page lists no controller support and guides found no official patch as of 2026 (only community Workshop mods).
Zero Sievert part is wrong: [GamingOnLinux, Feb 29, 2024](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2024/02/single-player-extraction-shooter-zero-sievert-adds-controller-support/) reports an update that "adds full controller support to all actions" with UI reworked for pads (SteamDeckHQ: update 0.41, later a controller radial menu). The SteamDeckHQ "inventory easier with a mouse" note is about comfort, not missing support. Zero Sievert 2 (announced early May 2026, about 100,000 wishlists in two weeks per [Rutab](https://rutab.net/b/video-games/2026/05/18/zero-sievert-2-sobrala-100-000-zhelayuschih-v-steam-za-dve-nedeli.html)) lists controller support and console ports as long-term goals. Corrected inference: the biggest top-down hits are single-player; Duckov has no native pad support, Zero Sievert has had pad support since 2024. The co-op gap stands; the "no pad in the niche" gap is only half true.

### 7. Zero Sievert 600,000 copies, solo developer (confirmed)
Sources read: [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1782120/ZERO_Sievert/) description: "Thank you for over 600,000 copies sold!" (also 85% of 8,137 English reviews, so the review figure the notes doubted is correct); [Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/zero-sievert/info) repeats over 600,000; [Game World Observer, Jan 23, 2023](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/01/23/zero-sievert-1-million-revenue-cabo-studio-extraction-shooter-steam): "solo developer Luca Carbonera", $1.3M first week. Third-party trackers disagree (SteamPulse about 443,000 units estimate). Team size in 2026 not checked.

### 8. Marathon peak and decline (confirmed)
Sources read: [The Game Post, Aug 20, 2026, updated Sep 6](https://thegamepost.com/marathon-player-count-96-launch-bungie-season-3/): all-time Steam peak 88,337 at launch Mar 5, 2026; 24-hour peak 3,507; more than 96% below. Search results (TweakTown, PCGamesN) attribute 88,337 to SteamDB; Steam Charts shows 77,358 for the same peak. Server Slam over 143,000. Forbes page returned 403, not read.

### 9. Helldivers 2 extraction timings (confirmed)
Source read: [helldivers.wiki.gg Extraction](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Extraction): deployment period 2 minutes (3 with Complex Stratagem Plotting; the Fandom wiki says 4) with at least one Helldiver inside the perimeter; the shuttle "will stay there until a Helldiver enters or until the mission timer reaches 0. After which, a 20 second countdown will begin"; reinforcements disabled once one Helldiver boards; when the mission timer runs out the Super Destroyer leaves low orbit and the shuttle is called automatically, so extraction is still possible. Fan wiki sources only; 2024 Steam threads report a bug where the shuttle sometimes left at once.

### 10. Fortnite Blitz Royale (corrected)
Match length holds: [Shacknews, Jun 18, 2025](https://www.shacknews.com/article/144783/fortnite-blitz-royale-mode): "no longer than five minutes", the quickest official mode. The 443,379 peak is real but not from the cited source: the [CGMagazine article](https://www.cgmagonline.com/articles/fortnite-blitz-royale-rapid-matches) gives no peak figure. The number comes from Fortnite.GG tracking, reported by EGW News (search summary; page returned 403) as a launch-week peak after the Jun 18, 2025 start; later Fortnite.GG snapshots may show a higher all-time peak (about 531,000, search summary only). Permanence is no longer just a claim: Tim Sweeney confirmed around Jul 18, 2025 that Blitz Royale stays (esports.gg and Beebom, search summary; pages returned 403). The "at least until 2027" detail remains a leak.

## Not checked (outside the ten)
ARC Raiders sales and SteamCharts monthly averages, Tarkov 1.0 launch figures, Helldivers 2 reinforcement counts, Chris Zukowski demo survey.
