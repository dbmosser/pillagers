# Extraction shooter design lessons (2024 to October 2026) for PILLAGERS

Scope note: researched 2026-10-25 with web search and fetch only. Many details come from search-result summaries of pages that could not be fetched in full (PC Gamer and playnews.gg pages were truncated or blocked); those are marked "(search summary)". Forum and guide sources are anecdotal and marked as such. Items older than 2025 are marked [older]. PILLAGERS fit notes check his binding rulings: no weak spots or headshots (his 10-13 answer 31), no team damage, bodies never come back, guns never wear, no tutorial prompts (Q70), no voices or raid music, nobody rejoins a raid after dying (except the 2-player demo respawn), no separate party mode.

## 1. ARC Raiders (launched Oct 30, 2025): what players loved and hated

### Takeaway
ARC Raiders proved that readable, physical machine enemies plus strong positional sound can carry an extraction game, and that most players lean co-operative (Embark's own data: 37% friendly, 27% mixed, 34% PvP; 51% want a PvE option). What hurt it: early enemies with no visible aim cone or unclear counterplay, solo players feeling preyed on, free-kit "gear fear" debates, and an Expedition wipe that devalued saved progress.

### Cited Findings
**Scale and reception**
- Released Oct 30, 2025 (PS5, Windows, Xbox), $40 after free-to-play was dropped; solo, duo or trio; raids last up to 30 minutes, up to 40 on some maps (Hidden Bunker, Locked Gate). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders))
- Sales: 4 million by Nov 11, 2025 and 14 million by Feb 2026; 250,000 Steam concurrent on launch day; over 700,000 concurrent across platforms by Nov 10, 2025. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders))
- Metacritic PC 86, PS5 85, Xbox 88; OpenCritic 92% recommend; won Best Multiplayer at The Game Awards 2025, Online Game of the Year at D.I.C.E., Most Innovative Gameplay at the Steam Awards 2025, and the BAFTA multiplayer award. Eurogamer gave 2/5. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders))
- Reviewers noted solo play is harder and that harsh loss consequences may frustrate casual players; Hardcore Gamer said the UI uses small fonts and feels built for mouse and keyboard rather than controller. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders))
- Steam all-time peak about 482,000 concurrent (Nov 16, 2025); monthly Steam averages fell from about 264,000 (Nov 2025) to about 241,000 (Jan 2026), 171,000 (Feb), 112,000 (Mar), 76,000 (Apr), 61,000 (May), 36,000 (Jun), about 28,600 in the latest 30 days. ([SteamCharts](https://steamcharts.com/app/1808500)); reported as "nearly 80%" Steam loss in 2026 ([players.com.ua](https://players.com.ua/en/news/arc-raiders-loses-nearly-80-of-its-steam-player-base-this-year/))
- Nexon reportedly confirmed a 960,000 cross-platform peak in Jan 2026 and about 6 million weekly active players; decline tied partly to players running out of relevant content after long play, which developers acknowledged. ([Beebom tracker, search summary](https://beebom.com/arc-raiders-player-count/amp/))

**Machine enemies: readability, telegraphs, counterplay**
- Embark treated enemies as physical machines that must navigate and survive in the world, blending learned (reinforcement learning) locomotion with behaviour trees. ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/inside-the-magic-of-machine-learning-that-powers-enemy-ai-in-arc-raiders), search summary); GDC 2026 talk "Learning to Move: Physics-Based Enemy Locomotion" (Martin Singh-Blom, Mar 11, 2026) ([GDC schedule](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/learning-to-move-physics-based-enemy-locomotion-in-arc-raiders/917319))
- Director Virgil Watkins avoids humanoid enemies because players become overly critical of humanoid movement and behaviour; ARC were first designed "sleek" and dialled back to highlight weak points. ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/arc-raiders-humanoid-enemies-why-not/), search summary)
- Production director Caio Braga (interview after his GDC 2026 talk): testers first reacted badly to the robots; analytics showed players liked the machine concept but hated specific types because counter-tactics were unclear and balance was off. The Hornet's armour "was simply insane" and players did not understand it; the Sentry had no aiming cone and could shoot in any direction (near 360 degree awareness, "like an aimbot"); the Fireball had no weak point that opens during its attack, which is now the core of fighting it. After reworks sentiment improved significantly. "Suicide bots" were cut as not fun; the Rocketeer was later made more aggressive. ([Shazoo, Mar 14, 2026](https://shazoo.ru/2026/03/14/180994/pervaia-versiia-vragov-v-arc-raiders-byla-sliskom-xardkornoi))
- Guides: the Leaper's most vulnerable window is right after it lands (about two seconds immobile); the Bastion is slow and best fought from cover and behind, with leg joints and back canisters as weak points (sources disagree on exact points). ([The Game Post guide](https://thegamepost.com/arc-raiders-enemy-weaknesses-hidden-weak-points/); [playnews.gg bestiary, search summary](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/arc-raiders-the-complete-arc-bestiary-guide-for-2026-the-13-machines-you-need-to-know-to-survive))
- Frozen Trail update (Oct 8, 2026) added the Bully (closes distance aggressively), the Skulker (stalks the edge of vision before attacking and retreating) and the Hydra (three-part turret guarding the Frigate). Embark said players had converged on favourite weapons, leaving much of the arsenal unused, and added branching weapon upgrades. ([IDC Games, Oct 2, 2026](https://idcgames.com/en/arc-raiders/news/arc-raiders-opens-the-studio-doors-again-to-explain-how-its-first-year-changed-the-game-2026-10-02-09-00-14009))

**Pace and sound**
- Braga at GDC 2026: after the extraction pivot the biggest change was "reducing the speed of everything" by about 60%, giving "slow and deliberate movements" and a stamina bar that "keeps you from zooming across the map". ([TheGamer via OpenCritic, Mar 13, 2026](https://opencritic.com/news/27964/the-first-thing-embark-did-after-making-arc-raiders-an-extraction-shooter-was-making-it-slower)); the slowdown showed how strong the maps, sound design and tool sandbox were ([iXBT](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/03/13/avtory-arc-raiders-tri-goda-sporili-o-koncepcii-zamedlenie-igry-stalo-vaznym-etapom-v-razrabotke.html)); Braga: "We stopped forcing the game we wanted, and we looked at the game we had." ([Inven Global, search summary](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/20259/nexon-reveals-arc-raiders-reboot-story-at-gdc-2026-after-massive-aaa-reset))
- Testing showed "substantial negative PvP sentiment" that turned out to be frustration with weapons and matchmaking, not PvP itself. ([PC Gamer headline](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/third-person-shooter/arc-raiders-received-substantial-negative-pvp-sentiment-during-testing-causing-the-devs-to-second-guess-its-big-pivot-before-realising-it-was-really-frustration-with-weapons-and-matchmaking/))
- Audio: live recordings with microphones at several distances; game logic places sound by surroundings (birds near trees, rain on a car's metal). ([Gaming Nexus, Sep 4, 2025](https://www.gamingnexus.com/News/70875/Embark-details-how-sound-design-is-a-cornerstone-of-ARC-Raiders)); spatialisation is meant to let players read distance and threats from sound instead of UI (search summary of the same coverage).
- Text-to-speech voice lines were the most cited complaint (Eurogamer "mediocre"; some re-recorded with actors by Mar 2026). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders)); one review said the raider voices sound more robotic than the ARC ([ESPN review, search summary](https://africa.espn.com/gaming/story/_/id/46883945/arc-raiders-review))

**Free loadout, gear fear, solo, onboarding**
- ComicBook.com (Jan 19, 2026): free kits ease entry but threaten gear fear; "risk loses its emotional weight" when gear costs nothing, and a free-kit player who kills someone takes a whole loadout. High-tier gear is better "but nowhere near overwhelmingly so". Suggested fixes: restrict free kits in Night Raids, phase them out with progression, or add a modest cooldown. ([ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/arc-raiders-free-kits-are-easing-entry-but-threatening-gear-fear/))
- Counterview: a reviewer said "I love the current system" ([Yahoo review, search summary](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/arc-raiders-review-setting-precedent-200312955.html)); players note that free kits, free basic crafting materials and the safe pocket let you fail often and still have something to work with ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1808500/discussions/0/691994761259663860/), anecdotal)
- New players describe being gunned down by fully kitted players ([Steam thread "New Player Experience is a little punishing"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1808500/discussions/0/600777519165158910/), anecdotal)
- Matchmaking changes: duo matchmaking Nov 12, 2025; from Jan 27, 2026 solos may queue into trios for higher rewards. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders))

**Downed and extraction details**
- Downed players get a long bleed-out (about two minutes per one guide) and can still use extraction terminals; a downed player inside the elevator zone when it departs gets out, and can crawl in if close. ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-extraction-elevators-hatches-and-timers-explained/); [8bittoast](https://8bittoast.com/how-to-extract-arc-raiders/)). Timer figures conflict: one guide says the elevator auto-departs after 90 s, another says about 15 s to open the route and a two-minute auto send. Raider hatches need keys ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders)) and reportedly open with no wait.
- Players share stories of crawling bleeding into the extraction train with their loot ([Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/arc-raiders-wollte-nicht-dass-spieler-extrahiert,1554796)), the kind of moment the downed-extract rule creates.

**Expeditions (progression resets)**
- Players called the first Expedition rewards weak (cosmetic toggles only) and asked "Was it worth it?" ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/third-person-shooter/arc-raiders-left-with-a-grand-question-after-sacrificing-their-characters-for-light-expedition-loot-was-it-worth-it/)); a revised system was blasted for requiring play on specific days and ignoring saved money: "F you if you can't play" ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/third-person-shooter/arc-raiders-players-blast-new-expedition-system-that-requires-you-to-play-on-specific-days-and-ignores-the-money-youve-saved-f-you-if-you-cant-play/)); one player had saved 3 million credits that became largely irrelevant (search summary).
- A 260-hour player said 5 talent points and under 10% bonus did not justify a wipe ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1808500/discussions/0/691995022453707420/), anecdotal); top Reddit comments supported Expeditions as an option, not a requirement (search summary of GamesRadar coverage).
- In Oct 2026 Embark removed Cred because veterans had hoarded enough to finish new reward decks instantly, undercutting them. ([IDC Games](https://idcgames.com/en/arc-raiders/news/arc-raiders-opens-the-studio-doors-again-to-explain-how-its-first-year-changed-the-game-2026-10-02-09-00-14009))

### Inferences
- The Sentry lesson maps straight onto PILLAGERS' "sentry": a turret must show where it is aiming (a cone or sweeping beam) and must not see behind itself, or it reads as an aimbot.
- The Fireball and Leaper lessons are about vulnerable moments in time, not hit locations. That suits his "no weak spots" ruling: a bulwark that drops or lifts its shield for a short beat after a slam gives counterplay without location weak spots. This is a design call for him; flag it rather than build it unasked.
- "Unclear counter-tactics" was the real cause of enemy hate, not difficulty. Each PILLAGERS machine should have one obvious answer a stranger can find in one encounter (flank the bulwark, bait the sentry's sweep, use a barrel).
- Slowing everything 60% made sound and maps matter. PILLAGERS feel tuning should resist speed creep; feel is his call.
- Humanoid enemies get judged harder than machines. PILLAGERS' pillager bots will draw more "dumb AI" criticism than the machines; spending AI polish on the bots is better value.
- Any future progression reset must be optional, additive, and never devalue saved money.

### Gaps
- No primary source on how ARC telegraphs each attack with sound (specific warning sounds per machine); the sound article covers recording, not gameplay cues.
- No exact free-loadout contents or cooldown from an official source.
- Exact current elevator timers unconfirmed.

## 2. Tarkov 1.0, Arena Breakout, Gray Zone Warfare, Marathon, Dark and Darker, Hunt: tension makers and newcomer killers

### Takeaway
Tension comes from loss on death, scarce information and long raids; the same features (no map or compass, 40-minute raids, insurance rules, stash tetris, quest items, long prep) are what reviewers and players cite as hostile to newcomers. Marathon (Mar 2026) shows that good reviews do not save a game that confuses new players: its Steam population fell over 96% from peak.

### Cited Findings
**Escape from Tarkov 1.0 (Steam, Nov 15, 2025)**
- Launch hit by login failures for more than ten hours; Steam reviews "Mixed" or worse; reported positive share 30% in one report, 45% in another (sources conflict). Complaints: pricing, beta keys not carrying over, third-party launcher, cheaters, matchmaking waits; about 47,800 peak Steam concurrent; servers stabilised about a week later. ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/escape-from-tarkov-shoots-to-the-top-of-downdetector-debuts-on-steam-to-mixed-reviews-as-players-struggle-to-log-in-best-extraction-shooter-that-you-cant-play/); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/escape-from-tarkov-1-0-launches-on-steam-with-login-issues-mixed-reviews); [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/escape-from-tarkovs-1-0-release-on-steam-met-with-criticism-over-technical-issues-slow-matchmaking); figures via search summary)
- 1.0 added a story campaign with branching endings, factions, a proper tutorial and the endgame Terminal map, the last reportedly locked behind months of grinding. ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/146943/escape-from-tarkov-10-is-the-same-game-with-extra-steps-but-thats-not-a-bad-thing), search summary)
- CGMagazine 8.5 (Nov 23, 2025): "not a game that holds your hand"; "no maps, no HUD compass, no ammo counters, and no safe zones"; "Death happens fast, and it happens a lot"; "meta-knowledge is essential"; PMC raids are "a 40-minute-long raid"; scav runs join in progress; over 100 weapons, 27 calibres, 182 ammo types. ([CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/escape-from-tarkov-pc/))
- Player critique: the stash is too small without the costly edition, so newcomers spend their time shopping, organising and preparing; beginners die immediately yet loading and prep take too long; insurance invites abuse. ([note.com post](https://note.com/noted_talkov/n/n3d7cf99e7e23?hl=en), single opinion)
- Insurance traps: quest and found-in-raid items may not be insurable; on some endgame maps insured items never return; seasonal characters have no insurance and players learn this after their first raid. ([escapefromtarkov.party guide](https://escapefromtarkov.party/en/guides/insurance-guide/)); a newcomer could not work out what to click in the in-raid inventory ([Steam Q&A](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3932890/discussions/1/686363730790038705/), anecdotal)

**Marathon (Bungie, Mar 5, 2026)**
- Steam peak 88,337 on launch day (Server Slam beta about 143,621). ([Forbes, Mar 6, 2026](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/03/06/the-marathon-day-one-steam-player-count-is-a-bit-concerning/)); 88% positive Steam reviews a month in, PS5 Metacritic 82 ([Forbes, Apr 9, 2026](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/04/09/checking-in-on-marathon-a-month-after-launch/))
- More than 96% below its Steam peak (24-hour peak 3,507) ([The Game Post](https://thegamepost.com/marathon-player-count-96-launch-bungie-season-3/)); Sep 2026 Steam average 1,892, down 33.1% from August ([Tech Insider](https://tech-insider.org/bungie-admits-marathon-flopped-33-percent-drop-2026/)); reportedly 2.2 million players in March ([Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/marathon-reportedly-had-2-2-million-players-in-launch-month/)); an Alinea estimate of 1.2 million copies by Mar 24 (search summary, outlet not confirmed).
- Steam reviews centre on confusing UI, rough onboarding and pressure on casual players ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-reviews-praise-Marathon-while-Reddit-warns-of-punishing-gameplay.1247580.0.html)); opening hours "unforgiving and confusing", new players stand at open containers reading item descriptions ([Military.com review](https://365.military.com/off-duty/games/marathon-review.html), search summary)
- Rook mode: a low-stakes solo run that joins a match in progress (one Rook run started about 13 minutes into a 25-minute Perimeter run); no contracts or progression beyond faction and seasonal XP. ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/148055/how-to-play-as-rook-in-marathon))

**Hunt: Showdown, Arena Breakout Infinite, Dark and Darker, Gray Zone Warfare**
- Hunt: players say the veteran skill gap drives newcomers away; one says losing items "isn't like tarkov" because gear is basic, another that a broke newcomer with a junk hunter feels there is nothing they can do. ([Steam threads](https://steamcommunity.com/app/594650/discussions/8/594021055832545503), anecdotal)
- Arena Breakout Infinite [older, 2024 beta]: praised quality-of-life features and post-raid reports; a casual Normal mode beside the Forbidden Zone, which needs gear worth at least 80,000 Koen; AI hit or miss (fun bosses, but some AI wanders or freezes). ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/arena-breakout-infinite-beta-review/)); aggregator users report hours learning inventory and maps before feeling comfortable ([PlayPile](https://playpile.gg/games/arena-breakout-infinite))
- Gray Zone Warfare is described as tense tactical play against deadly AI that can overwhelm you ([search summary of listicles](https://www.toomuchgaming.net/blog-news/best-extraction-shooters-2026)).

### Inferences
- The newcomer killers are information denial (no map, no ammo count), rule traps (insurance exceptions, quest items), and prep time longer than play. PILLAGERS already has fog and line of sight for tension; it should keep map, ammo and extract locations always readable.
- Pre-raid time should stay a small fraction of a 10-minute raid. Tarkov's stash and trader loop is the cautionary tale.
- Arena Breakout's two-tier entry (casual zone plus gear-gated high-stakes zone) and Marathon's Rook show the genre converging on a "low-stakes lane" for newcomers.

- Found no usable 2025 to 2026 source on Dark and Darker design reception, and no full Gray Zone Warfare or Arena Breakout 2026 review.
- Tarkov 1.0 current Steam rating (Oct 2026) not checked.

## 3. Top-down and 2D extraction games: vision, sound, aim, and what sold

### Takeaway
Top-down PvE extraction is commercially proven: Escape from Duckov (single-player, top-down, $17.99) sold 3 million in about three weeks with a 300,000+ Steam peak, and Zero Sievert (one-person studio) passed 600,000. Both use a vision cone with fog of war so the wide camera does not give away enemies, and both lean on hearing enemies before seeing them. Their main criticisms (no gamepad, mouse-first inventory, static tutorial, loot droughts, slow progression) are openings for a co-op, pad-friendly game.

### Cited Findings
**Escape from Duckov (Team Soda / Bilibili, Oct 16, 2025)**
- 1 million sold by Oct 22, 2 million by Oct 28, 3 million by Nov 8, 2025; peak over 300,000 Steam concurrent; the producer said pre-release that 300,000 units would be good. Metacritic 77, OpenCritic 67% recommend. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escape_from_Duckov)); 500,000 in its first 72 hours ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/duck-themed-extraction-shooter-escape-from-duckov-sold-500-000-steam-copies-in-its-first-3-days/))
- Steam: 93% of 10,727 English reviews positive, recent 89%; $17.99; single-player only; five maps with changing loot, enemies and weather; 50+ weapons; skill trees; Workshop mods. ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3167020/Escape_from_Duckov/))
- Vision: isometric camera balanced by a "light fog of war"; enemies and key items appear only inside your cone, so the wide camera gives no advantage. Sound: quacks and footsteps put you on edge; distant gunfire can mean enemies are fighting each other. Extraction: return to start or reach an extract and hold out until escape arrives. Death: on Balanced you can recover your backpack once (Dark Souls style); higher difficulty loses everything. Guns have durability. Criticisms: no gamepad support, slow progression, random loot causing long droughts of needed items. Score 7.5. ([Worthplaying, Dec 17, 2025](https://worthplaying.com/article/2025/12/17/reviews/148515-pc-review-escape-from-duckov/))
- PC Gamer: the limited sight cone creates tension because you never know if cover hides an enemy (search summary); "you can hear a duck first before you see them" ([GameGrin review](https://www.gamegrin.com/reviews/escape-from-duckov-review/)); headphones recommended ([GameGrin tips](https://www.gamegrin.com/articles/escape-from-duckov-tips-and-tricks-for-beginners/)); ambient sound almost absent, but enemy footsteps are vital for positions outside line of sight ([SDHQ](https://steamdeckhq.com/game-reviews/escape-from-duckov/))
- Framed as Tarkov's loop without punishing PvP, "top-down, arcade-style" ([Final Boss](https://finalboss.io/escape-from-duckov-is-tarkov-like-in-name)); praised for smooth controls but grindy ([Game8](https://game8.co/articles/reviews/escape-from-duckov-review)); accessible but wanting more originality ([Saving Content](https://www.savingcontent.com/2025/10/16/escape-from-duckov-review/))

**Zero Sievert (CABO Studio, 1.0 in 2024)**
- Limited cone of vision (fog of war) like Darkwood, further limited by weather, day and night, foliage and debris ([The Guide Hall](https://theguidehall.com/zero-sievert-review-pixel-s-t-a-l-k-e-r-experience/)); limited cone makes ambushes a constant risk ([God is a Geek](https://godisageek.com/reviews/zero-sievert-review/)); extraction points move each run on randomised maps (search summary)
- "Tense and taxing but wonderfully engaging" ([PC Gamer review, search summary](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/zero-sievert-review/)); criticisms: tutorial is a static image with no hands-on guidance; inventory easier with a mouse ([SteamDeckHQ](https://steamdeckhq.com/game-reviews/zero-sievert/), search summary)
- 1.0 added the Zakov City endgame map, boss and quest line ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/zero-sievert/full-launch-early-access)); over 600,000 copies (updated Jun 22, 2026) ([Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/zero-sievert/info)); one-person studio; $1.3 million first week of early access [older, 2023] ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/01/23/zero-sievert-1-million-revenue-cabo-studio-extraction-shooter-steam)); a Steam review figure of 85% of 8,137 was reported (search summary; count looks low, verify on the store page) ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1782120/ZERO_Sievert/))
- Zero Sievert 2 (announced 2026) aims for console ports and controller support; 100,000 wishlists in two weeks. ([Rutab, May 18, 2026](https://rutab.net/b/video-games/2026/05/18/zero-sievert-2-sobrala-100-000-zhelayuschih-v-steam-za-dve-nedeli.html))

**Others**
- Vaultbreakers: top-down extraction RPG, PvE or PvPvE, solo or with friends, in Steam closed beta. ([Playtester](https://playtester.io/categories/extraction-shooter))
- "Mad Island" is not an extraction shooter in the sources found (a survival crafting game, about 91% positive of 4,131 reviews per an aggregator). ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/2739590/mad_island/stats/details))

### Inferences
- The two biggest top-down hits are single-player and mouse-only. Same-PC 2-player co-op with full Xbox pad support is a clear gap in the niche, and Zero Sievert 2 chasing controllers confirms demand.
- Fog that hides enemies but not terrain is the accepted top-down answer to "the camera sees too much"; PILLAGERS' fog and line of sight are on genre consensus.
- Sound as an out-of-sight radar is the main tension tool in top-down games; reviewers notice when footsteps fail (Duckov bug threads).
- Duckov's loot droughts suggest a floor on key materials (forge parts) so a 10-minute raid never comes back with nothing to upgrade.
- Duckov's body recovery and gun durability are praised by some but are vetoed for PILLAGERS (bodies never come back; guns never wear). Do not adopt.

### Gaps
- No itch.io-specific top-down extraction successes found; no data on browser extraction games.
- "Hyper-style indie games" and "Deadside-likes" could not be matched to top-down titles.
- No official spec of how Duckov's hearing range or direction works.

## 4. PvE-only and co-op extraction: keeping tension without PvP; Helldivers lessons

### Takeaway
Embark's own PvE test (Oct 13 to 20, 2026) is the best primary evidence: it kept tension without PvP by lowering loot value in the safe mode, excluding the highest-tension conditions, and letting machines re-acquire downed players who crawl. Helldivers adds the co-op tools: a pooled reinforcement budget, a mission timer that strips support rather than ending the mission, and a two-minute defend-the-landing-zone extraction with a 20-second boarding window.

### Cited Findings
- ARC Raiders PvE toggle (post Sep 24, 2026; test Oct 13 to 20): zero player damage; solos, duos and trios share sessions; Night Raids, Hidden Bunker, Locked Gate and Frigate excluded because they rely on "the uncertainty of encountering a variety of players"; PvE loot value lowered "to compensate for the relative safety of PvE"; downed players may be re-acquired by ARC while crawling but ignored if they stay still, to "maintain some tension to compensate for the lack of a PvP threat". Survey: 37% friendly, 27% mixed, 34% PvP; over 70% of friendly and about 40% of PvP players want fewer hostile encounters; 51% want a dedicated PvE option. Embark: "It's possible that this will not work." The original concept was raid-based PvE co-op. ([ARC Raiders official](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-beta-test))
- Community view: going loud at a point of interest raises the threat level and brings more AI (anecdotal) ([ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/pve-and-extraction-shooters.1659742/), search summary); counterpoint: AI encounters lose meaning when constant player ambush risk dominates ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1808500/discussions/0/600777309722517701), search summary)
- Helldivers 2 reinforcements: 5 respawns per player (20 for four), trickling back one at a time on a 120-second timer once empty; players often leave with reinforcements unused. ([Helldivers wiki](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/zh/wiki/Flexible_Reinforcement_Budget); [VideoGamer](https://videogamer.com/news/helldivers-2-players-blast-flexible-reinforcement-budget-booster-call-it-an-absolute-troll-pick))
- Helldivers 2 timer: it represents how long the Super Destroyer can support; at zero you lose stratagems and reinforcements but can still extract. Calling the shuttle starts a defend phase of about two minutes; once the first player boards, the rest have 20 seconds. ([Helldivers wiki](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Extraction); [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/helldivers-2-extraction-extract))
- Helldivers 1 [older, 2015]: Reinforce is always available in multiplayer, cooldown 60 s cut to 45 s then 30 s with upgrades; a player can hold the beacon as they die to reinforce themselves. ([Helldivers wiki](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Helldivers_1:Reinforce)); top-down twin-stick with d-pad stratagem codes ([Stevivor](https://stevivor.com/reviews/review-helldivers/))
- Friendly fire: Pilestedt says it stays on and is part of the dark humour [older, 2024]. ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/139269/helldivers-2-friendly-fire-stays-on); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/helldivers-2/friendly-fire))
- Arrowhead (forum quote, weak source): shared resupply, samples dropped on death and reinforcement placement create dynamic "locations"; "don't mimic, understand deeply". ([TechPowerUp forum](https://techpowerup.com/forums/goto/post?id=5217851))

### Inferences
- PILLAGERS' AI-only design matches the majority preference in Embark's data. Tension must come from machines, the clock, weather and the extraction siege.
- "Crawling draws machines, lying still hides you" is a cheap rule that turns the downed state into a decision. It fits his "downed pillagers crawl toward cover" ruling but needs his call.
- Helldivers' timer-strips-support idea could map to the PILLAGERS clock: as time runs out, the forge closes and extracts shut one by one, so the end is a scramble rather than a sudden fail. Check current end-of-timer behaviour first.
- A 20-second "partner is boarding" window suits 2-player same-PC play and the existing 25-second beacon siege.
- Helldivers' friendly fire is central to its chaos, but PILLAGERS has no team damage by his ruling. Get co-op chaos from shared threats instead (barrels, siege waves).
- A pooled respawn budget conflicts with "nobody rejoins a raid" outside the 2-player demo respawn; not recommended for career.

### Gaps
- No post-test results from Embark's PvE toggle yet (test ended Oct 20, 2026).
- No primary Arrowhead source on the design rationale of reinforcement or extraction timers.

## 5. Short sessions: raid length, extraction count, risk curves, 5-minute walk-up play

### Takeaway
Big extraction raids run 25 to 40 minutes, which the genre treats as a barrier; the low-stakes entry modes (Marathon Rook joins with about 12 minutes left; Fortnite Blitz Royale caps matches at 5 minutes and peaked at 443,379 concurrent) show strong demand for short sessions. A 10-minute raid sits between these; for strangers at a party, the first loot, first fight and first extract decision should land within the first 3 to 5 minutes.

### Cited Findings
- Raid lengths: Tarkov PMC 40 minutes ([CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/escape-from-tarkov-pc/)); ARC Raiders up to 30, some maps 40 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders)); Marathon Perimeter 25, Rook can enter about 13 minutes in ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/148055/how-to-play-as-rook-in-marathon)); Duckov's first map takes several hours of runs, no timers mentioned ([Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2025/12/17/reviews/148515-pc-review-escape-from-duckov/))
- Fortnite Blitz Royale (2025): matches five minutes or less, the quickest official Fortnite mode ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/144783/fortnite-blitz-royale-mode)); peaked at 443,379 concurrent; praised as good for newcomers and mobile ([CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/articles/fortnite-blitz-royale-rapid-matches), search summary); extended to Aug 12, 2025 ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/fortnite/fortnites-blitz-royale-extended-to-august-12-with-various-themed-weeks-planned)); permanence claims unverified ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/fortnite-blitz-royale-extended-till-2027-its-claimed/))
- Demo playtime: a Chris Zukowski survey of about 130 games gave a median demo playtime of 14 minutes, average 19.75; top sellers' demos ran 38 to 65 minutes (self-reported, directional). A roughly 90-second window before a festival visitor decides to keep playing is cited but not clearly sourced. ([Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/how-long-should-a-steam-demo-be), search summary); [presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-to-build-game-demo)
- Extraction pacing in co-op: Helldivers' two-minute defend plus 20-second boarding ([GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/helldivers-2-extraction-extract)); ARC's elevator wait (15 s to 2 min, sources conflict) ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-extraction-elevators-hatches-and-timers-explained/))
- Multiple, moving extracts: Zero Sievert moves extraction points each run (search summary of [The Guide Hall](https://theguidehall.com/zero-sievert-review-pixel-s-t-a-l-k-e-r-experience/)); ARC mixes always-open elevators with key-locked hatches that open instantly ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders); [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-extraction-elevators-hatches-and-timers-explained/))

### Inferences
- In 10 minutes the risk curve has to be front-loaded: easy loot and a nearby extract in minutes 0 to 3, the forge and richer loot pulling players outward in 3 to 7, and the siege and clock squeeze in 7 to 10. That gives a party player a full ascend, pillage, extract loop inside 5 minutes if they choose to leave early.
- Extract choice is a risk dial: a near, slow, loud extract (long siege) versus a far, quick, quiet one, in the spirit of ARC's elevator versus hatch.
- Under his "party is the demo; no party mode" ruling, short-session tuning must live in the normal demo, not in a separate party build.
- Prep before a raid should take seconds; strangers will not sit through a stash screen.

### Gaps
- No published data on optimal extraction count per map size, or on risk curves inside short raids.
- No source on walk-up party or convention demo design specific to extraction games.

## 6. Top 15 mechanics or polish features worth stealing for PILLAGERS

### Takeaway
The highest-value steals are about readability and decisions, not new systems: show where machines aim, give each machine one obvious counter, let sound reveal what the fog hides, make extraction a held, shared moment, and keep the first minutes of a raid fast and readable. Several famous mechanics (weak spots, friendly fire, body recovery, gun wear, respawn budgets) conflict with his binding rulings and are listed as do-not-adopt.

### Cited Findings
Each line: mechanic, source game, one-line reason, PILLAGERS fit.
1. Visible aim cone on turrets (ARC Raiders Sentry rework). An omnidirectional Sentry played "like an aimbot" and was hated ([Shazoo](https://shazoo.ru/2026/03/14/180994/pervaia-versiia-vragov-v-arc-raiders-byla-sliskom-xardkornoi)). Fit: PILLAGERS sentry draws a sweeping cone and is blind behind.
2. Vulnerable window after an attack (ARC Fireball opens during its attack; Leaper about 2 s helpless after landing) ([Shazoo](https://shazoo.ru/2026/03/14/180994/pervaia-versiia-vragov-v-arc-raiders-byla-sliskom-xardkornoi); [The Game Post](https://thegamepost.com/arc-raiders-enemy-weaknesses-hidden-weak-points/)). Fit: bulwark shield lowers for a beat after a slam; a timing window, not a weak spot, but ask him given ruling 31.
3. One obvious counter per machine (ARC: hate came from "unclear counter-tactics", e.g. Hornet armour) ([Shazoo](https://shazoo.ru/2026/03/14/180994/pervaia-versiia-vragov-v-arc-raiders-byla-sliskom-xardkornoi)). Fit: bulwark beaten by flanking or a barrel, readable in one encounter.
4. Slow, deliberate pace (ARC cut everything about 60%) ([OpenCritic/TheGamer](https://opencritic.com/news/27964/the-first-thing-embark-did-after-making-arc-raiders-an-extraction-shooter-was-making-it-slower)). Fit: resist speed creep so sound, fog and map matter; feel is his call.
5. Hear before you see (Duckov, Zero Sievert, ARC spatial audio) ([GameGrin](https://www.gamegrin.com/reviews/escape-from-duckov-review/); [SDHQ](https://steamdeckhq.com/game-reviews/escape-from-duckov/)). Fit: footsteps and machine servos outside the cone shown as directional marks at the fog edge; no voices (veto).
6. Fog cone that hides enemies, narrowed by weather and night (Duckov, Zero Sievert) ([Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2025/12/17/reviews/148515-pc-review-escape-from-duckov/); [The Guide Hall](https://theguidehall.com/zero-sievert-review-pixel-s-t-a-l-k-e-r-experience/)). Fit: rain and fog shrink sight range, tying weather to tension.
7. Edge-of-vision stalker machine (ARC Skulker, Oct 2026) ([IDC Games](https://idcgames.com/en/arc-raiders/news/arc-raiders-opens-the-studio-doors-again-to-explain-how-its-first-year-changed-the-game-2026-10-02-09-00-14009)). Fit: a machine that lingers just outside the cone uses the fog system for scares; new feature, only on his order.
8. Downed players can crawl into extraction and still escape (ARC) ([allthings.how](https://allthings.how/arc-raiders-extraction-elevators-hatches-and-timers-explained/)). Fit: a downed pillager inside the zone at lift-off counts as extracted; creates "crawl to the ship" stories.
9. Crawling draws machines, lying still hides you (ARC PvE test) ([ARC official](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-beta-test)). Fit: makes the downed state a choice between waiting for the partner and crawling for cover.
10. Defend-then-board extraction with a partner window (Helldivers: about 2 min defend, 20 s to board after the first) ([GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/helldivers-2-extraction-extract)). Fit: the 25 s beacon siege plus a short "partner boarding" window in 2P.
11. Clock that strips support instead of ending the raid (Helldivers timer) ([Helldivers wiki](https://helldivers.wiki.gg/wiki/Extraction)). Fit: late in the raid the forge shuts and extracts close one by one, so the last minute is a scramble.
12. Risk-graded loot (ARC lowered loot value in the safe PvE mode) ([ARC official](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-beta-test)). Fit: richest containers sit near machines, in bad weather or far from extracts.
13. Low-stakes entry kit (ARC free loadout; Marathon Rook) ([ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/feature/arc-raiders-free-kits-are-easing-entry-but-threatening-gear-fear/); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/148055/how-to-play-as-rook-in-marathon)). Fit: "come back empty" should never mean unable to play; a basic kit keeps the next raid one click away, and with AI-only raids the gear-fear exploit (free kits killing loaded players) does not exist.
14. Safe pocket (ARC) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders)). Fit: PILLAGERS already has one; keep it prominent, since players cite it as what makes failure bearable ([Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1808500/discussions/0/691994761259663860/)).
15. Pad-first readable UI (ARC criticised for small fonts and KB+M-only feel; Duckov criticised for no gamepad; Zero Sievert 2 adding controllers) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARC_Raiders); [Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2025/12/17/reviews/148515-pc-review-escape-from-duckov/); [Rutab](https://rutab.net/b/video-games/2026/05/18/zero-sievert-2-sobrala-100-000-zhelayuschih-v-steam-za-dve-nedeli.html)). Fit: large text and pad-friendly loot and stash are a real gap in the top-down niche.

Runners-up: post-raid report of what happened ([Insider Gaming on Arena Breakout](https://insider-gaming.com/arena-breakout-infinite-beta-review/), [older]; PILLAGERS has a KIA/EXTRACTED recap); noise raises threat (anecdotal, [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/pve-and-extraction-shooters.1659742/)); a material floor against loot droughts (Duckov criticism, [Worthplaying](https://worthplaying.com/article/2025/12/17/reviews/148515-pc-review-escape-from-duckov/)).

Do not adopt (conflict with his rulings or proven to hurt): location weak spots (his 10-13 ruling); friendly fire ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/139269/helldivers-2-friendly-fire-stays-on); PILLAGERS has no team damage); backpack recovery (Duckov; bodies never come back); gun durability (Duckov; guns never wear); text-to-speech or any voice lines (ARC's top complaint; his no-voices veto); mandatory wipes that devalue savings ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/third-person-shooter/arc-raiders-players-blast-new-expedition-system-that-requires-you-to-play-on-specific-days-and-ignores-the-money-youve-saved-f-you-if-you-cant-play/)); Tarkov-style information denial and insurance rules ([CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/game/escape-from-tarkov-pc/)); suicide bots (cut by Embark as not fun, [Shazoo](https://shazoo.ru/2026/03/14/180994/pervaia-versiia-vragov-v-arc-raiders-byla-sliskom-xardkornoi)); static-image tutorials (Zero Sievert criticism); also note his Q70 bans tutorial prompts, so teach through the first room's layout instead.

### Inferences
- Items 1 to 6, 8, 12, 14 and 15 are tuning or readability work on systems PILLAGERS already has (sentry, bulwark, sound rings, fog, weather, downed, containers, safe pocket, pad support), so they fit "orders and data before changes". Items 7, 9, 10, 11 and 13 are new behaviour and need his order.
- For the Fri 10/30 party, the cheapest high-impact items are 1 (sentry aim cone), 3 (obvious bulwark counter), 5 (out-of-sight sound marks) and 8 (crawl-in extraction), because strangers learn machines in one encounter and remember a dramatic extract.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies how much telegraph readability improves retention; the ARC evidence is Embark's qualitative sentiment shift.
- Fortnite's specific lessons (rarity colours, loot readability) were not researched for lack of a 2025 to 2026 source; only Blitz Royale's short format is covered.
