Research date: Sun 2026-10-25. Web search and web fetch only. Sources older than 2024 are marked "(older, YEAR)". Where a finding came from a search-result summary and the page itself was not fetched, it says "(search summary, page not fetched)". Notes avoid payment, tax and store-account setup on purpose.

Project rulings that limit how these findings apply (from the project memory index, not from the web): co-op has no team damage (09-25); the party demo is plain localhost plus clicking the demo, with no launcher, no ?party mode and no party extras (10-19); no rejoining a raid (09-13); player text is all lower case short callouts and the lore word is "ascend"; P1 is amber and P2 is cyan (10-18); a "magnet" was removed on 10-15 (most likely the loot magnet, check before calling any aim help "magnetism").

## 1. Same-screen and same-PC co-op: camera, readability, revive and downed design, catch-up and leash rules, co-op moments

### Takeaway
Shared-screen co-op works because both players see the same events ("virtual body language"), so the camera must never let either player leave the frame: follow the midpoint, zoom out to a clamped limit, then leash or pull the straggler. Revive systems in shipped games use a bleed-out window of roughly 25 to 50 seconds that pauses while a teammate is reviving and shrinks on repeat downs, and the best co-op moments come from systems that make players want to be near each other (shared resupply, shared risk), not from forced roles.

### Cited Findings
**Camera and screen layout**
- Shared screen gives players "virtual body language": each player can see what the other is doing, which reduces the need for callouts. Split screen roughly doubles render cost and changes each view's aspect ratio, so solo-tuned feedback can be lost. (older, 2011) - [Richard Terrell, Game Developer, "Shared-Multi-Split Screen Design"](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/shared-multi-split-screen-design)
- Ways shipped games handle a player who leaves the shared view: teleport the lagging player forward (Donkey Kong Country Returns), kill a player who stays off screen too long (LittleBigPlanet), invisible walls that keep characters in view (New Super Mario Bros. Wii), zoom out to fit everyone, and a dynamic split once the zoom limit is reached (Bionic Commando Rearmed). Zooming out lowers visual feedback fidelity. (older, 2011) - [Terrell, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/shared-multi-split-screen-design)
- The same article recommends distinct audio cues per player so each player can tell which sound is theirs. (older, 2011) - [Terrell, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/shared-multi-split-screen-design)
- Standard shared-camera recipe: put the camera focus at the midpoint between the two players and lengthen the camera distance (zoom out) as they separate, every tick. - [Unreal Community Wiki, "Creating a shared camera for multiplayer games"](https://unrealcommunity.wiki/creating-a-shared-camera-for-multiplayer-games-rqedrb3w)
- Clamp the zoom so the camera does not pull out too far; zoom when a player reaches the screen edge. - [Godot Forum, "How do I make camera follow and zoom to 2 players?"](https://forum.godotengine.org/t/how-do-i-make-camera-follow-and-zoom-to-2-players/17203)
- Another option is to limit how far apart the players can be. - [Hacker News thread on local multiplayer perspective](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=38841328)
- Brotato (top-down arena shooter with local co-op) zooms its shared camera in and out during waves, and players asked for and use an option to fix the zoom to full screen when the zoom is distracting. (search summary, page not fetched) - [Steam discussion, Brotato](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1942280/discussions/0/4634860269337666778)
- A UE5 add-on combines both: track both players and zoom; when max zoom is hit, split the screen dynamically so relative positions stay readable. - [80.lv, dynamic split screen add-on](https://80.lv/articles/this-new-add-on-lets-you-add-dynamic-split-screen-to-your-unreal-engine-projects)

**Helldivers (the top-down original) and Arrowhead's co-op lessons**
- Arrowhead on the top-down original: "everyone knows what the team members are doing and shares the same reality"; the top-down distance gave shared awareness but "does not allow for more immersive experiences." (Mar 2024) - [PlayStation Blog, "Helldivers 2: how Arrowhead turned the twin-stick shooter original into a bombastic PS5 sequel"](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/03/12/helldivers-2-how-arrowhead-turned-the-twin-stick-shooter-original-into-a-bombastic-ps5-sequel/)
- Arrowhead's co-op rule: "A good co-op game forces players to play together, but a great one makes players want to play together." Their goal is that players feel no one can succeed alone. (Mar 2024) - [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/03/12/helldivers-2-how-arrowhead-turned-the-twin-stick-shooter-original-into-a-bombastic-ps5-sequel/)
- For the sequel they built a "watering hole": a shared resupply drop, sample containers dropped where a player dies, and reinforcement placement, all of which pull players back to one spot. (Mar 2024) - [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/03/12/helldivers-2-how-arrowhead-turned-the-twin-stick-shooter-original-into-a-bombastic-ps5-sequel/)
- The original Helldivers shipped 4-player local and online co-op in any mix, with full friendly fire. (older, 2015) - [Co-Optimus, Helldivers launch co-op info](https://www.co-optimus.com/amparticle/12894/helldivers-launch-trailer-and-co-op-information.html)
- A review credits limited ammo plus the constant threat of friendly fire for making it a "challenging and calculated shooter." (older, 2015) - [Game Informer review](https://gameinformer.com/games/helldivers/b/playstation4/archive/2015/03/03/helldivers-review-game-informer.aspx)
- Arrowhead's CEO said in Sept 2025 that local split screen for Helldivers 2 is "technically feasible" and on a "to investigate" list, which shows couch co-op is still a live request in 2025. - [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/helldivers-2-co-op-split-screen-is-technically-feasible-says-ceo/); [Multiplayer.it](https://multiplayer.it/notizie/co-op-schermo-condiviso-helldivers-2-tecnicamente-possibile-arrowhead.html)

**Enter the Gungeon co-op (revive and catch-up)**
- Player 2 must play The Cultist. A dead partner comes back at full health when the living player opens a chest (the partner bursts out instead of loot, at no cost to the living player), when a chest is destroyed, or after the living player beats the floor boss. The Cultist's Friendship Cookie revives the partner, and the passive "Number 2" buffs the survivor while the partner is dead. - [Enter the Gungeon Wiki, The Cultist](https://enterthegungeon.fandom.com/wiki/The_Cultist)
- Community complaint: the cookie works until the second death, after which one player can sit out for a long time. (search summary, page not fetched) - [Steam discussion, Enter the Gungeon co-op](https://steamcommunity.com/app/311690/discussions/0/135512555423296736)

**Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime (couch co-op communication)**
- Asteroid Base built the single-player mode first, with an AI space pet in place of player 2 commanded by a radial menu, and used it as a test of whether the co-op rules were clear enough to explain. "Building the single player mode informed our ability to do co-op design." (older, 2015) - [Game Developer, "Co-op communication in Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime"](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/co-op-communication-in-i-lovers-in-a-dangerous-spacetime-i-)
- Color-coded power-ups that restricted which player could use them confused players and were dropped; letting players pick their own roles worked better because testers liked to stick to one task. (older, 2015) - [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/co-op-communication-in-i-lovers-in-a-dangerous-spacetime-i-)
- Critical actions were kept simple so "pretty much anyone can jump onto the couch and have a fun time." (older) - [Nerdist interview with Asteroid Base](https://nerdist.com/article/interview-asteroid-base-talks-lovers-in-a-dangerous-spacetime/)

**Co-op moment frameworks**
- Daniel Cook's GDC 2024 talk "Kind Games: Designing for Prosocial Multiplayer" lists interdependence and safety as core values and flags "toxic altruism" (the cost of helping falls on one person) as an anti-pattern. (search summary; the slides PDF is not machine readable) - [GDC 2024 slides, Daniel Cook](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2024/Slides/GDC+slide+presentations/Kind+Games+-+GDC+2024+-+Daniel+Cook.pdf)
- Shared failure states (restarting, losing progress together) create a shared sense of risk and urgency, but also produce blame. It Takes Two and Unravel Two build interdependence with simultaneous actions and complementary abilities. - [Digital Thriving Playbook, "Beyond Co-Op: A Spectrum of Cooperation"](https://digitalthrivingplaybook.org/guide/beyond-co-op-a-spectrum-of-cooperation/)
- The Trust Spectrum framework layers shared versus separate resource pools on top of roles; shared pools are the "risk" lever. (older, 2018) - [Raph Koster, "The Trust Spectrum"](https://www.raphkoster.com/2018/03/16/the-trust-spectrum/)

**Downed and revive numbers from shipped games**
- Delta Force: first bleed-out timer 50 s, second knockdown 40 s, third and later 25 s; carrying a downed teammate pauses the timer (first timer only). - [HiTech Gazette, Delta Force medic mechanics](https://hitechgazette.com/hidden-mechanics-for-medics-in-delta-force/)
- Project Lazarus (zombies style): 45 s bleed-out, and the timer pauses while a revive is in progress, so a player being revived cannot die from the timer. - [Project Lazarus Wiki, Downed Players](https://project-lazarus.fandom.com/wiki/Downed_Players)
- Gears of War 3: being downed opens a short bleed-out window for a revive, but explosives and head shots kill outright and skip the window. - [Wikipedia, Gears of War 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gears_of_War_3)
- A Warzone design pitch trades mobility for time: crawl to safety with a slightly faster bleed-out, or hold still to slow it. - [Medium, CoD Warzone teammate drag mechanic pitch](https://trigrows.medium.com/call-of-duty-warzone-new-mechanic-one-page-pitch-b7601e8e575d)

**Player colors**
- The Okabe-Ito palette was designed to stay distinguishable under common color vision deficiencies; its orange is #E69F00 and its sky blue is #56B4E9. Nature Methods recommends it. - [NYU Siegal Lab color palette page](https://siegal.bio.nyu.edu/color-palette/); [R thematic docs, okabe_ito](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/thematic/html/okabe_ito.html)
- Game Accessibility Guidelines (Basic): "Ensure no essential information is conveyed by a fixed colour alone." - [Game Accessibility Guidelines, full list](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/)

### Inferences
- PILLAGERS already uses a flat top-down sim with one shared 3/4 view, so the Terrell options map directly: midpoint follow, a clamped zoom-out, then a soft leash at the clamp (invisible wall or a pull) is the cheapest correct rule. Teleport or off-screen death would feel harsh in a 10-minute extraction raid with loot on the line.
- P1 amber and P2 cyan are close to the Okabe-Ito orange and sky blue pair, which is a good, colorblind-safe pairing. To meet the GAG Basic rule, each player also needs a non-color cue (a "p1"/"p2" tag, ring shape or outline pattern).
- Arrowhead's friendly fire rationale does not transfer (his 09-25 ruling: no team damage). The transferable lessons are the "want to play together" pull: shared resupply, a death drop that pulls the partner back, and objectives that are faster with two.
- Enter the Gungeon shows the risk of a dead partner sitting out. In a 10-minute raid a dead P2 could watch for up to several minutes, which is bad at a walk-up party. Any fix has to respect his "no rejoining a raid" ruling (09-13), so this is a question to log for him, not a change to make.
- Revive design that the numbers support: keep the bleed-out window in the 25 to 50 s range used by shipped games, pause it while a revive is in progress, and shrink it on repeat downs. His 10-18 note "downed +0.5s" suggests the current timer is already tuned; compare against these ranges, do not reset it.

### Gaps
- No primary 2024 to 2026 source on off-screen player indicators (edge arrows, clamped markers) for shared-screen co-op was found.
- No developer source found for Overcooked, It Takes Two, Ultimate Chicken Horse, Synthetik or Nuclear Throne co-op camera or revive rules; the Helldivers 1 camera leash behavior itself was not documented in any source found.
- Daniel Cook's GDC 2024 slide text could not be read, so only the headline concepts are cited.

## 2. Game feel ("juice") for top-down shooters with numbers: hit stop, shake, recoil, muzzle flash, hit markers, hit reactions, sound, rumble, gamepad aim assist, over-juicing

### Takeaway
The best-documented top-down shooter numbers are Nuclear Throne's per-shot recipe (camera kicks 6 px back from the aim point, +4 shake that decays fast, enemy knockback 3 px per frame, a 10 to 20 ms freeze on every hit) and a 2026 guide's tiers (hit stop 35/70/90 ms capped at 120 ms; shake 4/8/12 px decaying over 0.2 to 0.3 s). Controlled studies say medium-to-high juice beats both none and extreme, and amplified feedback that is not tied to player success can lower motivation. For pads, use a radial dead zone around 0.3, smooth the aim, and keep assist small and invisible (Death's Door corrects at most 15 degrees; Hades can snap up to 90).

### Cited Findings
**Per-shot recipe (Nuclear Throne)**
- One pistol shot in Nuclear Throne: the camera moves back 6 px from the aim point; "add 4 to the screenshake," which decays quickly; gun kick set to 2 (sprite recoils then slides back); the bullet is a circle on its first frame then a bullet shape (a fake muzzle flash); shells eject at 2 to 4 px per frame, 100 to 150 degrees off the aim direction; bullet speed 16 px per frame with 0 to 4 degrees of random spread; the hit enemy moves 3 px per frame in the bullet's direction; the game freezes about 10 to 20 ms on any hit. Values are at 30 fps on a 320x240 screen. (older, 2013; quotes Rock Paper Shotgun's 21 Oct 2013 interview with Jan Willem Nijman) - [Tom Armitage, infovore.org, "Making Game Feel"](https://infovore.org/tag/feel/)
- Jan Willem Nijman's "The Art of Screenshake" talk followed a 2012 Nordic Game Jam talk on juicy effects by Martin Jonasson and Petri Purho ("Juice it or lose it"). (older, 2012 and 2013) - [infovore.org](https://infovore.org/tag/feel/)

**Hit stop and screen shake tiers**
- Hit stop scaled to blow weight: 35 ms light, 70 ms heavy, 90 ms perfect; cap at about 120 ms because longer pauses read as a dropped frame. The sim freezes while rendering continues. (Sep 2026) - [Sword Arcade, "Game Feel: Hit-Stop and Screen Shake Explained"](https://swordarcade.xyz/guides/game-feel-hitstop-and-screen-shake/)
- Shake amplitude 4 px light hit, 8 px heavy hit, 12 px when the player takes damage, random displacement decaying over 0.2 to 0.3 s. Shake only the play area inside its clip rectangle so letterbox edges stay still, and displace rendering only (hit detection, positions and collision use true coordinates). (Sep 2026) - [Sword Arcade](https://swordarcade.xyz/guides/game-feel-hitstop-and-screen-shake/)
- Over-juicing: stacking four effects at full strength on every light hit makes the screen "constantly convulse" and makes routine hits look important. Give light hits a little of every effect, scale up for heavy hits, and tune by removing each effect and seeing what is lost. (Sep 2026) - [Sword Arcade](https://swordarcade.xyz/guides/game-feel-hitstop-and-screen-shake/)
- Hit stop and shake turn off automatically under the system reduced-motion setting; floating score labels still draw because they carry information. (Sep 2026) - [Sword Arcade](https://swordarcade.xyz/guides/game-feel-hitstop-and-screen-shake/)

**What the studies say about how much juice**
- Kao (2020, Entertainment Computing): four versions of one action RPG (no, medium, high, extreme juice). Medium and high beat none and extreme on player experience, intrinsic motivation, play time and in-game performance. (secondary summary in a survey paper) - [arXiv 2011.09201, "Designing Game Feel. A Survey"](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.09201)
- Kao, Ballou, Gerling, Breitsohl, Deterding, CHI 2024, pre-registered, 1,699 participants: feedback that depends on success raised all measured motives; amplification alone unexpectedly reduced them (possibly by weakening the sense of agency); curiosity was the strongest predictor of enjoyment and the only predictor of playtime. - [Imperial College Spiral, "How does juicy game feedback motivate?"](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/entities/publication/253c32f5-d124-4a23-88b5-9aaaf114608d)
- A small 2016 preliminary study found players rated the juicy version higher but performed worse in it. (older, 2016; search summary) - [Elsevier Pure, "Good feedback for bad players"](https://adk.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/good-feedback-for-bad-players-a-preliminary-study-of-juicy-interf)

**Enemy hit reactions**
- White hit flash via a flash shader on the sprite for a short duration; a common bug is every enemy flashing at once because they share one material, and another is a timeScale (hit stop) that never recovers. - [Uhiyama Lab, Unity game feel primer](https://uhiyama-lab.com/en/notes/unity/unity-game-feel-hit-feedback/)
- Knockback direction should be from the bullet's position to the enemy, scaled by a knockback speed, so enemies are never pushed toward the player. - [Godot Forums, "Adding knockback to my top down game"](https://godotforums.org/d/24294-adding-knockback-to-my-top-down-game)
- In top-down views, a short hurt animation played just before knockback strengthens the illusion of impact. (search summary) - [Construct forum, pushback on hit](https://www.construct.net/en/forum/construct-2/how-do-i-18/pushback-hit-57708)

**Gamepad aiming and aim assist (twin stick)**
- Raw stick noise on good hardware is about 0.05 to 0.15; use a radial dead zone (length of the 2D vector) with an example threshold of 0.3; a per-axis dead zone makes diagonals "sticky." Never snap aim to the stick; interpolate even at about 12 degrees per frame at 60 fps, keep separate input and displayed directions, and ignore tiny differences to filter jitter. (older, 2015) - [Mark Venturelli, Game Developer, "Everything I Learned About Dual-Stick Shooter Controls"](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/everything-i-learned-about-dual-stick-shooter-controls)
- Same source: sticky aim (slower aim rotation when an enemy is on the aim path) should be subtle and switchable in Settings; auto-aim used a narrow cone of about 30 degrees, snapped, and changed the laser sight color to show it was active; check the angle after projecting the input onto the target so accuracy does not depend on distance; a gradual "nudge" approach caused drift and was dropped. (older, 2015) - [Venturelli, Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/everything-i-learned-about-dual-stick-shooter-controls)
- Same source: actions needed while aiming belong on triggers and shoulder buttons (thumb is on the right stick); keep them to about 2, never more than 3 or 4. Tween camera moves rather than snapping, which also cuts motion sickness. (older, 2015) - [Venturelli, Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/everything-i-learned-about-dual-stick-shooter-controls)
- Axial dead zones snap the stick toward cardinal directions in sweeping motions, which hurts twin-stick aiming; a radial dead zone fixes it, and for twin-stick games direction matters more than magnitude. (older) - [Game Developer, "Doing thumbstick dead zones right"](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/doing-thumbstick-dead-zones-right)
- A video essay that reverse-engineered both games: Hades can snap the attack by up to 90 degrees toward the target closest to the aim direction; Death's Door corrects by no more than 15 degrees and never shows the correction. Target selection filters valid targets, then picks the one closest to the input direction within a distance and arc. (transcript only, unverified) - [VoiceTube transcript of the aim assist video essay](https://tw.voicetube.com/videos/173139)
- A 2026 university project documents a twin-stick-style config: inner cone about 3 degrees with full pull, outer cone about 8 degrees where pull falls to zero, capped rotational pull, and no pull from stationary targets (assist only helps track movers), plus a separate slowdown while on target. (student project, Spring 2026) - [UCSD CSE 125 group 2, GamepadAimAssistConfig](https://cse125.ucsd.edu/2026/cse125g2/docs/structsystems_1_1GamepadAimAssistConfig.html)
- GAG (Intermediate): "Include assist modes such as auto-aim and assisted steering." - [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/)

**Controller rumble in the browser**
- The Gamepad API's `vibrationActuator.playEffect("dual-rumble", {duration, startDelay, strongMagnitude, weakMagnitude})` takes duration in ms and two magnitudes from 0.0 to 1.0 (strong = heavy motor, weak = light motor); it returns a promise; the feature is not Baseline, is inconsistent across browsers, and needs a secure context. Feature-detect `vibrationActuator` and check `effects` for "dual-rumble". - [MDN, GamepadHapticActuator.playEffect()](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/GamepadHapticActuator/playEffect); [web.dev, Gamepad](https://web.dev/gamepad)
- The large motor gives a heavy low-frequency thud (explosions, crashes); the small motor gives a high-frequency buzz for subtle effects. - [Evetech, dual rumble motors](https://evezone.evetech.co.za/deep-dives/dual-rumble-motors-vibration-settings); [W3C webapps list, weak+strong rumble discussion (older, 2018)](https://lists.w3.org/Archives/Public/public-webapps-github/2018Mar/0336.html)
- Rumble should be tied to specific events; constant or arbitrary rumble becomes noise, so save strong effects for explosions and damage. An example value "80% for 120 ms" is given as an illustration, not a standard. - [Bugnet, "How to add controller rumble that enhances feel"](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-add-controller-rumble-that-enhances-feel)
- XAG 110 is a dedicated guideline on haptic feedback (players should be able to adjust or turn it off; deep dive not fetched). - [Xbox Accessibility Guidelines index](https://devdocs.xbox.com/build/game-principles/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines.md)

### Inferences
- The Nuclear Throne numbers are at 320x240 and 30 fps. PILLAGERS renders at 1080p to 4K at 60 fps, so pixel values need scaling (roughly x4.5 at 1080p for the same on-screen size, and halved per-frame speeds at 60 fps). Treat them as proportions, not absolutes.
- The CHI 2024 result supports tying juice to success (kills, crits, forge upgrades, extraction) rather than raising it everywhere. It also argues for making the shake and flash budget scale with event weight.
- Localhost (the party PC) and itch.io (HTTPS) are both secure contexts, so dual-rumble should work in Chrome or Edge with an Xbox pad; it should still be optional and fail silently.
- Death's Door style assist (small, invisible, under about 15 degrees, only toward moving targets) fits a walk-up crowd better than a Hades-style snap, and it keeps KB+M parity. Check his 10-15 "magnet removed" ruling before naming or shipping anything as "magnetism."

### Gaps
- No primary source found with measured hit marker timings, muzzle flash frame counts or sound layering recipes for top-down shooters from 2024 to 2026.
- No platform guideline (Microsoft, Sony) with recommended millisecond ranges for gunfire or explosion rumble was found.
- The original "Juice it or lose it" (2012) and "The Art of Screenshake" (2013) talk contents were not available as text beyond the shot recipe above.

## 3. Walk-up demos and party play: setup, attract mode, short sessions, controls card, understanding a game in 30 seconds, lessons from expo developers

### Takeaway
Developers who have run show-floor demos converge on: keep the session short and easier than you think, use a controller, put the game's own name everywhere, loop gameplay when idle to draw a line, reset after about a minute of no input, and give exactly one spoken objective when asked. A web-demo guide puts the keep-or-quit decision at about 90 seconds, and a 2026 browser launch postmortem found most players left in the first two minutes because the hook only showed up later.

### Cited Findings
- One screen looping the trailer drew a crowd that then formed a natural line for the two playable screens; the top banner showed the company name instead of the game, so visitors asked to play a game that did not exist. Show one game and point all signage at it. (older, 2014) - [Brian McRae, Game Developer, "5 Expo Tips For Indies"](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/5-expo-tips-for-indies)
- When a player asks what to do, give one verbal objective; otherwise let them explore and watch. After a few minutes ask for impressions. Busy booths draw more people ("crowded restaurant effect"). Wearable buttons outlasted business cards as handouts. (older, 2014) - [McRae, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/5-expo-tips-for-indies)
- Throughput math: a 30-minute demo on one station over 8 hours a day for 4 days lets only 64 people play, so shorten the demo or add stations. Their title screen cuts to recorded gameplay after 20 s idle, other games play themselves with AI, and some games reset after 1 minute with no controller input. (older, 2015; search summary, page returned 403) - [Ryan Evans, "Tips on demoing your game in public"](https://rjevans.net/post/130569645275/tips-on-demoing-your-game-in-public)
- Keep the event demo short (around 10 minutes), make the difficulty lower than you think, use a controller instead of many keyboard buttons, and have a takeaway such as a Discord link. (older, 2019/2020; search summary) - [Bearmask, G-Star 2019 postmortem](https://bearmask.itch.io/lofi-room/devlog/133337/g-star-2019-postmortem-big-indie-pitch-win)
- At an expo, players missed warning indicators and did not understand the leaderboard controls, so they submitted empty names; the developer hot-fixed on site and kept a to-do list during play. (search summary) - [theonixie, "Post-Expo Thoughts"](https://theonixie.itch.io/conduit/devlog/707916/post-expo-thoughts)
- Observing players directly was the most useful feedback at a first conference. (search summary) - [ttkover, "Path Of The Indie: Our First Game Conference"](https://ttkover.itch.io/saturated-outer-space/devlog/186104/path-of-the-indie-our-first-game-conference)
- Kiosk convention: a 30 to 90 s idle timeout with a visible countdown and a way to cancel. - [Kiosk Industry, idle timeout](https://kioskindustry.org/kiosk-tips-self-service-contingency-planning/); Edge kiosk mode prompts after the idle period and resets 30 s later if nobody responds. - [Intune settings, Edge kiosk reset after idle](https://intunesettings.app/setting/user_vendor_msft_policy_config_browser_configurekioskresetafteridletimeout/)
- An open-source kiosk exposes its idle (90 s) and reset (20 s) timers as settings so testers can shorten them to seconds before the event. - [GitHub PR, kiosk inactivity timeout with countdown](https://github.com/aditi-dewangan/dubhacks-26/pull/4)
- A demo visitor gives you about 90 seconds before deciding whether to keep playing, so the opening must show genre, hook and visual identity. (2025/2026 guide) - [presskit.gg, "How to build a game demo"](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-to-build-game-demo)
- Get the player into the interesting part fast and let them feel competent quickly. - [Wayline, "Crafting Compelling Demo Loops"](https://wayline.io/blog/crafting-compelling-demo-loops)
- A solo browser game launch (vanilla JS and Canvas2D, 2026): the first two minutes read as generic, so most players and portal reviewers left before the differentiators, which only appeared around run two. Lesson: "fix the first session before driving traffic" and show the differentiators early. - [itch.io forum, launch week postmortem, solo browser TD](https://itch.io/t/6645181/launch-week-postmortem-solo-browser-td-product-hunt-a-portal-rejection-and-a-flagged-show-hn-numbers-inside)
- Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime's lead programmer recommends testing early and often on show floors (PAX, GDC, indie festivals) and watching how partners interact, because relationship dynamics clash with game systems in ways you cannot predict. (older, 2015) - [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/co-op-communication-in-i-lovers-in-a-dangerous-spacetime-i-)
- Large character cutouts visible from far away pulled passersby to an indie booth at GDC and PAX West, and the booth leaned on visuals to set tone in the first glance. (2024) - [Carper Creative case study](https://carpercreative.com/case-study-building-and-managing-an-indie-game-booths-for-gdc-and-pax/)

### Inferences
- A party is a smaller version of a booth: one station, two pads, a queue of strangers. The 64-player math says a full 10-minute raid lets about 6 pairs play per hour. A shorter first raid (or a natural early extraction) lets more guests play.
- His 10-19 ruling ("party is the demo: localhost plus click the demo; no launcher, no ?party mode, no party extras") means attract loops and idle resets should only be added if they are normal game behavior that also ships to itch (for example, the title screen showing live gameplay behind it, or a results screen that returns to the title after a countdown). Log the question rather than building a party-only mode.
- "One objective when asked" maps to one lower case callout at raid start (for example "loot. then extract.") rather than any explaining sentence, which fits his player-text rules.
- The browser TD postmortem and the 90-second figure both say the hook (in PILLAGERS: the forge and the extraction tension) needs to show inside the first raid's first minute or two.

### Gaps
- No 2025 or 2026 developer write-up from PAX, EGX, Day of the Devs or local meetups with concrete walk-up session lengths was found; most expo sources are 2014 to 2020.

## 4. Playtesting methods for a solo designer: what to watch, think-aloud vs silent, metrics, heatmaps, surveys, friends and family

### Takeaway
Watch silently, give one objective only when asked, then ask a few questions afterward; think-aloud during play is taxing and breaks immersion, so a retrospective review of a recording is the safer variant. Log deaths with location, time to first kill, session length, quick deaths and quit points, and treat a complaint as real when three or more people raise it independently. Friends and family over-report what they think you want to hear, so lean on behavior data over their opinions.

### Cited Findings
- Think-aloud is common in playtesting but is cognitively taxing, needs players who can verbalize, can be biased by the facilitator, and pulls players out of the game. - [IGI Global, "Playing Aloud"](https://igi-global.com/article/playing-aloud/296705)
- Death heatmap rule: if most players die at the same spot, that is a design flaw, not hardcore difficulty. Pair telemetry with retrospective think-aloud, where players watch their recording and explain what they were thinking. (2026 vendor guide) - [Articos, "Games User Research: The Ultimate 2026 Guide"](https://www.articos.com/blog/games-user-research)
- Basic telemetry list: deaths, clears, session length, rage-quit points, exit points; "rage quit" markers are rapid deaths (under 15 s) and menu exits without progress (author's heuristics, 2025). - [Medium, "Entering Indie Games in 2025: A Senior Engineer's Go-to-Market Playbook"](https://medium.com/design-bootcamp/entering-indie-games-in-2025-a-senior-engineers-go-to-market-playbook-cdc507f3bf0f)
- Asynchronous testing: players play alone and record sessions or fill in a questionnaire afterwards; data sources are closed and open survey questions, recordings, and automatic metrics like completion time and error counts. (about 2024) - [Game Developer, "6 steps to a successful playtesting process for an indie developer"](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/6-steps-to-a-successful-playtesting-process-for-an-indie-developer)
- Testing only with friends, family or coworkers misses the real audience; response bias (participants say what the researcher seems to want) gets worse when the developer runs the test. (older) - [Game Developer, "UX walkthrough: anatomy of a usability test in video games, part 1"](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/ux-walkthrough-anatomy-of-a-usability-test-in-video-games-part-1-)
- If 3 or more people independently complain about something, treat it as a serious problem; take a week to a month away from tuning controls before judging them again; switch between mouse and controller for a week at a time to find issues on each. (older, 2015) - [Venturelli, Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/everything-i-learned-about-dual-stick-shooter-controls)
- 2026 browser launch numbers worth comparing against: about 100 players in 2.5 days (55 on launch day, roughly halving daily), about 50 completed runs, 23 total hours, average session 11.6 min on day 1 rising to 80 min by day 3 (the stayers), and D1 retention of 2%. - [itch.io forum, solo browser TD postmortem](https://itch.io/t/6645181/launch-week-postmortem-solo-browser-td-product-hunt-a-portal-rejection-and-a-flagged-show-hn-numbers-inside)
- Curiosity was the strongest predictor of enjoyment and the only predictor of free-choice playtime in a 1,699-person study, so "do they want one more raid" is a better signal than "did they like it." (CHI 2024) - [Imperial Spiral, Kao et al.](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/entities/publication/253c32f5-d124-4a23-88b5-9aaaf114608d)
- Link-based playtest services (surveys plus screen recording, no account needed) exist for indies; in-game telemetry is a paid tier and Unity only. (vendor) - [PlayProbe](https://playprobe.io/)
- Itch.io is "a great place to quickly and cheaply test out a game idea" and Zukowski advises getting player feedback on itch, not Steam. (May 2025) - [How To Market A Game, itch.io benchmark](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/12/benchmark-itch-io-traffic/); [How To Market A Game, itch.io to Steam](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/22/more-games-that-made-the-itch-io-to-steam-transition/)
- NIMRODS sent player surveys to the audiences of its previous games when promoting its demo. (May 2025) - [How To Market A Game, itch.io to Steam](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/22/more-games-that-made-the-itch-io-to-steam-transition/)

### Inferences
- PILLAGERS already has a flight recorder and in-game notes that land in exports/. The cheapest gain is a per-raid summary row: seed, players (1 or 2), input (KB+M, pad preset), time to first kill, deaths with cause and map position, downs and revives, extraction yes/no and which point, raid length, and the screen the player was on when the session ended.
- For the private friends-and-family link, behavior (did they start a second raid, where did they die, did they extract) is more trustworthy than their praise, because of response bias.
- At the party, silent watching plus one objective when asked is the method that fits; a think-aloud would break a social, fun setting.

### Gaps
- Steve Bromley's specific position on think-aloud versus silent observation was not found.
- No 2025 or 2026 study with numbers on how solo developers combine heatmaps and quit surveys was found.
- No source on how to run playtests at a private party (as opposed to a lab or expo).

## 5. Launching a browser game on itch.io in 2025 and 2026: page setup, PWYW performance, featuring, devlogs, Reddit, short video, streamers, timing around Thanksgiving, realistic numbers

### Takeaway
A typical itch.io game gets about 1,600 lifetime views (median), browser-playable pages turn about 37% of views into plays versus about 7% downloads, and almost all traffic comes in the first month. What moves a game past that is staff featuring, itch.io's own social posts, tag charts and streamers finding it, so the page must be polished before the one-time "Most Recent" slot, and launch should be a sequence of small channel pushes. Pay-what-you-want browser games earn very little; at Thanksgiving 2026 there is no Steam seasonal sale overlapping (the next is the Winter Sale on Dec 17).

### Cited Findings
**Realistic numbers**
- Survey of 169 developers, lifetime itch.io views: average 54,483, 70th percentile about 12,150 to 13,380, median 1,582, 30th percentile about 400. Browser plays as a share of views: 37% at the median and 70th percentile, 27% at the 30th; downloads as a share of views: about 6 to 7%. Two browser examples converted 65% and 67% of views to plays. (May 2025; the article has a few internal inconsistencies, including a sentence that reads "3x more people will play your game if it is downloadable," which appears to be a typo for browser-playable) - [How To Market A Game, "Benchmark: Itch.io traffic"](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/12/benchmark-itch-io-traffic/)
- Same source: tiers of under 5,000 lifetime views ("nothing"), 5,000 to 10,000 ("minor featuring"), and 10,000 to 1,000,000+ ("the magic," usually with itch.io tweets, an itch.io YouTube trailer, storefront featuring and big YouTubers). One itch.io tweet sent Die in the Dungeon about 10,000 views in a day. Most games get zero traffic after the first month. "You will not make money from Itch.io." - [How To Market A Game, benchmark](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/12/benchmark-itch-io-traffic/)
- Examples: Die in the Dungeon 1,409,064 views and 919,621 browser plays, featured by itch unprompted, with its first big spikes about a month after upload; NIMRODS (browser action roguelike) 123,000 views and 81,800 plays with a launch peak of only 300 views and 180 plays per day; Tiny Kingdom's front-page feature added about 250 Steam wishlists. A single day with 1,000+ views is a useful signal. Top itch games have decorated pages with strong art, not plain jam dumps. (May 2025) - [How To Market A Game, itch.io to Steam](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/22/more-games-that-made-the-itch-io-to-steam-transition/)
- One developer's 2025 itch.io stats: 71,659 page views and 19,794 browser plays across their catalog; their games earned $24 lifetime out of 52,217 game views (fonts earned most of their income). (itch.io user blog, not official itch.io data) - [itch.io blog post "2025 in review" by Not Jam](https://itch.io/blog/1284030/2025-in-review)

**Page setup (official itch.io docs)**
- HTML5 embed modes: "Embed in page" with set viewport dimensions, or "Click to launch in fullscreen" (code must adapt to screen size). "Click to play" is on by default and browsers may mute audio when a game auto-starts. itch.io can overlay a fullscreen button at the bottom right if the game has none. Enable "Mobile Friendly" only if the game works on mobile browsers; on mobile the page always uses fullscreen click-to-launch, and without the flag mobile viewers see a warning. - [itch.io creator docs, Uploading HTML5 games](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5)
- A single self-contained HTML file can be uploaded without zipping. Zip limits: 1,000 files, 500 MB extracted, 200 MB per file, 240-character paths; itch auto-gzips html and js. Use a canvas that resizes to its container, especially with fullscreen. - [itch.io creator docs, HTML5](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5)
- The cover image is "typically the first element someone sees" on browse pages; animated GIF covers are allowed but must avoid seizure-inducing effects (WCAG); screenshots (GIFs allowed) are still encouraged for embedded browser games. Prefer suggested tags over new ones, no synonyms, no tagging your own name, and select only platforms you have tested. Publishing puts a page on Most Recent once and "It can not be put on the top of the Most Recent page again," so polish before publishing; drafts and restricted pages are not eligible for browse. Misleading tags or metadata can remove a page from browse and search. - [itch.io creator docs, Quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines)

**Featuring, devlogs, community**
- Staff curate two slots, Featured (top, sticks longer) and Fresh (added many times a day, some from user collections). There is no application; moderators say make a good game and page, post in "Release Announcements," promote normally; popular new games are more likely to be spotted by curators; post devlogs to keep attention on updates. - [itch.io forum, "What does it take to get your game featured or to appear in fresh games"](https://itch.io/t/1060924/what-does-it-take-to-get-your-game-featured-or-to-appear-in-fresh-games); [itch.io forum, "How to get featured"](https://itch.io/t/3114009/how-to-get-featured)
- Drivers of itch traffic, in order of impact reported by developers: front-page featuring ("by far the biggest impact"), itch.io's Twitter and YouTube, trending and tag charts, streamers finding the game unprompted, community posts. - [How To Market A Game, benchmark](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/12/benchmark-itch-io-traffic/)
- One developer got about 75% of views from itch.io after reaching #8 on a popular tag and about 10% from Reddit posts, and suspected first-day Reddit traffic helped trigger itch's charts. (search summary) - [itch.io devlog, release stats](https://itch.io/devlog/90210/itchio-release-stats-and-chapter-3-update.amp)

- Reddit is hostile to anything that looks like promotion; participate first and share value-led posts. - [games.gg (Dutch), indie marketing without budget](https://games.gg/nl/news/indie-game-marketing-zonder-budget-strategie%C3%ABn/)

(2026) - [itch.io forum, browser TD postmortem](https://itch.io/t/6645181/launch-week-postmortem-solo-browser-td-product-hunt-a-portal-rejection-and-a-flagged-show-hn-numbers-inside)

## 6. Accessibility options players expect in 2025 and 2026 action games

### Takeaway
The baseline set in both the Game Accessibility Guidelines and the Xbox Accessibility Guidelines is: remappable controls, alternatives to holding buttons, sensitivity options, never relying on color or sound alone, subtitles or visual cues for important sounds, independent volume sliders, an opt-out for camera shake and other motion, difficulty or assist options including aim assist, and photosensitivity care. Defaults matter: when subtitles were on by default, 95 to 97% of players kept them on.

### Cited Findings
- GAG Basic: "Allow controls to be remapped / reconfigured"; "Include an option to adjust the sensitivity of controls"; "Ensure no essential information is conveyed by a fixed colour alone"; "Use an easily readable default font size"; "Avoid flickering images and repetitive patterns"; "Ensure no essential information is conveyed by sounds alone"; "Provide subtitles for all important speech"; "Include an option to adjust the game speed." - [Game Accessibility Guidelines, full list](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/)
- GAG Intermediate: "Avoid / provide alternatives to requiring buttons to be held down"; "Allow difficulty level to be altered during gameplay"; "Provide an option to turn off / hide background movement"; "Provide captions or visuals for significant background sounds"; "Include assist modes such as auto-aim and assisted steering"; "Support more than one input device." Screen shake has no Basic or Intermediate entry; the closest is Advanced "Avoid any sudden unexpected movement or events." - [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/)
- XAG covers 23 guidelines; the current published version is XAG v3.2 (Jun 8, 2023). XAG 117 (Visual distractions and motion) covers camera shake, parallax and motion blur, with the expectation that players can opt out; XAG 105 calls for independent volume sliders for music, dialog, effects and chat; XAG 103 says not to rely on a single sense for any cue; XAG 107 covers remapping and hold-versus-tap alternatives; XAG 108 covers difficulty and granular assists; XAG 110 haptics; XAG 118 photosensitivity. - [Xbox Accessibility Guidelines](https://devdocs.xbox.com/build/game-principles/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines.md)
- XAG 107: "It's fine to offer a primary input for your game. It's not fine to require it." Provide alternate inputs with equivalent function and pair analog inputs with single-press digital equivalents. - [XAG 107 Input deep dive](https://devdocs.xbox.com/build/game-principles/accessibility/xag-deep-dives/xag-107-input.md)
- Ubisoft telemetry relayed by Ian Hamilton: 97% of Far Cry New Dawn and 95% of Assassin's Creed Odyssey players kept subtitles on when on by default, versus about 60% who turned them on in Origins when off by default. (older data, secondary source, unverified) - [VoxBooster, game accessibility features statistics 2026](https://voxbooster.com/blog/game-accessibility-features-statistics-2026)
- Developer adoption cited from GDC's 2026 State of the Game Industry: button remapping 65%, color settings 58%, larger subtitles 52%. (secondary, unverified against the GDC report) - [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/game-accessibility-features-statistics-2026)
- Survey of 93 players with disabilities (Mindtrek 2022): the settings they use and want fall into six groups (controls, language, gameplay, visuals, audio, adaptive devices); "already existing accessibility settings are useful and should be added to all games." (older, 2022) - [UniversityXP summary of Baltzar, Turunen, Hassan](https://www.universityxp.com/research/2024/3/11/popular-accessibility-settings-in-digital-games-what-accessibility-settings-do-players-with-disabilities-use-and-need)
- Hit stop and shake can follow the system reduced-motion setting with gameplay unchanged. (Sep 2026) - [Sword Arcade](https://swordarcade.xyz/guides/game-feel-hitstop-and-screen-shake/)
- Venturelli made sticky aim switchable in Settings. (older, 2015) - [Venturelli, Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/everything-i-learned-about-dual-stick-shooter-controls)
- itch.io asks that GIF covers avoid seizure-inducing effects per WCAG. - [itch.io Quality guidelines](https://itch.io/docs/creators/quality-guidelines)

### Inferences
- For PILLAGERS the cheap, expected set is: a shake and flash strength slider with an off position (and honoring prefers-reduced-motion), hold or toggle for any held action, KB+M remap alongside the pad presets, separate music and effects volume, visual cues for important sounds (his noise rings already do this for footsteps), a non-color player cue, and an aim assist toggle.
- Because the party guests are strangers, defaults matter more than menus: whatever ships on by default is what nearly everyone will play with, as the subtitle data shows.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025 or 2026 player telemetry on which accessibility settings action-game players actually use was found; the VoxBooster figures could not be checked against Ubisoft, Activision or GDC originals.
- The XAG 117 and 110 deep-dive pages (exact minimums) were not fetched.

## 7. The top 15 concrete things PILLAGERS should do in the next month from this research

### Takeaway
The highest-value items for the Fri 10/30 party are camera and readability rules that keep both players on screen and identifiable, a tiered juice budget with a motion off switch, small invisible pad aim help with a toggle, and a first minute that shows the hook. For the Thanksgiving itch launch: a polished browser page published once, a launch run as a sequence of small channel pushes, and per-raid logging from the friends-and-family link. Items that touch his rulings are flagged for a logged question, not a change.

### Cited Findings
1. **Tier the juice by event weight and cap it.** Light hits get a little of every effect, heavy and damage events get more; hit stop around 35/70/90 ms capped near 120 ms, shake around 4/8/12 px (scaled to resolution) decaying over 0.2 to 0.3 s, render-only. Reason: medium-high juice beats extreme, and amplified feedback not tied to success lowers motivation. - [Sword Arcade, Sep 2026](https://swordarcade.xyz/guides/game-feel-hitstop-and-screen-shake/); [Kao et al., CHI 2024](https://spiral.imperial.ac.uk/entities/publication/253c32f5-d124-4a23-88b5-9aaaf114608d); [arXiv 2011.09201](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.09201)
2. **Audit one shot of each gun against the Nuclear Throne recipe.** Camera kick away from aim, gun sprite kick, first-frame muzzle shape, shell ejection, enemy knockback away from the bullet, a 10 to 20 ms freeze on hit. Reason: the most complete published per-shot recipe for a top-down shooter. - [infovore.org quoting RPS, older 2013](https://infovore.org/tag/feel/); [Godot Forums knockback direction](https://godotforums.org/d/24294-adding-knockback-to-my-top-down-game)
3. **Add a motion setting: shake and flash strength with an off position, honoring prefers-reduced-motion.** Reason: XAG 117 expects an opt-out for camera shake, and info-carrying labels can stay on. - [Xbox Accessibility Guidelines](https://devdocs.xbox.com/build/game-principles/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines.md); [Sword Arcade](https://swordarcade.xyz/guides/game-feel-hitstop-and-screen-shake/)
4. **Use a radial dead zone (about 0.3) on both sticks and smooth the aim direction instead of snapping.** Reason: axial dead zones snap toward cardinals and raw stick noise is 0.05 to 0.15. - [Venturelli, Game Developer, older 2015](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/everything-i-learned-about-dual-stick-shooter-controls); [Game Developer, dead zones, older](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/disciplines/doing-thumbstick-dead-zones-right)
5. **Give pads a small, invisible aim correction (Death's Door style, at most about 15 degrees, toward moving targets) with a Settings toggle.** Reason: strangers on pads at the party need help, and GAG lists auto-aim as an Intermediate assist; a small cone avoids the Hades-style snap. Check his 10-15 "magnet removed" ruling first. - [VoiceTube transcript, aim assist essay](https://tw.voicetube.com/videos/173139); [UCSD CSE125 2026 config](https://cse125.ucsd.edu/2026/cse125g2/docs/structsystems_1_1GamepadAimAssistConfig.html); [GAG](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/)
6. **Add event-tied dual-rumble: weak motor for your own shots, strong motor for explosions and taking damage, short pulses, feature-detected, with an off switch.** Reason: the Gamepad API supports it on secure contexts, and constant rumble becomes noise. - [MDN playEffect](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/GamepadHapticActuator/playEffect); [Bugnet rumble](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-add-controller-rumble-that-enhances-feel)
7. **Lock the 2-player camera rule: midpoint follow, zoom out to a clamped maximum, then a soft leash so neither player can leave the frame.** Reason: these are the standard shared-screen answers, and a lost player at a party is a lost player. - [Terrell, Game Developer, older 2011](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/shared-multi-split-screen-design); [Unreal Community Wiki](https://unrealcommunity.wiki/creating-a-shared-camera-for-multiplayer-games-rqedrb3w); [Godot Forum](https://forum.godotengine.org/t/how-do-i-make-camera-follow-and-zoom-to-2-players/17203)
8. **Give each player a non-color identity cue (p1 and p2 tag or ring shape) in addition to amber and cyan, plus distinct per-player shot sounds.** Reason: GAG Basic forbids color-only information; amber and cyan already sit close to the colorblind-safe Okabe-Ito pair. - [GAG](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/); [NYU Siegal Lab palette](https://siegal.bio.nyu.edu/color-palette/); [Terrell](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/shared-multi-split-screen-design)
9. **Check the downed rules against shipped ranges: bleed-out in the 25 to 50 s band, paused while a revive is in progress, shorter on repeat downs.** Reason: these three rules appear across shipped co-op shooters and stop "died mid-revive" frustration. Log a question to him about a dead partner sitting out the rest of a raid (Enter the Gungeon brings partners back via chests and bosses), since his 09-13 "no rejoining a raid" ruling may apply. - [Project Lazarus Wiki](https://project-lazarus.fandom.com/wiki/Downed_Players); [HiTech Gazette, Delta Force](https://hitechgazette.com/hidden-mechanics-for-medics-in-delta-force/); [EtG Wiki, The Cultist](https://enterthegungeon.fandom.com/wiki/The_Cultist)
10. **Make the demo's first minute show the hook: first input reaches gameplay fast, one lower case objective callout, difficulty a notch lower than feels right.** Reason: visitors decide in about 90 seconds, and expo developers repeatedly say short and easy beats long and hard. - [presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-to-build-game-demo); [Bearmask G-Star postmortem](https://bearmask.itch.io/lofi-room/devlog/133337/g-star-2019-postmortem-big-indie-pitch-win); [McRae, Game Developer, older 2014](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/5-expo-tips-for-indies)

### Inferences
- Items 1 to 9 are feel, camera and readability fixes that can ship before the Fri 10/30 party copy refresh if each passes the gate; items 3, 5, 6 and 8 also serve the accessibility baseline for the itch release.
- Items 5, 9 and 11 touch existing rulings (10-15 magnet, 09-13 no rejoining, 10-19 no party extras) and should be logged as questions with a default, per the project's "a question never holds up work" rule.
- Feel items (1, 2, 5, 6) cannot be judged by the test corpus; he should watch for screen readability during big fights, whether pad players feel helped or steered, and whether rumble is welcome or tiring.
