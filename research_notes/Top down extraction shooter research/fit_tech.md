# Fit check: browser_canvas_tech.md against the real game (v34.83, 2026-10-25)

Source: research_notes/Top down extraction shooter research/browser_canvas_tech.md (the ranked list in section 9 plus the other actionable inferences). Checked by grep of dark_raiders.html (line numbers are HEAD v34.83), DESIGN.md headings, tools/handoff/console/tasks and waiting, the console notes, and the binding memory files.

Status key: have, partly, missing, against_ruling, unclear. Party value is for the 2-player walk-up demo on Fri 10/30 (two 4K TVs at 120 or 144 Hz, two Xbox pads, his PC on localhost).

Bottom line: most of the frame-pacing, lighting-layer, context-loss and save-file advice is already in the game. The real gaps that matter for the party are a real-GPU 4K two-window frame measurement (job 120k covers part of it), the per-frame gradients in the smooth look (now the default), the two-window two-pad rig test (J1, never run), and gun or impact voice caps. One risk turned up for itch: pollPad calls navigator.getGamepads() with no try (line 19623) and loop calls pollPad() with no try (line 53747). A browser that blocks pads in an iframe throws SecurityError there, which would stop every frame after the pad poll. One suggestion (one window for both players) goes against his same-PC two-window order.
