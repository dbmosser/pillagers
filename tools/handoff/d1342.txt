## v13.42 - OUT OF A CONSUMABLE, YOU SWITCH BACK TO THE GUN YOURSELF

His ruling of 2026-09-13, in his words: if i switch to bandages or other
throwables/consumables, it should not autofire the gun when I am out of those
consumables, i should have to switch back to the gun.

WHAT IT DID. Since v2.92 the belt had a safety valve: spending the last of a stack
selected the gun, so the trigger was never dead. v12.08 and v12.31 extended it to a
press on an empty throwable and a press on a blank cell, and v12.41 to the use key on
an empty throwable. The press that emptied the cell never fired the gun, but the NEXT
click did. Clicking on a bandage until it ran out, or a grenade, meant the click after
the last one fired his rifle. That is the thing he is describing.

THE BUILD. Five places selected the gun for him, and all five now leave the highlight
where he put it:
  the press on a blank cell
  the press on an empty throwable cell
  spending the last of a stack with a click
  letting go of the last cooked grenade
  the use key on an empty throwable cell
Every press on a cell with nothing left says what is missing and names the key of the
belt cell that holds his gun, for example: No Bandage left. Press 1 for your gun.
The key comes from the cell that actually holds the gun in his hands, so a gun bound to
key 5 says 5.

WHY THE RAID START CHANGED. The blank-cell fall-back existed because a raid with the
held gun bound to a higher key started on a blank cell 1, and the first click of the
raid did nothing (v12.31). A raid now starts with the gun cell selected, wherever his
belt put it, so the first click fires as it did before.

MEASURED. Check 13.42, on seed 4242: one Bandage on the Medical cell, one click spends
it, then three held clicks must fire nothing, keep the Medical cell and name the gun
key; one cooked Frag thrown, then three held clicks must fire nothing, keep the Frag
cell, start no cook and name the key; the use key on that empty Frag cell must keep it
and name the key; selecting the gun cell and clicking must fire (the control that shows
the zeros are the ruling, not a dead gun); and with the held gun bound to key 5 the raid
must start on key 5. It fails on v13.41. Checks 12.08 and 12.31 asserted the fall-back
and are repaired to the ruling: no fall-back, no shot, and the line names key 5.

Not verified: the controller, whose trigger reaches the same code but was not driven;
the pause between the empty line and a later line written over it; and a Stim or an
assigned valuable run to zero, which take the same spent-cell branch but were not staged.
