updateEnts catches him, so he runs the ordinary pillager chain: below 30 percent health he flees and extracts, the game says he

then die, or abandon. In endRaid the mercDead branch is skipped, mercEnt is null so the extraction branch is skipped, and in the

rule says the cut needs you both out. Only the sentence was wrong, and the comment above it and the What's New entry both say a
hire who gets out earlier should no longer read that way.

you got out afterwards, which is false on a death) and echoes "makes his own way out" in the paid line. The left-out-there line
stays for a hire still on the map. Neither sentence is a TXSHIP key. New player text only, no money moves and no number moves.
