## v11.69 - WITH PILLAGERS OFF, THE EXTRACTION-HEAT ROW READ CUSTOM

From the v11.46 read-only audit, P2. Two settings rows disagreed about who
owns one dial. Other pillagers: None turns the pillager waves off (v8.42,
because the wave loop treated an empty map as urgent and kept delivering
four pillagers), and applyGameOpts gives the pillager row the last word on
that (v8.74, his order: None means none). But the extraction-heat row's Heavy
and Standard still carried raiderWaves:1, so with the waves off neither of
them matched the live dials and the row showed CUSTOM in amber, with the
tuning-console hint, for a setting he had never touched. The v8.74 comment on
that row already says it describes the RING only.

THE FIX. The waves belong to the pillager row, on every option: Many, Standard
and Few write them on, None writes them off. The heat row carries siegeVol
alone. Every combination of choices lands on the same dial values it did
before, with one exception that is itself a fix: choosing None and then going
back to Standard with the heat on Light used to leave the waves off, because
nothing wrote them back; the pillager row writes them now.

MEASURED. Check 11.69 sets pillagers to None, applies, and requires the waves
off (control) and the heat row reading its own word rather than CUSTOM; then
sets pillagers back to Standard and requires the waves back on and both rows
reading Standard. On the v11.68 fixture the heat row reads CUSTOM with
pillagers off and the check fails saying so. The dials this build writes are
the same numbers as before; nothing in a raid changes.

Not verified: his own play; the None-then-Standard-with-Light case in a
played raid (measured on the dials, not on a spawned map).
