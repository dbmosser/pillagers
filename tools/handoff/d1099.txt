## v10.99 - NOTE 17: NO PILLAGER HAS EVER WORN A HAT, A BEARD OR A TATTOO

Found at v10.94 while proving the eye instrument, by asking each rack whether it
changes a NON-hero figure at all. The same rack, on the same call, at nine times
raid size:

    headgear   the operator 31,978 pixels changed, a pillager 0
    beard      the operator  5,221 pixels changed, a pillager 0
    tattoo     the operator    801 pixels changed, a pillager 0

**CAUSE.** The block that draws the operator's fringe also contains the tattoo,
the beard and the headgear, and the whole block sits behind a hero-only branch.
Hair colour and hair cut are outside it, which is why the crowd looks varied
enough that this went unnoticed for months.

**WHAT IT COST.** The Undercroft crowd rolls a hat, a beard and a tattoo for
every single member and none of them were drawn, and two lines of the new-in card
promise that the pillagers dress from the same racks he does. A shipped promise
that was not true.

**THE FIX.** The branch closes after the FRINGE, which is the one thing in there
that is genuinely hers, her own hair styling. The tattoo, the beard and the
headgear fall outside it and reach everybody. Every one of them already reads a
pillager's own look through st; that code was written for this and had never once
been reachable.

**MEASURED AFTER:** headgear 32,557 pixels on a pillager, the beard 3,233, the
tattoo 1,418, and the operator unchanged.

**NEW CHECK v10.99** requires each of the three racks to change a pillager by a
real amount, requires each to still change the operator, so this cannot be passed
by taking a rack off her, and requires the operator and a pillager wearing the
same things to still differ by thousands of pixels, which is her fringe and which
would collapse if I had ungated the whole branch. One more control: a full-body
suit must still overrule the headgear, which is the order v10.54 set and which
this change reaches straight through.

**AN INSTRUMENT NOTE I AM NOT GOING TO DRESS UP.** Run standalone, six
consecutive draws of the same pillager are byte identical. Run as a check inside
the corpus, the FIRST pair differs by 621 pixels and every pair after it is
exact. I did not find what settles on that first pair. Rather than assert on a
number I cannot explain, every measurement is now the SETTLED reading: the
smaller of two consecutive pairs, each pair drawn back to back. With that, the
noise floor reads 0 and the old build reports all three racks at exactly 0, which
is the defect stated precisely. Before that change the drift was larger than the
tattoo signal, and the tattoo would have passed on a build that draws no tattoo.

**AND A THING FOR HIM TO DECIDE, MEASURED WHILE TESTING.** The crowd's look
roller picks one item from every rack including the OUTFIT rack, which holds Own
Clothes plus seven full-body suits. So roughly seven of every eight people in the
Undercroft are wearing a Skeleton, a Machine, a Trooper, an Android, a Tomb
Explorer, a Baller or a Street Poet, and a suit overrules every other rack by
design. Measured: 3 of 16 crowd rolls show a visible hat. So this fix is real and
the raid will show it, but the Undercroft floor will still be mostly costumes
until he says otherwise. That is his call and it is logged as note 19.

Not verified: how the crowd reads to him on the floor now, and whether any hat
sits wrongly on a pillager's head at raid zoom, since the check measures that the
hat is drawn and not that it is drawn beautifully.
