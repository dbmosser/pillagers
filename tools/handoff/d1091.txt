## v10.91 - HIS NOTE: THE RESIZE GRIP HAS NOWHERE TO DRAG TO

"corner drag to size for lower right corner needs to be in lower left side,
otherwise there's nowhere to drag to increase size", 2026-09-04.

REPRODUCED, every panel measured at 1920x1080, distance from the right of the
screen:

  gear      8      (and 5 from the bottom)
  cond      17
  body      1374
  legend    1445
  raiders   1529

The grip has always been the panel's bottom-RIGHT corner, and the panel is sized
by how much further the pointer gets from the corner OPPOSITE it. On the gear
panel that leaves 8 pixels of travel before the pointer runs off the monitor, so
there is effectively no way to make it bigger. That is his note exactly, and the
conditions panel has the same problem with 17.

THE FIX IS HIS RULE: the grip goes on the corner that faces INTO the screen. A
panel whose right edge is within a grip-width and ten pixels of the screen edge
grips on its LEFT and is sized from its top-right corner instead. Every panel
with room keeps the bottom-right corner every window has used for this since
before he was born, because a grip that moved on panels that were already fine
would be a change for its own sake.

ONE FUNCTION DECIDES AND FOUR PLACES ASK IT: the mousedown that starts a resize,
the drag that sizes it, the drawing of the three little diagonals, which are
mirrored when the grip is on the left so they still point into the panel, and the
cursor shape. Those four disagreeing is precisely how a grip ends up looking like
it is somewhere it is not, and they were four separate copies of the same
arithmetic before this.

NO DIAL MOVES, per his instruction of 2026-09-04.

THE CHECK walks every resizable panel and asks five things: a panel pinned to the
right edge grips left and one with room grips right; every grip has at least 120
pixels of travel before the pointer would leave the screen, which is the actual
complaint; the anchor really is the corner the grip is not, or the panel would
shrink as he pulls it outward; a click just inside the grip is accepted; and a
click on the opposite corner is refused. Its two controls are that at least one
pinned panel and at least one roomy panel are present in the run, or it is
testing half a rule and does not know it.

Not verified: this is measured at 1920x1080. At 1440p and 4K the panels sit at
different distances from the edge and a panel that is pinned here may not be
there, which is correct behaviour but is not proven. And nobody has dragged one
with a mouse: the check drives the hit test and the geometry, not a real gesture.
