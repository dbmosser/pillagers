v12.78: a gun you chose is not swapped out behind your back

His note after a live round. A found gun took the second slot when it was empty,
which is right. When it was not empty, the pickup replaced the gun in your hands
on a tier comparison and bagged the one you were holding, so a rifle you had
chosen could be knocked out of your hands by the next thing out of a crate.

A slot yields now if it is empty, holds Bare Hands, or holds the Scav Pistol,
which is the starter and is meant to be replaced by the first real find. Any
other gun is yours. With two real weapons on you the find goes to the backpack
and equipping it is your call.

Check 12.78 drives the real grant three times on one raid: two real guns must
both stay put with the find bagged, the Scav Pistol must still be booted, and an
empty second slot must still take it. Fails on v12.77.
