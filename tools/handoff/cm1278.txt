v12.78: the price of walking out is the price you actually pay

The fine for walking out is at least a hundred XP, taken with a floor at zero so
it can never push you negative. That floor is right. But the button quoted the
fine from before the floor, and the line afterwards announced the same unfloored
number.

So a first-time player with almost no XP opens the pause box, reads that
abandoning costs a hundred and six XP, presses it, is told XP minus a hundred and
six, and loses nothing, because there was nothing to take. That is the kind of
fiction that keeps a new player inside a run he wanted to leave.

Both places now use the figure that will actually be charged. The amount, the
sixty second grace and the floor are unchanged.

Most of the finding this came from was wrong and is not built: the fine is not
invisible to the outcome card, because it lands before the raid ends.

Check 12.78 presses the real two-stage button in a real raid past the grace and
reads what he is shown and told; the control requires a character who has the XP
to still read the full price and still pay exactly it. Fails on v12.77.
