## v15.89 - YOUR HIRE ON FOLLOW KEEPS FOLLOWING PAST A LOCKED ROOM

Doors audit finding (MEDIUM), entry 7 of the confirmed list.

Hire a pillager; FOLLOW is the default order. Under FOLLOW, when no fight is on and you are within 420 of him, he picks any
container within 160 of him and 420 of you, walks to it, and after two seconds within 30 of it marks it opened and copies its
loot into his pack (updateEnts, the v15.19 pick loop). The filter on that pick was _CT.opened||_CT.locked||_CT.mine||prog>0,
and nothing in the file ever sets .locked or .mine on a container: mkContainer returns none, and the only .locked in the game
is the map's room list. The three caches inside each locked room are marked inLocked (the room placer), the strongbox is put
in a random locked room by spotIn, and ordinary crates that land inside one are kept there on purpose by the reach pass. All
of them passed the filter. On COLD STORAGE, BLAST FREEZER and FOREMAN OFFICE are 230 by 230 and their caches sit inside the
room, so walking past the south side of either, or standing at its door prompt, put him within 160 of one. He picked it.
navPath returns null when start and goal are in different regions (its own v3.71 note names this case), so seekPoint steered
him straight at the cache and he ground along the shell wall. The drop line lets go only when the box is opened, searched by
you or more than 280 away, which a 230 room never allows from outside its wall, and the FOLLOW walk sits in the else of the
goal branch, so he stood there and stopped following for as long as you stayed within 420 of him. When you spent the key he
was already aimed at the cache, got a route, walked in and took the vault cache into his pack before you reached it. Doors
cannot be destroyed (noDes, lockWall and door walls all refuse), and the key spend in updatePlayer is the only thing that sets
a room's open flag, so the room's own flag is the right thing to test. Not a TXSHIP key, not balance, no ruling covers it.
