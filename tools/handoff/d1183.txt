## v11.83 - HIS SPEC: THE STIM IS TEN SECONDS OF LEGS

His note of 2026-09-06, from his fourth run this morning: "stim injector
should give unlimited stamina and 1.2 speed boost for 10 seconds".

WHAT IT WAS. A Stim Injector refilled the stamina bar once and cleared the
sprint lock, and that was all: a bandage for legs, spent in the two seconds
it took to sprint the bar back down. He has given it a real shape.

THE BUILD. One verb, useStim, and two numbers, STIM_SEC 10 and STIM_SPD 1.2.
Using a stim, through the belt key or any later route, sets a ten second
clock; while it runs the stamina line in the player update holds the bar
full and skips the drain, so the release latch that guards against
fluttering the sprint key never arms, and the speed line multiplies every
speed by 1.2 on top of sprint, crouch, load and water. A second stim while
one runs tops the clock back up to ten rather than stacking. The game says
when it starts and when it wears off. The tooltip says what it does now.

MEASURED. Check 11.83 puts a stim on key 3 and uses it through the real key
handler, requires the ten second clock running, then sprints from an
extraction ring along a line proven clear for 220 units, with Shift and the
movement key held through the real player update, and requires the bar
still full and the clock run down; then makes the identical sprint from the
same spot without the stim and requires the plain run to have drained
stamina and the stim run to have covered 1.2 times the ground, within a
tolerance of 0.08. On the v11.82 fixture there is no clock, the bar drains
under the stim, and the two sprints cover the same ground.

Not verified: his own play, and whether ten seconds and a fifth are the
feel he wants; the stim in the sim bot, which never uses one; what a stim
does to the footstep and noise model at the higher speed, which is
unchanged and simply faster.
