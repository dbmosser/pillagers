v12.57: walking through another extraction point no longer abandons your own

The game keeps one pointer at the extraction that matters, and that pointer was
set by standing in a ring. So walking into any other open ring while your own
extraction was inbound moved the pointer onto the one under your feet, which
had nothing coming.

From there everything followed the wrong door: the banner named that ring, the
middle of the screen offered a hold over a ring with nothing arriving, the
approach pings and the last-seconds warning went quiet because both watch the
pointer, and holding E started a fresh call with its own full wait while the
extraction you had already paid for left without you. A man on the floor also
had his surrender refused against the wrong ring.

It needs no deliberate double call. A pillager can open a second ring himself
and you only have to walk across it.

A ring with a live countdown or a window still running now keeps the pointer.
Calling moves it, which is a new line at the call, because calling is a choice
rather than a place you walked through.

Check 12.57 puts a countdown on one point, stands him in a second, and requires
the pointer and his countdown untouched; one control requires the pointer to
still follow him when nothing is running, another requires a deliberate call at
the second point to move it; fails on v12.56.
