## v11.11 - THE FRESH PROFILE HOUR, AND IT FOUND NOTHING

His notes are all shipped and the named audit backlog is empty, so this tick went
to the thing the alpha actually needs and nobody had done: **what a friend sees**.
A profile with no runs, no extracts, no stash, no contracts, no racks, one pistol
and 600 credits, and then every station on the Undercroft floor walked up to and
pressed.

**THE SWEEP FOUND NOTHING.** Nine stations. None threw, in the call or in a
handler afterwards. None opened a panel with nothing in it. None did nothing. The
stash reads correctly with nothing in it, saying "Nothing in the stash. Ascend,
pillage, extract.", and its tabs switch and filter.

**THREE TIMES MY OWN PROBE CRIED WOLF, and those are worth more than the clean
result**, because each was one sentence away from being reported to him as a bug:

  1. **"The stash station does nothing."** It opens a SCREEN and not a modal, and
     my sweep only looked for modals.
  2. **"The GUNS tab does not switch."** The tab elements are two levels down
     inside their container, so I had clicked the container.
  3. **"The GUNS tab counts one gun and shows an empty shelf."** An owned gun
     draws as an ICON with no text in it, and I was counting words. Counted in
     cells, the shelf has exactly the one gun on it.

**NEW CHECK v11.11** holds that sweep. It builds the fresh profile in memory,
walks every station, and requires no throw, a panel with real content or the hub
screen, and never a station that does nothing. Then it reads the stash in CELLS
rather than in words, requires the GUNS tab to switch and to draw his gun, and
requires SALVAGE to draw nothing at all on a profile that has never looted.

Two controls. SALVAGE drawing cells would mean the tabs are not filtering, and a
deliberate throw inside a click must be heard, because an error in a DOM handler
never reaches its caller and without that the clean result above proves nothing.

**There is no control that fails on the previous build, because there is no
defect.** This check passes on v11.10 and on v11.11 and exists to catch the day a
station starts throwing on a profile that has nothing.

Not verified: the first hour past the floor. This walks the Undercroft and its
panels; it does not go up, loot, die and come back, and the parts of the first
hour that need a raid behind them are still only covered by the checks that were
already there.

**AND IT BROKE TWO CHECKS THE FIRST TIME IT RAN IN THE CORPUS, WHICH IS MINE.**
v11.11 rewrote the profile to a fresh one, restored a LIST of twenty-four fields,
and then called saveProfile, writing whatever survived that list to disk. Two
checks downstream read fields the list had never heard of:

  - v11.09 reported a third typeface. Opening the Fashion station drew the
    clothing rack, and the 23 on a jersey swatch is set in Impact.
  - v10.99 reported that headgear, beards and tattoos had been taken off the
    operator, all three at zero pixels. Her hat had not gone anywhere: cosOwned
    derives what she owns from runs, extracts, level and warden kills, and the
    fresh profile had zeroed them, so cosWorn returned nothing to draw.

Three fixes, and the first is the rule: **a check may borrow the profile, it may
not persist what it borrowed, and it may not restore field by field, because the
next check depends on a field this one has never heard of.** v11.11 snapshots the
whole object and puts the whole thing back, and no longer saves. v10.99 unlocks
the rack for its own hero arm rather than trusting whatever progress happens to
be on the profile. And the jersey numeral is named as an exception by WHERE IT
LIVES, inside the Fashion figure and its swatches, because the span carries no
class to name it by; an Impact element planted anywhere else is still caught,
which is checked.
