## v12.42 - A GUN YOU STOW KEEPS ITS ROUNDS

From the 2026-09-07 read-only audit (P2), specced from the source. Verified by
reading at v12.41.

WHAT IT WAS. Loaded rounds live in exactly two places in the whole game: the
count for the gun in your hands and the count for the gun on your back. The
backpack holds a bare key string, and a string has nowhere for a number to
live. So a gun that went into the backpack left its magazine nowhere at all.
Thirty loaded rounds simply stopped existing, and nothing downstream put them
back.

Taking the gun out again handed it half a magazine, conjured from nothing: no
reserve was charged for it. So the same trip is a loss one way and a gift the
other. Stow a full gun and you lose the load. Stow an empty one and you get
half a magazine, and you can do it again, and again.

Three doors put a gun into the backpack and all three did it: dragging a gun
off the belt, equipping a second gun so the first is displaced, and picking a
gun up in the field, which stows the one in your hands.

THE BUILD. The rounds now ride with the gun, in a queue kept per gun id. That
is exactly as precise as the backpack itself, because the backpack holds keys
and not instances: two identical rifles in there are already indistinguishable,
so first in, first out is the truest answer that exists. All three doors carry
the load in, and the equip takes it back out.

A gun FOUND in the field has no entry in that queue, so it still comes back on
half a magazine. That is his rule, written in the README, and it does not move.

WHAT IT COSTS. This one is not free by construction, and I am not claiming it
is. It closes a faucet, so a player who was riding the drag-and-re-equip loop
for free ammunition can no longer do that, and it also stops the loss, so a
player who stowed a full gun keeps rounds he used to lose. Both are corrections
of a conservation error rather than a dial being moved: no number in the
settings changes, and the half-magazine rule for a find is untouched. The bot
never bags a gun, so no paired number can measure it either way.

MEASURED. Check 12.42 measures a conserved quantity, loaded plus reserve,
rather than a level, because a level could be calibrated on the bug itself. It
stages forty seven rounds in a sixty round magazine with an empty reserve:
forty seven is a number no roll, no pickup and no half magazine can produce,
and half of sixty is thirty, so a conjured magazine can never be mistaken for
the real load. It requires that total to survive the stow and to survive the
equip, and the gun to come back on the same forty seven. The second arm runs an
empty gun, which is the faucet, and requires it to come back empty. The control
puts a gun that was never stowed into the backpack and requires it to arrive on
half a magazine, so a fix that simply handed out nothing could not read green.

Not verified: it calls the two verbs directly rather than dragging the gun with
the mouse, which the shipped v11.90 and v11.97 checks already stage; it does
not test two identical guns stowed with different loads, where the queue is
first in first out by construction and there is nothing in the bag to tell them
apart anyway; it does not cover a raid ending with a gun in the bag, since the
queue lives and dies with the raid exactly as the bag does; and the bot cannot
reach any of it.
