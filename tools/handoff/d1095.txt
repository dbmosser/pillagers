## v10.95 - HIS NOTE 18: THE LOOT POP IS STILL THE WRONG FONT

"the loot text that pops on screen upon looting that tells you what item you got
is still too bolded -- use the entire font across the entire game", his note of
2026-09-04. STILL, so this is the second time, after his August note about looted
names being super-bold and hard to read.

**REPRODUCED BY COUNTING, NOT BY LOOKING.** Every text draw in a raid frame, its
HUD, the map screen and four Undercroft frames was traced and its font recorded.
Almost everything came back in the game's own family in one of the five roles, at
three different sizes because the HUD scale multiplies them. Three things did
not:

    17.2px "Titan One"        the item name on the loot pop
    15.6px "Titan One"        PICKED UP, and every other world label
    114 to 244px "Titan One"  the faint district numbers baked into the ground

Titan One is the toybox display face the title wordmark is set in. At fifteen
pixels it does not read as a bold word, it reads as a slab. **That is his "too
bolded", and it was never a weight at all: it was a second typeface.**

**WHY IT SURVIVED THE FIRST PASS.** The type system built for his August note
gives five roles and every call site was moved onto them, but the world label
function measures and draws with a font string of its own, so it never went
through a role and the sweep never reached it. A check that counted families
would have caught it then. There is one now.

**THE FIX.** The world label measures and draws in the same two roles as
everything else, the small one at micro and the large one at label. The small
label lands at exactly the size it already was, 13 rendered pixels, so nothing
moves; the large one goes from 14.3 to 15.6 and from weight 400 in a heavy
display face to weight 600 in the game's face, which is the whole point. The
district numbers on the ground and the placeholder dash on an item with no
portrait, the last call in the file naming a family of its own, come across too.
The only Titan One left in the file is the game's own name, on the title screen
and in the corner brand, which is a wordmark and is meant to be a wordmark.

**NEW CHECK v10.95** traces every word drawn while the map is built, a raid frame
and its HUD are drawn, the map screen is opened, and the Undercroft runs four
frames, and requires every one of them to be in a single family. It asks about
the FAMILY and never the size, because the size legitimately varies: the HUD
scale multiplies every role, so one role shows up at three sizes in a single
frame. Three controls. The loot pop must actually have been drawn, by a marker
string the check plants and then looks for, or a clean result would mean the
label never reached the screen. At least sixty words must have been drawn.
And the tracer must be able to fail: it draws one word in a deliberately foreign
face and requires itself to catch it. The map is built INSIDE the trace on
purpose, because the ground is baked once when the map is made and a check that
arrives afterwards never sees what was painted into it.

Fails on a v10.94 fixture: thirty words in a second typeface.

Not verified: how the lighter loot pop reads to him in a real raid, which is the
judgement he will make himself; and the DOM, which this check does not trace, is
set in the game font by the stylesheet but was not measured word by word.
