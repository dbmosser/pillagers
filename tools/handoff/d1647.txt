## v16.47 - FRAME RATE AT 4K

His note of 2026-09-27: see what you can do to improve frame rate without sacrificing anything; the game will be played mostly
in 4K fullscreen. A 4K profile of a raid (headless Chromium at 3840x2160, tools/cloud/prof.mjs) puts nearly all the frame in
canvas fill work, not script. Two of the full screen passes are the darkness sheet (litC) and the fog of war sheet (fogC): each
is cleared, filled, cut with soft radial gradients and composited over the whole screen every frame. They were the size of the
screen, so at 4K each was 8.3 million pixels. They are now capped at 1920 pixels wide (LSC, the layer scale) and stretched as
they are drawn onto the world, so at 1080p nothing changes and at 4K both layers fill a quarter of the pixels. The layers are
soft by nature; the only visible edge is the fog line along walls, which at 4K is drawn at 1080p detail.

MEASURED. Check 16.47 passes and fails on v16.46. Headless frame time at 3840x2160 before and after is in the audit row; the
headless browser has no GPU, so the absolute numbers are slower than his PC and only the change is meaningful.
