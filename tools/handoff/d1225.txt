## v12.25 - HIS NOTE: THE CORNER READOUT SCALES WITH THE MONITOR

His note of 2026-09-07 morning: "credits and xp in right hand corner way too
small -- all menus, words, UI, hud etc should scale depending on 1080p vs
1440p vs 4k". Traced by the 2026-09-07 read-only notes investigation and
verified by reading at v12.24.

WHAT IT WAS. The game already has a monitor factor, in two coats: hudRes()
for the canvas HUD (every corner panel, the belt, the map, the clock and the
reticle) and titleRes() for the DOM, which applyMenuZoom multiplies by the
Text size setting and writes as a zoom onto every window (.modal, .pausebox,
.outcome, #hub) and onto the title screen. The corner readout of credits and
XP (v11.52) is none of those: it is a fixed element at the body with hard
32px figures and 14px labels, and nothing in the file ever wrote a zoom on
it. So it painted the same 44 pixels at 1080p, 1440p and 4K while the stash
beside it was drawn at 1.73 and 2.47 times, and at 1080p it was the one
surface not carrying the 1.3 the Text size setting gives everything else.
Two more body-level fixed surfaces had the same omission: the answer line on
the Undercroft floor (#hubtoast) and the label under the pointer while
dragging (#grabghost).

THE BUILD. applyMenuZoom now writes the same factor onto the readout, the
floor answer line and the drag label. A zoomed fixed element takes its own
offsets in zoomed pixels, so right:20px and bottom:38px grow with it and the
readout keeps its corner. The drag label is created lazily on the first grab,
after applyMenuZoom has run, so it takes the factor itself, and its pointer
offsets divide by the zoom, or the label would sit 2.47 times too far from
the pointer at 4K. The stash top row keeps 560 pixels clear of the readout
instead of 480, because at 1080p the readout is now 1.3 times wider in real
pixels. The raid HUD needs no change: topRightBottom() reads the readout's
real box, so the CONDITIONS box starts under the bigger readout by itself.

MEASURED. Check 12.25 forces the canvas to 4K, 1440p and 1080p in turn with
the Text size at its default and a seven-figure balance, and requires the
readout zoom to equal the windows' factor at 4K, its height to follow the
monitor (4K taller than 1080p by 1.4 or more, 1440p between), and the floor
answer line to carry the same zoom. It then presses the real Text size button
in Settings and requires the readout to move. Two controls guard what the
growth could break at 1080p: the readout must not cover any button in the
stash top row (CLOSE), and a raid must still start its CONDITIONS box under
the readout. On the v12.24 fixture the readout zoom is empty where the
windows are at 2.47 and the height is 44 at every size.

Not verified: his own eye at 4K; the raid HUD's canvas text outside the
zoomed panels (the "N notes logged" line under the gear panel is drawn with
no monitor factor, and every other FS() call outside a hudZoomIn block is
the same one-line change each; not touched here); the pad and keyboard, which
never see the drag label; the readout at the widest balance he can reach
against the 560 clearance beyond the seven figures the check stages.
