## v15.86 - A CONTROLLER CAN CHOOSE A SECTOR

Sector audit finding (HIGH), entry 4 of the confirmed list.

Play with only a controller. Walk to the lift and press A (go up), or press the map button on the floor: both call renderSector()
and openModal('sectormodal'), and the sector page opens with COLD STORAGE and THE COLD MILE listed, both offered (BETA_ONE_MAP is
true and both maps carry beta:1). With a panel up, pollPad hands the pad to padMenu, which moves the highlight only between the
controls padFocusables lists and presses A only as PAD.focus.click(). padFocusables built its list from
'button,.cell,.vcell,.crow,.invtab,.scard,.vpill,input,select', and renderSector builds each sector card as
<div class="row sectorpick" data-map=...>: a plain div whose classes match none of those (.crow is a different class). So the
D-pad and the stick moved only between DAY, NIGHT, the six weather buttons, ASCEND TO THIS SECTOR and CLOSE; the highlight could
never land on a sector card and A could never press one. The card's onclick is the only thing that sets P.mapIx, so a pad-only
player always went up to whatever P.mapIx already held, COLD STORAGE on a new profile; THE COLD MILE was unreachable and the
page's own question, Where are you going?, could not be answered. No comment or ruling says this is deliberate, it is not
balance, and no TXSHIP key is involved.

THE BUILD. One edit in padFocusables, the finding's own fix: .sectorpick joins the selector list, between .vpill and input, with
a v15.86 comment at the top of the function. The cards are display:block and visible, pointer-events is never none on them, and
a div is never disabled, so they pass every filter the function applies; the .padfocus rule is an outline, so it draws on a div;
A runs the same onclick a mouse click runs (mapOffered, P.mapIx, saveProfile, syncMapBtn, renderSector); and when that click
rebuilds the cards the v14.22 place keeping in padMenu puts the highlight back on the card at the same place in the list, because
the cards come first in the panel in document order and their count does not change. The list is scoped to the open panel and
.sectorpick exists only inside #sectorlist on the sector page, so no other panel gains a control. No word of player text was
touched, no number, dial, loot table or seeded draw moved; map building is not touched, so the seed 4242 fingerprint in
__verifySafe (ents 85/374, containers 165/593) cannot move.
