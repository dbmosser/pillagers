## v12.35 - HIS ORDER: THE SAFE POCKET IS DELETED

His order of 2026-09-08: "remove the safe pocket from the stash and just
delete the entire concept."

WHY, IN HIS WORDS AND MINE. He asked two things first: why a gun and a
throwable were refused, and how he was supposed to put anything in the
pocket during a raid or take it out. The second question had no good answer.
The pocket lived on the stash screen only and its choice was frozen at the
lift, so it could protect something he had carried up from home and never
the thing he had just pulled out of a vault, which is the only thing anyone
would want to protect. The feature was six builds of upkeep for a promise it
could not keep in the case it existed for. He decided it goes.

WHAT WENT. The panel and its label on the stash screen, the styles that only
ever dressed it, the two functions that read it, the setter, the renderer and
its drop target, the verb in the item menu, the line on the ascent page, the
arming at the drop with the six lines of comment that explained it, the
branch in the death payout, the entry in the player-facing history, and both
fields on the profile, which are deleted from any save that still carries
them. The line on the death card that said "Safe pocket held N items" is fed
by the automatic rebate, a separate developer dial that has sat at zero since
v5.57, not by the pocket at all; it printed the pocket's name, so with the
pocket gone it would have been the last place in the game still claiming one.
It names the rebate now.

WHAT DID NOT GO, and this is the trap in the word. The world container called
a safe, the secure cases in buildings and camps, the contract that asks you
to search them and every line that draws them are a different thing with the
same five letters, and they are untouched. The check proves it rather than
trusting me.

DATED HISTORY STAYS. Comments that say what the pocket WAS, in the places
where a past decision is being explained, are left alone; a comment that
told the reader where to find the live code is not, because that is how the
next reader learns that a deleted feature is still here.

MEASURED. Check 12.35 requires the four functions to be undefined, the panel
and its counter to be absent from a floor that has actually been opened and
rendered, and the words "safe pocket" to appear nowhere on the stash screen.
It then runs a real death: the outcome card must open on KILLED IN ACTION,
must still list what was lost, which is the ledger the deleted branch sat
inside, and must not say safe pocket. Finally it deploys again and requires
the map to have built at least one secure case, which is the guard on the
name collision. The corpus loses the check that tested the pocket, because a
check that can only skip is not a check, and the stash-screen check that
counted five parts now counts four. On the v12.34 fixture the first six
assertions fail.

Not verified: the profile migration itself, which runs at load and cannot be
re-run on a fixture that has already loaded, so an old save carrying a pocket
is cleaned by a line I have read and not driven; whether he wants anything in
its place, which is his call and which I have not assumed; the automatic
rebate dial, still at zero and still untested, which is the only remaining
way an item can survive a death.
