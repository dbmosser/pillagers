## v15.75 - THE PARTY SEES EACH OTHER ON THE UNDERCROFT FLOOR

His order of 2026-09-25: native multiplayer, co-op first, up to four. This is phase 1, build 2 of tools/multiplayer/plan.md
(section E), with the corrections in tools/multiplayer/critique.md. Raids stay solo and there is no voice yet (build 3).

THE BUILD.
- Every copy in a party sends where its man stands on the Undercroft floor about ten times a second: x, y, facing, walking or
  not, and what is left of a roll. What he is wearing (one rack id per kind: hair, hat, skin, fit, cut, beard, eyes, face,
  boots, gloves, pack, patch, tattoo, outfit) rides in his first packet, then once a second and whenever it changes. The message
  is JSON {t:'st',x,y,f,m,r,lk}; a friend sends it to the host; the host sends its own with s:0 and passes each friend on to the
  others with s set to that friend's seat, never back to him. The host files a friend under the seat it gave his link and never
  reads a seat a friend claims.
- THE CHANNEL. A second data channel on every link, negotiated like the first as id 1, unordered with maxRetransmits 0, so a lost
  packet is replaced by the next one a tenth of a second later instead of holding up the queue. Negotiated channels are not
  written into the offer, so the invite code does not grow. A link whose fast channel is not open uses the reliable channel
  (id 0), which is what a stand-in link in the check has. The fast channel closes with its link.
- netHubTick(dt), called from updateHubWorld while NET.on, AHEAD of the window test: a man reading the PARTY window at the lift
  is still seen. It eases each of the others toward where he last said he stood (1 - e^(-12 dt) a frame, about 14 units behind
  the last spot a walking man sent), turns him on the shortest arc, runs his walk cycle while he said he was walking, takes anyone whose
  seat left the roster off the floor list, and every 0.1 s sends where this man stands to every link that is in.
- netDrawPeers(DR), called from drawHubWorld while NET.on, puts each of the others into the floor's y-sorted list, so walls,
  posts, the crowd and the player cover him and are covered by him as they do each other; the list draws him with netDrawPeer,
  which is drawOp, the body the crowd and the pillagers use: hero 0, his coat from his fit, every other rack from his look,
  walking, standing or rolling as he said. netDrawTags(S,ox,oy), called after the station names, paints his name over his head
  in amber on a dark plate, above the darkness like the station names.
- The first word from a man, a jump past 160 units, or a word after 3 seconds of silence puts him where he said at once. Nobody
  is drawn who is not in the roster, nobody on this copy's own seat, and nobody who has said nothing for 3 seconds (up top in a
  raid, or his window hidden); he comes back when he speaks. A bye or a lost link takes his seat out of the roster and he is
  gone from the next frame; netReset clears the floor list.
- A look off the wire keeps only ids the racks know, each under its own kind (cosFind(id).kind), so nothing another copy sends
  reaches drawOp but a real rack id. Ownership is not checked: a copy that wears a rack it has not earned shows it to the others,
  which is cosmetic only. Positions must be numbers and are clamped to the room (700 by 470); a facing outside -100 to 100 is 0.
- The PARTY window blurb now says the others walk the floor with you, with their names over their heads, and every raid is still
  played alone. That sentence was new in v15.74 and is in none of his edits; no TXSHIP key is touched.
- The ?netslot test handle window.__net gains me() (where this copy stands, and its seat), floor() (who it draws, where, and
  whether shown) and fast() (how many fast channels are open), for tools/nettest.html.
