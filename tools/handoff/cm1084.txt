v10.84: machines could not find the door

His note: a crawler got close and did not hurt him. Reproduced, and it is worse
than a crawler. A machine inside a building could not route out to a player
standing outside it: twenty seconds, never closer than 97 units, zero damage,
while the same crawler in the open kills in ten.

The router says so itself, carrying pathFail for the whole chase. The pathing
grid inflates every wall by 12 units on 16 unit cells, so a 64 unit doorway
keeps 40 units of width, and anything that narrows it further seals the door on
the grid while leaving it open in the world. Five of the twenty buildings on
COLD STORAGE were boxes nothing could path out of.

Doorways are now recorded and their cells opened, but only where the cell centre
is outside every wall rectangle with no padding, so no route can be cut through
anything solid. 0 of 20 trapped after, and entities, containers and walls are
identical to v10.83.
