## v13.32 - THE QUICK ASCENT SAYS YOUR OWN GUN IS IN THE STASH

THE SECOND DOOR. v13.31 put the stash-gun line on the sector page. The lift's quick
ascent key skips that page by design and starts the raid at once. Since his ruling a
new player's pack guns wait in the stash with nothing equipped, so that door rolls
him a random starter, and buildRaid issues it without a word. The only message about
issued guns fires when he tries to stow one.

ONE LINE AT LANDING, only when the gun in his hands was issued and a usable gun of his
own is in the stash. It is said on every door, because the raid is where the loaner is
actually in his hands, so a player who came through the sector page and still did not
equip hears it once more.

IT WAITS ITS TURN. say() holds one message for 3.2 seconds, and a second say in the
same frame overwrites the first, which is the fault his notes found twice. Landing
already says the weather line. While a message is showing, the line is kept in
G.msgNext and the frame loop shows it the moment the current one runs out. Nothing
else uses that slot.

floor update, steps the real frame loop about five seconds, past the weather line, and
records every message shown. With a gun in the stash the line must appear; with none,
or with a gun of his own equipped, it must not. Fails on v13.31.
