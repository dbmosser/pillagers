## v14.32 - THE LIGHTNING FLASH RUNS OUT AFTER THE STORM PASSES

Weather audit finding 2 (MEDIUM). A bolt sets a 0.34 second flash that lights the street and lifts the fog. The renderer
counted it down only while the current weather had lightning. Weather changes by blending over a 14 second turn, and the
lightning flag switches at the halfway point. A bolt in the last third of a second before a storm's turn passed halfway left
the flash above zero with nothing left to count it down. The flash stayed near full and the fog sheet stayed lifted for the
rest of the raid; at night the fog fell from 0.60 to about 0.16 opacity. Roughly one turn in 25 out of a storm.

THE BUILD. The flash counts down on every drawn frame, whatever the weather. In a storm it runs exactly as before.

MEASURED. Check 14.32 starts a raid, sets a 0.3 second flash and draws five 0.1 second frames in storm weather (control:
out), then does the same in weather with no lightning (must be out). It fails on v14.31.

Not verified: a storm turning over in a live raid, by eye.
