## v10.59 - BEHIND A WALL YOU LOOK LIKE YOURSELF, FADED

His note, 2026-09-03 about 21:30: **"wheb ny characterr stands behind a
wall they change color lol"**.

### What it was

The v9.27 see-through pass, his #45, paints the operator again after the
sorted pass when a wall's drawn top covers him, so he never vanishes. It
painted him as one flat light-blue colour, coat #9fd8ff with no hero racks,
at half alpha. Behind cover he turned blue. The Undercroft's pass (v10.51)
already draws the real figure, so the two rooms disagreed.

### Now

The raid's pass makes the same call the sorted pass makes for him: his own
coat, his racks, his outfit if he wears one, his stance, his gun, the roll
and the reload, at 0.55 alpha and without the hurt shimmer. Behind a wall he
looks like himself seen through it. Nothing else about the pass changes:
the same test decides when it fires and the same lift places it.

### Measured

The check starts a raid at seed 4242, finds a wall at least 60 by 60 with
open ground under its drawn top, stands the operator there, renders a
frame with the pass off and one with it on, requires at least 80 pixels to
differ, then samples the chest, 16 units up the figure, in both frames and
compares blue over red: the ghost may not be bluer than the wall top behind
it by more than 15. On v10.58 the chest is bluer by 30, which is the
finding; on this build the check returns null. My first version of this
check measured the light-blue share of the whole figure and passed the old
build, because the cutout was blue only on the torso and the rest was
outline, skin and hair; the control run caught it before anything shipped.

Not verified: the look by eye, which is his; and a downed operator behind
a wall, which takes the down pose through the same call and was not
rendered here.
