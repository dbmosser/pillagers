v12.30: a crawler that reaches you bites

The audit's top finding, and his standing crawler note. A crawler's wander
timer and its bite cooldown were one field: on patrol it rolls three to seven
seconds whenever the timer runs out, and a bite only fires when that field is
at zero. Nothing on the way into a chase cleared it, so a crawler that
reached you stood there serving out the rest of its wander before biting.

My first draft clamped the field at three of the ways into a chase. The
draft review killed it: there are five, the two it missed are the ones you
cause by shooting, and one of the three could never fire. The wander has its
own field on a crawler now, which covers every way in at once. Sentries keep
the shared field on purpose, since a faster first shot from a sentry is a
balance call.

Check 12.30 stages nothing on the clock: it parks a crawler on patrol 25
units from a still player, lets the game roll its own wander, and requires
the first bite inside a second and a half, with a chasing crawler as the
ruler that the room can bite at all and a wandering one to show the wander
still runs and leaves the cooldown clear; fails on v12.29.
