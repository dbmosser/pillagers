## v15.11 - AN INSTANT QUIT OR A RELOAD FORGETS THE FREEBIE KIT PACKING

Quit audit finding 9 (LOW). Pack a Medkit and Bandages, choose TAKE THE FREEBIE KIT and ascend. commitKit sets that packing
aside in P.kitBeforeFree and saves it, so a death card can say the packed items never left the stash. The only clear was at the
end of endRaid, after the raid settles, and two routes never reached it. An abandon in the first 1.5 s with no steps, looting or
shots takes the instant-quit branch, which saves and returns before the clear. A raid that ended with the page going away (a
closed tab, F5, a crash) left the list saved, and nothing cleared it at load. Next session, pack your own kit, choose MY
LOADOUT, ascend and die: the card counted the old list against the stash and said the items packed before choosing the freebie
kit were in the stash, on a raid where no freebie kit was taken. The stale list also stuck: commitKit keeps a list that is not
empty, so a later freebie kit taken at the stash was counted against the old packing rather than the new one.

THE BUILD. The instant-quit branch clears P.kitBeforeFree, P.hotBeforeFree and P.gunBeforeFree before its save, as every other
raid end already does. applyLoadedProfile clears the same three beside the v14.03 raidSpliced cleanup, where no raid is running;
its callers are boot and the Settings restore from a backup file, which replaces the whole character (restoreApply already
clears the list the same way). hotBeforeFree and gunBeforeFree are written but never read and are cleared so no stale copy is
left. No player text changes and no number moves.

MEASURED. Check 15.11 runs three arms in order. One: a save holding a three-item list is loaded through the loader (control: the
loader replaced the profile and deleted a staged raidSpliced, so the block with the fix ran); the list must be gone. Two: the
freebie kit is taken through commitKit over the same packing (control: the list is set aside), the raid goes up at seed 4242
through startRaid with the clock zeroed (controls: the raid carries the freebie kit, the list survived the ascent, and it is an
instant quit by clock, distance, containers and shots) and is abandoned (controls: the run ended as an abandon, G is null and the
run count did not move); the list must be gone. Three: his own kit goes up with no freebie kit at seed 4242 and he dies (controls:
the stash holds copies of the old packing, the raid is not a freebie raid, and the death card is drawn); the card must not carry
the line about items packed before the freebie kit, read with an assembled needle. A control that cannot run after a failure
keeps the failure. It restores the profile through the loader. It fails on v15.10.
