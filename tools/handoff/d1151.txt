## v11.51 - MY v11.42 BAKE NAMED THE WRONG MAP ON THE SECTOR PAGE

From the v11.46 read-only audit, P1, and mine. When his 71 wording edits were
baked in at v11.42, every number-shaped line was also turned into a digit-shape
pattern, the same thing his own editor does, so a line like the credit counter
refills its live numbers. Two of his lines are the sector-facts line under each
map: "you: 2 runs here, 420 by 340 metres, 14.3 hectares, 5 named zones, 2 keyed
rooms, the test robot extracts in 39.2% of its raids in Cold Storage." and the
same for The Cold Mile. Strip the digits and the two are one shape. The
derivation keyed both to that shape, the second overwrote the first, and TX
then matched ANY sector line of that shape by its digits, so COLD STORAGE could
end "in The Cold Mile." and a player with two runs on the mile could read "in
Cold Storage." The exact lines, with his own figures, were right; every other
figure took the wrong map.

THE FIX. Those two lines are exact-only. The derivation skips any key that
carries "test robot extracts", so each map keeps its own baked line with his
own figures, and a line drawn with other figures is left exactly as the game
drew it, which is the original wording, correct for its map.

MEASURED. Check 11.51 reads both sector keys from the shipped map at run time
(they hold a middot, so they are never typed into a check), requires the exact
line to still map to his wording, then changes the first figure in each and
requires TX to hand the changed line back untouched. On the v11.50 fixture the
changed line is rewritten by shape and the check fails naming the map it
printed.

Not verified: his own eye on the sector page; his OWN profile, whose editor
also derived these two shapes into P.txp and will still cross the names for
him until he clears those two edits (his profile wins over the shipped map by
design); whether a third map would need the same exemption.
