## v11.14 - NO PIECE OF FURNITURE SITS IN A DOORWAY

The second half of the thing v11.12 found. That build stopped machines walking
into windows and then said plainly that it freed nobody on COLD STORAGE, because
the doors themselves were blocked. This is the doors.

**MEASURED FIRST, on v11.13 at seed 4242.** A doorway is plugged when the longest
clear run across its 64 unit gap, counting furniture within 40 units of the wall
on either side, is under the 30 units a body needs. **11 of 37 doorways on COLD
STORAGE and 35 of 151 on THE COLD MILE were plugged**, and 2 and 27 more were
tight, under 44. Building 8 on COLD STORAGE is the case traced at v11.12: a 36 by
26 piece inside the doorway, 10.5 units one side and 17.5 the other, and the
crawler inside never came within 240 units of a player standing outside, at seven
seconds or at thirty. This is the v10.40 "niches plugged by furniture" line, and
it was never cosmetic; it sealed machines in and it is the last piece of
"crawlers aren't attacking properly" I can find.

**WHY IT HAPPENED.** The furniture pass shoves every piece after the first flush
against the nearest wall, on purpose, so that a piece cannot split a room and
seal it. A wall with a door in it is still the nearest wall, and nothing in that
pass had ever heard of doors. The doorways are recorded before the furniture is
placed, in the same function, so the information was there all along.

**THE FIX.** Each doorway owns a zone: the gap grown 40 units through the wall
both ways and 12 along it. A piece landing in a zone is not placed. Re-rolling
it would have drawn from the seeded stream and moved every wall, crawler and
container placed after it, which is the mistake v10.81 made and measured. Not
placing it draws nothing.

**MY FIRST CUT SLID THE PIECE ALONG ITS WALL INSTEAD, AND THE CORPUS CAUGHT IT
SEALING TWO ROOMS.** Buildings 26 and 51 on THE COLD MILE held floor nothing
could reach, because the interior partitions have doorways of their own that the
door list has never heard of, and a slid piece lands wherever the zone ends. A
piece that is not placed cannot seal anything, so that is what ships. The cost is
the doorway pieces themselves: **furniture 169 to 136 on COLD STORAGE and 540 to
440 on THE COLD MILE**, four fifths kept on both. That is one piece in five, and
every one of them was standing in a door.

**MEASURED AFTER, both maps, the dial off against on:** plugged doorways 11 to 0
and 35 to 0, tight doorways 2 to 0 and 27 to 0. Entities 85 and 374 both ways,
containers 165 and 593 both ways. Walls 610 to 580 and 2403 to 2308, outdoor
cover on the mile 483 to 488. **The crawler in building 8 now reaches the
player**, inside 60 units at eleven seconds, against never in thirty before.

**AND THE WORLD DID MOVE, IN ONE PLACE I DID NOT EXPECT, WHICH THE JERSEY CHECK
FOUND.** v10.46 reads the pale numeral on the 23 jersey at the drop point and it
went from 20 pixels to 2. Not the jersey: the light. Lamps are placed by asking
the wall list where one may stand, so with thirty fewer walls one more lamp fits
on COLD STORAGE, 59 to 60, and each lamp draws four numbers from the seeded
stream. The time of day and the weather are rolled AFTER the lamps, so seed 4242
went from noon in fog to six in the evening under a clear sky, and the golden
light pulled the white numeral under the check's brightness line. Entities and
containers are placed before the lamps and did not move. So: **for any given
seed the sky may be different from what it was; nothing on the ground is.** Same
mechanism as the cover count that v9.77 and this build re-pin, and it is why a
check that measures colour must pin the light it measures under, which v10.46
now does.

**THREE OLDER CHECKS MOVED WITH IT, and each says why in its own text.** v9.81
pins the wall count and v9.65 the cover count, the way v9.77 moved them before;
both now carry 2308 and 580, and 488, with the entity line beside them as the
half of the fingerprint that says the seeded stream did not move where it
matters. v9.79 asked for no unreachable floor anywhere with no size floor at
all, and had only ever passed because the buildings holding slivers between
furniture pieces were the same buildings whose plugged doorway got them stripped
bare. With the doors open those slivers show. v10.40 already ruled a pocket
narrower than a body cosmetic and its own line, so v9.79 now asks for a room,
32 by 32 units, and the niche line stays open in AUDIT.md exactly as it was.

**NEW CHECK v11.14** builds both maps twice, dial off and on, and requires zero
plugged doorways with the dial on. Controls: the dial off must still show at
least 8 and 25 plugged, or the A/B is two copies of the same map; the entity
count must be identical either way, or the rule drew a random number; furniture
must keep at least seven tenths, with the four fifths measured named in the
message; and the crawler in building 8, by its 2520,900,380,340 fingerprint, must
reach a player outside inside 60 units within fifteen seconds. On a v11.13 fixture it fails on both
plugged counts and on the crawler, at 273 units.

Not verified: the extract rate, again. This is the build that actually lets
machines out of buildings, so it is the one that changes what a raid feels like,
and every number I have for it is a doorway count and one crawler. The paired
run at the 320-seed standard is several hours here and was not run. furnDoor 0
puts every piece back where it was.
