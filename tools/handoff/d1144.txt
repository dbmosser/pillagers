## v11.44 - THE RIG-BUYBACK MIGRATION SAVED DEFAULTS OVER THE PLAYER'S CONFIG

Queued at v11.31 ("a save mid-load writes P.cfg before the cfgv block reads it")
and now reproduced. loadProfile runs a chain of one-time migrations on the saved
profile as it loads. One of them, mig945, is the v9.45 rig buyback: rigs left
the game, so anything the player still owns is bought back once. It ended with a
call to saveProfile to lock the buyback in. saveProfile does P.cfg=CFG and
P.cfgv=17. The trouble is WHERE it runs: mig945 sits near the top of loadProfile,
well before the cfgv block that loads CFG from the saved P.cfg. At that point CFG
is still the file-scope default (preset B), so saveProfile wrote the DEFAULT dials
over the player's saved ones and stamped cfgv 17. The cfgv block then saw cfgv 17
and applied none of its migrations, and loaded the defaults it had just written.
The net effect: the first time an older save is opened, the player's tuning is
silently reset and every pending cfgv migration is skipped.

**REPRODUCED.** A pre-mig945 profile carrying a distinctive ambient of 250 (the
default is 190, and the cfgv-12 migration only moves 150 to 190, so 250 should be
left alone) with a rig in the stash and cfgv 12. Driven through the real loader:
ambient came back as 190, cfgv became 17, and the credits rose by the rig payout
(so the buyback itself ran). The saved dial was gone.

**THE FIX.** mig945 persists through storeSet, the raw writer, instead of
saveProfile. storeSet writes the profile exactly as it stands, saved cfg and cfgv
intact, and still locks in the buyback (credits and stash) and the mig945 stamp.
Nothing downstream changes: the cfgv block now sees the real saved cfgv and cfg
and migrates them as it always meant to.

**MEASURED after the fix.** The same profile loads with ambient still 250 in the
profile and live in CFG, cfgv left at 12, and the buyback still paid with the rig
gone from the stash. Because loadProfile is asynchronous and the corpus runner is
synchronous, the post-load body was factored into a named applyLoadedProfile (a
pure extraction, storeGet().then(applyLoadedProfile) runs the identical code), so
the check drives the REAL loader synchronously rather than a copy of it. The
check requires the saved dial to survive in both the profile and CFG, cfgv not to
be stamped 17, and two controls that the buyback still pays and removes the rig.

The control for teeth: the exact same scenario run on the v11.43 build through the
async loader clobbered ambient to 190 and stamped cfgv 17, and a throwaway v11.44
build with the saveProfile clobber put back fails the check on the ambient
assertion. The corpus control fixture from v11.43 has no applyLoadedProfile seam
and so skips the check, which the check says plainly.

Not verified: his own play; whether his current live save still carries the
damage from when mig945 first ran under the old code (it would have reset his
tuning once, back at v9.45, and cannot be undone from here); saves in storage
slots other than the active one are not exercised by the check.
