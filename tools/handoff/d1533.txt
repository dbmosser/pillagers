## v15.33 - A MISSED EXTRACTION OFFERS ANOTHER CALL ONLY WHEN ONE CAN ARRIVE

Extraction audit finding. When a landed extraction window runs out with him within 1400 units, tickExtractPoints clanks and says
"Extraction left without you. Hold E to call for another, 0:25 out." It said that on every missed window, and in two cases the
call it offers cannot work:
- THE CLOCK. The window is clamped to the raid clock (z.hold is at most timeLeft-1), so a call made with 0:50 left lands at 0:25
  with a 24 s window that ends with 0:01 on the clock. A new call takes extractWait (25 s by default), and the frame loop ends the
  raid on the timer long before it could arrive. The call line already knows this case and warns that the raid clock runs out
  first. Every missed window that ends with less than extractWait on the clock hit this, which is any call made with under about
  80 s left.
- THE CLOSING. A point past its closing time stays open only while it is called. The missed-window branch clears the call, so the
  close loop later in the same tickExtractPoints shuts the point in that same tick and queues "Extraction A is closed." behind the
  wrong line. Holding E there only gets "This point is CLOSED."
Neither sentence is a key in the TXSHIP table.

THE BUILD. The line keeps its first sentence, "Extraction left without you.", and then:
- past the point's closing time (closeAt set and timeLeft at or under it), nothing more, so the queued closed line that follows is
  the news and he does not read it twice;
- with the raid clock on and less than extractWait left, " Another call cannot beat the raid clock.";
- otherwise the old " Hold E to call for another, 0:25 out." exactly as before.
closeAt is undefined with the clock off (buildRaid), so a raid with no clock always gets the old line. The clank, the counters and
the clearing of the window are untouched. No balance number, dial, loot table or sound moves.

Left alone, outside this finding: "Hold E" in this line still names E on a controller (listed open at v15.32).

MEASURED. Check 15.33 deploys a raid, clears the machines, records say, sayWhenFree and blip, and stages one point at a time: every
point quiet, this one landed (beaconT -1) with 0.01 s of window left, him 300 units off its middle, the raid clock set, and one
tickExtractPoints(0.05). Controls: the raid clock is on; at 200 s on a point with no closing time the window ends (window cleared,
clank, the point still open) and the missed extraction line is said, on either build; past a closing time the point is shut in the
same tick and its closed line queued. Kept: at 200 s the line still offers another call. The finding: with half an extraction wait
on the clock (12 s) the line must not offer a call and must name the raid clock; 5 s past another point's closing time it must
offer no call and not blame the clock. v15.32 offers the call in both, so the check fails there. The needles are assembled from
words, so the check never matches its own source. Everything staged (zones, the pointer and mirrors, the clock, the message and
its queue, the machines, his position, the missed-window counter) is put back in finally before the raid is abandoned.

Dry run on scratch copies of the page and the fixture (1527 is already applied to both): p1528 to p1533 and f1528 to f1533 applied
in order, every anchor a whole line matching once, the version and DEVNOW moved to 15.33, and the patched block and the new check
parsed under JScript.

Not verified: check 15.33 has not been run in a browser, and no real raid has missed a window with the clock short or at a closing
point.
