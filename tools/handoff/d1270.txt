## v12.70 - A SEARCH CONTRACT NAMES A PLACE ON THE SECTOR YOU ARE PLAYING

From the 2026-09-08 read-only audit, confirmed by a skeptic against the source.
The last defect on that list. Verified by reading at v12.69.

WHAT IT WAS. A district contract names a place, and it worked that name out
once, when the card was written, against whichever sector was selected at that
moment. The sentence was then frozen on the card. The sector can be changed
afterwards with one click, and nothing on the board notices.

The two sectors have places whose names are one letter apart. Roll the card on
one and read it on the other, and it sends him to a place with almost the right
name, in the far corner from the district it actually wants. He searches it out
and the counter does not move, because the containers there carry a different
district number. The panel during the raid repeats the same wrong place, because
it prints the stored sentence verbatim.

THE CARD ITSELF WAS NEVER BROKEN. The district number on it is right, and it
still ticks on the right containers wherever they are on whichever map. Only the
label was stale. That is why the fix is not to store a sector on the card:
contracts deliberately stopped carrying one, and reversing that would undo a
decision rather than repair a fault.

THE BUILD. The sentence is no longer frozen. The district is what the card
holds, and the place it names is worked out on the way to the screen, so it
follows the sector he is actually playing. Both readers, the board and the panel
in the raid, ask the same question.

IT COSTS NOTHING ANYWHERE IT CAN BE MEASURED. No card changes what it asks for,
no reward moves, and the progress test is untouched.

MEASURED. Check 12.70 writes a district card whose sentence names a place that
does not exist on the sector it will be read on, which is exactly the state a
sector change leaves behind, and then asks what the card says. The place names
come off the maps themselves rather than out of the check, so a renamed zone
moves the check with it, and it skips honestly if both sectors happen to call
that district the same thing. One control switches back and requires the
sentence to follow again rather than sticking; another requires a card of any
other type to read exactly what it was written with.

Not verified: it reads the sentence through the one function both screens now
use rather than opening the board and the raid panel; it does not walk a raid to
confirm the counter moves in the right district, which the progress test already
covers; and it does not re-word cards already saved on a profile, which now
simply have their stale sentence ignored.
