## v15.79 - THE PARTY ASCENDS TOGETHER INTO ONE SURFACE AND SEES EACH OTHER UP TOP

His order of 2026-09-25: native multiplayer, co-op first. This is phase 2, build 1 of tools/multiplayer/plan.md with the corrections
in critique.md: the smallest slice of the shared raid. No shared pillagers, machines, loot, bullets or extraction yet. Each window
still runs its own pillagers and machines locally (host snapshots replace them in the next slices), and each player still extracts,
dies or abandons on its own, coming back empty on its own save as today. His rulings hold: no team damage of any kind (nobody up
top is a target for anything yet, and nobody collides); the host dropping mid-raid does not end the others in THIS slice (the
abandon-for-everyone ruling is the next slice, once the world is the host's); revives later; numbers frozen.

THE BUILD.
- ASCEND TOGETHER. The host ascends as always: the sector page (ASCEND TO THIS SECTOR), the loadout question (MY LOADOUT or
  FREEBIE KIT), ascendNow, startRaid; or quick ascent (R at the lift), which also ends in startRaid. Right after buildRaid returns
  in startRaid, the party is told on the reliable channel (netUpAnnounce, behind NET.on and the host role):
    {t:'raid',seed,mapIx,cond,wx,iss,terms,merc,cfg,fp}
  seed is G.seed; mapIx the sector; cond 'day' or 'night' (P.cond, which buildRaid reads through isDay for the night counts); wx the
  id of the weather the host got (pickWeather is one seeded draw whose ANSWER a pinned pick replaces, so the linked window pins it
  to the host answer and the draw still happens); iss 1 or 0, whether the host drew a loaner from the seeded stream (G.issDraw,
  stamped in the raid literal from the draw site); terms the host terms (hasTerm('silence') places a Listener and blackout moves
  the hour); merc the host hire id (P.merc splices the identity pool and adds a body); cfg every key of CFG that differs from DEF
  (the Settings rows and console overrides; the fixture pins are such keys); fp the fingerprint of the built world (below).
- THE LINKED WINDOW (netUpTake on a join role, from the real netOnMsg): if it is not on the Undercroft floor past the title with no
  raid in hand (up top already, on the title, still on the run card) it is told in the PARTY window that the party went up without
  it and why, and the host is told {t:'up',st:'no',why} and names it in its status line; the word is otherwise ignored. In the
  Undercroft, netUpStart: a JSON snapshot of P is taken; commitKit runs (its own packed kit, exactly as MY LOADOUT commits it,
  saved under its own dials); its CFG is copied aside and the host world fields go in: every DEF key reset to its default, the
  host cfg on top (keys of DEF only, scalars only), P.mapIx, P.cond, P.terms, P.merc, P.wxPick=wx, P.intel=0; NET.upHold is set
  and NET.upIss=iss; pendSeed=seed; startRaid() runs, the real one. In finally the guard comes off, pendSeed is cleared, CFG comes
  back whole, and P.terms, P.merc, P.intel and P.wxPick come back. P.mapIx and P.cond stay the host ones: the fog saves under
  P.mapIx (critique 4.2), and the hour is the hour of this raid.
  saveProfile returns at once while NET.upHold is set (the one line added to it): buildRaid saves inside its kit block and
  startRaid saves when it spends the intel core, and either would have written P.cfg=CFG with the host dials in (critique A.6 and
  A.7). Once the world fields are out the build is saved once, so the kit that went up, the sector and the hour are on disk under
  its own dials. The intel core is not spent (P.intel is 0 for the build and comes back), and G.intel is not set, so a linked
  window gets no key marks off a core it keeps (the plan: clients' intel is off in v1).
- THE LOANER DRAW. buildRaid draws WEAPONS[pick(STARTERS)] only when the player has no gun equipped, one seeded draw before the
  spawn and population rolls (net.md 2: a client building with its own kit would place a different population). In a party build
  (NET.upHold) buildRaid reads NET.upIss: a window that would draw when the host did not draws its loaner with RNGS saved and put
  back round the draw; a window that would not draw when the host did makes the same draw and drops it. Outside a party build the
  local is null and the block is what it was. The population lands where the host put it, and the window keeps its own gun or gets
  its loaner as before.
- THE FINGERPRINT (netUpFp): the counts of bodies, containers and walls, the weather id, and an FNV-1a hash (Math.imul, never the
  seeded stream) over every wall by x, y, w, h, every body by kind, x, y and every container by x, y, rounded. It is computed on
  the host right after the build and on the linked window right after its own, and compared field by field (netUpFpSame). A
  mismatch, or a throw inside startRaid, never plays a different surface: G is dropped and keys cleared, showScreen('hub'), the P
  snapshot is put back in place (netUpPutP writes into the same object every closure holds, deleting keys the snapshot lacks) and
  saved, the PARTY window says which world each side built and that this window stays in the Undercroft, the floor says so in one
  line, and the host is told {t:'up',st:'no',why:'surface'}. The kit committed for the ascent is back in the stash.
- SEE EACH OTHER. The loop's raid branch calls netUpTick(dt) while NET.on, ahead of the pause and superhot branches so a paused
  or frozen player is still seen where he stands. It eases everyone on NET.up as the floor does (1 - e^(-12 dt), shortest arc,
  the walk cycle while he said he was walking, the roll running down), takes off anyone whose seat left the roster, and every 0.1 s
  sends the v15.75 word from up top on the fast channel (the reliable one where the fast is not open):
    {t:'st',k:'r',sd,x,y,f,m,r,c,sp,dn,w[,lk][,s]}
  k:'r' marks a word from up top; sd is the seed of the raid the sender is in; c crouched, sp sprinting, dn downed, w the id of
  the gun in hand; lk the look as on the floor, in the first word, once a second and on change; the host names its seat 0, a
  friend names none. netOnState files a word with k r on NET.up (clamped to a 20000 by 20000 surface, not the 700 by 470 floor),
  never on NET.floor, keeps c, sp, dn, w and sd on the entry, and the host passes it on to the other links as it came, never back
  to the sender. render2D pushes each shown entry into the depth list right after the player, keyed by y minus the deck lift
  (netUpDraw), and the list draws it with netUpDrawOne: drawOp as the player is drawn up top, hero 0, his fit colour, his racks
  from his look, his gun from w (own carries the gun and the hair lag, as any body), mode down, roll, crouch, none (no gun) or
  standing, walking and sprinting as he said, lifted by the deck he stands on, wrapped so a fault in one body never costs the
  frame. netUpTags paints his name in amber on a dark plate over his head, in the world pass right before the pillager
  nameplates, so it sits where those do. Shown means: said something, said it in the last 3 s, not this seat, in the roster, and
  the same seed as this raid (a word from another seed is filed and not drawn, so a window still in an old raid never draws into
  a new one).
- ENDINGS. endRaid, right after G.over is set and while NET.on, calls netUpEnd: NET.up is emptied, the seed and fingerprint let go,
  and every link is told {t:'up',st:'out',how}. On receipt (netUpWord) the seat's entry is dropped at once, so a player whose raid
  ended is hidden the same frame; the host passes up words on under the seat of the link they came from. A bye or a lost link takes
  the seat out of the roster and the next tick takes him off. netReset clears NET.up with the floor list. The host ending its own
  raid does not end anyone else's in this slice.
- NET gains up (the list by seat), upAcc, upN (the send clock), upSeed, upFp, upHold (the party build guard) and upIss (the loaner
  word). The ?netslot and ?p2 test handle window.__net gains up(): whether this window is up top and whether its raid is over, the
  seed and fingerprint it holds, where its player stands, and the list with each entry's seat, name, place, target, seed and shown.
