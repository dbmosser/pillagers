## v12.91 - THE LEFT STICK CRAWLS WHEN YOU ARE DOWN

Finding 11 of the 2026-09-11 read-only audit. The downed screen asks him to do one
thing, and on a controller that one thing is the only thing he cannot do.

WHAT HAPPENS. He is on the pad, and the game itself told him the left stick moves.
He is shot to the floor 200 units short of the ring. The downed overlay says CRAWL
TO THE RING, says "Crawl to an open extraction point if possible", and tells him
how far the nearest one is. He pushes the stick and does not move a single unit for
the whole bleed-out, then dies where he fell.

WHY. The downed branch builds its movement vector from keyboard codes alone and
then moves the body. The stick lives in PAD.mx and PAD.my, which pollPad refreshes
every frame and which every OTHER movement site reads: the standing branch, the
roll direction, and the Undercroft walk. This branch is the one place it was never
added, and nothing anywhere synthesises a movement key from the pad.

THE PAD IS NOT DEAD WHILE DOWNED, WHICH MAKES IT WORSE RATHER THAN BETTER. D-DOWN
still self-revives and X still extracts, because both go through keys the pad does
write. And the left stick still swings the aim cursor, so he turns on the spot
while going nowhere. Movement alone was missing, so nothing on screen suggested the
controller had stopped being read.

THE BUILD. The same one-line stick override the other three movement sites use,
placed before the magnitude so the flat 30-units-a-second crawl normalisation is
untouched. This adds an input, not a speed.

MEASURED, AND THE FIRST VERSION OF THE CHECK WAS WRONG IN A WAY WORTH RECORDING.
Setting PAD.mx by hand proves nothing: pollPad runs every frame before the raid
update, and with no gamepad present its first act is to set PAD.on false and PAD.mx
and PAD.my to zero and return, so anything poked into PAD is gone before
updatePlayer reads it. The check went red on the fixed build for that reason and
not for the build's. It now stubs navigator.getGamepads to hand back one connected
pad with the axes it wants, so the whole real chain runs: pollPad reads the axes,
applies its own deadzone and normalisation, sets PAD, and the crawl reads it.

Check 12.91 crawls all four directions twice, once on the keys with a pad connected
and the stick at rest, and once on the stick. The keyboard arm says whether the way
is open at all, so a body lying against a wall cannot make this check fail for the
map rather than the build, and it doubles as the control that reading the stick has
not taken the keyboard away from a pad player. The pairing is also the speed
control: the stick must cover the same ground and not more. Three arms fail on the
v12.90 fixture, each reporting 0 units on the stick against 15 or 16 on the keys.

Not verified: the fake pad reports axes and no buttons, so it does not re-test
D-DOWN self-revive or X extract, which the audit confirmed already work; and it
does not check the analog magnitude, because the crawl is deliberately flat-speed
and a half-pushed stick should crawl at the same rate as a full one.
