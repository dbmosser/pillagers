v10.83: the map marks the buildings that have fallen

A destroyed building has been a tactical fact since v10.81 and the map painted
it exactly like a standing one. Measured on COLD STORAGE: the two fallen
buildings read 69.29 and 64.31 mean brightness against 72.86 to 82.22 for the
standing ones near them, a gap of 3.57 where the standing ones vary by 9.36
among themselves, so they were only darker by accident.

They are now drawn at less than half the fill alpha, hatched with diagonals at
a fixed screen spacing, and outlined with a broken line rather than a closed
box.

Two wrong instruments before the right one: brightness cannot judge it because
the mile's standing buildings vary by 58 points, and edge counting cannot
because a busy square reads 49 where a hatched ruin reads 31. Redrawing the
same map with the ruin flags cleared isolates the mark exactly: every fallen
building moves 80 to 95 percent of its square, every standing one moves 0.
