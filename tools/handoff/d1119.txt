## v11.19 - THE MEASUREMENT THE LAST SEVEN BUILDS OWED

Nothing in the game changes in this build. Seven builds in a row, v11.12 to
v11.18, ended with the same line: the extract rate was not measured, because a
paired run at the 320-seed standard was several hours in this environment. This
tick it was not, and it ran.

**THE RUN.** 320 seeds, 9001 to 9320, COLD STORAGE, the bot at its pinned
greed of 14, each seed raided twice: once with every building rule since v11.12
switched off (winWalk, furnDoor, navBody, doorClear, furnIDoor, furnGap,
partDoor, all 0), which is the world of v11.11, and once with all seven on,
which is the world that ships. Same seed, same map, same drop, same robot; only
the rules differ. About thirty five minutes in two batches.

**THE RATE DID NOT MOVE.** Old rules extract **34.7 percent**, new rules **35.0
percent**. Both arms extract on 55 seeds, neither on 152, the old arm alone on
56 and the new arm alone on 57. That is 113 discordant pairs splitting 56 to
57, z of minus 0.09, which is as close to no difference as a run of this size
can read. Machines getting out of buildings, routes that respect windows and
body width, doors clear of furniture and walls: **none of it changed how often
the bot comes home.**

**WHAT DID MOVE IS WHERE IT DIES, and by how long it lives.** Killed by a
pillager: 20 to 9. Ran out the clock: 5 to 12. Warden 4 to 9, Bulwark 5 to 8,
sentry 70 to 66, crawler 88 to 90. Mean raid length, extract or death, 124
seconds to 141. Mean haul 2,083 to 2,065. Read together: on honest routes the
bot walks further to get anywhere, so its raids run seventeen seconds longer, it
meets the big machines more and the pillagers less, and it runs the clock out
more than twice as often. The crawlers, which every one of the seven builds was
about, kill it the same number of times either way; they could not reach it
before and mostly still do not, because the bot does not go into buildings the
way he does.

**WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY.** It says the alpha's difficulty for the
robot is unchanged by seven builds of geometry and routing work, which is the
thing I could not say before. It does not say what the changes do to HIM: the
bot never hides and never fights pillagers, so the crawler that could not see a
hidden man through a window, the one that stood in a doorway plugged by a
table, and the one that caught on a partition corner were all bugs a person met
and the bot did not. The measurement is of the machine's world, which is what
the sim measures.

**NEW CHECK v11.19** pins the seven rules on by name in the shipping defaults,
and requires that switching all seven off builds a different world for the same
seed, different walls and furniture and a different route grid, while entities
and containers stay identical, which is what makes a paired seed a fair
comparison. Its message names the rule that ships off, if one ever does.

Not verified: THE COLD MILE, which was not run, and his own play, which no sim
covers. furnIDoor 0 and the other six put the old world back for any seed.
