## v15.21 - A SELF-REVIVE RESETS YOUR HIRE PICKUP

he kneels beside you for his 3.2 second pickup. Press F before he finishes. selfRevive stands you up, and the hire pickup branch
of updateEnts, gated on p.downed, stops running with e.revSaid still 1 and e.revProgP at whatever his clock had reached, say 2.8.
Nothing else writes either field: the only clear was inside a pickup that finished, selfRevive and the down in damagePlayer never

is the only way up. The line is skipped because revSaid is still 1, and if he is still within 44 (under FOLLOW he only moves once
you are past 120) his clock carries on from 2.8, so he pulls you up after about 0.4 seconds instead of 3.2.

reaches that line (the seen-you block skips him, and the earlier continues he can hit are brief: a roll, his own down and a mortar
ring). The next down then says his line and starts his clock at zero, as the first down does; his line replacing the DOWN toast
through say() is what the first down already does. No player text changes, and the 3.2 seconds and the 40 percent stand-up are
