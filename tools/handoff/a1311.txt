| THE WAY OUT NOW HAS A TEST, AND THE RAID CLOCK BUG WAS MINE | v13.11 | Guard build, nothing in the game changed. v13.10 shipped a note claiming a deployed raid driven by the loop does not run the raid clock and that the extraction pull never starts; both halves were wrong and both were my instrument, which I own here. The clock does advance, measured 0.1335 seconds over six frames, being the first frame clamped to 0.05 plus five at 16.7ms, exactly what the loop promises; the pull never started because calling the ship is a 1.6 second hold and I held E for six frames, a tenth of a second. The note is rewritten rather than deleted so the measurement that refutes it sits where the wrong one sat. WHAT WAS ACTUALLY UNGUARDED: every other check in the corpus ends a raid through the harness, which jumps to the payout and never touches the two-stage pull, so the call, the inbound wait, the landing, the cancel on release and the board had run in front of a player hundreds of times and in front of a test zero times, which is the single most important thirty seconds in the game being the least tested code in the file. WHAT THE DRIVE FOUND, each measured on a live raid at seed 4242 with real frames and the real key: hold E 2.5 seconds on an open point and it calls, inbound 24.1 seconds, with the pointer for the way out moving onto that point; wind the inbound down and the ship lands with a 29.7 second window; hold E again and the board fills at real time and the raid ends as an extraction at 1.4028 of 1.4; release mid-board and the hold is gone on the next frame rather than banking; get knocked down mid-board and the standing hold is lost as its comment promises, the hold restarts from the floor, and the board still completes, which is the hauled-aboard rule he chose at v0.90 holding; search a body with X while the ship is down and hold E at the same time and both run, the container opens, and the extraction still fires; put the raid clock at 0.30 with the board at 1.30 and the extraction wins the race by one frame rather than the timer killing a player who did everything right. ONE HYPOTHESIS RAISED AND REFUTED BEFORE IT COST A BUILD: I thought the 1.6 second call might be silent, which would have made a tap read as a dead key, and it is not, the call bar is drawn in three places, on the pad marker, on the extraction row and on the downed overlay, all reading the same value, recorded so nobody builds it twice. THE CHECK has five arms on a live raid: a hold well under 1.6 seconds must not call and the bar must be filling while it is held, the full hold must call and must move the pointer, the landing must open a window, releasing must clear the board, and holding again must end the raid as an extraction. NO FAILING CONTROL EXISTS FOR THIS BUILD AND I DID NOT INVENT ONE: the rule is that a fix is proved by a control that fails on the previous fixture, this build fixes nothing, so all five arms pass on v13.10 as they should, and what was run instead is the negative arm, the same staging with E never pressed, which fires four of the five and is what proves the check has teeth. NOT VERIFIED: the inbound countdown itself, wound down rather than waited out because it is minutes long; the siege that spawns during it; the departure when the window is ignored; and the second and later extraction points on a landing, since every arm runs on the first one |
