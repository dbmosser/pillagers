## v15.16 - AN IMPORTED FRIEND KEEPS THEIR FAVOURITE GUN FOR THE RAID

Mainframe audit finding 1 (MEDIUM). Import a friend's run report at the Mainframe where most runs used the Scav Pistol. The
import line says they favour the Scav Pistol, and the Mainframe hint promises a pillager with their favourite gun. Ascend.
applyGhost takes the first pillager that is not your hire, renames it and hands it the pistol, but mkRaider already pushed that
body's own rolled gun into e.bag as 'gun_'+wk, and that roll is an SMG, Auto Rifle, carbine or scattergun six times in nine. On
the first updateEnts frame raiderUseKit runs for every pillager (raiderKit defaults to 1). kitT starts unset, so the equip test
runs at once, finds a gun of a higher WTIER than the pistol, equips it and drops the pistol into the bag. The friend then
fights the whole raid with the body's gun: its damage, range, sound and tracer. An SMG favourite on a tier-3 body goes the
same way, as does any favourite beaten by a gun the friend loots later.

THE BUILD. The equip test in raiderUseKit now opens with !e.ghost, so the imported friend never swaps guns. Only the equip block
is skipped: it closes before the HEAL section, so the friend still heals from what they carry, and raiderThrow is a separate
function, so they still throw. The body's gun stays in e.bag and is still worth taking off the friend's body. applyGhost is the
only other path that sets a ghost's gun, and blessElite runs at build time before it. Your hire and every other pillager swap
exactly as before. No player text changes and no number moves.

MEASURED. Check 15.16 imports a friend favouring the Scav Pistol and ascends at seed 4242 through __deploy (controls: the kit dial
is on, the friend was placed holding the Scav Pistol, and a plain pillager is in the raid). It stages the friend carrying an
Auto Rifle and a Bandage at 40 percent health, and the plain pillager holding a Scav Pistol and carrying an Auto Rifle, both with
kitT at zero, then runs one updateEnts frame (controls: the plain pillager now holds the Auto Rifle with two guns between hand
and carry, so the swap ran on that frame; the friend is still in the raid and up). The friend must still hold the Scav Pistol,
still carry the Auto Rifle, and have started the Bandage. It restores the profile through the loader and puts the ghost back.
It fails on v15.15.

Not verified: a real imported report walked by eye; a friend who loots a better gun mid-raid, which the same test covers but no
arm drives; the sim, where applyGhost never runs.
