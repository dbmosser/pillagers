## v15.24 - THE RAID CLOCK WARNS IN ITS OWN VOICE

His note: "i got kileld by the timer and i never got any type of sound warning". The raid clock warnings at five, four, three,
two and one minute and at thirty seconds each say a line and play blip('alarm'): a 0.3 s sawtooth at gain 0.06, the same sound
sfx('alarm') plays when a machine or a pillager raises an alarm, and the one a call for extraction plays as the siege is
roused. In a fight a clock warning sounded like one more machine, the line sits on screen 3.2 s and any
other line writes over it, and from thirty seconds to zero nothing else sounds until MERIDIAN IS BURNING THE SITE. The warnings
also fired only when Math.ceil(timeLeft) EQUALLED a mark. The loop caps a frame at 0.05 s, so a live frame cannot skip a whole
second today; the equality test is a latent gap, not the cause of his death.

THE BUILD. blip gains two voices. clockwarn: three rising square sweeps, 520 to 1040 Hz, 0.3 s apart over 0.9 s, lowpassed at
2600 Hz, at gain 0.12 (twice the alarm), centred, through the same OUT bus. clocktick: one 0.1 s square knock at 1320 Hz, gain
0.09. The warning block in the loop moves to tickClockWarn(prev, now) beside outHint, called once per raid frame with G.warnPrev
(the clock at the end of the last frame, written on every call) and G.timeLeft. A mark in 300, 240, 180, 120, 60, 30 fires when
prev > mark >= now; for a whole number mark this is exactly the old rule on every frame of a falling clock (checked by simulating
a 540 s raid at 60 fps through both: same lines on the same frames), and a frame that jumps past a mark still fires it. The
latest mark crossed speaks with the same say lines as before, unchanged, and plays clockwarn in place of the alarm. Each whole
second from 10 to 1 that is crossed plays one clocktick, and crossing 10 also says TEN SECONDS. followed by outHint(1), the same
hint the thirty second line uses. On the first frame of a raid prev is taken as one whole second above now, which keeps the old
rule of warning when a raid starts on a mark. G.lastWarn is gone; nothing else read it. The bot sim never calls it (the sim
steps the clock in its own path, and blip returns in a sim). No number moves: raid length, the burn and ring timings are as they
were. Player text: one new line, TEN SECONDS. and the hint; no existing sentence changes.

MEASURED. Check 15.24 deploys at seed 4242 (control: the raid runs a clock) and wraps say and blip to record, then steps real
frames of 0.016 s through the loop, staging the clock the frame before (G.warnPrev on this build, G.lastWarn on the old one).
Control: a frame at 100 s runs the clock and records no siren, no tick and no thirty second line. Control: a frame from 30.02 to
29.99 says the thirty second line on either build. The fix: that line plays clockwarn, not the alarm; a frame from 31.2 to 28.9
says the thirty second line and plays clockwarn; a frame from 10.4 to 9.6 plays clocktick and says TEN SECONDS. Then, on a
stand-in audio context through the real blip (control: the alarm starts at 0.06), clockwarn builds sound sources and starts at
least 1.5 times the alarm level, and clocktick builds a sound source. A control that cannot run after a failure keeps the failure.
It restores say, blip and the audio holders, ends the raid and cleans the profile. It fails on v15.23.

Not verified: the siren and the tick by ear, against music and gunfire at his volume settings; a whole raid run down to zero by
hand; the minute marks were not stepped by the check (same function, same path as thirty).
