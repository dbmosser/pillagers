## v10.61 - THE UNDERCROFT IS DARKER AND GLOOMIER

His note, 2026-09-03 about 21:50: **"undercroft music needs to be darker
and gloomier, feels too happy"**. This is the second time: v9.87 answered
"music needs more of a dark and low tone, it sounds too friendly" by taking
every tune down an octave, closing the filter from 3200 to 1500 and capping
the room at a hundred beats a minute. He has listened to that and it is
still too happy, so register was only part of it.

### What it was

Three things were still cheerful after v9.87. The tune was led by a SQUARE
wave, which is the brightest timbre in the mix and the sound of a toy,
carried at 0.105 with a soft triangle doubling it at 0.075 underneath. The
shimmer rolled a note every eighth, and a steady sparkle reads as cheerful
whatever notes it plays. And a hundred beats a minute is a walking pace.

### Now

The triangle carries the tune at 0.115 and the square sits under it at
0.035, a third of what it was, so the edge is still in the sound and
nothing is led by it. The room slows from a hundred beats a minute to
seventy-six, a sixteenth every 0.197 seconds. The filter closes from 1500
to 900. The shimmer plays one note a quarter instead of one an eighth, at
0.04 instead of 0.06, and rings a little longer, so it reads as a drip
rather than a sparkle. The bass is held 3.4 seconds instead of 1.9, long
enough to run under the next chord, so the floor of the room hums.

All of it is behind the same dial as before: with the dark setting off, the
bright square, the open filter and the old pace all come back.

### Measured

Nobody here can hear it, so the check reads what the music SCHEDULES, the
rule since v2.48. It plays the whole Undercroft piece dry and requires: a
soft voice in the tune, with the square quieter than it; a sixteenth no
faster than 0.1875 seconds; a corner at or under 1000; no more than 4.5
shimmer notes a bar and none louder than 0.05; and a bass note held at
least 2.5 seconds. Its control turns the dial off and requires the square
and the open filter to come back, so the check is reading the dial rather
than a rewritten table. On v10.60 the square leads at 0.105 against 0.075,
the room runs at 0.15 a sixteenth, the corner is 1500 and the shimmer plays
eight notes a bar, which is the finding.

One check had to change with it. v9.87 picked the tune out of the recording
by its timbre, so a build that changed the timbre read as a build that had
deleted the tune. It picks the tune out by its length now, which is what a
tune is: the long voice that is not the bass. Its register tests are
untouched and still pass.

The what-is-new card a friend reads on the way in names the darker room too,
since that card is the game's introduction and an alpha goes out in three
days.

Not verified: how it sounds, which is his and needs the play build; and the
other four themes, which take the same tempo, filter and voice changes but
