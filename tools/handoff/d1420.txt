## v14.20 - THE PAD LETS GO WHILE THE STALL IS OPEN

Controller audit finding 2 (MEDIUM). While the Peddler's stall is open, the pad poll takes its own branch: the D-pad moves
the row marker, A takes a row, X walks away. That branch returned before the raid branch could release anything the pad
was holding. So with A held when the stall opened, the fire flag, the aim and any held key stayed on until the stall
closed. In updatePlayer the fire and grenade cook code runs before the stall's own early return, so an automatic gun kept
shooting and auto-reloading through the reserve behind the panel, and a frag selected on the bumpers cooked off in his hand.

THE BUILD. The stall branch calls padRelease first. It clears only what the pad itself set (held keys, the fire flag, the
aim) and does not touch the recorded button states, so A, X and the D-pad still read as presses on the stall rows.

MEASURED. Check 14.20 fakes a pad, holds A through two raid frames (control: the fire flag and the aim are on), opens a
stall and polls once with A still held: the fire flag, the pad's firing mark and the aim must all be off. It fails on
v14.19.

Not verified: a real controller holding fire into a stall.
