v12.95: the pause box in the Undercroft owns the keyboard

Finding 10 of the 2026-09-11 audit, and the floor twin of the raid rule at v12.22.

Press ESC on the floor, read the key line the box itself prints, press SPACE: a clank
from nowhere and a roll stored, which the frozen floor spends the instant you close
the box, so the character dodge-rolls out of nothing on resume. TAB opens the backpack
invisibly underneath. H flips the controls panel behind it. ENTER throws away the NEW
IN card you had not read.

The floor's busy test counts modals, the character screen and the terminal. The pause
box is not a modal. The room really is frozen and the box really does say PAUSED; only
the key handler disagreed.

A regression, not an omission: until v12.22 the box focused its note field and the
TEXTAREA bail swallowed floor keys as a side effect. v12.22 removed that focus and
gave the raid a replacement guard. The floor never got one.

A lockout, not another entry in the busy list: the list needs updating every time a
key is added and has been missed once already, and ESC and P must still reach the
toggle that closes the box.

Check 12.95 presses each key twice, paused and unpaused, and requires the unpaused
press to work. Five arms fail on v12.94.
