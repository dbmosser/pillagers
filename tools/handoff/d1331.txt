## v13.31 - THE ASCENT SCREEN SAYS HOW TO TAKE A GUN FROM THE STASH

FOLLOWS FROM HIS RULING. Since v13.29 a new player's welcome pack guns sit in his
stash as items. The ascent screen warns "Going up with no gun of your own. That is
allowed", and the obvious next move is to click the gun in the stash panel beside it.

THAT CLICK PACKS IT AS LOOT. It is "carry one": the gun goes into the backpack.
buildRaid takes the gun in his hands only from the equipped slot and issues a random
starter when that is empty, so he climbed with his pack gun as loot and a loaner in
his hands, and nothing on the screen said otherwise.

THE PATH THAT WORKS ALREADY EXISTED. Every stash cell on that screen is built by
invCell and carries the item menu, and "Equip as your gun" on a stash gun moves it
into the armoury and equips it. Nothing told him it was there.

THE WARNING NOW NAMES IT, ONLY WHEN IT APPLIES: no gun equipped and a usable gun in
the stash. It names the menu choice rather than right-click, because a controller
opens the same menu another way.

NOT DONE, AND HIS CALL: making a plain click on a gun equip it instead of packing it.
That would change what a click does on every stash gun, including a spare he means to
carry up and sell, so it is a design decision rather than a fix.

MEASURED. Check 13.31 draws the ascent screen through renderStage for a player with no
gun equipped, once with a gun in his stash and once without, requires the warning to
name Equip as your gun only in the first case, and fails on v13.30.

Not verified: the in-raid path, where a gun carried in the backpack can still be
equipped from the backpack; and whether he would rather the click itself equip.
