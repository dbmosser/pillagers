## v12.56 - Q MOVES THE BELT AS WELL AS YOUR HAND

The last open line of the 2026-09-06 in-raid audit, and the piece v12.41
deliberately left behind because it is a different act. Verified by reading at
v12.55.

WHAT IT WAS. There are two fields that say which grenade is in your hand, and
they are not the same field. One is the cell the tactical belt highlights. The
other is the throwable selector the throw itself reads.

Q is the key whose whole job is to choose a different grenade, and it wrote
only the second one. So pressing Q moved what you would throw and left the belt
highlighting the cell you were no longer holding: the caption named one
grenade, the highlight sat on its cell, and the trigger threw a different one.

v12.41 closed the other half of the same split, where a USE key on an empty
cell walked that selector on behind your back. This is the deliberate half.
Choosing is fine and is the point of the key. The belt simply has to follow the
choice.

THE BUILD. The highlight moves to the cell that actually holds what you chose,
whether that is the derived cell or one you dragged the grenade onto yourself.
Nothing else changes: Q still cycles, the toast is the same, and the throw is
untouched.

IT COSTS NOTHING ANYWHERE IT CAN BE MEASURED. The bot never presses Q. No dial
moves and nothing in a raid behaves differently; what changes is that the
screen stops disagreeing with itself.

MEASURED. Check 12.56 gives him two kinds of grenade and no third, so the cycle
has exactly one place to go and the answer cannot be a coincidence. It points
the belt at one, presses the real key on the real window, and requires the
highlight, the highlighted cell's identity, the caption and the hidden selector
all to name the same grenade afterwards. Two controls cover the ways a fix
could cheat: a man carrying only one kind must be left exactly where he was,
with neither the highlight nor the selector moving; and when the grenade has
been dragged onto a key of his own, the highlight must land on THAT cell rather
than on any cell at all.

Not verified: no frame is drawn, so the highlight is read as state rather than
as pixels; the gamepad is not driven; and it does not follow the throw itself,
since v12.41 already covers which grenade leaves the hand.
