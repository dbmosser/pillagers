## v12.26 - THE BELT SHOWS WHAT THE TRIGGER WILL THROW

From the 2026-09-06 read-only in-raid audit (P2 WRONG STATE: Q cycles a
hidden selector, so the belt highlights one throwable and the trigger
throws another), verified by reading at v12.20.

WHAT IT WAS. Two selectors, one act. The trigger (startCook, doThrow) reads
G.tsel, the throwable selector; every belt cell reads G.hot, the highlight.
The belt keys point both at the same cell (v11.60), so pressing a grenade
key has always been honest. Q was not: cycleThrow turned G.tsel to the next
throwable in the pouch and said its name, and the highlight stayed where it
was. A player who had the smoke lit, pressed Q, heard "Frag Charge ready",
and pulled the trigger cooked a frag while the belt lit the smoke; a player
who pressed Q and looked at the belt saw nothing change and reasonably
concluded Q was dead. The trigger's own fallback, which calls cycleThrow
when the selected throwable has run out, disagreed with the belt the same
way. Q is taught nowhere, which is why nobody had reported it, and why a
friend mashing keys at alpha will find it.

THE BUILD. cycleThrow moves the highlight to the belt cell holding the
throwable it just made ready. The belt always carries the three throw
cells, one per throwable, so the cell is always there. The highlight is set
directly rather than through setHot, because setHot on a throw cell throws
it. Nothing else changes: the belt keys, the trigger and G work as before,
and Q with nothing in the pouch still says No throwables and moves nothing.

MEASURED. Check 12.26 deploys, puts a smoke and a frag in the pouch, lights
the smoke cell with the selector on the smoke, and presses Q through the
real key path. It requires the selector on the frag, the highlight on the
frag cell, and a cook that produces the grenade the lit cell names. Then it
empties the pouch, presses Q, and requires No throwables with the
highlight unmoved. On the v12.25 fixture the selector turns to the frag and
the belt keeps lighting the smoke.

Not verified: his own eye on the belt; the fallback path inside the
trigger (the selected throwable running out mid-press), which now moves the
highlight too and is not staged here; the gamepad, which has no Q.
