| THE GAME NOW CHECKS THAT THE WORDS HE WROTE STILL APPEAR | v13.02 | The guard for what v13.01 caught by hand, and nothing in this build changes the game. His wording is applied by exact match on a whole rendered sentence, so rewording that sentence anywhere stops his version appearing, silently, with no error, no log line and nothing red; v12.98 did it to the bar blurb and only a hand sweep found it at v13.01, leaving nothing between the next reworded line and the same silent loss. A source search cannot do this job, because most of these lines are assembled at run time out of pieces so the finished sentence exists only once a panel has been drawn: two attempts at a source sweep during the investigation gave 4 false hits and then 40, because the search either found the map own keys or could not find a sentence that does not exist until it is built, and the only honest instrument is to draw the panels and read them. Opening a panel means finding its element, adding the class and calling its own renderer, and the renderers live inside the closure where a check can reach them and nothing else can, so this build adds one table naming the panels that can be drawn without a raid and the renderer for each; the table draws nothing by itself. The rule the check asserts names NO line: if his edit is landing, the ORIGINAL sentence can never be on screen because his replacement is there instead, so any raw key visible on a drawn panel with his replacement absent from that panel is an edit that has stopped working, which holds for every line he has ever edited and every line he edits next without the check knowing any of them, so it cannot go stale. His replacement must be absent too and that is not a detail: several of his edits keep the original and add to it, so the raw text is a prefix of his own replacement and a naive search would flag those every run; the pair is the test. Two controls, because a sweep that sees nothing reports nothing: at least one panel must have drawn text and at least one of his replacements must have been found, or its silence would mean the same on a build that had lost every edit he made. Six panels drawn, the bar, Settings, the shop, the terms, the carry screen and Wirt, with no raw key visible and his wording found; on the v13.01 fixture the check fails outright because the table it walks does not exist. NOT MEASURED: six panels is not every screen, and the Undercroft floor, the raid HUD, the outcome card and the Mainframe are not in the table, so an edit of his on those is still unguarded; the table is the place to add them and each one needs a way to be drawn without a live raid |
