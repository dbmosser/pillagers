## v11.60 - A MERC WHO BOARDED AN EARLIER SHIP WAS NEVER PAID

From the v11.46 read-only audit, P1. The deal with a hired merc (v6.72): if you
BOTH get out you take ten percent of what he carried. v8.46 taught endRaid to
settle that from the ROSTER, because a merc who fled low and boarded an earlier
ship is gone from the world by the time you extract: "the roster remembers the
man and what he carried out", and the boarding code stamps out and val onto
the roster row whose ref is the boarding entity. The gap: only ordinary raiders
were ever pushed to the roster, at spawn and on each wave. The hired merc was
built and pushed to ents alone. So there was never a row to stamp when he
boarded and never a row for endRaid to find, and the card said "was left out
there": no cut, no standing, every time he got out before you.

THE FIX. The merc gets a roster row at spawn, marked merc, so boarding stamps
his haul and endRaid finds him. And drawRaiderBoard skips merc rows, so your
hire is not listed among CURRENT PILLAGERS as a rival; the board is unchanged
for everyone else on it.

MEASURED. Check 11.60 hires a merc (P.merc set to a real identity), deploys,
finds him in the world, and requires his roster row to exist; then plays the
boarding the way the game does (stamps out and a haul of 1,234 on the row,
removes him from the world) and ends the raid as an extract, requiring the card
to say he extracted earlier and the credits to have moved by at least his ten
percent. On the v11.59 fixture there is no row and the check fails on the first
line.

Not verified: his own play; the merc's standing bump on that path, which is
part of the same settle branch and is exercised but not asserted; the shape of
the board with the merc row skipped, which is read and not painted here.
