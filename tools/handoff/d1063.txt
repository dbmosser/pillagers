## v10.63 - ONE CHIRP DID SEVENTY JOBS

His note, 2026-09-03 about 19:40: **"everything should sound unique and
crisp like a triple-A game"**. The fourth family: the sounds that were one
sound.

### What it was

The audit found the pickup chirp, a single sine from 660 to 1180 hertz, at
about seventy call sites: picking up loot, opening a door, applying a heal,
swapping a gun, assigning a hotbar slot, reviving yourself, selling at a
pedestal, and every menu click in the Undercroft. Opening a door and
clicking a shop button were the same sound. And the Depot's buy tiles had
asked for a sound named cache since v9.99, which matched no recipe and
played nothing.

### Now

A door is a latch click and a short low creak of the hinge. A heal is the
wrapper tearing and a soft rising pair of notes. A menu click is one dry
tick with a tiny thock under it; every menu site keeps asking for the old
name and the synth routes it to the tick when there is no raid, so the
forty hub sites needed no edit and a pickup in a raid keeps its chirp. A
sale at the Depot rings up: two bright notes and the drawer.

### Measured

The check installs a recording audio context, starts a raid at seed 4242,
and requires the pickup to schedule its chirp and the door, the heal, the
tick and the sale each to schedule a voice different from the chirp and
from each other; then clears the raid and requires the pickup name to
schedule the tick and not the chirp. On v10.62 the door, heal and tick
names schedule nothing and the sale schedules nothing, which is the
finding; on this build the check returns null.

Not verified: how it sounds, which is his; the gun swap, hotbar assign,
self-revive and pedestal sale, which still share the chirp in a raid and
are the next split; and the in-raid pause menu, whose few clicks now chirp
like a pickup because a raid exists under them.
