## v14.15 - A SECOND HIRE DOES NOT EAT THE FIRST FEE

Undercroft audit finding 1 (HIGH). On the hire bench, with someone already hired, clicking another row charged the new fee
and replaced the hire. The first fee was gone with no question and no message. Hire nobody has asked first since v8.17,
because that fee is not refunded; switching hires skipped the question entirely. A row he could not afford answered the
click with nothing at all.

THE BUILD. With someone hired, a click on another row changes nothing and says "<name> is already hired. Press Hire nobody
first; that fee is not refunded." A row he cannot afford says "Not enough credits to hire <name>." Hiring with nobody hired
is unchanged. No price moves.

MEASURED. Check 14.15 hires once with 1,000,000 credits (control: hired, charged the fee once), clicks a second row (the
credits and the hire must not change and a line must be said), then clicks with 0 credits (a line must be said). It fails
on v14.14.

Not verified: the line read on the bench at 1920x1080 by eye.
