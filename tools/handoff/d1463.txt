## v14.63 - A SECOND COPY OF THE GUN IN HIS HANDS SHOWS IN THE BACKPACK

Backpack audit finding 5 (LOW to MEDIUM). Since v8.78 an item is on the belt or in the backpack, never both: every belt
assignment claims one backpack copy of its item, so that copy is drawn on the belt instead. A belt key naming a gun he is
holding is different: the belt shows the gun in his hands on that key, not a backpack copy. The claim was counted anyway.
With the SMG on key 5 in the Undercroft plan and an SMG in hand, a second SMG looted into the backpack (auto-belting refuses
it, the key is taken) was claimed by that key. It was in neither the grid nor the belt, so it could not be dropped, equipped,
given or sold, and the header said BELT 1. It still banked on extraction.

THE BUILD. A belt key naming a gun in either hand claims no backpack copy. The rule is one helper used by all three counts:
the backpack stacks, the backpack header and the stall's claim count.

MEASURED. Check 14.63 starts a raid, binds a Medkit in the backpack to a key (control: not shown as a stack), then puts a gun
in his hand, binds that gun to key 5 and puts a second copy in the backpack: it must be shown as a backpack stack. It fails
on v14.62.

Not verified: looting a second copy of the gun in hand in a live raid.
