v13.98: a crew pick-up clears your kill credit

A pillager you downed and his crew picked up kept your kill flag, so a later
death you had no part in was credited to you. The crew pick-up now clears it,
as your own revive does.

Check 13.98 stages a crew pick-up of a pillager you downed. Fails on v13.97.
