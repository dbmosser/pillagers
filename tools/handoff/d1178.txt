## v11.78 - HIS NOTE: THE CORNER READOUT WAS TOO SMALL TO BE WORTH HAVING

His note of 2026-09-06: "credits and xp in the corner in the undercroft are so
small that they are useless".

WHY IT WAS SMALL. The v11.52 readout sits in the top right at all times, in a
raid and on the floor. In a raid the CONDITIONS box starts thirty units down
from the top, so the readout was drawn at 15px in a 24px band to stay above it.
The floor has no CONDITIONS box, and the same 15px there is a whisper in the
corner of a big dark room.

THE BUILD. On the Undercroft floor the readout is twice the size, 30px type in
a 46px band, a little further from the corner; in a raid it keeps the compact
size, because that band is what fits. It is the same element with one class,
toggled by the writer that already runs every frame and on every save, and only
written when the answer changes. The stash screen's top row keeps a wider
margin so its CLOSE button stays clear of the bigger readout.

MEASURED. Check 11.78 enters the floor, saves so the writer runs, and requires
the floor class, at least 26px of type and at least 40px of height; then
deploys, draws, saves, and requires the class gone, the height back under 28px,
and the readout's bottom above the CONDITIONS box measured through that box's
own zoom, the same clearance test v11.52 made. On the v11.77 fixture the floor
size never appears and the check names the 15px he called useless.

Not verified: his own eye, at his monitor and his HUD scale; the ascent and
character screens, which sit in hub state and so show the floor size, and were
not measured for collisions; 1440p and 2160p.
