## v12.04 - THE HEAL VERB TELLS THE TRUTH

From the two 2026-09-06 read-only reviews: the first-ten-minutes audit
found the wrong sentence, and the review of the shipped v11.82 found the
wasted Bandage.

THE FINDINGS, three in one function. The heal verb asked the picker first
and the picker skips anything whose ceiling is at or under your health, so
with two Bandages in the bag and health at 85 (their ceiling) it returned
nothing and the verb said No medical supplies, while the belt cell beside
it read Medical x2. The sentence written for exactly that case, "Only
bandages left, and they will not take you past 85", sat twelve lines lower
behind a guard the first line had already returned on: dead code since
v9.62. The same order made a Medkit at full health say No medical supplies
instead of Already at full. And since v11.82 let heals stack, the ceiling
tests still read health alone, so a second Bandage pressed while the first
was still landing was spent even when the first already reached the
ceiling; the surplus was silently discarded. The named belt slot for a heal
had no ceiling test at all.

THE BUILD. The full-health test comes first. The picker counts what is
already inbound when it decides whether an item can raise you, so it
reaches past a useless Bandage to a Medkit and returns nothing when nothing
can help. Nothing usable then means either no medical at all or only
Bandages beneath you, and the verb says which. The named belt slot gets the
item's ceiling, counting inbound, with its own sentence. No dial moves.

MEASURED. Check 12.04 deploys and drives the real verb through four cases:
two Bandages at their ceiling (kept, and the Only bandages sentence); a
Medkit at full health (kept, Already at full); one Bandage with another
already inbound to the ceiling (kept); and, as the control, one Bandage
thirty under the ceiling with nothing inbound (spent). On the v12.03
fixture the first two say No medical supplies and the third Bandage is
spent.

Not verified: the belt slot arm, which needs a key assignment the check
does not stage; the bot, which shares the picker and now also counts
inbound; his own feel of the change, which is fewer refusals of nothing and
one fewer wasted Bandage.
