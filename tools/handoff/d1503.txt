## v15.03 - THE LIMITED TIME OFFER CARD KEEPS TIME WHILE THE PANEL IS OPEN

Wirt audit finding (MEDIUM). renderWirtLot reads the clock once, when it draws: the minutes under the offer, the lot on the card,
and whether this window's lot is bought. Only renderGamble calls it, and renderGamble runs only on E at the counter, a roll and a
buy. The file has no setInterval and no timer touches the panel. Steps: buy the Limited Time Offer, so the card reads "Bought. New
item in 3 minutes". Leave the panel open for 4 minutes. It still says 3 minutes, a new lot is on sale, and there is no Buy button
until he closes and reopens the panel or pays for a roll. Unbought, "New item in 2 minutes" stays at 2 and the old lot stays on the
card after the window turns. The countdown promises an item that never arrives on screen.

THE BUILD. A new closure function, wirtLotTick, redraws the card only while #gamblemodal is open; with the panel closed it cancels
its timer and stops. renderWirtLot arms it with setTimeout for 50 ms past the moment its minute drops, and at one minute that is 50
ms past the window turn, so the next lot and its Buy button draw. It is armed after the empty-counter return and before the bought
branch, so both cards keep time. renderGamble is not called, so Wirt's line does not rotate, and a rebuilt Buy button keeps its
place in the pad order. No player text changes ("New item in" is untouched) and no number moves.

MEASURED. Check 15.03 stubs Date.now to 60.5 seconds before a window turns, calls renderGamble, opens the panel and draws the card
again with setTimeout recorded instead of armed. Control: the card reads 2 minutes, which proves the stubbed clock reached the card.
The draw must arm a redraw due just past the minute turn (500 ms to 1.5 s ahead); the check moves the clock to that moment, runs the
armed redraw, and requires 1 minute and a further redraw armed. A bought card half a second before the turn (control: no Buy
button) must arm a redraw just past the turn that shows the next item and its Buy button, and with the panel closed the next armed
redraw must leave the card as it was and arm nothing more. It restores Date.now, setTimeout, the lot mark and Wirt's line, closes
the panel and cancels the timer. It fails on v15.02: drawing the card arms nothing, so nothing in that build can redraw it.

Not verified: the real timer firing with the panel open in a visible tab (the check runs the armed redraw itself, and a hidden tab
throttles timers); the pad focus staying on a rebuilt Buy button, by eye.
