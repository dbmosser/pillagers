Finding 9 of the 2026-09-11 audit, and the raid twin of the v12.11 fix on the
Undercroft floor. His 2026-09-03 answer: CLOSE and ESC on every menu.

Mid-raid, TAB opens the backpack and ESC is what every other window in this game
teaches you. The pause box rose OVER the panel and froze the raid; a second ESC
closed the box and the panel was still there. Only TAB, I or M shut it, and while
the box was up the v12.22 lockout blocked all three.

It was already inconsistent inside one raid: ESC closes the emote bar and the
Peddler stall, because both are handled above that line.

ESC now has its own branch above the pause toggle. The map is in front of the
backpack, since drawMapOverlay runs after drawBag; the pause box is in front of
both, which is what pauseOpen is doing in the guard. A held drag goes with the bag.

Check 12.89 opens each panel with its own key and requires ESC to shut it with the
box staying down, requires one press to take the map and not the bag when both are
open, and drives the three layers with P. Five arms fail on v12.88.

ESC with nothing open is not readable from a synthetic press: the v11.04 Escape
listener is registered on window after the main one, so a dispatch AT window runs
raidKey first and that listener second, closing the box raidKey just opened. In
real play it runs in the capture phase and does nothing. That is the instrument,
not the build, so the controls use P and the check says so.
