# Progression brainstorm (11-01, his ask, no game changes yet)

His words 11-01: "the progression on the mainframe is not compelling -- could we add a skill tree like arc raiders where the player improves over time -- what else to make it better? thoughts? lets just brainstrorm/plan, no game changes yet"

Today (v37.03): Mainframe = contracts (8, three tiers), a 100-step XP reward ladder (top 1.2M XP, out of reach), racks (passive $ on extract), stats + 12 achievements. Level gates nothing. No skills, no perks, no base upgrades. Shop is thin: consumables, 2 guns, gamble, merc.

Rulings that collide (his newer word wins; ask him):
- 10-12 #26 "NO level-up unlocks"
- 10-12 third 50: DEMO and CAREER gameplay identical
- cosmetics earned, never bought; never touch the pickup stack, loot sounds or loot rows unless he asks

Ideas (ranked by impact):
1. Skill tree at the Mainframe: one point per level; 3 branches + co-op; small steps, a pick-one capstone per branch; free respec at the Mainframe.
2. XP that means something: level shown again, ladder retuned so the top is reachable in a few weeks.
3. Undercroft station upgrades with raid materials (workbench, racks, peddler, stash), visible in the hub.
4. Contract chains from named contacts with a payoff at the end; one elite weekly.
5. Gun mastery: kills with a gun earn its attachments and earned cosmetics.
6. Raid-end screen shows every bar that moved (next point, next station, next contract).

Plan: picture page of the tree first (his mockup rule), then phase 1 (skills + XP), phase 2 (stations), phase 3 (chains, mastery).

"Fortnite has a good system but i can't really commit to seasons in that manner (yet) -- ... we are in season zero until otherwise stated." "the division/borderlands/diablo iv ... Could we do something like that, though maybe not as deep? eg gear (not guns) the player wears that improve play as you level, e.g. rings, hat, cloak, armor, pants." "arc raiders progression is extremely compelling to me also." Other favorites: chrono trigger, escape from duckov, ballionaire, balatro, deep rock galactic survivor, vampire survivors, minecraft dungeons, halo infinite (ok), frostpunk.

Shape: three loops.
- Every raid: pick 1 of 3 upgrades on in-raid level-ups (DRG Survivor, Vampire Survivors); rule-bending trinkets (Balatro, Ballionaire); Forge overclocks with a tradeoff (DRG).
- Every session: rank names + $ to spend (Reach); worn gear, 6 slots, 4 rarities, 1-2 traits, a few sets, enchant points per piece (Diablo-lite, Minecraft Dungeons); 2P combo moves (Chrono Trigger dual techs).
- Every week, season zero: skill tree (ARC); Undercroft stations built from materials (Duckov, ARC); a hub law each week with a tradeoff (Frostpunk); a season pass that never expires (Halo Infinite); a weekly seeded challenge raid with modifiers (Balatro, DRG).
Open: is worn gear lost on death (no insurance ruling 09-30); is $ for earned cosmetics "earned"?
