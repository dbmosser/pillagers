v13.16: a looted armour plate is not armour yet

His note of 2026-09-12: a looted plate should go to the tactical belt or the
backpack and be equipped, which takes time, before it counts on the bar.

The game already half had this rule and contradicted it. The two second armour
wind-up has been the equip path since v3.54, and his own baked text for the plate
says plates do not auto equip. The pickup line made that a lie for every plate
found in a container, and a plate soaked into the bar could never be carried
home, sold, belted, or spent when he chose.

The branch is deleted with no replacement: the chain already ends in a default
that pushes the item to the backpack and offers it to the belt, and the belt
builds its plate cell from the bag. His ruling was every plate, not just looted
ones, and that needed no code: the Peddler and the freebie kit already do this.

Check 13.16 searches a real container on the real key and requires the bar not to
move, the plate to be in the backpack, the belt to offer it, and the wind-up to
be what moves the bar. Two arms fail on v13.15.
