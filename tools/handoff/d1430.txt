## v14.30 - THE LEVEL IS WORKED OUT FROM THE XP WHEN A PROFILE LOADS

Progression audit finding 3 (LOW). The level is stored on the profile as xpLevel and recomputed from XP only when XP is
added (a run, or a stash sale). Nothing recomputed it when a profile loaded. A save whose stored level had fallen behind its
XP therefore loaded at the wrong level:

- a save from v10.02 to v12.66 whose last XP came from a stash sale, when those sales did not update the level,
- a save migrated through the old reputation merge in the loader, which adds XP,
- a save whose broken xp value the loader's number cleanup resets.

Level-gated cosmetics (the Number 23 Jersey at level 4 and the others) showed locked or unlocked against that level, the
Undercroft card printed it, and a worn level cosmetic could be taken off when the wardrobe checked what was unlocked. It put
itself right after the next raid or sale.

THE BUILD. The loader recomputes the level from the XP straight after it cleans the numbers.

MEASURED. Check 14.30 takes the clean profile, sets 1,980 XP and a stored level of 1, loads it through the real loader and
requires level 4, then restores the snapshot through the loader. It fails on v14.29.

Not verified: an old save file from v12 loaded by hand.
