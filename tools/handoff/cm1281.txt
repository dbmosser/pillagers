v12.81: the downed screen stops offering a surrender it will not take

v9.71 gave the surrender a guard it did not have at first: while an extraction
is waiting on the point you are lying in, the space bar cannot end your raid.
The reasoning is right. That same overlay is telling you that extracting while
downed is permitted, so a hand resting on the space bar must not throw away a
full backpack in the one situation where being down ends well.

Nothing ever told the player. The surrender row is drawn on a single test, that
he has spent his self-revive, and asks nothing about where he is lying. So the
screen printed HOLD SPACE TO SURRENDER, he held it, and nothing happened: the
row never lit, the bar never appeared, and no line explained why.

The guard is now one named test with two readers, so the key and the screen can
never disagree again, and the row says what is true instead. The refusal itself
is unchanged.

Check 12.81 runs the control first, the same man downed nowhere near a waiting
extraction, requiring the prompt drawn and the hold started. The finding arm
lays him inside a point with an extraction waiting and requires no hold, no
prompt, and the refusal in its place; fails on v12.80.
