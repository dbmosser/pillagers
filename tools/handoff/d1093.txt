## v10.93 - HIS NOTE: THE UNDERCROFT TEXT IS TOO SEE-THROUGH, AND THE DEPOT IS NOW FASHION

"text in the undercroft should be less transparent/more opaque -- change
'Discount Fashion Depot' to 'Fashion' wherever it occurs", his note of
2026-09-04.

**REPRODUCED, AND THE CAUSE IS NOT THE ONE THE NOTE POINTS AT.** The station
names were dimmed twice. They were painted at 75 percent alpha, which is the
obvious half. They were also painted into the world BEFORE the room's darkness
went over it, a full-screen sheet of rgba(14,12,36,.40) divided by the
brightness dial, with holes punched out where the lamps are. So a name away from
a lamp kept about three quarters of what was left of three quarters.

**THE INSTRUMENT, because "looks faint" is not a number.** Draw the same frame
twice, once with the station names not painted, and difference the two. What is
left inside each name's rectangle is exactly the ink that name contributes to
the FINISHED picture, after every layer that goes over it. Both draws go through
the frame call that draws and advances nothing, so the lamps flicker identically
and the only difference on the canvas is the text.

**MEASURED on v10.92**, all eight stations: an average of 78 out of a possible
152, with the lift at 67 and the cheat box at 64. Wind the brightness dial down
so the darkness sheet more than doubles and that 78 falls to 49, a ratio of
**0.63**, which is the sheet eating the names.

**THE FIX.** The names are how you find a station, which makes them UI and not
scenery, and a dark room is no reason for its signage to be dark. The whole
block is lifted out of the world pass into hubStationNames, which is called
after the light sheet is composited, with the same camera handed to it rather
than worked out a second time, so the names land in exactly the same place at
exactly the same size. The text goes opaque, #cfd8e2 in place of
rgba(160,172,184,.75), on a plate at .86 rather than .5, because a half
transparent plate showed the floor through the letters as well as dimming them.
The bar's asterisks and the season banner came up with it. Two more see-through
lines in the same room: the controls line along the bottom, which was
rgba(140,152,163,.75), and the line under each station prompt, which was
#8a96a1, the dimmest grey in the file.

**MEASURED after: an average of 169**, worst station 151, and a ratio of **1.02**
when the room goes dark, meaning the names no longer care how dark the room is.

**HIS SECOND HALF, THE NAME.** Every player-facing place the station was called
by its old three word name, or "the Depot" for short, which is the same station
under a second name and his one word per thing rule does not allow it: the
station on the Undercroft floor, the title of the racks window, four lines of
the new-in card, and the line in Settings about every rack being open. It is
FASHION in all of them.

**NEW CHECK v10.93** requires an average of at least 130 ink and no single
station below 120, a ratio of at least 0.90 when the room is darkened, the racks
station to be named FASHION and not the old name, no line of the new-in card and
not the racks window title to carry the old name. Three controls: at least four
station names must have been found on the drawn frame, no station may have
painted almost no ink either way, and the brightness dial must actually have
darkened the whole picture by 15 percent or the darkness test proves nothing. It
fails on a v10.92 fixture on all of it: 78 ink, every station named, ratio 0.63.

**MY MISTAKE, and the new check caught it on my own build:** the line I wrote for
the new-in card announced the rename by spelling out the old three word name,
which is the name still occurring, which is the thing he asked to stop. Reworded
to announce the change without reprinting what it replaced.

Not verified: how the new weight reads to him on his own monitor, which is a
judgement and not a measurement; and the two remaining mentions of the old name
are code comments recording why v9.99 was built, which no player sees.
