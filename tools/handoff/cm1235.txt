v12.35: his order, the safe pocket is deleted

He asked how he was supposed to put anything in the pocket during a raid or
take it out, and there was no good answer: it lived on the stash screen only
and its choice froze at the lift, so it could protect something carried up
from home and never the thing just pulled out of a vault. He said delete the
concept.

Out goes the panel and its label, the styles, the two readers, the setter,
the renderer and its drop target, the item-menu verb, the line on the ascent
page, the arming at the drop and the comment block explaining it, the branch
in the death payout, two entries in the player-facing history, one stale
pointer comment, and both fields on the profile, which are cleared from any
save that still carries them. The death card line naming the pocket is
actually fed by the automatic rebate dial, off since v5.57, so it names the
rebate now instead of a feature that no longer exists.

The world container called a safe is a different thing with the same five
letters and is untouched; the check proves that rather than trusting me.
Check 12.35 also requires the functions gone, the panel absent from a
rendered floor, and a real death to open its card and still list its losses;
fails on v12.34.
