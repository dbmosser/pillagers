## v11.80 - HIS NOTE: A HUM ON THE FLOOR AFTER COMPLETING A RUN

His note of 2026-09-06: "strange humming sound in undercroft after completing
a run".

WHAT IT WAS. The raid has an ambient bed: two low sines, a loop of room noise,
a weather voice and a dread voice, all started once and never stopped, driven
every frame by one function that writes their gains. v10.85 found the bed held
its last level after a raid and cut it in endRaid; v10.86 found the same on
pause and ducked it there. Both were right and both missed the frame after.
The raid branch of the loop keeps running while the outcome card is up, the
drive call is in it, and its "alive" argument is "not downed". After a death
he is downed, so the bed is written to 0 and stays there. After an EXTRACTION
he is not, so from the very next frame the bed was written back up to its
floor of 0.16, every frame, for as long as the card was open; and when LOG RUN
AND RETURN dropped the raid there was nothing left to drive it, so it held
that level on the Undercroft floor for as long as the page was open. That is
the hum, and that is why it followed a run he completed and never one he
died in.

THE FIX. One line. Over the card the bed is driven with threat 0, alive false
and dread 0, which is silence, the same thing endRaid asked for. The frames
after the end now agree with the cut instead of undoing it.

MEASURED. This fixture blocks AudioContext, so the gain cannot be read; check
11.80 wraps the drive function and computes, from its own arguments and with
its own formula, the target it would write. It deploys, steps twelve frames
and requires the bed to have been driven above 0 (so silence afterwards means
something), ends the raid by extraction, steps thirty more, and requires every
target written after the end to be 0 and the last one to be 0. On the v11.79
fixture the bed is driven back up thirty times to 0.16, and the check says so.

Not verified: by ear, which this fixture cannot do; the pause duck of v10.86,
which is unchanged; whether the hub has any voice of its own that he might
also be hearing, which nothing in his note suggests.
