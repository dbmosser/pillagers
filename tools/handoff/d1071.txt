## v10.71 - THE SAFE POCKET TELLS THE TRUTH, INCLUDING ABOUT MY OWN v10.70

The safe pocket NAMES one item key. One copy of it comes home if you die
CARRYING it, which is the spec from v6.01 and is right. It is a name for
something in your backpack, not a slot that holds anything of its own, and
the deploy has always been honest about that: it arms only when the named
key is in the kit going up. Every screen that talks about it was wrong in
some way, and one of those ways I shipped yesterday.

### My mistake, first

v10.70 fixed the LOADOUT total to count the copies living on tactical belt
keys, which was right, and in the same line added the safe pocket, which was
wrong. Measured on v10.70:

    medkit + plate                          550c
    medkit + plate + bandage                610c     correct, bandage is 60
    the same three, bandage NAMED as safe   670c     counted twice
    medkit + plate, bandage named as safe   610c     counted, and it stays home

Wrong in both directions from one line. The pocket is taken back out of the
total. What v10.70 got right, the belt copies, stands.

### The older fault, and the worse one

The stash screen showed the pocket as 1/1 whether or not the named item was
going up, because safeKey accepts it sitting in the stash. Measured: a
bandage in the stash, armed, with a backpack of a medkit and a plate, reads
"Safe pocket 1/1" and deploys with P.safeUp null. The one promise that
protects you from losing everything was doing nothing, and the screen said
it was armed. The two cases were pixel for pixel identical.

The label now reads NOT PACKED instead of 1/1 in that case, the cell says so
in hazard red, and its tooltip says what will actually happen. A new
safeUpKey answers the question the player is really asking, which is not "is
something named" but "will anything come home", using the same test the
deploy uses.

### And the ascent check never mentioned it at all

The page calls itself the last stop before the lift where everything can
still be changed, and it did not name the one thing that survives your
death. Its summary line now ends with the safe pocket in one of three
states: named and packed, named but not packed in hazard red, or no safe
pocket at all.

### Measured

The check arms the pocket three ways and reads both screens plus the deploy.
Named but not packed must not read as 1/1, and the deploy must arm nothing.
Named and packed must read as armed and the deploy must arm it. The loadout
total must be identical whether or not a packed item is also named, and must
not move at all for naming one that stays at home, with a control that
packing that item really does move it by its own 60. Then the ascent check
must name the item, must say "not packed" in the second case, and must say
so when there is no pocket. On a v10.70 fixture it fails on the first
assertion and on both totals.

Not verified: what the freebie kit does to the safe pocket, because the
fixture's deploy takes an explicit kit and does not go down the freebie path,
so the summary line for that branch is untouched and unmeasured; and whether
NOT PACKED is the wording he wants on a label that has always been a
fraction.
