v11.06: the 23 jersey reads as a jersey again

His note: "micheal jordan character, can't see his jersey bc its blocked by a
black block (maybe supposed to be backpack straps?)".

Not the backpack, which is drawn behind the torso. Measured on one baller at nine
times raid size, every pixel in a chest box counted by colour: red 3,222, the
jersey's own black side panels 2,384, white of the 23 1,582, ink 1,506, chest rig
882. The panels were 2.4 wide each on a chest 13 wide, thirty-seven percent of
the shirt in near-black, which at sprite size reads as straps. And the chest rig
is a near-black band the full width of the chest, drawn immediately after the
jersey, straight across the lower half of the number.

Panels are trim now, 1.4 wide, and the jersey is drawn after the rig instead of
before it. After: the white of the 23 goes from 1,582 to 2,735, the red from
3,222 to 3,661, and the black to red ratio from 0.74 to 0.54.

New check v11.06 finds the chest from the drawing, using the jersey red as the
marker, and requires 2,000 pale pixels of numeral and a black to red ratio no
worse than 0.62. Controls: a figure with no jersey must produce almost no red and
few pale pixels. Fails on a v11.05 fixture with both findings.

The v10.46 jersey check wanted 8 ink pixels of panel at raid scale, a floor
calibrated on the block he complained about. Rather than widen the art back until
the old number returned, it was measured again: red +58, pale +24, ink +4, and
the floor is now 2, half the live reading.

Not verified: how it reads at play zoom and on the Fashion figure, and whether
the chest rig band belongs on a jersey at all.
