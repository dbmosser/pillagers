Pre-raid redesign

Four ways to rebuild the page you see before you ascend. Each one keeps everything the page does today: cold storage, day or night, weather, the four kits, the pack and the data core. The cash control is gone everywhere, because the stash now pays in the raid.

To pick: press the pick button under the one you like (it copies a line to paste), or just reply with the letter. Click any picture to see it full size.

These are pictures, not the game yet. Once you pick, a helper builds that one into the real page.

What changes

  • Map grows from about 420px wide to 840px with real zone names at 20px and exits and keyed rooms marked
  • Surface and weather merge into one vertical conditions list, with the xp bonus as a small +10% or +20% badge instead of a sentence, and "host sets" marks them as read-only for p2
  • The stats line shrinks to "420 x 340 m · 5 zones · 2 keyed rooms · 3 exits", and the dev-sim line and hectares are gone
  • Kit becomes a 2x2 set of tiles, and the pack becomes grouped rows (heals, throw, armour, guns, data core) with a rarity edge, an icon and one stepper each
  • No cash control, and the going-up sentences become chips (gun, gun 2, and an amber "no armour equipped" warning)
  • Ascend is a big lime plate pinned to the foot of the right column, the focus ring and p1/p2 chips use seat colours so lime only ever means selected, and a key and pad hint strip runs along the bottom

On a pad

Focus opens on ascend, lb and rb jump between the conditions list, the kit tiles and the pack, the d-pad moves straight up and down inside each list (about 24 stops instead of 41), and (a) presses, (b) closes and (x)/(y) pack all and clear.

The catch

The map is a fixed shape, so it cannot grow without squeezing the conditions list, and the pack rows are tight at 40px each, so a stash with many extra items (ammo, keys, tripwire, decoy, stim) will need a scrolling list.

The map shape here is made up. The real build draws the real cold storage map.

What you carry up is the hero. A big gun card with rarity colour and forge tier, an armour plate, a data core slot and item tiles with one -/+ stepper each fill the left side. The four kits sit above it as a switch, cold storage shrinks to a small card with its conditions, and one big lime ascend button sits bottom right, focused by default.

  • The pack becomes a bench: gun 1 and gun 2 cards with rarity colour and forge pips, armour and data core slots, and grouped item tiles (heal, throw, ammo, key) with a single stepper each, replacing the ragged chip rows
  • The four kits become one segmented switch with lb/rb on the ends

On a pad

The pad starts on ascend, lb/rb switch kit, the d-pad moves tile to tile in a fixed grid, left/right on a tile changes its count, and b closes.

The catch

The bench packs a lot onto one screen (7 item tiles, 2 gun cards, 3 conditions groups), so it is the densest of the layouts and only works while the pack stays under about 10 distinct things.

The busiest of the four. It works while the pack holds fewer than about 10 different things.

C

Three big steps

The screen asks one thing at a time (where, then conditions, then kit) across a stepper at the top, and finished steps stay there as lime-check chips so nobody gets lost. The kit choice owns the middle of the card. The "going up with" bar and the big ascend button never leave the bottom, so a pad player can ascend from any step.

What changes

  • Four equal rows become a three-step stepper with done steps as check chips
  • The map shrinks to a read-only recap card with readable zone names, a short stats line (420 x 340 m, 5 zones, 2 keyed rooms, 3 exits) and teal exit rings, and the "test robot" line is gone

On a pad

(lb) and (rb) change step, the d-pad moves through one short list at a time (4 kit tiles, then 10 pack tiles where left and right change the count), (a) picks, (y) ascends from anywhere, and (b) still closes.

The catch

Changing the map or sky after picking a kit costs a step back, and the read-only map recap spends a quarter of the screen on something already decided.

What changes

  • The map, stats line and zone list become the left page, drawn in ink on paper with teal exit rings, and the "test robot" line, "never raided" and hectares are gone
  • Surface and weather merge into one block of stamped boxes with xp badges (+20%, +10%) in place of the hint sentences
  • Kit is a 4-cell row that shows what each kit resolves to
  • Pack is a grouped checklist (heals, throwables, armour, guns, data core) with one stepper per line, and no max buttons or cash chip
  • The three-sentence summary becomes a dark "going up with" tag of chips, where "no armour equipped" is the only amber item
  • Ascend is a 440 px lime button pinned bottom right with a white focus ring, next to a keys and pad hint strip, so lime now means only selected and ascend

On a pad

Focus starts on ascend, up and down walk five stops in reading order (surface, weather, kit, pack, ascend) with left and right moving inside a stop, and the pack stop opens into its nine lines with a/b to change counts, so a standard ascent is one press instead of about 41.

The catch

The cream paper sheet is the brightest thing on screen and the zone threat pips are made-up placeholders until the game has real threat data to show.

The cream paper is not a game colour, and it is the brightest thing on screen. If you pick d, it can be done in the dark game colours instead.

Made 10-19. Ideas drawn from ARC Raiders (one-press free kit, conditions next to the map), Hunt: Showdown (dim what is not in focus, vertical lists), Helldivers (few fixed slots), Fortnite and Delta Force (one big button in a fixed corner), and the opposite of Marathon (5 or 6 menus deep). Every option opens with the pad on ascend, so a standard ascent is one press.
