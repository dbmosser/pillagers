NAME: loadout bench
PITCH: What you carry up is the hero. A big gun card with rarity colour and forge tier, an armour plate, a data core slot and item tiles with one -/+ stepper each fill the left side. The four kits sit above it as a switch, cold storage shrinks to a small card with its conditions, and one big lime ascend button sits bottom right, focused by default.
CHANGES: the pack becomes a bench: gun 1 and gun 2 cards with rarity colour and forge pips, armour and data core slots, and grouped item tiles (heal, throw, ammo, key) with a single stepper each, replacing the ragged chip rows; the four kits become one segmented switch with lb/rb on the ends; the 370px map banner becomes a small card with an "ascending here" tag, a real-palette map with 5 labelled zones, teal exit rings and keyed rooms (blast freezer lit because its key is packed), and stats cut to "420 x 340 m · 5 zones · 2 keyed rooms · 3 exits"; surface and weather merge into one conditions card with xp shown as short badges instead of sentences; the going-up sentence becomes chips, with "no armour equipped" as an amber warning chip; ascend is a 56px lime button pinned bottom right, and lime now only means selected (the pad focus ring uses the p1 seat colour amber, with a p1 host and p2 guest chip in the header)
PAD: the pad starts on ascend, lb/rb switch kit, the d-pad moves tile to tile in a fixed grid, left/right on a tile changes its count, and b closes.
TRADEOFFS: the bench packs a lot onto one screen (7 item tiles, 2 gun cards, 3 conditions groups), so it is the densest of the layouts and only works while the pack stays under about 10 distinct things.
