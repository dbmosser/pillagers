## Per game

**ARC Raiders.**
- [V] You pick a map, then a loadout type: custom or free.
- [V] A conditions window sits with each map. Some conditions show a warning chip and disable the free loadout.
- [V] Custom gives 16-24 backpack slots, 1-3 safe pockets and a weight cap of 40-80. Free is a random kit with 14 slots, no safe pocket and a cap of 35.
- [V] Conditions rotate hourly. The hub, Speranza, is a diegetic market.
- [I] About 3 decisions on screen: map, loadout, squad.
- Takeaway: offer a one-press free kit, and tie conditions to loadout rules with a visible warning chip.

**Zero Sievert.**
- [V] A bunker hub with a shooting range. Six maps unlock progressively.
- [I] Deploying means walking out of the bunker, not pressing a button.
- Sources were thin here.
- Takeaway: a physical hub makes deploy feel earned, but it is slow for a party demo.

**Helldivers 1/2.**
- [V] The flow runs ship hub, map, mission, loadout, hellpod.
- [V] HD1 gives four stratagem slots plus a drop-zone pick.
- [V] Map icons show enemy type and mission type. Missions you lack gear for are flagged.
- [V] The gamepad map opens centered on you, and the D-pad scrolls it.
- [V] The galactic map is criticized as cluttered, and supply lines were cut for that reason.
- Takeaway: four fixed slots are glanceable, and an icon-coded destination reads fast. Keep our map small.

**Escape from Tarkov.**
- [V] You drag gear onto a character model. A separate insurance screen has tick boxes and "Insure All", then Ready. The location map moved to its own button. A queue bar shows position and estimated wait.
- Takeaway: a safe-item step works as one toggle list with an "all" shortcut. The mouse-heavy drag is not pad-friendly.

**Hunt: Showdown.**
- [V] Contracts give a random map, time and boss. Fixed contracts were removed.
- [V] The 1896 UI was rebuilt for console and backlashed on PC. Complaints: backing out resets to the Play tab, horizontal scrolling pushes items off-screen, and it is unclear which loadout is being edited.
- [V] The fix is a vertical loadout list with 10 visible, and custom and preset loadouts side by side.
- [V] On console, unfocused sections dim to guide focus.
- Takeaway: dim whatever is not focused, keep lists vertical, and always show what you are editing.

**Dark and Darker.**
- [V] The Dungeon Map button leads to dungeon and region, then each player clicks Ready (lower-left). The host presses Start, and everyone loads into a tavern lobby.
- [V] Six dungeons, each with normal and high-roller versions. High-roller needs level 20 and a 100 gold entry fee.
- [V] A Squire NPC sets base gear, up to 3 saved sets.
- Takeaway: per-player ready plus a host start suits two windows. The lobby doubles as a gear check.

**Marathon.**
- [V] The flow is shell (6), armory, vault, loadout, skins, title screen, zone, squad or solo, deploy. It takes one to ten minutes.
- [V] A sponsored kit is the free option.
- [V] Complaints: "5-6 menus deep", hold-to-open buttons, tiny pixelated icons, double confirm, and slow cursor navigation on console.
- Takeaway: a cautionary tale. ARC Raiders is cited as the clean contrast.

**Delta Force (Operations).**
- [V] The squad screen is reached with Tab. Prepare sits lower-right and opens location plus difficulty. Gear Up is on the right and Confirm follows.
- [V] A low-supplies reminder can interrupt. A search screen has checkboxes (Squad Fill) above Start, lower-right. Operator is picked after a match is found, and there are 3+ screens in between.
- [V] Gear tickets give a free kit, and loadouts can be imported by code.
- Takeaway: the pinned lower-right primary action and the low-supplies warning are good. The step count is not.

**Gray Zone Warfare.**
- [V] There is no deploy screen. You open a map and call a helicopter to a landing zone.
- Takeaway: a persistent world, little transferable. The default kit lacking spare ammo is a reminder to warn about low supplies.

**CoD DMZ.**
- [V] One insured weapon slot at the start, up to 3 through faction tiers. A two-hour cooldown after losing it, reducible by extracting.
- Takeaway: a single "protected item" pick is a clear, simple risk decision.

**Fortnite.**
- [V] The mode-select screen was removed, so you land in the lobby. A CHANGE button sits above PLAY. Options moved left, a central top bar, level at bottom-right.
- Takeaway: one dominant Play button with a small secondary mode change.

## Patterns

1. Order the choices as map, conditions, loadout, deploy. Conditions sit next to their map, not on another screen.
2. A zero-friction default exists: free kit, sponsored kit, gear ticket or saved set. It is one press.
3. The primary action is one big button, pinned to a fixed corner (Delta Force lower-right, Fortnite Play, Dark and Darker ready lower-left).
4. Slot counts are small and fixed (4 stratagems, 1-3 safe pockets, 1-3 insured slots), so a loadout can be read at a glance.
5. Warning chips attach to the thing they affect (ARC conditions, Delta Force low supplies).
6. A protected or safe item is a separate, explicit pick.
7. Rotating conditions come with a visible schedule or timer.
8. Ready is per player, the host (or either player) starts. Co-op games treat this as standard.
9. Gamepad focus is made obvious by dimming everything unfocused (Hunt console).
10. The hub is a place or a character, not a spreadsheet (ARC Raiders, Helldivers).

## Anti-patterns

1. Depth of 5+ menus before deploy (Marathon, Delta Force).
2. Backing out resets to a home tab (Hunt 1896).
3. Horizontal scroll that pushes content off-screen (Hunt).
4. Icon decoding: tiny pixel icons, legends that eat space, a map that shows everything (Marathon, Hunt Team Details, Helldivers galactic map).
5. Double confirms and forced pop-ups (Marathon).
6. Cursor-style navigation on a pad (Marathon console, Tarkov drag-to-model).
7. Unclear what you are editing (Hunt).

## Inferred sizing for PILLAGERS [I]

- At 4K from about 3 m, body text should be at least 36-40 px and key labels 56 px or more.
- Keep every control inside the central 80% of the screen.
- Hold the visible decisions to 3-4: map, conditions (read-only), loadout or safe item, and ready.
- Give the deploy button about 20% of the width, and make the focused panel obviously brighter than the rest.
- Show both players' ready states as large badges.
