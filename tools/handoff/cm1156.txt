v11.56: a pillager you downed and then saved was still your kill

The downing shot stamps byPlayer, the one door for a kill to be credited to
you. Reviving him with E stood him up, cleared hostile and grudge and made him
friendly, and never cleared byPlayer; a crawler bite writes none. So when a
crawler downed the man you saved and he bled out, the stale flag gave you the
kill, ticked a kill contract, and wrote a permanent -2 grudge on his crew. The
revive now clears byPlayer with the rest; his next death is yours only if you
down him again. Check 11.56 lays a marked pillager down within reach, holds E
through one step, controls that the revive happened and requires the flag
clear; fails on v11.55. From the v11.46 audit, P1.
