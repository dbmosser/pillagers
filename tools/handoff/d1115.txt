## v11.15 - THE ROUTE GRID IS PADDED FOR THE BODY THAT WALKS IT

The third build in a row on machines getting out of buildings, and the one that
closes the v10.84 line "finds a route and does not walk it".

**MEASURED FIRST, on v11.14 at seed 4242.** One crawler in the middle of each
building on COLD STORAGE with open ground outside it, the player on that ground,
the chase held on, a fresh deploy per trial, fifteen seconds each. **7 of 13
buildings deliver the crawler onto the player.** Building 5 has no route.
Buildings 15 and 18 HAVE a route and the crawler never leaves the building.

**TRACED, both of them.** Building 18: the door runs 2695 to 2759 on the east
wall and the route's waypoint sits at 2728,2696, one unit inside the top jamb. A
body of radius 15 steering at it walks into the wall beside the door and stands
there. Building 15: the string pull straightens the route from 3176,1934 to
3240,1976 with a zero width line that grazes the end of a partition at
3216,1960; the body catches on that corner at 3226,1949 and stays.

**WHY.** The route grid is padded 12 for bodies of radius 15, so a route cell
can be a place the body cannot stand; door cells were opened with no padding at
all, so a waypoint could sit on a jamb; and the pull tested a line with no width,
so it straightened across corners the body cannot round. Three places that did
not know how big the walker is.

**THE FIX, and why the world does not move.** The ROUTE grid, map.navD, is now
built padded 15 while map.nav stays at 12 and keeps deciding what the map places
where, so entities and containers are untouched. Door cells must sit 15 clear of
both jambs. The pull tests three lines, the centre and one a body's width either
side, with the edges asked only when the centre passes so the cost lands on
accepted corners. And the follower counts a waypoint done at 10 units, or at 30
only when it can go straight to the next one; at a plain 30 it turned for the
next waypoint while still inside the door and walked into the jamb, which the
building 8 guard caught on my first cut.

**MEASURED AFTER, the same sweep on v11.15: 10 of 13.** Buildings 15 and 18 walk
out and are on the player at 36 and 34 units, building 11 joins them, and every
building that delivered before still does. Building 19 leaves at four seconds
and is still 109 units off at fifteen, as before; building 6 leaves and is far
off, as before; building 5 has no route either way. Route existence is
unchanged: the same three buildings across both maps have no route with either
grid, so nothing was closed by the wider pad.

**NEW CHECK v11.15** drives the crawler out of buildings 15 and 18, each by its
fingerprint, and requires it to leave and be on the player inside 60 units in
fifteen seconds. Controls: with navBody 0 neither crawler may leave its building
in ten seconds, or the old grid does not show the fault; the route grid must open
a different number of door cells with the dial on and off, or the dial builds
nothing different; and the building 8 crawler must still reach the player, which
is the guard that failed on the first cut at 255 units. On a v11.14 fixture the
finding fires for both buildings.

Not verified: the extract rate, for the third build running, and THE COLD MILE by
walking. Route existence was measured on both maps; the walk sweep was COLD
STORAGE only, thirteen buildings, and the mile has fifty nine. The paired run at
the 320-seed standard is several hours here and was not run. navBody 0 restores
the old grid, the old door cells, the zero width pull and the plain 30, all at
once.
