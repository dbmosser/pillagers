v12.29: his order, fewer machines and a lighter extraction by default

"Make the default setting for machines 'few' and the default setting for
'Heat when you call for extraction' to 'light'". Dials, moved because he
moved them; nothing else in the table is touched.

The numbers come off the two Settings rows rather than being invented, so a
fresh profile carries exactly what those rows mean: twelve sentries, twenty
crawlers and a floor of one and a half crawlers a house, and a siege volume
of six tenths. Each row's own default index moves with the numbers, or both
buttons would have drawn in the colour that means changed on a profile
nobody had touched.

The harness pinned the two machine counts and never the per-house rate, so
moving the default would have moved the world every check measures. The rate
is pinned explicitly at the old figure now, and check 12.29 guards it: a
fresh profile must carry the Few and Light numbers, both rows must read
those words and call them their own default, and the pin must bring the old
world back. Fails on v12.28.
