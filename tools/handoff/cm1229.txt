v12.29: a crawler that sees you bites when it reaches you

The audit's top finding, and his standing crawler note. A crawler's wander
clock and its bite cooldown are one field: on patrol it re-rolls three to
seven seconds whenever the clock runs out, and nothing on the way into a
chase reset it, so a crawler that saw you and closed in under a second stood
next to you for the rest of that clock before its first bite. On the three
ways into a chase (sighting, pack call, waking from playing dead) a crawler
now carries no more than 0.3 s of clock. Sentries share the field and are
left alone, since a faster first shot is a balance call. Check 12.29 parks a
crawler with 6.5 s of clock 120 units from a still player and requires the
first bite inside 2 s, with a zero-clock control and a pack-call arm; fails
on v12.28 at about 6.5 s.
