## v15.29 - A PILLAGER GOING DOWN SOUNDS WHERE HE FALLS

Sound audit finding (MEDIUM). A pillager who takes lethal damage goes down (raiderDown, on by default), and the downed branch
in updateEnts draws the DOWNED label out in the world and called blip('hit') with no distance and no pan. blip plays a call
with no distance at full volume and a call with no pan centred, so this was the square 240 to 70 Hz hit at gain 0.10: the
same call damagePlayer makes when you are hit. Machines fight pillagers anywhere on the map (machVsRaider, on by default, and
machHuntRaider has no check on distance from you), so a sentry dropping a pillager 2000 units away played your own hit sound
with nothing on screen and no health lost. It taught you to ignore the one sound that means you are being hit.

THE BUILD. The hit sound now plays at his distance and pan from you, read with earsOf, the helper the enemy voices already
use (it returns d, the distance from the player, and pan, dx/520 clamped to -1..1). blip fades it with distance and it goes
silent past about 880 units, so a man downed beside you is still heard, on his side, and one downed out of earshot is not.
The DOWNED label, the sound itself, the downed rules and every number are unchanged, and it stays inside the existing !G.sim
guard with no seeded draw. It stays blip with earsOf rather than sfx, because sfx would also leave a noise ring, which is a
separate change. One side effect: a pillager you down yourself at range is now quieter by his distance (about half volume at
450 units), where it was always full volume before.

MEASURED. Check 15.29 deploys a raid, takes one pillager, leaves him alone in the entity list and records every hit sound.
Near arm: at no health 60 units west of the player, one entity update. Controls, on either build: he goes down, the player
loses no health, and exactly one hit sound is heard. That sound must carry a distance of about 60 and a pan to the left. Far
arm: 1400 units east, the same controls for going down and health; a hit sound, if any, must be at 882 units or more (silent)
and on the right. On v15.28 both arms fail: the hit sound carries no distance and no pan. A JScript run of the check against
the old and new downed branch with a stand-in raid failed on v15.28 and passed on the build; a sound with no distance, no pan,
a flipped pan or a zero distance all fail; leaving the far sound out entirely passes; a build with no downed sound at all
skips on the recorder control. Everything it changes (blip, the entity list, the pillager, the raid) is put back in finally.
Dry run on scratch copies of v15.27: p1528 and f1528, then p1529 and f1529, all applied, every anchor a whole line matching
once.

Not verified: a live raid by ear, a machine downing a pillager out of sight.
