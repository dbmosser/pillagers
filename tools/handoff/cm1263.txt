v12.63: a contract that pays a gun you already have pays its value instead

A contract whose whole physical payout is a gun paid nothing at all if you had
come by that gun since the card was written, and the receipt said it paid.

The gear is rolled once, when the card is made, and that roll refuses a gun you
already own at that moment. The card then sits on the board for as long as you
like, and the panel says so itself: they do not expire while you are down here.
Between the roll and the claim the gun is easy to come by. Claim it, and the
payout adds the gun only if it is new, then returns the same sentence either
way. The credits arrive, the gear does not exist, and you are told it did.

You are paid what the gun is worth instead, from the armoury's own value, and
the line says exactly that.

I considered re-rolling the gear at claim time to keep the payout physical, and
did not: the card has been sitting on your board naming a specific gun, and
swapping what it pays at the moment you claim it would be a second lie in place
of the first.

Check 12.63 pays through the real payout with the gun already owned, taking the
gun and its value off the game's own tables; the control requires an unowned gun
to still arrive as a gun; fails on v12.62.
