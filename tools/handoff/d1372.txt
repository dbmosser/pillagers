controller, X closed the stall and it opened again on the next frame:

- Frame one: the stall branch of pollPad sees the X press, closes the stall and releases E.
  updatePlayer sees E up and clears the Peddler lock.
- Frame two: the stall is shut, so the ordinary pad path runs. X is still held, so it holds
  E. updatePlayer finds the Peddler in reach, E down and no lock, and opens the stall again.

treats X as up until it is released once.

real frames of pollPad and updatePlayer, and requires the stall shut on every frame. With X
let go, the keyboard E must open the stall on the same path (the control). It fails on v13.71.
