## v10.58 - THE TRAILING NOISE AFTER A GUN IS SHORT

His notes, 2026-09-03 about 21:25 and 21:27: **"trailing noise is way
overdone"** and **"trailing noises need to be much shorter, i can hear them
wayy later"**. Heard on the play build with v10.56's gun voices.

### What it was

v10.56 gave the three big guns a tail, the room answering the crack, at
0.35 of the crack's level and 1.6 times its length, so the Longshot's tail
was 12,160 samples, a quarter of a second, and put the action clicks up to
0.42 seconds after the shot: the pump's second click landed nearly half a
second late. The louder crack also rang the room reverb longer. Together
that is what he could hear "wayy later".

### Now

Nothing a shot schedules starts later than 0.30 seconds after it. The tail
is shorter than the crack, 0.9 of its length with a faster fall, at a tenth
of its level and darker. The big cracks themselves are shorter: the
Longshot from 7,600 samples to 5,600, the Lance 6,400 to 5,000, the Magnum
4,800 to 3,800, the Marksman 5,200 to 4,200, with their bodies falling
faster. The bolt clicks at 0.16 seconds, the pump at 0.16 and 0.26.

### Measured

The check installs a recording context that writes down when each node
starts relative to the call and how long each buffer is, fires all sixteen
guns, and requires no node past 0.30 seconds and no buffer past 6,000
samples; then fires the Longshot and requires the tail buffer no longer
than the crack and the quietest layer at most 0.12 of the loudest. On
v10.57 the Longshot schedules a 12,160 sample tail, the Scattergun and the
Scuttle click at 420 ms, and the tail sits at 0.35 of the crack, which is
the finding; on this build the check returns null.

Not verified: how it sounds, which is his; and the room reverb, which is
untouched and still rings after a loud crack in a vault, by design of
v2.5x, so a big gun indoors still has a room around it.
