One step on the MAIN PC (about a minute): press Windows+R, paste %LOCALAPPDATA%\PillagersBridge\share\night-memory-setup.cmd and press Enter, then click Yes. The VM then borrows spare memory every night from 23:00 and gives it back at 06:00, so all three helpers and the crash robot fit. A "Pillagers Dev (live)" shortcut appears on your desktop. The VM also gets a safety snapshot every Sunday at 05:00 (the newest 2 are kept), and while a game works your graphics card the VM goes light (30% of its processors, nothing new starts) until 10 minutes after you stop.
