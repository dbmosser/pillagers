Do:
1. PIL_TRAITS: about 16 small traits, good and bad, each a short lower case name and one or two {s, v} pairs in the WORN_STATS vocabulary only (spd, stam, reload, steady, quiet, hide, heal, grit, revive, search, tough); a bad trait has a negative v. Sizes about a third to a half of one gear trait (WORN_STATS base), so a trait is felt but gear matters more. Examples: light step (quiet +), loud boots (quiet -), steady hands (steady +), shaky (steady -), quick hands (reload +), slow hands (reload -), tough hide (tough +), glass bones (tough -), long legs (spd +), short wind (stam -), field medic (heal +, revive +), keen eye (search +), and so on. Mostly good: an heir is a reason to play, not a punishment.
2. TRAITS FEED THE ONE READER: raidWornMods adds P.pil.traits onto the gear sum, so wornM stays the only reader (no new stat sites). Every stat keeps its cap on the plus side and gets a floor on the minus side (no lower than minus the stat's base) so every (1-x) and (1+x) multiplier stays sane. With no traits and nothing worn it still returns null, so every older check and the robots are unchanged. G.sim and the demo still carry nothing.
3. NAMES: PIL_NAMES, about 40 short rough lower case names (brakka, vosk, tamsin, gurt, ...); the numeral is how many pillagers of that name the lineage has had, living and in P.hall, plus one, in roman numerals, only from ii up ("brakka", then "brakka ii"). pilName(r) gives the shown name.
4. HEIRS: pilHeirs(p) rolls three heirs on wornRand seeded from the account (a hash of P.acctName and its first born time) and the next generation number, so a reload shows the same three and seed 4242 never moves (never RNGS or FXS). Each heir: a pilMake record with a name from the pool (no two the same in one roll), one or two traits (never a trait and its opposite). P.heirs keeps the roll until one is chosen. pilBirth(p, i) makes heir i the living pillager (gen = next) and clears P.heirs; pilBirth(p) with no heir rolls and takes the first, so 00p-m2a's call keeps working. Generation one (an old save's character) keeps no traits.
5. Show the living pillager's traits as short lines on the account card under its name, good in the green and bad in the red the game already uses (two lines at most; no explaining sentence).
