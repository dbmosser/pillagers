The birth card: pick a layout

After a death, this card opens when you land in the Undercroft. You pick one of three heirs, rename it, change its look, and pick a starter kit. Option a is the one built in the game. Tell the Gameplay Edit Intaker chat a, b or c, and a follow-up job swaps it if you want a different one. These are drawings of the layout, not game pictures.

option a (built)

Three heir cards side by side

All three heirs show at once with their traits, so you compare at a glance. Everything is on one screen and one button goes. Best from the couch at 4K: the names and traits are big and nothing is hidden.
