# Season zero build log (one line per ship: job, version, what he can see)
- m3b cw-m3doll + cw-m3doll2 + cw-m3doll3, v37.43, v37.45 and v37.48: the stash doll. "gear n/9" on the stash row and "worn n of 9" on the pre-raid page open a full window: nine slots beside the figure (name when empty, rarity colour and icon when filled) with the sum of what you wear and a cap mark; every piece in a scrolling list with power, pips and traits. Click, A or enter wears (a ring takes the empty ring, else the weaker ring); a click on a filled slot takes it off; a lit slot filters the list. A tip says better, worse or even against what you wear. Up to five named outfits dress in one tap and show how many pieces are missing. Not in a raid, the demo or the player 2 window. Picture a built; his pick page is progression/doll-pick-1101.html.
- m2d cw-m2hall + cw-m2hallb, v37.34 and v37.36: the hall of the fallen. A "the hall" tab at the Mainframe (R, or click it): records on top (longest life, richest haul, most kills, highest level), then the living pillager first and every ended one newest first with its epithet, generation, how and where it ended, killer, level, raids, best haul and kills. A new wall in the back wall's bay left of the lift shows the newest three fallen with their names; E there opens the same tab. Generation one from an old save shows its old totals while living; once dead its record holds only what was counted since v37.29.

- m0 cw-p0acct, v37.14: nothing new in play; an old save is paid once in $ for ladder rewards it earned and never claimed ("$N paid for unclaimed rewards" on the floor once); account name, living pillager (generation one), hall, lifetime earnings in the save.
- m0 cw-p0gear, v37.16: nothing new in play; stash gear and nine worn slots in the save, pieces rolled on their own dice.
- m0 cw-p0stat, v37.17: nothing new in play (nothing is worn yet); speed, stamina, reload, aim, noise, how far you are seen, bandage time, time on the floor, revive, search and damage taken read what you wear. m0 DONE.
- m1a cw-m1xpoff, v37.19: no XP anywhere: nothing earns or costs XP; the Mainframe has contracts, racks and your stats only (no rewards tab, no claim, no "rewards waiting"); no xp or level on the run cards, corner, save slots or stats; looks a level or the reward board gated are open; an old save is paid once in $ for ladder rewards earned since m0 ("$N paid for unclaimed rewards", added to lifetime earnings). SP and the career board unchanged.
- m1c cw-m1sell, v37.18: every stash sale (sell one, sell all N, sell all salvage) pays one price in $ only, no XP; a "buy back" strip under the sell button lists the sales of the day and a chip buys that sale back at the price paid; locked items never sell; "net worth" card on your stats (cash plus stash at sale prices). Today's sales bank into lifetime earnings when the day closes.
- m1d cw-m1mkt + cw-m1mkt2 + cw-m1mkt3, v37.20, v37.22 and v37.24: sale prices move with a market: each item drifts 80 to 120 percent of its value day to day (same all day), what you sold a lot of this week pays less (10 sold in a day takes it to 70 percent) and creeps back up over the week; three wanted items a day pay 50 percent more, shown as "wanted +50%" with icon, name and price in the stash header beside the held count, and as a wanted line on the Peddler panel; the Peddler pays market prices too (still 55 percent of them); buy back returns exactly what the sale paid; lifetime earnings never raise a price.
- m1e cw-m1ranks + cw-m1rankui, v37.21 and v37.23: 30 ranks (scrounger i to warlord v) from lifetime earnings, first rank up at $20,000, warlord v at $1,500,000; a reward at every rank ($ or a kit of existing items), paid once with "rank up: scrounger ii  bandages and ammo" on the floor; the Mainframe shows rank, lifetime earnings, the next rank, its reward and a bar on every tab; your stats has a lifetime earnings card with the rank; an old save starts at the rank its lifetime earnings reach, nothing paid twice; a new account starts with $1,000. m1 DONE: ask him for a playtest.
- m1b cw-m1nocash + cw-m1bonusgap, v37.25 and v37.26: no cash in raids: an extraction pays "extraction bonus +$N" (10 percent of the secured haul) onto the account and into lifetime earnings, a death or walk out pays none; a Peddler sale is banked at once (pickup row says banked, card "stall sales $N banked") so a death keeps it; the survivor and raid goals pay salvage of the same value instead of $; contracts pay salvage worth their old reward plus their gear (board says "worth $N" and shows the items), a gun you already own comes as a spare to the stash; racks and arrays pay rack parts worth what they paid ("mainframe parts: ..." on the card); hire cuts count in lifetime earnings.
- m2b cw-p2heirs, v37.30: your stats opens with a card "your Nth pillager" with its name and numeral ("brakka ii") and its traits in green and red; the next pillager after a death is one of three heirs (the first until m2c draws the choice) with one or two small traits from 17 (10 good, 7 bad, each a third to a half of one gear trait: light step, loud boots, steady hands, shaky, heavy feet, field medic...) and a rough name from 40; traits work through the same reader as gear. Generation one (his current character) has no traits.
- m2a cw-m2death, v37.29: a death, or a quit after doing anything, ends the pillager: it goes to the hall (how, map, zone, killer, $ lost, up to 7 lost items, worn slots, look) and the next generation is born at once; the floor says "<name> is gone  pillager <n>" once on the next arrival. Every raid adds to the living pillager's raids, extracts, kills and best haul. Cash, stash, guns kept, lifetime earnings and rank do not move. The free walk-back, sim raids, the demo and the player 2 window change nothing. Fixture: __cleanProfile now starts each check at generation 1 with an empty hall.
- m2c cw-m2birth + cw-m2birth2 + cw-m2birth3, v37.33, v37.35 and v37.37: a death no longer births by itself. The first arrival in the Undercroft opens the birth card: three heirs with traits, a typed name, the figure with edit look (random once per birth, edits kept), starter kit nothing free, basic $300 (compact smg, 3 bandages), privileged $1,500 (burst carbine, medkit, armor plate). "ascend as <name>" births what is lit and opens the map page; close, esc and B birth what is lit and stay on the floor. Kits go up the freebie's way: handed out when the raid starts, kept on an extract, gone on a death. Nothing ascends with nobody living. Old saves, the demo and the player 2 window never see the card.
- m2e cw-m2retire + cw-m2retire2, v37.38 and v37.39: the hall tab has a rename box and a red "retire <name>  +$N" button: hold one second (keys and pad get a confirm card) and it pays $250 a level into cash and lifetime earnings, moves worn gear to the stash, puts the pillager in the hall as retired and opens the birth card; a pillager needs one raid first. The name on the title is the account name: menus, save slots and the lobby show it; a partner sees the pillager's name with numeral over the head; the run card names the pillager. Restore codes carry the lineage, old codes still restore. m2 DONE: ask him for a playtest.
- m3e cw-m3bench, v37.42: the trader has a "gear" tab beside craft: every stash piece (not worn ones) with name in rarity colour, power, pips and traits. Each trait has a reroll button: a quarter of the sale value, up by half each reroll of that piece, "not enough $" when short. Hold to sell pays the sale price at once and counts in lifetime earnings. Hold to salvage gives 2, 4, 8 or 14 of scrap metal, copper wire, cracked cell and circuit board, about 80 percent of the sale. Keys and pad get a confirm card. Net worth counts gear in the stash and worn. Never in the demo. Nothing drops gear until m3a ships, so the tab reads "no gear" on his save for now.
- m3f cw-m3shop, v37.60: the shop counter has 4 gear pieces a day after its stock (scrap, sound or rare, the same all day, about twice the sale price) with power, traits and better, worse or even before buying; a bought piece goes to the gear stash. Wirt can roll a worn piece of any rarity (12 in 112 rolls, about 1 in 9), same $2,500, his other prizes in the same proportions, shown in his log in its rarity colour. About one contract in three names a piece ("gear: rare gloves" on the board, an icon on the rewards strip), more on hard and elite cards, rolled at the claim; cards already on his board are unchanged. Sacker i, sacker iv, reaver ii, reaver v, marauder iii, warlord i and warlord iv pay a gear crate ("rank up: sacker i  gear: sound armor"); ranks he already reached never pay again. The privileged kit (still $1,500) reads "burst carbine, medkit, sound armor": the armor is worn at birth, or goes to the gear stash when armor is already worn. Never in the demo.
- m3a cw-m3drops, v37.44: about one map box in three holds a worn piece (34 percent, from his 19 real raids; wrecks, pillagers and keycard rooms come with m3d). While he holds search a small tip beside the bar names it in rarity colour with each trait and better, worse or even; the same tip shows in the backpack. It comes out as a pickup row in its rarity colour with a kind icon and one trait line. An extract puts it in the gear stash, a death loses it. Armor pieces read scav vest, plated vest, breacher plate. A box with no piece loots as it did.
- m3c cw-m3death, v37.61 (+ cw-m3pipgap v37.64): a death, or a quit after doing anything, takes everything the pillager wore; the gear stash, cash, lifetime earnings and rank stay. The KIA card shows each worn piece as a tile in its rarity colour, says "n worn pieces lost" and its $ includes them (the hall value lost too). A piece carried in the backpack is never saved by a safe pocket. Each extract gives every worn piece a pip, up to 3, with a line per piece on the extract card ("rare boots pip 2", at most 4 then +n); nothing ever wears gear down. The free walk-back, the demo and the player 2 window are unchanged.
- m3d cw-m3dsrc, v37.62: machine wrecks can hold a worn piece, more often and rarer the bigger the machine (crawler and snitch 12 percent, sentry 25, listener 40, bulwark and howler 55, pillbox 65, warden 85; the overseer and the juggernaut always, often two). A dead pillager's body holds what he wore: 0 to 2 pieces, more on a medium or heavy rig or an elite (he looks and fights the same). Every box in a keycard room holds a piece of sound or better. All rates sit in one table, WORN_SRC; about 4.4 pieces a raid in all, from his 26 real raids. No new wreck or body appears, and plain boxes roll what they did.
