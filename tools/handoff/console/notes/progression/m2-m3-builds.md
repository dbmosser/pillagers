## m2 the lineage
- m2a (cw-p2end) death ends the pillager. endRaid 'dead' and 'abandon' (quitting early counts as a death, his answer 31) call pilEnd(P, {outcome, map, district, by, raids, worn snapshot}); every raid updates P.pil (raids, ext, kills, best haul). A two-player revive keeps him alive (answer 28). Demo untouched. Until m2c lands, a new pillager is born at once with pilBirth and an auto name (no screen yet). Death keeps stash cash, lifetime earnings, the account (his round 5).
- m2b (cw-p2heirs) three heirs. PIL_TRAITS, about 16 small traits good and bad (light step quieter, nearsighted shorter sight in fog, heavy hands slower reload and harder melee, steady less sway, and so on), each a small signed stat in the WORN_STATS vocabulary so raidWornMods adds them on the same reader (a bad trait is a negative value; caps still hold). Each heir rolls one or two, no $ bonus for a worse draw (answer 3), rolled on wornRand with a seed from the account and the generation. Names: a pool of short rough names; the numeral is how many of that name the lineage had ("brakka iv"); the generation count is P.pilGen ("your 14th pillager").
- m2c (cw-p2birth) the birth moment in the Undercroft: name card, traits, rename, the body editor (random to start, edit what you like: today's character editor), starter kit choice: nothing (free), basic $300 (a plain gun and bandages), privileged $1,500 (a better gun, a sound armor piece once m3 exists, a medkit) (open question 2 default). Quick to skip through. Mockups first (his rule 53: a brand new screen needs 2 or 3 pictures he picks from): make the picture page, then build his pick; if he has not picked within a day, build the first.
- m2d (cw-p2hall) the hall: a Mainframe tab with every pillager (how and where it died, killed by, level, epithet, raids lived, best haul, kills) and lineage records on top (longest life, richest haul, most kills, highest level); a wall in the Undercroft with a portrait of each in what it last wore (today's body look until m6). Epithets from play (the quiet, the greedy, the lucky...) set at the end from its record.
- m2e (cw-p2retire) retire a living pillager: worn gear to the stash, $250 a level through lifeEarnAdd, then the heirs; rename at the Mainframe (answer 6 note); the account name in menus and the two-player lobby (answer 7); the restore code carries the lineage (P.pil, P.hall, P.pilGen, P.acctName) so a restore does not wear another account's lineage.

## m3 worn gear
