Stash gear: pick a layout

The stash gets a place to dress your pillager before you ascend: nine slots, your whole gear list, a better or worse tip, and saved outfits. Option a is the one built in the game. Tell the Gameplay Edit Intaker chat a, b or c, and a follow-up job swaps it if you want a different one. These are drawings of the layout, not game pictures.

option a (built)

A gear tab that opens its own full window

A gear button joins armory and trophies on the stash row and opens a full card. Doll on the left with the slots either side of the figure, gear list in the middle, the tip and outfits on the right. Nothing in the stash gets squeezed, and every line stays big enough to read from the couch at 4K. The cost: you cannot see the backpack while you dress.

The doll takes the stash grid's place

A gear tab on the stash category row swaps the stash grid for the doll and the gear list, and the backpack stays on the right, so you dress and pack on one screen. At 4K the backpack column is already squeezed (the reason for your backpack and keys tabs of 10-28), so the doll and the list share what is left and read smaller.

Slots in a ring around a big figure

A full window with a large figure in the middle and the nine slots standing around it like a character sheet. It looks the most like an RPG and gives the figure pride of place, ready for when gear changes the look later. The list and outfits get squeezed to the two edges, so long lists mean more scrolling.
