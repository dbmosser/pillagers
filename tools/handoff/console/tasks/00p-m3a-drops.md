Design (decided, build this):
1. A PIECE IN A RAID IS A KEY THAT SPELLS ITSELF OUT: 'wg_<kind>_<r>_<pw>_<seed>'. wornItemEnsure(key) makes the piece with wornMake from the key's own four values and registers ITEMS[key] with Object.defineProperty(ITEMS,key,{value:...,enumerable:false,configurable:true}) (name wornName(pc), r mapped scrap common, sound uncommon, rare rare, relic elite, val wornPrice(pc), use 'wornpiece', pc the piece), so a key means the same piece on any PC and no loop over ITEMS (the shop, Wirt's pool, the market, the wanted list, checks over every item) ever sees one. Call it at every door a key comes through: where a piece is rolled, the top of the grantLoot loop, and bankItem. autoBelt must leave a wornpiece alone.
2. THE SIDE ROLL: at the moment a box is first opened (openContainer, !G.sim, not the demo, not ct.dropped, once per box: ct.gearDone), wornRand seeded from G.seed and the box's fixed position decides whether it holds a piece, its kind (each of the 8 kinds alike), rarity (scrap most, relic rare: start about 60, 28, 10, 2 percent) and seed. It never reads RNGS or FXS and never moves them, so the fingerprint holds. Push the key with setLoot's sort so it comes out in value order with the rest. Do not change ct.best for the box's look. Tune the chance per box so a normal raid averages about 4 or 5 pieces once 00p-m3d adds its sources too (boxes about 3 or 4): take containers opened per raid from real numbers (his run logs in exports/ and P.log, T.containers), never a guess, and write the numbers in DESIGN.md. WORN_DROPS_ON is a plain var, default true.
3. POWER, wornDropPw(kind): the best pw you own of that kind, worn or in P.gear. A roll lands from 2 under that best to 1 over; about one drop in ten lands 3 to 5 over (the step above). With none of that kind, the same from your best of any kind minus 5. With none at all, about 15 to 20. Capped at WORN_PW_MAX. After a death the stash keeps you from starting at zero.
4. BEFORE YOU TAKE IT: while he holds search on a box whose loot still holds a piece, a small tip beside the search bar shows the next piece: name in rarity colour, each trait ("reload +8%") and wornTip's better, worse or even against what he wears. Letting go of E leaves it in the box, the choice the staged pull already gives. Same tip when hovering the piece in the backpack. The tip is new; the search bar and its feel are not changed.
5. THE NEW ROW: when a piece is granted its pickup row uses the existing row layout, its rarity colour, its name and icon, and one short trait line. Simple icons per kind in the existing icon route. The row says no more than the others do. Use the existing pickup sound.
6. THE BAG AND HOME: a piece sits in G.bag by its key, so the backpack, the drop pile, the death ledger and the extract card all see it through ITEMS. On an extract bankItem turns it into wornAdd(P, piece). A pile he dropped gives back the same piece. A piece in the bag at a death is lost with the rest; it never takes a safe pocket (00p-m3c proves it). It counts in the haul at its value like any loot.
7. ARMOR NAMES: answer 73: rigs left the game at v5.83 and every operator wears the same armour (myRig), so nothing is converted and no speed or noise cost comes back. Armor pieces carry the old names in wornName: scrap scav vest, sound plated vest, rare and relic breacher plate.
8. FIXTURE (fixq, under the lock): __cleanProfile clears P.worn and P.gear, and __runPrep sets WORN_DROPS_ON=false so the older checks that count box contents keep their meaning; a check that wants drops turns it on and restores it.
