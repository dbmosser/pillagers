v11.48: right-click Equip as your gun on a stash gun lost the gun

itemMenuRows builds its rows for key, but the equip-as-gun row spliced the gun
out of P.stash and then called clearKeysFor(k); k is undeclared in that
function and the IIFE is use strict, so it threw ReferenceError after the
splice and the push to P.weapons, the equip and the save never ran. The gun was
in neither the stash nor the armoury. One token: clearKeysFor(key). Check 11.48
drives the real rows through a new hook: a gun item the profile does not own
goes in the stash, the equip row runs, and the gun must reach the armoury and
be equipped; fails on v11.47 with "in neither place: lost". Second of the two
P0 data-loss lines from the v11.46 audit.
