## v13.19 - A REFUSED ROLL SAYS SO

HIS REPORT, 2026-09-12: "roll w space bar not working at all." He withdrew it ten
minutes later as a false alarm. It was not a false alarm. It was this.

A ROLL COSTS 45 OF 100 STAMINA. Measured on a live raid: 100, then 55 after one
roll, then 16 after the second. The third is refused by the first line of
tryRoll, which simply returns. No sound, no line, no bar flash, nothing at all.
The key is dead in the hand and the game does not say why, so a man who has just
rolled twice concludes the key is broken. He did, and he reported it.

THIS IS HIS OWN RECORDED PATTERN. The crafting note was the same shape: a working
hold behind a button that answered every wrong gesture with silence. The rule
that came out of that one was "fix the words". There were no words here.

ONLY STAMINA GETS A LINE, and that is the point. Four things refuse a roll and
three of them are states he can already see: he knows he is down, he can see the
roll happening, and the cooldown is a tenth of a second after a roll he just
watched. Stamina is the one with no visible reason, and it is the one that costs
45, so the third roll in a row is always the one that dies quietly.

NO DIAL MOVES. The cost stays 45, the cooldown stays 0.85. The roll is exactly as
expensive as it was. The only change is that being refused is audible.

RATE LIMITED TO ONCE EVERY 1.5 SECONDS, because Space is a key people hold. A
refusal that repeated every frame would bury every other line the raid is trying
to say.

MEASURED, AND THE SECOND ARM IS THE ONE WITH TEETH. A refused roll must speak. A
roll that CAN happen must stay silent, or the line is wallpaper rather than an
explanation. And holding the key must not repeat it. The check wraps say() for
its duration and puts it back, because the fixture overrides say and guessing at
it has lied before.

Not verified: whether the wording is the right wording, which is his call; and
whether the stamina bar itself needs to flash, which would be the visual half of
the same answer and is a change to the HUD rather than to the roll.
