## v12.29 - A CRAWLER THAT SEES YOU BITES WHEN IT REACHES YOU

From the 2026-09-07 read-only audit (P1, confirmed by three refuters), and
his standing crawler note ("crawler attacks and pathfinding were still kinda
messed up", "standing still and crawler didn't hurt me even though he was
close"). Verified by reading at v12.28.

WHAT IT WAS. e.cd is two clocks on one field. On patrol the idle branch
re-rolls the wander target whenever cd reaches zero and writes cd=rnd(3,7),
so a patrolling crawler carries a residual clock of up to seven seconds at
every moment (about 2.6 s on average, the way a bus you did not time
arrives). The sighting transition into chase sets the state, the alert and
the target and never touches cd for a machine; the only cd write there is
the raider's beat to react. packCall, which sends the neighbourhood into the
rush, does not touch it either, nor does the possum wake. In chase the bite
requires cd<=0, and cd only drains by dt. So a crawler that had just rolled
a six-second wander, saw him from a hundred units and closed in under a
second stood inside bite reach for five seconds doing nothing before its
first bite. That is his note: a crawler next to a man standing still, not
hurting him.

THE BUILD. Three transitions into chase, one clamp each, crawlers only: on
the sighting, on the pack call and on the possum wake, a crawler carries no
more than 0.3 s of clock into the chase, so its first bite waits no longer
than the clock a bite itself sets (0.7 s between bites). The sentry shares
the field and the same residual delays its first shot; a faster first shot
is a balance call he has not made (NO BALANCING BEFORE ALPHA), so it is
named here and left alone.

MEASURED. Check 12.29 deploys, keeps one crawler, parks it on patrol 120
units from a still, visible player with a clear sight line, its wander
target away from him and 6.5 s on its clock, and steps the entities at 60
frames a second; it requires the first bite inside 2 s. As the control the
same crawler with its clock at zero must also bite inside 2 s, so the room
can tell a stalled crawler from a slow one. Then the crawler is parked again
with 6.5 s and sent in by packCall from a fake caller, and must bite inside
3 s. On the v12.28 fixture the first arm bites at about 6.5 s and the third
the same.

Not verified: the sentry's first shot, deliberately unchanged; a crawler
that enters chase from investigate by the same sighting line (the clamp
covers it, the check does not drive it); his own runs, which the bot
cannot stand in for; whether 0.3 s reads as fair at the moment of contact.
