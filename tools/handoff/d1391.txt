## v13.91 - WHAT YOU BUY AT THE STALL IS NOT LOOT YOU EARNED

The contracts, notoriety and waves audit of 2026-09-14, finding 4. Since v10.27 the game stamps
what the lift carried in (G.carriedIn, and since v13.55 which items, G.carriedKit). Every measure
of what the run earned subtracts it:

- hazard pay (v13.53),
- the haul card (v12.65),
- the item card (v13.55),
- the XP haul term, the best haul and the run earnings on the log.

pedBuy took banked Credits and put the item straight into the backpack with no stamp. So a gun
bought from the stall completed a haul card, paid hazard pay on its value under the Terms, and an
Optics Lens or Servo Actuator counted toward an item card, all while he kept the goods. Stash money
turned into contract payouts.

THE BUILD. A purchase joins the stamp: its value is added to G.carriedIn and its key to
G.carriedKit, exactly as if the lift had carried it up. Every measure above then leaves it out
without being touched. Ammunition goes to the reserve and carries no haul value, so it is not
stamped.

MEASURED. Check 13.91 buys a rifle from the stall with banked Credits into an empty backpack, runs
contractExtract with the backpack's value, and requires a haul card worth the rifle left
incomplete. The same rifle found in the raid must complete it (the control). It fails on v13.90.

Not verified: hazard pay and the item card by drive, which read the same stamp; and a bought item
sold back to the stall, which stays on the stamp.
