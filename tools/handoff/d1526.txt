## v15.26 - A BANDAGE ON A RUNNING MEDKIT STOPS AT 85

His ruling of 2026-09-16, verbatim: "bandages should only let players heal to 85 HP -- only medkit gets player back to 100".

v15.23 closed the biggest way past the Bandage ceiling (out-of-combat regen) and left this one for its own build. A heal runs
over seconds, and since v11.82 a second item put on while one is still running adds its health to the queue, takes the faster
rate and the higher ceiling. So a Bandage put on while a Medkit was still healing shared the Medkit's ceiling of 100: from 30,
a Medkit and then a Bandage with about 6 of the Medkit left ended near 96, where the Medkit alone stops near 76 and a Bandage
after it can only add up to 85 plus those 6 (about 91).

THE BUILD. Stacking, the faster rate, the higher ceiling, every heal amount and rate, and the 85 and 100 ceilings all stay. The
queue now also remembers the lowest ceiling in it (a Bandage's 85) and how much of it may go past that ceiling (the health a
Medkit brought). Past 85 only that Medkit health goes on; once it is spent, whatever is left of the queue cannot raise him and
is dropped. In practice: a Bandage over a running Medkit ends at 85 plus what the Medkit still had to give, capped at 100 and at
what was actually queued, which is the same place a Bandage put on first and a Medkit after it would end. A Medkit over a
Bandage still reaches 100. Going down clears the new fields with the rest of the queue.

The four places that ask how high a running heal will go before refusing an item (the heal verb's "Already healing", the item
picker, and the two belt-key refusals) now read one helper, healReach, which gives the same answer as the drain. Without it a
Medkit could be refused as "Already healing" by a queue that would really stop at 91. The refusal sentences are unchanged.

With the heal ceilings dial off every ceiling is full health, so nothing is tracked and healing stacks exactly as before.

MEASURED. Check 15.26 deploys a raid and drives applyHeal and tickHeal directly: three Bandages from 50 end at 85 (control); a
Medkit from 60 ends at 100; a Medkit from 30 drained to about 6 left, then a Bandage, must end no higher than 85 plus what was
left (it ends near 96 on v15.25, so the check fails there); a Bandage from 70, part spent, then a Medkit still reaches 100; and
with the ceilings off the Medkit then Bandage case stacks past 85 plus the remainder again. Dry run on scratch copies: p1514 to
p1523 and their f scripts, then p1526 and f1526 with the version anchors pointed at 15.23 (1524 and 1525 were not drafted yet),
all applied, every anchor a whole line matching once; a JScript run of the old and new heal code gave 96.00 on v15.25 and 90.91 (limit 90.91) on the
build, and over 32,000 random stacked heals behind the game's refusals the helper matched where the drain ended to within 0.001.

Not verified: a real raid by hand, a Medkit then a Bandage from low health. Also left alone: the green incoming-heal block on the
health bar still draws health plus the whole queue, and has never stopped at a ceiling, Bandage alone included.
