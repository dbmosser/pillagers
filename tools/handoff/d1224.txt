## v12.24 - THE CLOCK SWITCHED OFF IS NOT A CLOCK AT ZERO

From the 2026-09-06 read-only in-raid audit (P2 TEXT vs CODE: with the raid
clock set to OFF the boarding window is 3 seconds, not 30), verified by
reading at v12.19.

WHAT IT WAS. v9.35 gave the raid clock an OFF position on his order that
nothing should be meant to end the raid: raidSec 0, and the timer, the death
on the timer and the ring closures all stopped reading the clock. Two
readers were missed. With the clock off G.timeLeft starts at 0 and never
moves, and both of them took 0 for one second left. The boarding window is
min(30, max(3, timeLeft - 1)), so the ship touched down and left after
three seconds instead of thirty, every time, for anyone who had switched
the clock off. And the Pulled line, which warns "The raid clock runs out
first." whenever timeLeft is under the inbound wait, said it on every call,
about a clock that did not exist. The HUD already knew the case: v9.35
made the readout count up when there is no clock.

THE BUILD. Both readers ask whether there is a clock before reading it.
With the clock off the window is the full thirty seconds and the call line
says "then hold E to extract." With the clock on nothing changes: the
window is still capped by the seconds left, so the ship never promises more
than the raid has.

MEASURED. Check 12.24 deploys, switches the clock off the way Settings does
(raidSec 0, timeLeft 0), calls the ship by holding E at the ring edge and
reads the call line, then lands the ship one tick past the inbound wait and
reads the window; it requires the line without the warning and a window of
30. Then with the clock on and 12 seconds left it lands the ship again and
requires the window of 11 the clock rule gives. On the v12.23 fixture the
call says the clock runs out first and the window is 3.

Not verified: his own play with the clock off; the bot, which reads the
clock to decide when to leave and is pinned to a running clock in every
sim; any further reader of timeLeft I did not find (the grep found the
timer, the death, the closures, the HUD, the bot and these two).
