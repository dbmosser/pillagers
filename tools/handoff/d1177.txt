## v11.77 - HIS ORDER: FRAG CHARGES REACH FURTHER AND HIT HARDER

His order of 2026-09-06: "grenades should have larger explosion radius and do
more damage". He set the rule that no dial moves before alpha, on 2026-09-04,
and he has overruled it for these dials himself. Nothing else moves.

THE NUMBERS. Radius 150 to 190 units, so the area of the blast is about six
tenths larger. To a machine or a pillager, 115 at the centre falling to 25 at
the edge; it was 85 falling to 15. To him, 80 at the centre falling to 18; it
was 60 falling to 12. The centre figure for him is 98 on purpose: a man at full
health standing on his own charge lives by two points, because he died to his
own charge twice last night and the fuse is still 1.1 seconds, which is his
ruling to make and has not been made. The wall damage formula is untouched, but
it is applied inside the radius, so a wider radius reaches more wall; two
charges still open a building, one now scars a little more of it.

THE SAVED DIAL. The radius is a config dial that rides in every save, so
changing the default alone would reach nobody who has ever saved, which is
everybody. The config version goes 17 to 18 and a saved radius still sitting on
the old 150 is moved to 190 on load; a radius he set himself in the console is
left where he put it, which is the discipline that block records for every
change before it.

MEASURED. Check 11.77 reads the default radius and requires 190; deploys,
finds a pillager with a clear line to a point forty units away, stands the
player far outside any blast, lets the charge off there, and requires the
pillager's loss to match the new formula within a point and a half and to
exceed the old formula by at least five, so the numbers cannot have moved
nowhere; then drives the real loader with a version 17 save carrying 150 and
requires 190 after load, and with a hand-set 140 and requires it untouched. On
the v11.76 fixture the default reads 150 and the loss matches the old formula,
and the check names both.

A HARNESS REPAIR IN THE SAME BUILD. The first cut of this check handed the
loader a bare profile and left it there: the loader replaces the profile with
what it is given, and the fixture's clean-up keeps what it finds rather than
rebuilding, so every later check that leans on the profile lost its stash,
its weapons and its seventeen cosmetic fields. Three corpus runs went red on
three different checks (8.72 belt to stash, 10.34 and 10.33 sprite
cosmetics), one each, which is the signature of a poisoned profile and not
of a broken build. The check now snapshots the clean profile after its own
clean-up and puts it back through the real loader at the end.
Not verified: his own play, and whether these are the numbers he wants, which
are five figures in two lines; the fuse; how much more wall a raid now loses to
grenades, which is a consequence and not a dial.
