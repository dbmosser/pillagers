v12.39: a gun you died with is not coming up with you

A death splices the guns you were carrying out of the armoury. The primary has
been put back on a gun you still own since v2.82; nothing has ever done the
same for the sidearm. So a death that took your second gun left gun 2 still
naming a gun the splice thirty lines above had just removed.

The deploy itself was not fooled, because it tests the armoury before it bakes
a sidearm, so you went up with an empty second slot. What lied was the ascent
check, the last screen before the lift, which read the field raw and printed
the gun you had just lost with your body as the gun going up in your hands.

Two edits. The sidearm now gets the same repair the primary already gets,
back to the empty slot that has been the default since v5.37. And the ascent
check makes the same test the deploy makes, so that screen can never name a
gun that will not come up; that also covers a slot 2 that has come to name the
gun already in slot 1.

Check 12.39 stages two guns no starter roll, shop or freebie kit can hand out,
dies carrying the second, and requires the slot empty and that screen to stop
naming the lost gun; a control puts the gun back and requires the same page to
name it again; fails on v12.38.
