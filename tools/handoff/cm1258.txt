v12.58: a weather you pinned stops asking to change

The last open line of the 2026-09-06 in-raid audit, and the only one on it a
player never sees. It is here because it corrupts my numbers rather than your
game.

The weather turn clock expires and the picker is asked for something different
from what is up. With the weather pinned in Settings it can only ever answer
with the pinned one, so the guard loop asks twelve times, gets the same answer
twelve times, and returns without putting the clock forward. The clock stays
expired and it all happens again on the next frame: thirteen seeded draws every
frame for the rest of the raid.

Nothing on screen shows it, which is exactly why it matters. The seeded stream
is what makes two runs of the same seed comparable, and this advances it by
hundreds of draws a second, so every paired measurement taken with the weather
held still was comparing two different streams.

One line, before the loop, drawing nothing itself: a weather that cannot change
has no turns left to make. With the weather unpinned nothing runs differently.

Check 12.58 counts the calls to the picker itself, because nothing downstream
shows the waste. The control runs first on an unpinned weather and requires the
picker to be asked; the finding arm pins it and requires no calls at all across
sixty frames; fails on v12.57.
