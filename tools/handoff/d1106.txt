## v11.06 - HIS NOTE 20: THE BALLER'S JERSEY IS BEHIND A BLACK BLOCK

"micheal jordan character, can't see his jersey bc its blocked by a black block
(maybe supposed to be backpack straps?)", 2026-09-04.

**IT IS NOT THE BACKPACK.** The pack is drawn before the torso and the torso
covers it. Two other things are on that chest and I put both of them there.

**MEASURED**, one baller drawn alone at nine times raid size, every painted pixel
in a 125 by 130 box around the chest counted by colour:

    3,222  the red of the jersey
    2,384  the jersey's own black side panels
    1,602  the red in shadow
    1,582  the white of the 23
    1,506  the ink outline
      882  the chest rig band

The black side panels alone were 74 percent of the red. They were 2.4 wide each
on a chest 13 wide, so 4.8 of 13, **thirty-seven percent of the shirt in
near-black**. At sprite size two black bars down a chest read as straps, which is
exactly the word he used. And underneath them, the chest rig is a near-black band
the FULL width of the chest, drawn immediately after the jersey, straight across
the lower half of the numeral.

**THE FIX, both of them.** The side panels go from 2.4 to 1.4, which is trim
rather than a block. And the jersey is drawn AFTER the chest rig instead of
before it, so the 23 sits on top of the band. The rig still reads, between the
number and the panels.

**MEASURED AFTER:** the white of the 23 goes from 1,582 to **2,735**, a 73
percent gain, because nothing is painted across it any more. The red goes from
3,222 to 3,661. The black falls from 2,384 to 1,994 and its ratio to the red from
0.74 to 0.54. The black fell less than the panel width did because that exact
colour appears elsewhere on the figure, which is worth knowing before anybody
reads too much into that one number.

**NEW CHECK v11.06** finds the chest from the drawing rather than from
coordinates typed into it, using the jersey red as the marker, then requires at
least 2,000 pale pixels of numeral and a black-to-red ratio no worse than 0.62.
Two controls: a figure with no jersey must produce almost no jersey red and few
pale pixels, which proves the red is the shirt and the pale is the number rather
than the skin.

Fails on a v11.05 fixture with both findings and their numbers.

**AND THE v10.46 JERSEY CHECK HAD TO MOVE WITH IT.** It required at least 8 ink
pixels of side panel at raid scale, a floor calibrated on the very block he
complained about. Widening the art back until the old number returned would be
fitting the game to the test. Measured again at raid scale with the trim: red
+58, pale +24, ink +4. The floor is 2, half the live reading, which still catches
the panels being deleted and no longer demands a block.

Not verified: how it reads to him at play zoom and on the Fashion figure, which
is the judgement he will make; and whether the chest rig band should be there at
all on a jersey, which is a look decision rather than a defect.
