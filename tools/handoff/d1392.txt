names from a shuffle of all the pillager identities, with no look at who he hired. The hire is
built from the same identity list. So:

- On roughly half of raids a second man with his hire's name and tag was on the map.
- The rivals ledger keeps one record per identity. Killing that stranger wrote a kill against
  the hire's record.
- From then on the hire's identity spawned hostile and refused parley, and the hire screen refused
  him for good, for a kill on a different man.

the opening roster draws (only a raid of 28 or more pillagers would reach it). The move happens
after the seeded draws, so the seeded stream is untouched.

with no hire (the control), hires that identity, deploys the same sixteen seeds, and requires no
stranger carrying it. It fails on v13.91.
