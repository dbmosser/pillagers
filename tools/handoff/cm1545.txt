v15.45: a round into a pillager you downed keeps your kill when he bleeds out

Bodies audit finding. Any round of yours that did not kill cleared the mark
that credits you with a kill, so one more round into a pillager you had
downed, fired while he was still on the floor, took the kill away: when he
bled out there was no kill in the run report, no contract tick and no
grudge. A killing round still marks him yours and a round into a standing
man still clears it, but a round into a downed man that does not finish him
now leaves the mark with whoever downed him.

Check 15.45. Fails on v15.44.
