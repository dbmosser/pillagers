v12.45: cutting the seal no longer stops the world

Cutting the seal returns out of the player update on every frame of the hold,
and two things live below that return: the only recovery tick on the standing
path, and the only real call to the extraction clocks.

So for the forty to a hundred and fifteen seconds a cut takes, the health bar
was flat and the clock that decides when you start healing did not even
advance. And every extraction clock was frozen with it: an extraction you had
already called for stopped closing, its window stopped running, and no pillager
wave could arrive, at the exact moment you make the loudest noise in the game
standing still with your back to the room.

The roll branch and the trade window both carry these two calls past their own
returns for exactly this reason. The seal was the last early return that did
not.

Honestly: the recovery half is a pure repair, but the wave half means a wave
that could never arrive during a cut now can, so cutting is riskier than it
has been. No dial moves, and the bot never cuts a seal.

Check 12.45 holds the real key at the real seal through the real frame loop for
fifteen seconds and asserts a rise on the cut, the recovery clock, the health
and an extraction already called for, with a control of the same fifteen seconds standing beside
the seal not cutting; fails on v12.44.
