## v13.60 - BUYING WIRT'S LOT NEVER LOCKS YOU OUT OF THE NEXT ONE

The Undercroft audit of 2026-09-14, finding 2. Wirt's lot card is drawn when the station
opens and captures its lot then; since v11.61 the Buy button buys exactly that lot. But the
bought stamp (P.wirtLotBought) took the clock at the moment of the click, and nothing
redraws the card on a timer. So opening Wirt near the end of a five minute window and
clicking Buy after the change paid for the old lot, which is right, and stamped the new
window as bought, which is wrong: the card read Bought, new item in five minutes, and he
was locked out of a lot he never saw.

THE BUILD. The card remembers the window it was drawn for, and the purchase stamps that
window. The lot bought and its price are unchanged.

MEASURED. Check 13.60 fixes the lot, draws the card in one window, moves the clock to the
next window before clicking Buy, and requires the stamp to name the window the card was
drawn for. The control buys with no change of window and requires that window. It fails on
v13.59.

Not verified: the card still does not redraw itself when the window changes, so it keeps
showing the old lot until the station is reopened; that is a separate polish item.
