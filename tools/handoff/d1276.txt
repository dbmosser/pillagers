## v12.76 - HOW FAR YOU DIED FROM EXTRACTION IS MEASURED TO THE NEAREST WAY OUT

From the 2026-09-08 first-hour audit, confirmed by a skeptic and then by me
reading both sites. It is my own half-done fix from v8.58, found again.

WHAT IT WAS. The death card says KILLED BY X, N METRES FROM EXTRACTION. That N
was measured to the ring the raid had nominated, and that is not the nearest way
out. It is a ring the dice picked when the raid was built, and it is only ever
re-pointed when he physically stands inside a ring, or when the one it points at
closes. A player who dies without ever reaching a ring, which is exactly how a
first raid ends, was therefore measured against a ring chosen for him at random.

AND IT CONTRADICTED TWO THINGS AT ONCE. The compass over his head spends the
whole raid pointing at the nearest OPEN ring. And the line printed directly
underneath this one, on the same card, the closest he ever came to extraction, is
measured to the nearest open ring as well. So the card disagreed with the compass
he had been following, and with itself, six pixels apart.

THIS IS MY MISTAKE, MADE THE SAME WAY AS BEFORE. The helper that measures the
nearest open ring already exists. I wrote it at v8.58 for the closest-approach
figure, and all four of its call sites carry a comment saying "the nearest OPEN
ring, not the targeted one". I did not apply it to the death distance, which sits
twenty feet away in the same file. That is the same shape as the Peddler and the
counter, and as the lift and quick ascent: fix one instance, leave its twin.

MEASURED. Check 12.76 kills him for real, standing beside one open ring with the
nomination pointed at the farthest other one, and reads the number the card is
built from off the banked run record. It asserts its own staging rather than
assuming it: the two rings must be far enough apart that the right answer and the
wrong one cannot be confused, and it says so and skips if the seed does not offer
that. The control kills him beside the nominated ring itself, where the old
answer and the new one agree, so a build that had simply started reporting some
other number would fail there rather than pass.

Not verified: it reads the banked number rather than the drawn card, though the
card prints exactly that field; it uses one seed on one sector; and it does not
change the flight recorder line, which has always carried the same field and now
carries the corrected one.
