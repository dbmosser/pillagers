## v15.32 - ON A CONTROLLER THE EXTRACT PROMPT NAMES THE X BUTTON

Extraction audit finding. On a controller the X button holds E in a raid (PADHOLD 2, and pollPad holds E whenever no search is
starting), and keyLabel turns KeyE into X while a pad is connected. Every call prompt already asks keyLabel: HOLD X TO CALL FOR
EXTRACTION standing and down, and the ring badge reads [X] EXTRACT. Two extraction lines did not, and said E on every input:
- the centre banner in drawHUD, drawn standing in a landed ring. The ring badge is skipped for the ring he stands in (v6.57), so
  this was the only verb on screen, and on a pad it named a key the pad does not have, for the same button he had just called
  with.
- the downedVerb line, drawn at the huge size down in a landed ring with automatic pickup off (hauledAboard 0, the shipped
  default). The ring badge is not drawn while he is down, so nothing on screen named X there either.
Neither line is a key in the TXSHIP table, and nothing rewrites it.

THE BUILD. Both lines build the verb as 'HOLD '+keyLabel('KeyE','E')+' TO EXTRACT', the same helper and the same fallback the
call prompts use. On the keyboard keyLabel returns E, so the keyboard text is exactly what it was, and the existing checks that
look for the E line with no pad still see it. No balance number, dial, loot table or sound moves.

Left alone, outside this finding: HOLD E TO CUT THE SEAL is hard-coded the same way, and the two say() lines at the landing and
the missed window ("Hold E to extract", "Hold E to call for another") still name E on a pad.

MEASURED. Check 15.32 deploys a raid, fakes a connected pad with every button up through navigator.getGamepads and one pollPad,
stages the ring (every ring quiet, this one landed with beaconT -1 and a 20 s window, or uncalled), puts him in the middle of it,
runs tickExtractPoints(0) so the HUD mirrors come from the ring, and records every string drawHUD passes to ctx.fillText.
Controls: the pad turns padOn on and labels KeyE as X; standing in an uncalled ring the HUD draws HOLD X TO CALL FOR EXTRACTION,
and down in one the downed screen does too, on either build; the landed ring reaches G.beaconT and G.shipHold. The finding:
standing in the landed ring, and down in it, the HUD must draw HOLD X TO EXTRACT and never the E line; v15.31 draws the E line in
both, so the check fails there. The keyboard: with the pad let go, the same landed ring still draws the E line standing and down.
The needles are assembled from words, so the check never matches its own source. Dry run on scratch copies of the page and the
fixture (1527 already in both): p1528 to p1537 and f1528 to f1537 applied in version order, every anchor a whole line matching
once, the version and DEVNOW moved to 15.32 and on, and the patched drawHUD and the new check compiled under JScript. The check
still passes over v15.34, which moves the standing verb onto the point he stands in: it stages that point landed, not only the
mirrors.

Not verified: check 15.32 has not been run in a browser, and no real controller has stood or lain in a landed ring.
