## v15.30 - AN AUTOMATIC GUN THAT RUNS DRY ON A HELD TRIGGER CLICKS ONCE

Sound audit finding (LOW). Since v10.56 an empty gun plays one dull dry click and says "Out of ammo." when the trigger is
pulled with nothing left in the backpack. In updatePlayer the empty-magazine branch lets a pull in with
if(!p.fired||p.wep.auto), so automatics get in on every rate-of-fire tick, but the click itself was gated on !p.fired. An
automatic sets p.fired on every round it fires and only letting go of the trigger clears it. So holding the trigger of the
Sputter, the Chatter, the Compact SMG or any other automatic through its last rounds with no ammo in the backpack just stopped
the gun: no click, no line, only the NO AMMO text by the crosshair. A single shot gun was never affected, because it needs a
fresh pull, and an automatic with spare ammo reloads on the held trigger. Only the empty automatic was silent.

THE BUILD. The click keeps its own flag now, p.dryTold. The empty branch plays the click and the line when the flag is off and
sets it; letting go of the trigger clears it, next to p.fired. So an automatic run dry on a held trigger clicks and says Out of
ammo once, neither repeats while the trigger stays held, and the next pull clicks again. A single shot gun still clicks once on
each pull, as before: its way into the branch is unchanged. The same sound, the same line (not reworded), no new text, no
dial, no balance number and no seeded draw. The flag starts unset on a new player, which counts as off.

MEASURED. Check 15.30 deploys a raid, empties the entity list and puts a copy of an automatic in his hands (his own gun if it
is one, else the Sputter) with the jam roll off, no ammo in the magazine and none in the backpack, and records every blip and
every line said. It drives updatePlayer with the trigger held, staging the rate-of-fire clock as run out on each frame.
Controls: the selected belt cell is a gun cell; a fresh pull held three frames clicks and says Out of ammo (exactly once, or
it fails); two rounds loaded and held for six frames fire both and leave the magazine empty with no reload. Then the four
empty held frames must give exactly one dry click and one Out of ammo line; on v15.29 they give none, so the check fails there.
After letting go, a fresh pull must click once again. Everything it changes (blip, say, the trigger and keys, the entity list,
the selected cell, the gun and the player fields, the raid) is put back in finally.
A JScript run of the real fire block cut from scratch copies of v15.29 and the build, with a stand-in player: v15.29 fails on
the held run dry, the build passes; a mutant that clicks every frame fails (4 clicks), a mutant that never clears the flag
fails; a single shot gun gives the same result on both builds (last round held: no click; each later pull: one click and one
line). Dry run on scratch copies of v15.27: p1528 and f1528, p1529 and f1529, then p1530 and f1530, all applied, every anchor
whole lines matching once, and the new check compiles.

Not verified: a live raid by ear, holding an automatic through its last magazine.
