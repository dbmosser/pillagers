v12.74: the Peddler stall knows what it just paid you

Selling at the stall does not pay in Credits. That money rides on the run, banks
only if you walk out, and dies where you fall. That is deliberate and it stays.

The panel did not know it. The balance line read your banked Credits, and so did
the affordability of every row of stock. So a first-time player at zero sells
seven things for four thousand, is told exactly that, and one line down the same
panel in the same frame reads that he holds nothing, with the whole shelf greyed
out. Pressing buy then said he could not cover it.

The panel names both pockets now, and the refusal says which one it means. The
rate, the arithmetic and what the stall will sell are untouched. Whether carried
money should be spendable at the stall is his call, not mine.

The sale line also said cash. This game says Credits.

Check 12.74 sells at a real stall, reads the panel off the canvas and the refusal
off the message line, then runs it again carrying nothing and requires the plain
wording back. Fails on v12.73.
