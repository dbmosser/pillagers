Finding 15 of the 2026-09-11 audit. The worst case is not a mislabel but a loss: the
button the lift called "go up" is the one that skips the loadout question.

The lift prompt read [X] go up. On the floor X is bound to KeyR, so pressing it
launched the raid: no sector page, no question about what you were taking up. Every
station is shifted the same way, and the bottom prompt read [X] THE STASH over a
station that opens on A.

One label table was serving two binding sets. In a raid the pad really does put KeyE
on X and KeyR on Y, and PADLABEL says so. The Undercroft keeps the first act on A
deliberately, then hangs the rest on X, Y and RB: one button lower all the way along,
and PADLABEL has no entry for A. Only RB lined up.

The v6.83 comment claimed these matched the labels PADLABEL prints. They did not, and
that sentence is removed rather than left to mislead the next reader.

The fix is a second table, not a rebinding: moving the floor onto the raid buttons
would take A away from the stations, which v6.83 kept on purpose.
