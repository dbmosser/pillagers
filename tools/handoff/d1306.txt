## v13.06 - THE FREEBIE KIT NEVER TOUCHED THE GUN YOU OWN, AND NOW THE CODE SAYS SO

A field written in five places and read in none, and two builds have described what it
does. Found while reading the freebie kit for his come-back-empty note of 2026-09-11.

WHAT IT CLAIMED. P._gunSlot is cleared when the freebie kit is taken, kept aside in the
snapshot, and put back when he switches to his own loadout. v12.82 is written up as
restoring "the items, the tactical belt plan and the gun slot", and the comment beside
the code says the same. A reader would take all three to be real.

WHAT IT DOES. Nothing. Five writes, no reads, anywhere in the file. What a raid arms
him with comes from P.equipped and P.equippedSec, which none of it ever touched.

THE INVARIANT IT PRETENDED TO PROTECT IS ALREADY TRUE FOR A DIFFERENT REASON, and that
is the part worth keeping: taking the freebie kit does not change the gun he owns or
the one in his hands, because the raid ignores both while the kit is on. That is what
should have been asserted all along, instead of a field nothing reads.

THE FIELD GOES, and the two claims about it go with it. A comment that describes a
no-op is worse than no comment: it is the reason nobody looked.

13.04 AND 13.05 ARE NOT SKIPPED BY ACCIDENT. They were spent on harness-only checks
that carry no game build: the outcome-card sweep and the canvas sweep for his text
edits.

MEASURED, AND THE CONTROL TOOK THREE GOES. A deletion has no behaviour change to
control on, so the honest control is that the thing is deleted, plus proof that what it
pretended to do still holds. The first version asserted the field was absent on the
profile, which fails on a profile saved by an older build and is not this build's
fault; the second cleared it first but drove the freebie kit by setting the flag, which
never writes the field on either build, so the arm asserted nothing and passed
everywhere. It presses the real stash button now, which is one of the two places the
field was written, and it fails on the v13.03 fixture and passes here. The behaviour
arms require the gun he owns and the gun in his hands to be exactly what they were,
through the kit, through a raid, and back.

Not verified: it drives the stash button and not the lift answer, which is the other
place that wrote the field; both are gone by inspection but only one is pressed. And it
does not check the second weapon slot beyond requiring it unchanged.
