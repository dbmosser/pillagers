v12.55: an imported ghost fights at his own gun range

A pillager's gun, his damage and his engagement range are one thing held in
three fields, and the file says so twice. The elite blessing moves all three
together, a man who picks a gun out of a kit moves all three together, and the
comment beside one of them says why: an elite with a Longshot that kept an
SMG's engagement range would read as broken.

The imported ghost is the one re-gun in the game that moved only the gun. So a
ghost whose body rolled an Auto Rifle, and whose report says he favoured the
Riot Scattergun, opened fire at 374 units holding a gun whose rounds die at
260, and every round he fired in that band was thrown away short. The other way
round he never engaged past his body's range at all.

The same two lines the kit path already uses, with the same cap, so there is
one rule for all three re-gun paths.

Check 12.55 records what the body rolled, imports a ghost carrying the
Longshot, which no body can roll, and requires the range and the damage to have
moved with the gun. The control imports the gun that body already carries and
requires nothing to move; fails on v12.54.
