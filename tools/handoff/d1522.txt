## v15.22 - THE HIRE IS NEVER ALSO A STRANGER

Hire audit, refiled and verified (MEDIUM). Raid pillagers take identities from a shuffled pool of 28 names; past that they take
numbered names. v13.92 kept the hired identity off the map by moving it to the back of the pool, which works only while a map
draws 28 strangers or fewer. THE COLD MILE is large: at Standard it draws 33 strangers and at Many 49, so stranger 28 wore the
hire's exact identity, name and look. A kill on a named pillager is recorded against his identity, and a hire with a recorded kill
refuses to work ("will not work for you"), for good. Few, and every setting on COLD STORAGE, never reach 28 and were unaffected.

THE BUILD. The hired identity is removed from the pool instead of moved to its back. Stranger 28 onward takes the numbered names
the pool already falls back to, which never equal a hire's identity. No random draw is spent, so the seeded stream, the counts and
the 28 names are unchanged.

MEASURED. Check 15.22 deploys THE COLD MILE at Standard with no hire (control: more strangers than names, and exactly one wears the
followed identity), then with that identity hired: no stranger may wear it. It restores the profile and the setting. It fails on
v15.21.

Not verified: a real hire on THE COLD MILE at Standard by hand.
