## v11.75 - HIS ORDER: HOLD THE CRAFT BUTTON, AND THE BUTTON IS THE BAR

His order of 2026-09-06: "you should have to hold the craft button for just a
moment (1s?) to craft the item and the button itself should act like a status
bar that fills up".

THE BUILD. The CRAFT button in the detail panel is a hold. Press and keep the
button down and it fills from the left over one second, amber over its own
colour; when it is full the craft happens, once. Let go before it is full and
nothing is spent and the fill drains to nothing. Let go anywhere on the page,
not only on the button, and the same happens, so a release off the edge cannot
leave a hold filling towards a purchase he did not make. A plain click no longer
crafts anything. The SERVICE button for gun repair is the same control drawn
with a different word, and behaves the same way; its tooltip says so.

The hold is stepped by the frame loop, on the same clock everything else in the
game runs on, rather than by wall time, so it pauses when the game pauses and a
check can step it exactly. The fill is painted as a background image only, so
an untouched button keeps its own colour and looks exactly as it did.

One second is his number with a question mark on it; CRAFT_HOLD is that one
number and moves in one line.

MEASURED. Check 11.75 starts a hold on a stub button through the same function
the real button uses, steps the clock to half the hold and requires nothing
fired and a half fill on the button, steps past the full hold and requires
exactly one firing and no fill left, starts again and cancels at seven tenths
and requires nothing fired and nothing left on the button, and requires a
disabled button not to craft however long it is held. Two controls read the
panel's own source and require it to wire the hold and no longer to craft on a
click. On the v11.74 fixture there is no hold to drive and the check says the
button is a plain click.

Not verified: his own hand and eye, which is what "just a moment" is about; the
pad, which reaches CRAFT through a button press it cannot hold; whether the
fill colour reads against the button on his monitor.
