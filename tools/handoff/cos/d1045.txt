## v10.45 - THREE GATES THAT READ HOW HE PLAYS

His note, 2026-09-03 about 14:10, and the Reach research: the commendations
"tracked how you play", pistol kills, headshots, rounds without dying, so
play style became the collection, and players named that among what kept
them coming back.

Every gate on the racks was a count of raids, extractions, levels or
Wardens: how much, never how. Three gates now read how he plays:

| gate | reads | first patch on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| kills | every machine and pillager he has put down, summed from the tally the profile already keeps | Headhunter, fifty |
| night | extractions made in the dark | Night Owl, three |
| streak | extractions in a row; a death or a logged abandon ends it, and the best is kept | Unbroken, three |

Two counters join the profile, night extractions and the streak with its
best, written where the run is banked, beside the runs and extractions that
were already counted there. The next-piece line and the requirement text
know the new gates. Three patches hang on them so each is earnable today.

### Measured

The check requires the three patches on the racks and unowned on a fresh
profile, owned once the counters are set, and with requirement text; then
runs two extractions in the dark and a death and requires the night count
at one after the first, the streak at two and the best at two after the
second, the streak at zero and the best still two after a death; the
control is twenty extractions still earning the Spartan helmet. On v10.44 no
piece on the racks read anything but the four old counters, which is the
finding.

Not verified: kills by weapon, which the tally does not keep; a gate on
pistol kills needs a per-gun tally first. Not verified: whether the streak
should survive a death that he could not have avoided; it does not.
