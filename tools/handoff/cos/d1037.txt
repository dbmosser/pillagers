## v10.37 - THE MESSAGE LINE GROWS WITH THE MONITOR

His answer 25, 2026-09-03, on what bothers him most on the HUD: the controls
legend, the conditions panel, the pillager board, **and on-HUD messaging**.
His answer 30: he plays at 4K, and the game must be accessible at 1080p,
1440p and 4K.

### Measured

With the text trace on v10.09, at 1920x1080 and 3840x2160, every panel scales
with the monitor through hudRes: the body panel's text runs 22 to 44 pixels at
1080p and 44 to 87 at 4K, the conditions panel 16 to 23 against 33, the board
18 against 36 to 47. The message line, the one line the game uses to talk to
him, was 19 pixels at 1080p and 19 pixels at 4K. It scaled with the HUD size
dial and not with the monitor, so on his screen it was about a third the
height of everything around it, at the top centre where the eye is meant to
land. That is the on-HUD messaging he named.

It is drawn inside the same monitor scale as the panels now, anchored to the
top centre so it stays centred, and grows with them: about 38 pixels at 4K.

### Measured again

The check deploys a raid, writes a message, and reads its drawn size at
1080p, 1440p and 4K off the text trace: at 4K it must be at least 1.8 times
the 1080p size and at 1440p at least 1.2 times; the controls are that it stays
centred at both sizes and sits where it always sat at 1080p. On v10.36 it was
the same 19 pixels at every size, which is the finding.

Not verified: the legend, the conditions panel and the pillager board, which
he also named and which measure as scaling correctly and staying inside their
boxes; what is wonky about them is something the numbers do not show, and it
needs his eyes or his words. Not verified: a very long message at 4K, which
grows with the scale and could reach the panels either side; the longest
line the game writes fits at 1080p with room.
