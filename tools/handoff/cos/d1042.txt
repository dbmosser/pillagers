## v10.42 - THE DEPOT IS A PAPER DOLL

His note, 2026-09-03 about 14:10: **"i would love to make the cosmetics
screen kinda look like how you equip your character in diablo"**.

The Depot's operator panel was the figure with the slots stacked under it in
a list, one row each, which read as a settings page. It is an equipment
screen now: the figure stands in the middle, drawn larger on a faint pool of
light, and every slot is a square tile beside it, the things on the head down
the left (headgear, hair, hairstyle, beard, eyes, face, tattoo) and the
things on the body down the right (build, skin, clothing, patch, gloves,
boots, backpack). A tile shows the swatch, the rack name and what is worn,
with the full name on hover; clicking it narrows the racks to that slot and
clicking it again brings every rack back, as the rows did. The operator
column is wider to hold it.

No slot line moved. The slot function files each tile into its column by
what it is, and the return assembles the doll, so every rack added since the
first six keeps its place without touching this again.
