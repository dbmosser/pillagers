## v10.46 - THE FIRST CAPSTONE

His note, 2026-09-03 about 14:10, and the Reach research: the Haunted
helmet cost one credit and needed the top rank plus every other helmet
owned. Players remembered it as the thing that made a finished rack mean
something.

A gate that reads the rack itself. A piece gated on its own rack is earned
when every other piece of that kind is owned; capstones do not gate each
other, so a second one on the same rack would not deadlock. The first is
Platinum on the hair rack, the pale metal that only shows up on a finished
rack. The requirement text says "every other piece on this rack", and the
next-piece line counts it in pieces still to collect ("1 MORE PIECE ON ITS
RACK").

### Measured

The check requires the capstone unowned and with text, colour and swatch on
a fresh profile, owned once every other hair is earned, still locked with
Violet locked at level one and one piece away on the distance, and as the
control Ash still gated on eight raids. On v10.45 no piece read the rack it
hangs on, which is the finding.

Not verified: a capstone on each of the other thirteen racks; they are one
line each once he says which piece deserves it, and the headgear one wants a
drawing.
