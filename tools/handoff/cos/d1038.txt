## v10.38 - THE FULL CONTROLS LIST IS ON THE SCREEN

His answer 25, 2026-09-03: the controls legend is one of the four HUD things
that bother him most. His answer 30: he plays at 4K.

### Measured

With the text trace on v10.14, the full list, the one H cycles to:

| | where it drew |
| --- | --- |
| 1920x1080 | the MOVE heading at y -52, above the screen; the rule cards at x 1454 to 1865, y 50 to 152, inside the conditions panel's box, five lines of them |
| 3840x2160 | the whole list at x 4797 and beyond, on a screen 3840 wide |

On his monitor, pressing H to the full list showed nothing at all. That is
the controls legend he named.

### The cause is one line

The full list centres itself on W in unscaled space, and it is then drawn
inside the compact panel's corner zoom, anchored at the left edge and the
panel's bottom target, so its centre lands at W times the zoom over two and
its top at the target minus the scaled height. That zoom is right for the
compact panel it was written for, in the bottom left, and wrong for a
centred list. The full list is scaled about the bottom centre now, and the
scale is capped so the whole list is on the screen at any size; the compact
panel keeps its corner zoom and its drag.

### Measured again

The check draws the full list at 1080p, 1440p and 4K and requires the MOVE
heading, the hide line and the sound key inside the screen, the list centred
within twelve percent of the middle, and no rule line inside the conditions
panel's box; the control is the compact legend still bottom left and inside.
On v10.37 the MOVE heading is at y -52 at 1080p and x 4797 at 4K, which is
the finding.

Not verified: the conditions panel and the pillager board, the other two he
named, which measure as inside their boxes and clear of each other; and
on-HUD messaging, which v10.37 scaled. If they still look wonky to him it is
