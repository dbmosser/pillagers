## v10.44 - A RAID THAT EARNS A PIECE SAYS SO

His note, 2026-09-03 about 14:10, and the Reach research: "when I ranked up
new items would pop up". The moment of earning was the moment players
remembered, and here it passed in silence: a piece became yours somewhere in
the counters and you found out, if ever, by wandering the racks.

Now the racks are snapshotted when the lift moves, and when the outcome card
is written, after the run is banked, whatever is owned now and was not then
is named on the card in the extraction colour: "EARNED THIS RAID: SPARTAN
HELMET (HEADGEAR). ON YOUR RACK AT THE DEPOT.", above the next piece coming.
A raid that earned nothing shows nothing. The rack names moved into one
function shared with the next-piece line.

requires the card to name the Spartan helmet the twentieth earned and none of

death and requires the line empty. On v10.43 the card had no earned line,
which is the finding.
