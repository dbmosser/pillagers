v11.41: a claimed contract could refill its slot with a duplicate job

freed slot with a bare genContract, so a claimed job could be replaced by one
already on the board: 182 of 400 finished-then-claimed boards ended with two
identical rows. New helper freshContract dedups against every other slot; both
the claim path and the render-time repair use it. New window.__contracts hook
drives the real code; check 11.41 requires zero duplicates over 200 seeds with
two controls that the refill still happens and the board stays at eight.
