## v11.97 - THE BIGGER BLAST, THREE THINGS THAT DID NOT MOVE WITH IT

From the read-only review of this morning's shipped builds, on v11.77.

WHAT IT WAS. v11.77 took the frag radius from 150 to 190 on his order, and
three things that had been calibrated to 150 stayed where they were. A
pillager throws a charge only in a band whose near edge, 180, cleared his
own blast at 150; at 190 that edge is inside it, so at the near edge of
every throw he stood in his own charge, which is the mistake the band
exists to prevent. The "It hit cover. MOVE." warning, for a charge that
bounces off cover you are standing against, fired only inside 110 units,
half the new radius, so the ground where you were in your own blast with
no warning had doubled. And the NEW IN card printed the formula's
coefficients as if they were the damage at the centre: the centre is
coefficient plus floor, 140 to a machine or a pillager (was 100) and 98 to
you (was 72), which is what the DESIGN entry said and the card did not.

THE BUILD. The near edge of the throw band follows the radius plus forty,
so it moves with the dial the way the blast does; the cover warning covers
the whole radius for a frag; the card says 140 and 98. The same build
repairs check 11.44, whose cfgv sentinel was pinned at 17 by the v11.77
stamp and so could never fire again, and a comment that still quoted the
old stamp.

MEASURED. Check 11.97 deploys with the radius pinned at 190, hands a real
pillager a real frag with his throw clock ready, asks him to throw at the
player from 200 units and requires no throw and no charge in the air, then
from 260 and requires a throw, so the band is proven live; and reads the
card's frag line for the true centre figures. On the v11.96 fixture the
200-unit throw happens and the card prints 115 and 80.

Not verified: his own play; the far edge of the band, 520, which was not
calibrated to the radius and stays; the cover warning itself, which is not
staged here (a comment-level reading; the line now reads the dial).
