## v15.82 - HOLDING E AT THE PEDDLER KEEPS THE STALL OPEN

Trade audit finding (MEDIUM), entry 0 of the confirmed list.

Walk up to the Peddler so the plate reads THE PEDDLER [E] DEAL, and press and hold E, the way the game trains you to for every
crate search and for HOLD E TO EXTRACT. On the first frame updatePlayer opens the stall (ped, no crate underfoot, keys KeyE,
no pedLock: G.trade=ped, G.pedLock=1). About half a second later, at the Windows key-repeat delay, the browser sends keydown E
with repeat true; the window listener has no repeat filter for E and ends in raidKey(e.code,e.repeat,e). The open-stall block
in raidKey, if(code==='Escape'||code==='KeyE'||code==='Tab'||code==='KeyI'||code==='KeyB'){ G.trade=null; G.pedLock=1;
return; }, had no repeat test, so the repeat shut the stall, and because E was still down pedLock (cleared only when the key
is up, updatePlayer) kept it shut. The panel flashed open and vanished with nothing said, and he had to let go and tap E
again. Every other one-press toggle in raidKey ignores repeats (v15.49), and the Digit line right under this one already
did. The controller is not affected: pad X calls raidKey('KeyE',false,null) on a fresh press only. Nothing here is a TXSHIP
key, no comment calls the old behaviour deliberate, and it is not balance.

THE BUILD. One edit in raidKey, the finding's own fix: the closing line reads if(!repeat){ G.trade=null; G.pedLock=1; }
return;. A key repeat of E, ESC, TAB, I or B at the open stall now returns with no effect, which also keeps it from the
number row and the preventDefault list below. A fresh E, ESC, I or B still closes the stall and holds the key; a fresh TAB is
taken earlier by backOut, as before. No number, no dial, no loot table and no word of player text moved, and map building is
not touched, so the seed 4242 fingerprint in __verifySafe cannot move.

MEASURED. Check 15.82 clears the card and profile, snapshots the profile, deploys at seed 4242 on map 0, clears the keys and
the open things, empties G.ents and marks every unopened container within 60 units opened (put back in finally), and makes a
Peddler 40 units to his right. CONTROL: a frame with no key finds the Peddler (G.nearPed) with nothing else claiming E
(pedBlocked, nearContainer). CONTROL: a frame with E let go then a frame with E held opens the stall (G.trade is the
Peddler), on either build. THE FIX, ONE: with E still down, raidKey('KeyE',true,null), the repeat as the listener hands it
over; the stall must still be open, and so must it be a frame later with E still down. On v15.81 the repeat sets G.trade null,
so the check fails for the finding's own reason. THE FIX, TWO, on the real path: with state raid, the stall reopened the same
way, a fresh keydown E dispatched at window (repeat false) must close the stall and hold the key (GUARD, and it proves the
page path reaches the raid keys); the stall reopened again, a keydown E dispatched at window with repeat true must leave it
open. GUARD: the stall reopened, a fresh raidKey('Escape',false,null) still closes it. Each reopen that cannot happen skips
while keeping any failure already found. In finally the keys and the mouse are cleared, G.trade, pedLock, nearPed, pedBlocked
and the search are reset, the shut containers are reopened, G.ents is put back, the player position and iv are put back, the
raid is abandoned, the profile snapshot is restored through __applyLoaded, and the card, dials and profile are cleaned.

Older fixture checks: none restaged. No check sends a repeat of E, ESC, TAB, I or B with the stall open: the fixture has no
raidKey('KeyE',true and no keydown with repeat true beside 15.49, which holds ESC and P with G.trade null. The stall checks
(15.35, 15.36, 15.37) open and close it with held keys through updatePlayer and fresh raidKey presses, which are unchanged.
