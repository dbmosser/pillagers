## v11.07 - HIS NOTE 22, ASKED TWICE: CRAWLERS ARE NOT ATTACKING PROPERLY

"Once again, crawlers aren't attacking properly?", 2026-09-04, and before that, on
2026-09-03: "i saw atleast one instance where i was standing still and crawler
didn't hurt me even though he was close".

**REPRODUCED, AND THE NUMBER IS SHARP.** One crawler stood 30 units from a still
player and facing him. Its bite reaches 36 (its own body is 15, the player's 11,
and the reach is 10 on top). Three seconds each time, varying only how well
hidden he was:

    concealment 1.00   65 damage
    concealment 0.80   65 damage
    concealment 0.60   65 damage
    concealment 0.45   65 damage
    concealment 0.35   65 damage
    concealment 0.25   ZERO, in patrol for the whole three seconds
    concealment 0.15   ZERO
    concealment 0.05   ZERO

**THE CAUSE: a crawler's eyes shrink with your concealment and its reach does
not.** Its sight is 100 units multiplied by concealment; its bite reaches 36. Below
about 0.3 there is a band where it is close enough to bite and cannot see, and the
bite lives inside the chase branch, so it never enters chase. It stands there
touching him and does nothing. That is exactly what he described, both times.

**WHY THE FIRST FIX DID NOT CATCH IT.** The 2026-09-03 report led to the two
if/else chains finding, where a non-raider fell into the idle wander branch mid
chase and wiped the attack cooldown every frame. That was a real bug and it was
fixed. It was also a different bug. Checking the same ground again: that wander
branch is inside the snitch block and ends in a continue, so a crawler cannot
reach it. The cooldown is not the problem now; being seen is.

**THE FIX: you cannot hide from something that is already touching you.** The
crawler's sight gets a floor equal to its own reach plus a small margin, applied
to the corner of the eye as well as to the cone, because you do not hide from
something you are touching by standing behind it. A wall still stops it: canSee
runs its own line of sight and the bite tests for one separately.

**THIS CHANGES WHAT CRAWLERS DO, and I am not pretending otherwise.** It sits on
a dial, touchSees, so the old behaviour stays measurable rather than deleted.

**NEW CHECK v11.07** puts a crawler at 85 percent of its own reach, taken from
the entity rather than typed in, in front of a still, hidden player, and requires
damage inside three seconds. Three controls, and they matter more than the
assertion: at 2.2 times the reach the same hidden man must still be safe, or
concealment has stopped meaning anything; a man in plain view must still be
bitten, or the check is measuring the wrong thing; and turning the dial off must
bring the silence back and turning it on must restore the bite, or the fix is not
what is being tested.

Fails on a v11.06 fixture with his own case in its own words: "a crawler 31 units
away, with a reach of 36, did nothing at all in three seconds while he stood
still and hidden".

Not verified: what this does to the bot's extract rate. Crawlers are the one
enemy the sim bot actually fights, so a crawler that notices it at touching range
may move that number, and I have not run the paired seeds to say by how much. The
alternative was to sit on a bug he has now reported twice, which is worse.
