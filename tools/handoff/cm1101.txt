v11.01: the end of raid buttons ask about this game

His note: "change the feedback buttons at the end of a raid to something more
current".

Reproduced by reading the list, whose own comment dates it to v5.72. Eight of the
twenty-four were one-machine questions from that era: Listener beatable and
unfair, Pillbox worth it and a chore, Bulwark great and unfair, Howler great and
unfair. All four machines still exist and he has logged dozens of runs against
them, so those are answered by his own log rather than open. Nothing from the
last thirty builds had a tag at all, and there was no way to say "it crashed" or
"it looked wrong", which are the two things a friend cannot tell him any other
way.

That matters now because his friends are about to play and a tag is the only
feedback most people ever give.

The new set is thirty tags, every one judgeable in a single raid, opening with
Something crashed, Something looked wrong and Sound was off, then readability,
feel, what he carried, who was up there, the newest systems, the place, and
getting out. Old records keep their strings.

New check v11.01 requires a way to say it crashed, looked wrong, sounded wrong
and read badly, allows at most two of the eight old questions to survive, forbids
duplicates and over-long labels, and then drives the real thing: deploy, end the
raid, press two buttons on the real card, press a third twice, press the real way
out, and require the two on the logged run and the third absent. Fails on a
v11.00 fixture with all five findings.

Not verified: whether he agrees with the eight I removed, and how thirty tags
wrap at 1440p and 4K.
