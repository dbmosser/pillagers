### Important
1. **New saves count every kill twice for medals.** A new save is not born with achScan, the stamp of the one-time scan that credits
   runs from before live counting. Live runs already count their kills, so the first time a new save opens its stats page the scan adds
   every run again. Proof (v35.38): two runs of 7 pillager kills read 14, then 28 after the first look at the stats page, and the
   25-kill medal unlocked; machines went 60 to 120. Who it hits: every new save, including party guests and every Thanksgiving player.
   Old saves are untouched by the fix; a save that already doubled stays doubled (there is no record to repair from).
   **Draft: tools/handoff/drafts/draft-cloud-ach-born-scan.json, GATE PASS as v35.43 on v35.42** (parse, check twice on the draft,
   FAIL on the current build, newest 80 checks clean, fingerprint ents 85/374 containers 165/593, node --check, ESLint gate).
2. **Player 2 never chooses easy or normal in the 2-player demo.** His answer 2.28 c: each player chooses before the run. Only
   arcadeDeploy asks, and only player 1 runs it; player 2 enters through netUpTake. Proof (v35.38, real 2-window rig): with both in the
   raid, player 1 held the card (easyAsk {waitMap:0}, picture 2p-player1-easy-card-up.jpg), player 2 held easyAsk null (picture
   2p-player2-no-easy-card.jpg). A yes player 2 gave at the second-death offer also carried into the next run.
   **Draft: tools/handoff/drafts/draft-cloud-p2-easy-card.json, GATE PASS as v35.43 on v35.42** (same gates).
   Watch at the party: player 2 sees "want easy mode?" after its opening map and answers with A or B.
   The two drafts apply together cleanly (checked); each ship takes the next version number.

### Fixed in this audit (tools only, no game change)
3. **The "settings that do nothing" lint was blind.** The defaults moved to var DEF={ (var CFG={} is now empty), so class A found an
   empty object and printed zero without checking anything. tools/lint.ps1 now reads DEF, skips comments inside it, counts a check that
   could not run as a hit instead of a zero, and finds its files from its own folder (runs on his PC and in the cloud). Result: all
   196 settings are read somewhere. Its class C now flags item 1 (achScan); that line clears once item 1 ships. Its six class D hits
   were read and recorded as cleared (false alarms: a one-line function let the scan run on, the rest are early returns or separate feed rows).
4. **Two out-of-date belt checks were missing from the known-red list.** 23.05 and 11.96 still demand the guns-only bar his 10-27
   items-on-hotbar order reversed (both pass on v34.94, fail from v35.12, fail alone on v35.38). Added to tools/handoff/stable-known-red.txt
   beside the other nine, so stable does not count them as new failures.
