## v11.95 - THE BENCH TELLS THE TRUTH ABOUT ITS GUNS

From the read-only review of this morning's shipped builds, on v11.79.

WHAT IT WAS. v11.79 put four guns on the crafting bench on his order and
called two of them green. The Burst Carbine is blue everywhere the player
looks: a gun's shown rarity comes from its tier, and the price row's own
rarity field is the one v7.99 said is not a gun's rarity. So the bench
holds one green gun and three blue, the NEW IN card and the v11.79 entry
said two of each, and the check agreed with them only because it read the
same wrong field. Three more things followed from putting guns where no
recipe had ever put one: the bench's cream detail panel described a
crafted gun as salvage to sell at the terminal, because its blurb had no
gun branch; the same panel read the gun's rarity from the price row, the
one screen in the game that could disagree with every other; and the
stash's KEEP FOR reason for a servo and an optic dropped from "contracts"
to plain "crafting", because the recipe lookup runs first and the reason
table had no entry for either, while craftPart still said the servo is in
no recipe when four eat it. The shop panel still told the player the
Carbine and the Scattergun come only from Wirt or a container.

THE BUILD. The card says one green and three blue, and this entry corrects
the v11.79 entry. The blurb describes a gun as a gun. The servo and the
optic are craft parts again with their own reasons, so SELL ALL keeps them
and the stash says why. The shop panel adds one line that three of its
unstocked guns can be built; the existing sentence is left alone because
it is a key for his own rewording of it. The same build repairs check
11.79, which now reads the shown rarity and the game's own purchase price
(the shop stocks no blue gun, so three of its four price controls were
silent no-ops), and the v9.43 check that asserted a servo is in no recipe.

MEASURED. Check 11.95 reads the bench through the shown rarity and
requires at least one green and one blue and nothing else; requires the
card not to call the Carbine green; requires the blurb for a crafted gun to
say gun and not salvage; requires the stash reasons for a servo and an
optic to name contracts; and opens the bench, selects the Carbine and
requires its detail pill to read RARE. On the v11.94 fixture the card says
green, the blurb says salvage, the reasons say crafting and the pill says
UNCOMMON.

Not verified: his own eye on the bench; whether he wants a second green
gun there (only the Compact SMG is green by tier; the Magnum and the
Carbine are blue), which is his call; the detail panel's rarity pill, which
the same build changes only where the review quoted it.
