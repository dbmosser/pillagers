v11.07: a crawler close enough to bite you has found you

His note, asked twice: "Once again, crawlers aren't attacking properly?"

Reproduced with a sharp number. One crawler 30 units from a still player, facing
him, with a bite that reaches 36. Three seconds each, varying only concealment:
at 1.00, 0.80, 0.60, 0.45 and 0.35 it deals 65 damage. At 0.25, 0.15 and 0.05 it
deals ZERO and sits in patrol for the whole three seconds.

A crawler's eyes shrink with your concealment and its reach does not. Sight is
100 units times concealment; the bite reaches 36. Below about 0.3 there is a band
where it is close enough to bite and cannot see, and the bite lives inside the
chase branch, so it never enters chase. It stands there touching him, doing
nothing.

The first report led to the two if/else chains fix, which was a real bug and a
different one: that wander branch is inside the snitch block and ends in a
continue, so a crawler cannot reach it. The cooldown is not the problem now.

The crawler's sight now has a floor equal to its own reach plus a margin, applied
to the corner of the eye as well as the cone. Walls still stop it. This changes
what crawlers do, so it sits on the dial touchSees.

New check v11.07 puts a crawler at 85 percent of its own reach, taken from the
entity rather than typed in, in front of a still hidden player, and requires
damage within three seconds. Controls: at 2.2 times the reach the same hidden man
must still be safe, a man in plain view must still be bitten, and the dial must
both remove and restore the bite. Fails on a v11.06 fixture in his own words.

Not verified: what this does to the bot extract rate. Crawlers are the one enemy
the sim bot actually fights, and I have not run the paired seeds to say by how
much. The alternative was sitting on a bug reported twice.
