## v15.31 - A HELD X ON A CONTROLLER SEARCHES ONE BOX, THEN CALLS OR EXTRACTS

Extraction audit finding. On a controller, X in an extraction point searches first (v13.48) and, with no search started 0.35 s
into the press, holds E for the call or the extraction (v13.51). A search that has started in the hold keeps X "until the box is
done", through a latch, PAD.xSearched. That latch was only cleared by letting go of X. So when the box was done the same hold went
on to search the next box in reach, then the next, and only held E when nothing was left: fight a siege from inside the ring, two
crawlers die at his feet, hold X when EXTRACT NOW shows, and he searches both wrecks (about a second each) before the 1.4 s hold
even starts. With the window nearly shut, or the raid clock at zero, he searches instead of leaving. The 1.6 s call in an uncalled
ring waited the same way. A second path: a backpack that filled part way through a hold refused the next search, and the latch
kept X on that refusal for as long as the button was held, so he could not call at all. The keyboard was never affected, because
E and X are separate keys there.

THE BUILD. One line in pollPad. While X is held and no search is running, a latch that was set means a search in this hold has
ended, so the latch is spent and the 0.35 s grace with it (PAD.xDownAt goes far into the past), and the same hold turns to E: the
call or the extraction hold starts, the way the keyboard E would. The press frame is unchanged, since the latch is not set yet,
so X still searches the box in reach first. The full backpack grace from v13.51 is unchanged, since that path never sets the
latch. Letting go still resets both, so a fresh press searches the next box first, as v13.51 intends. No balance number, dial,
loot table or player text moves.

MEASURED. Check 15.31 deploys a raid, fakes a pad through navigator.getGamepads, lands the ring (beaconT -1, a 20 s window,
G.active on it), clears the map of machines and boxes, and steps pollPad and updatePlayer at 60 frames a second. Control: one
Scrap Metal wreck (0.6 s search) at his feet; X held opens it, and half a second later the extraction hold is running (above
0.3 s of 1.4), on either build. Control: a second wreck 30 units off is found in reach on its own. The finding: both wrecks, X
held; the nearer opens, and half a second later the second wreck must be untouched and the extraction hold running. On v15.30 the
second wreck is half searched and the hold has not started, so the check fails there. Kept from v13.51: let go and pressed again,
X searches the second wreck. Dry run on scratch copies of the page and the fixture (1527 already in both): p1528 to p1537 and
f1528 to f1537 applied in version order, every anchor a whole line matching once, and the patched pollPad and the new check both
compiled under JScript. The existing pad checks 13.48 and 13.51 hold X for a few frames over one box or a refused one, so the
latch never ends a search inside their holds and they read as before. A JScript model of the latch and the search with the check's own sequence gave, on
the old code, the second wreck at 0.50 of its search with no hold, and on the new code the second wreck untouched with the hold
at 0.50 s; both controls passed on both.

Not verified: check 15.31 has not been run in a browser, and no real controller has held X over two wrecks in a live ring.
