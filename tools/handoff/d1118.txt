## v11.18 - NO INTERIOR WALL ENDS INSIDE A DOORWAY

The map generation fault v11.16 found while walking THE COLD MILE, and closed.

**MEASURED FIRST, on v11.17 at seed 4242.** A spine corridor wall runs from
side wall to side wall, an L split runs from top wall to bottom, and an alcove
hangs off a wall at a rolled height, and none of the three plans ever knew where
the shell had put its doors. A door is touched when a partition reaches into its
opening zone, the gap grown 40 units through the wall, and dead when that
partition leaves under 30 units on both sides of itself. **COLD STORAGE: 3 of 37
doors touched, none dead. THE COLD MILE: 19 of 151 touched, 4 dead**, among them
the door at 2960,3167 that v11.16 traced, 26 units above the wall and 22 below.
A dead door is a door nothing 30 wide can use, and every route was already
being planned round it.

**THE FIX.** Every partition that reaches into a door's zone on its own building,
the gap grown 30 along the wall and 40 through it, has that part cut away, so
the door opens onto floor. A cut in the middle of a partition splits it in two,
each half a clone that keeps every tag the wall had, and an end shorter than 3
units is dropped.

**MY FIRST CUT MOVED THE WORLD, AND THE CHECK SAID SO.** I ran the trim inside
makeBuilding, before anything was placed, and the mile's 374 entities became
369: crawlers are counted into the rooms the partitions make, and a corridor
wall cut back from a door is a corridor joined to a room. The trim now runs at
the END of the map build, after every roll, where the ruin pass runs, so
placement sees the walls it always saw and only the finished geometry changes.
Entities 85 and 374 both ways, containers 165 and 593 both ways, furniture
identical both ways because it was placed before the cut.

**MEASURED AFTER:** touched 3 to 0 and 19 to 0, dead 0 to 0 and 4 to 0. Walls
552 to 551 and 2206 to 2205, one segment a map net, and v9.81 re-pins to that
with the reason in its text. Lamps 59 to 58 and 265 to 262, since the lamp pass
asks the finished walls where it may stand; the sky for the seed did not roll
this time.

**NEW CHECK v11.18** surveys every door on both maps with the rule on and
requires no door touched and none dead. Controls: entities and containers must
be identical with the rule off, or the cut drew a random number, which is the
control that caught my first cut; and with the rule off the mile must still
show at least 3 dead doors and 12 touched, or the dial does not restore the old
partitions. v11.16's old arm switches this rule off as well as its own, because
the partition it stood the crawler against is the one this build cuts back. On a
v11.17 fixture the finding fires for both maps.

Not verified: the extract rate, still. And the cut is judged by geometry and by
routes, not by eye: a corridor that now opens at its end next to a door reads
differently from one that met the wall, and no picture was taken. partDoor 0
restores the old partitions.
