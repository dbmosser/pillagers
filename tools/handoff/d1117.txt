## v11.17 - THE DOORWAYS INSIDE BUILDINGS ARE CLEAR OF FURNITURE TOO

The fifth build on machines getting out of buildings, and the first about
getting INTO them. v11.14 kept furniture out of the shell doorways and nothing
in the game knew that the plans cut doorways in the partitions as well.

**MEASURED FIRST, on v11.16 at seed 4242.** A building is sealed inside when no
route on the route grid runs from just inside any of its own front doors to its
middle. **COLD STORAGE 0 of 20. THE COLD MILE 5 of 84: buildings 32, 33, 37, 38
and 74**, every one of them open with the furniture stripped from the wall list,
and two of them shut to the player's own padding as well, so those rooms and
what was placed in them could be seen and never reached, by him or by anything
hunting him.

**TWO CAUSES, TRACED.** Four of the five are furniture parked in a partition
doorway, the gap a spine, a strongroom or an L split cuts in an interior wall,
which v11.14's rule never saw because only the shell doors were recorded. The
fifth, building 74, is a free standing piece that landed between the ends of two
alcove partitions, 8 units below one and 14 beside the other, and split the
building in two for anything wider than 8. The sealed room repair pass never saw
either, because it floods a grid with no body in it and an 8 unit sliver reads
as a corridor there.

**THE FIX, two rules, neither drawing a random number.** The plans now record
the doorways they cut, the way the shell records its doors, and furniture keeps
the same zone clear of them. And a piece that would leave a gap between 2.5 and
30 units to any wall or partition of its building is not placed: flush is fine,
overlapping is as it was, a body's width or more is fine, and the band between is
where a room gets sealed. Furniture against other furniture is left alone,
because a sliver between two pieces is the v10.40 niche, cosmetic, and cannot
close a room on its own.

**MY TWO WRONG CUTS, both caught by measuring before shipping.** The gap rule
first read a one unit gap as a gap, and the hug places every piece one unit off
its wall on purpose: 136 pieces became 9 and 440 became 33. Touching is under
2.5 now. Then, counting every neighbouring piece as well as every wall, it still
cost three pieces in ten, 136 to 96 and 440 to 310, and he asked for MORE
furniture, not less. Walls and partitions only is what ships.

**MEASURED AFTER, both maps, the dials off against on:** sealed interiors 5 to 0
on the mile and 0 to 0 on COLD STORAGE. Entities 85 and 374 both ways,
containers 165 and 593 both ways. **Furniture 136 to 108 and 440 to 336**, about
one piece in four, each of them either in a doorway or wedged where a body could
not pass it. Walls 580 to 552 and 2308 to 2206, mile lamps 265 to 269, and so the
sky for seed 4242 rolled again, COLD STORAGE back to noon in fog and the mile to
morning fog, for the reason v11.14 wrote down. The crawler in the middle of
building 32 on the mile, whose front room was sealed off, now walks out and
reaches a player outside inside 60 units; with the rules off it never leaves.

**NEW CHECK v11.17** surveys both maps with the rules on and requires zero
sealed interiors, the target being the nearest open route cell to each middle
rather than the exact centre, which can sit inside a table's padding. Controls:
the map must record interior doorways at all; entities and containers must be
identical with the rules off; the rules off must still seal at least 4 on the
mile; and the building 32 crawler must reach a player outside with the rules on
and never leave with them off. On a v11.16 fixture the finding fires for the
five and the map records no interior doorways.

**THREE OLDER CHECKS MOVED WITH IT, each saying why in its own text.** v9.81 re-pins
the wall count to 2206 and 552 and v9.65 the mile cover to 490, the way they
followed v11.14. And v11.14 itself: its dial-off arm is all three furniture
rules off now, because the gap rule alone clears a doorway piece, so its plugged
count control reads the old placement again; and its furniture floor, which
now spans the three rules together, sits at a half against the 0.64 and 0.62
measured.

Not verified: the extract rate, still, and what a quarter fewer pieces looks
like to his eye; every one removed was in a doorway or in a gap a body could not
pass, and no picture was taken. furnIDoor 0 and furnGap 0 restore the old
placement piece for piece.
