v12.70: a search contract names a place on the sector you are playing

A district contract names a place, and it worked that name out once, when the
card was written, against whichever sector was selected at that moment. The
sentence was then frozen. The sector can be changed afterwards with one click,
and nothing on the board notices.

The two sectors have places whose names are one letter apart. Roll the card on
one and read it on the other, and it sends you to almost the right name in the
far corner from the district it actually wants. You search it out and the
counter does not move, because the containers there carry a different district
number. The panel in the raid repeats the same wrong place.

The card itself was never broken: the district number on it is right and it
still ticks on the right containers. Only the label was stale. So the fix is not
to store a sector on the card, since contracts deliberately stopped carrying
one; the sentence is simply worked out on the way to the screen, and follows the
sector you are playing.

Check 12.70 writes a card naming a place that does not exist on the sector it
will be read on, taking the names off the maps themselves; controls require the
sentence to follow back and other card types to be untouched; fails on v12.69.
