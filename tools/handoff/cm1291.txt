v12.91: the left stick crawls when you are down

Finding 11 of the 2026-09-11 audit. The downed screen asks you to do one thing, and
on a controller that one thing was the only thing you could not do.

The downed branch built its movement vector from keyboard codes alone. The stick
lives in PAD.mx and PAD.my, which every other movement site reads: standing, the
roll direction, and the Undercroft walk. This branch was the one place it was never
added.

The pad was not dead while downed, which made it worse: D-DOWN self-revived and X
extracted, and the stick still swung the aim cursor, so you turned on the spot and
nothing on screen suggested the controller had stopped being read.

The fix is the same one-line override the other sites use, before the magnitude, so
the flat 30/s crawl is untouched. An input, not a speed.

The first version of the check was wrong and it is recorded: poking PAD.mx proves
nothing, because pollPad zeroes it every frame with no gamepad present, so the check
went red on the FIXED build for its own reason. It now stubs navigator.getGamepads
with one connected pad and the whole real chain runs.

Check 12.91 crawls all four directions on the keys and on the stick. The keyboard
arm says whether the way is open, so a wall cannot fail this for the map, and it
doubles as the control that the keyboard still works with a pad connected. Three
arms fail on v12.90 at 0 units against 15.
