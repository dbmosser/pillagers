v11.08: the sound key teaches the colours the game actually draws

His question: "sometimes my shots make a red noise circle when they hit
something, sometimes they don't --whats up with that?"

Measured, one pistol round into one sentry at 160 units with its return fire held
off: an idle machine that survives gives one ring and it is the machine MOVING;
an awake machine that survives, the same; a machine killed outright gives NO RING
AT ALL.

The ring is not his hit. A landed round pings nothing. The ring that follows is
the thing he hit taking a step, and a target killed outright never takes it. His
own noises have drawn no ring since v5.31. So no ring after a hit means the thing
is dead.

A probe of mine nearly became a finding: the first trial showed zero damage,
which looked like a patrolling machine ignoring a bullet. Run a third time, an
idle machine took the hit at frame 5 like the others. It was the first shot after
a deploy not landing inside my window.

The thing meant to teach that rule was wrong twice. The sound key carried a cream
swatch labelled "you", for a ring that cannot exist because ping refuses to draw
the player's own noises, and #e65100 for pillager firing, an orange the game
stopped drawing at v9.63 on his own one-word instruction: RED.

The key is now built from the colour table rather than typed out beside it, so a
swatch cannot drift from what is drawn again. The impossible row becomes "never
your own", which answers his question where he would ask it.

New check v11.08 requires every swatch to be a colour the game really draws,
cross-referenced against the table itself, forbids a row saying a ring can be
him, and requires the rule to be stated. Controls: at least four colours in the
table, all four dangerous cases still explained accepting either wording, and the
legend still drawing without throwing. Fails on a v11.07 fixture.

Not verified: whether he wants the rule changed rather than stated. A landed hit
plays a sound and draws no ring, which is arguably a hole in a visualiser
documented as mirroring what you hear, but changing it puts a new ring on every
landed shot and that is his call.
