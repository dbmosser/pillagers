## v13.90 - AN ISSUED BANDAGE PICKED BACK UP IS STILL ISSUED

The searching and loot audit of 2026-09-14, finding 6, and an edge of my own v13.67. Since
v13.67 the issued Bandage count falls whenever the backpack holds fewer Bandages, and a rise
counts as a find. So:

- Drop both issued Bandages from the backpack. The count falls to zero.
- Search both piles back up. The backpack rises by two, which counts as two finds.
- The stall then bought them (v13.71 kept only issued ones), and the extraction banked them.

THE BUILD. Dropping an issued Bandage takes it off the issued count at the drop, and off the
count the frame tick last saw so it is not taken off twice, and marks its pile. Searching that
pile back up puts it back on the issued count. A found Bandage dropped and picked up stays
found.

MEASURED. Check 13.90 drops both issued Bandages, searches both piles back up, and requires the
issued count back at two. A found Bandage dropped and picked up must stay found (the guard). It
fails on v13.89.
