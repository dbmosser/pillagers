v12.36: whoever killed you killed you

Dying does not end a raid at once. It zeroes your health, clears the downed
flag, opens a beat of a second and a half and leaves your body in the world,
and the frame loop keeps running the machines and the bullets underneath it.
The damage path had no dead test at all, so a round landing on the corpse
walked past the downed branch and fell into the down path: a second DOWN
toast over the death fade, another down added to your record, and the killer
renamed, which is the name the KILLED IN ACTION card reads out while the
ledger beside it still names the machine that actually did it.

One guard at the very top of the damage path, which is where it has to be,
because the direction stamp, the hurt stamp and the name are all written
before the invulnerability test. Nothing measurable moves: the bot ends a sim
raid outright so the beat never exists there, and the round still sparks,
still counts and is still consumed.

Check 12.36 downs him with a real round, kills him with a second, then lands
a third on the body inside the beat through the real frame loop, fired by a
machine with a name no map uses, and requires the downs figure, the downed
flag, the toast and the killer name to be unchanged and the card to name the
real killer; fails on v12.35.
