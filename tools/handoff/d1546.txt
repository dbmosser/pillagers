## v15.46 - REVIVING A PILLAGER NEVER LOSES THE GUN IN HIS HANDS

Bodies audit finding (MEDIUM).

Pick a downed pillager up with E and he pays you out of his own kit: the payout closure in updatePlayer takes the first gun_ item in
his pack, on the belief written above it that this is the gun in his hands, and then sets downRdr.paidRevive=1 whatever it paid. The
death path adds the held gun to the body only when paidRevive is unset (and the pack does not already carry it), so the flag was
written to stop a revive-then-kill paying the same gun twice. The belief does not hold for an elite, ever: mkRaider pushes the gun
he spawned with (pistol, SMG, rifle, carbine or scattergun) into his pack first, and blessElite then puts a Marksman Rifle, Support
MG, Magnum, Whisper, Longshot or Meridian Lance in his hands without touching the pack. The two lists share nothing. So an elite
holding a Marksman Rifle, picked up, said he puts his Scav Pistol (field) in your hands, the flag went on, and when he died later
(he turns on you, or a machine gets him) his body held no Marksman Rifle. The gun you watched him fire was never paid and never
dropped. Elites are on by default (eliteRate 0.08, eliteGuns 1, raiderDown 1). An elite who swapped his blessed gun away and an
imported friend (applyGhost swaps e.wep and leaves the pack) reach the same mismatch.

THE BUILD. One line in the revive payout closure in updatePlayer, under the v9.10 THE BODY MUST NOT PAY IT AGAIN comment:
  if(_rpk==='gun_'+(downRdr.wep&&downRdr.wep.id)) downRdr.paidRevive=1;
The flag goes on only when the gun paid is the gun in his hands. Paid from the pack, or from his hands by the empty-pack fallback:
flag set, so that gun still pays once and a second revive still pays nothing from his hands (v13.88). A different gun paid, or a
non-gun item: no flag, so the death path puts the gun in his hands on his body, and its own indexOf test still stops a duplicate
when the pack carries a copy. A man with no gun in his hands matches nothing. This is the finding's fix as written; the verdict
found it correct and gave no correctedFix. No player text, no number, no dial, no loot table and no seeded draw moved.

OLDER CHECKS. None needed restaging. Check 9.10 revives and kills the first pillager in the seed 4242 raid and fails only if the
gun paid is also on the body; with the fix a different gun in his hands can reach the body, never the one paid. Check 13.88
revives an empty-packed SMG holder twice: the fallback pays the SMG in his hands, which matches, so the flag is set and the second
revive pays nothing, as before. Check 15.07 pays a Medkit and a Frag Charge and never kills the man. Check 11.64 reads only the kill
mark. No older check reads a body after a revive that paid a gun other than the one in his hands.
