## v15.04 - BUYING THE LIMITED TIME OFFER IS NOT LOGGED AS A GAMBLE ROLL

Wirt audit finding (LOW). THE GAMBLE lists the last eight rolls from P.gambleLog, or "Nothing rolled yet. $2,500 per roll." when
there are none. The Buy handler of the Limited Time Offer (renderWirtLot) banked the whole lot, then pushed the lot's first item
onto that same log. Steps: a profile with at least $10,000 and no rolls; THE GAMBLE reads "Nothing rolled yet." Buy the Limited
Time Offer. THE GAMBLE now lists the lot's headline gun and its value as if a $2,500 roll had produced it, and the rest of the lot
(the plate and medkit that come with the DMR or LMG lot, for example) is not listed. He never rolled, and a $10,000 purchase sits
in the roll history.

THE BUILD. The two lines that pushed the lot onto P.gambleLog, and capped it at 40, are gone from the Buy handler. The Gamble
button's own push still records every real roll, and nothing else reads the log. No player text changes and no price, odds or
pool moves. Lot entries already saved in a profile's history stay until eight real rolls push them off the list.

MEASURED. Check 15.04 cleans the profile, empties the roll history and the stash, clears the bought mark, sets credits to one lot
plus one roll and draws the panel. Controls: no roll row is listed and the Buy button is live before the click; after the click the
price was paid and the lot was banked. It requires the roll history still empty and no roll row under THE GAMBLE. It then rolls once
with the Gamble button and requires exactly that item logged and listed, so real rolls still record. It silences Wirt's line, puts
back the seeded stream the roll spent, and restores the profile through the loader. It fails on v15.03: the history holds the lot's
headline item and THE GAMBLE lists it as a roll.

Not verified: THE GAMBLE panel after a real purchase, by eye.
