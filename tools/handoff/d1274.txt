## v12.74 - THE PEDDLER STALL KNOWS WHAT IT JUST PAID YOU

From the 2026-09-08 first-hour audit, confirmed by a skeptic and then by me
reading the sale, the panel and the purchase.

WHAT IS NOT WRONG, AND IS NOT CHANGED. Selling at the stall does not pay in
Credits. The money rides on the run, is banked only if he walks out, and dies
where he falls. That is a deliberate decision with a comment saying so, and it
stays. Whether stall money ought to be spendable at the stall is his call, not
mine, and nothing here changes what he can buy with.

WHAT IS WRONG. The panel does not know any of that. The balance line on the stall
reads his BANKED Credits, and so does the affordability of every row of the
stock. So a first-time player standing at zero sells seven things for four
thousand, is told exactly that by the game, and one line further down the same
panel, in the same frame, reads that he holds nothing, with every item on the
shelf greyed out. Two numbers about the same pocket, disagreeing, six pixels
apart.

Then he presses a key to buy a plate and is told he cannot cover it, which reads
as being broke one second after being paid.

THE BUILD. The panel names both pockets: what is banked, and what is on him and
not banked yet. The refusal says which one it means. That is all. The arithmetic,
the rate, what he can afford and what the stall will sell him are untouched.

AND ONE WORD. The sale line said cash. This game says Credits.

MEASURED. Check 12.74 opens the stall in a real raid, makes a real sale through
the real function, and then reads the panel the way he does, by watching what the
frame actually writes to the canvas: the money it just paid him has to appear
there. It presses buy and reads the refusal off the message line. Then it runs
the whole thing again carrying nothing and requires the plain wording back, so a
build that simply always printed the longer sentence would fail.

Not verified: it reads the strings the frame writes rather than pixels, so it
proves the line is drawn and not that it fits the panel; it does not check the
greyed colour of the stock rows, only the words; and it does not decide whether
carried money should be spendable, which is his to answer.
