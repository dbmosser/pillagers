## v26.22 - MUSIC AT THE END OF A RAID

Co-op hunt while he was away. His order: a short piece of music when a raid ends well and another when it ends badly. EXTRACTED plays a rising piece of about five and a half seconds: a low heartbeat that climbs under D minor, F, G minor and A with the bass walking up, then a hit and a bright D major with a shimmer above it that rings out. KILLED IN ACTION plays a dull hit over a bare low drone that fades, with a slow line falling over it and a thin D minor under the last note. In the DEMO the same two play on the RUN OVER card (a run that ends on an extraction gets the rising one, a death or the burn the falling one), and in a two player pair each window plays its own: the second window hears the ending the host run reached. An abandon plays nothing, because backing out is not a result to score and a tune for a choice would sound wrong either way. This is not raid music: nothing plays while a raid is live (a piece still ringing is cut the moment the next raid goes up), and the title music waits until the piece has rung out, so the two never overlap. They ride the Music path, so the Music and Master volume and the Music OFF setting silence them, and a window with its sound off stays silent. The sounds are made in code, with no seeded draws and nothing under the sim.
