## v13.68 - YOUR HIRE SHOOTS THE PILLAGERS, NOT YOU

The hire and peddler audit of 2026-09-14, finding 1, and the standing raid audit line about a
merc that never shoots raiders. Since v8.24, mercEngage aims the hire at hostile pillagers and
fires on the enemy bullet path. That path had two faults:

- It tested the player first, with no look at who fired. A round from your own hire crossing
  your position hit you, was logged under his name, and could down you.
- A pillager is only hit by another pillager's round when feudFoe allows it, and feudFoe
  refuses whenever either side is a hire. Every round he fired passed through the man he was
  aiming at and kept flying, often into you.

THE BUILD. A round your hire fired passes through you, the same rule as your rounds passing
through him since v6.72. It hits a hostile pillager, using the same test his target picker
already uses. Machines were always hit and still are.

MEASURED. Check 13.68 stages the hire, a hostile pillager 150 units along a clear line, and you
65 units beyond him. Ten rounds from the hire must hurt the pillager and leave you at full
health. Ten rounds from the pillager at you must hit you (the control). It fails on v13.67.

Not verified: how much more the hire now kills, and whether pillagers answering his fire
change the feel of a raid with him along.
