v11.02: why changing save leaves fullscreen, said in the game

His question: "if i click fullscreen and then click to change the save, it kicks
me out of fullscreen --why??"

Because clicking another save writes the slot pointer and reloads the page. The
profile key is read once at file scope and the whole game is built from it. Every
browser drops fullscreen on a page load, and it cannot be re-entered on its own,
because requestFullscreen needs a user gesture and a gesture does not survive a
navigation.

Two honest fixes: switch saves without reloading, which means re-reading the
profile in place and re-initialising every panel built from it, a deep change I
am not making 55 hours from an alpha; or stop it being a mystery and put the way
back one click away. This is the second and it says so.

There is now a permanent line under the saves: changing save reloads the game,
which is why it leaves fullscreen. And if he was in fullscreen when he switched,
the title screen tells him on the way back in with GO FULLSCREEN directly above
it. The flag has its own storage key, never the profile, because it belongs to
the tab and not to a character and has to survive a switch between two saves. It
is read once and cleared once.

New check v11.02 requires the panel to say both reload and fullscreen; requires
switching in fullscreen to be remembered and switching outside it not to be;
requires the line to appear once, clear its own flag, and be gone next boot; and
controls that it never fires while he is already in fullscreen. Fullscreen cannot
be entered by a check, so the check makes the game believe it is and puts the
real answer back afterwards. Fails on a v11.01 fixture on both halves.

A check bug of mine, fixed in the same build: the missing-memory guard used a
return and threw away the panel finding already recorded, so the old build
reported half the defect.
