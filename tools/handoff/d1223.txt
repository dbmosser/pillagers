## v12.23 - GOING DOWN LETS GO OF THE GRENADE

From the 2026-09-06 read-only in-raid audit (P2 WRONG STATE: going down
mid-cook freezes the fuse for the whole bleed-out), verified by reading at
v12.19.

WHAT IT WAS. damagePlayer's down branch cleared the heal in progress, the
pull on the boarding ring and the thing being prepared (p.prep), and left
the cook alone: p.cooking, p.cookT and p.cookKind rode into the bleed-out
untouched. The cook clock and the release both sit past the downed return
in updatePlayer, so nothing advanced the fuse and nothing let go of it. The
COOKING clock, drawn over the player whenever something is cooking, stayed
painted over the man on the floor with the same number for the whole
bleed-out, and the one self-revive stood him up still holding the grenade,
fuse half burned, the clock running again from where it froze. killPlayer
has dropped a cook on a death since the pin-out rule was written; the down
never did. He has died to his own grenade twice in his exports; this is
one more way to.

THE BUILD. A man going down lets go of what is in his hand. The pin is out,
so the grenade goes the way a release sends it, the same verb as letting go
of the button: toward the aim, stopping at the first wall, carrying the time
it has cooked. Smoke and decoy have no fuse and leave the hand the same way.
One line in the down branch, before the fall is recorded, so the throw is
made by a man still standing.

MEASURED. Check 12.23 deploys, puts a frag in the pouch, selects it, aims
200 units east, pulls the pin and burns half a second, then lands a real hit
that takes him to the floor. It requires him down, nothing cooking, exactly
one throw added and that throw a frag carrying 0.5 s of cook, no text
beginning COOKING painted by the frame or the HUD over him, and a
self-revive that stands him up empty-handed. Control: the same hit with
nothing in hand adds no throw. On the v12.22 fixture he goes down still
cooking, no throw leaves his hand, the COOKING clock is painted, and he gets
up holding it.

Not verified: his own eye on the throw leaving a falling man; the flight of
that throw, which is the ordinary throw path; a death (not a down) while
cooking, which killPlayer has always dropped silently and still does.
