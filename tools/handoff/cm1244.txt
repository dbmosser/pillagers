v12.44: the boarding window never outlives the clock

The comment two lines above the boarding window says the hold can never promise
more than the raid clock has left, because saying thirty and then killing him on
the timer at twelve is a lie. A floor of three seconds sat underneath it and
broke that promise at the very end of a raid.

Call the extraction with 27 seconds left. No warning is drawn, because the
warning only fires under 25. The ship lands with 2 seconds on the clock, the
floor sets the window to 3, and the banner, the ring badge, the pad prompt and
the voice line all say three. Two seconds later the raid ends as a death by the
timer with a second still showing on all four.

One clamp under the floor: if the window would outlive the clock, it is cut to
the clock. Half a second is kept underneath so the window is never a zero that
reads as no window at all. If that is all the raid has left, that is the truth.

The clock-off defect in the same expression was closed at v12.24. This is the
live-clock sibling.

Check 12.44 lands a ship by running the real extraction ticker one step past
the end of the beacon with two seconds on a live clock, reads the number and
the words on the ring badge, and controls that a full clock and a clock
switched off both still give the ordinary thirty; fails on v12.43.
