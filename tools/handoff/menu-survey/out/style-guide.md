## 5. motion (time-based, never frame-counted; his q09, q10, q28, q32)
- Window or dialog OPEN 280 ms: opacity 0 to 1 linearly in the first 120 ms; scale .92 at 0, 1.02 at 55% (ease-out cubic (.215,.61,.355,1) between), 1.00 at 100% (ease-in-out (.645,.045,.355,1) between); scrim 0 to 55% over 200 ms (his q09 picture ran the scrim to 60%; the darkness he chose in q07 is 55%, so it ends at 55%; the blurred backdrop cross-fades in with it). CLOSE 140 ms (.4,0,1,1): scale 1.00 to .96, opacity to 0, scrim to 0. No rise, no stagger on open.
- HIGHLIGHT (focus bar, wash, ring) moves as an under-damped spring: omega 30 rad/s, damping ratio .70 (x(t) = 1 - e^(-21t)(cos 21.4t + .98 sin 21.4t)); reaches the target at 110 ms, peaks 4.6% past it at 147 ms, within 2% at 200 ms. Cap the visible overshoot at 8px (for steps over 170px use damping .85). Retarget from the current position and speed. The focused row text brightens 70 to 100% in 90 ms (the rest back to 70%). Combat belt selection does NOT spring (it snaps with a 90 ms scale).
- RESULT CARDS, COUNT-UPS, BUTTON PRESSES: ease-out-back c1 1.2: f(t) = 1 + 2.2(t-1)^3 + 1.2(t-1)^2, peak 1.053 (cubic-bezier(.34,1.4,.64,1)). Card arrives in 240 ms (rise 48px, scale .92 to 1.00, opacity over the first 40%); the score counts up over 900 ms starting 120 ms after the card and pops 1 to 1.06 to 1 in 160 ms at the end; a button press goes 1 to .96 and back to 1.00 in 140 ms. Opacity never overshoots.
- TAB SWITCH (stash filters, shop/craft/hire, controls tabs, party tabs): outgoing rows go in 80 ms (opacity to 0, no movement); incoming rows rise from +12px, 160 ms each, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), each starting 25 ms after the row above (4 rows done at 235 ms, 8 rows at 335 ms); the underline glides x += (target - x)(1 - exp(-dt/.035)), no overshoot. Settings chips jump-scroll the page (250 ms ease-out), no row rise.
- Feed plates slide in from the left in 160 ms (.2,.8,.2,1). Pills drop 12px and fade in 160 ms. Loot tiles in the haul reveal pop in 40 ms apart.
- A press mid-animation retargets from the current value; never ignored. Never over 6% overshoot (the spring peaks 4.6%, cards 5.3%, the menu pop 2%).
- Never static: slow drift of the live backdrop on title screens; new items one 600 ms glimmer sweep.
