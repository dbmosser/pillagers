## v12.61 - QUICK ASCENT STARTS AT DAY LIKE THE OTHER DOOR DOES

From the 2026-09-08 read-only audit, confirmed by a skeptic against the source.
Verified by reading at v12.60.

WHAT IT WAS. His answer 24 is day by default, every ascent. The line that keeps
that promise lives inside the lift's E act, the one that opens the sector page,
and nowhere else in the game.

The lift has two doors. E opens the page. R is quick ascent, and the floor's own
sign advertises it. So pick night on the page, ascend, come back down, and press
R: the surface is still night, because nothing on that path ever names day. The
ground palette, the lamps, the night XP multiplier and the night flag on the run
record all follow it, and nothing on screen says a word before the raid loads. A
night you chose once follows you up every time until you happen to open the page
again.

v10.05 shipped that promise and its check drives the E door only, which is why
this has been true underneath a green check ever since.

THE BUILD. One named reset, used by both doors, so they cannot drift apart
again. The E act calls it where its two lines used to be, and quick ascent calls
it before it commits the kit.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO. It does not take the choice away. A night you pick on the
page and ascend from the page is still night, because the page's own ascend runs
after your choice rather than before it. The default coming back is the point;
overriding you would be a different and worse bug, and the check's third arm is
there to catch me doing it.

IT COSTS NOTHING ANYWHERE IT CAN BE MEASURED. The bot never uses the lift, and
no dial moves.

MEASURED. Check 12.61 drives both doors through the real floor update rather
than calling their actions directly. It sets the surface to night, presses the
quick ascent key, and requires the raid to come up at day. The first control
presses the page key instead and requires the same, so this build cannot pass by
breaking what v10.05 fixed. The second control is the one that matters: a night
chosen and ascended from the page must still be night.

Not verified: the keys are set rather than sent as keyboard events, which the
floor reads the same way; it does not read the ground palette or the lamps, only
the flag they all follow; and it does not cover the restore code path, which
writes the same field from a pasted save.
