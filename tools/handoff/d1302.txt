## v13.02 - THE GAME NOW CHECKS THAT THE WORDS HE WROTE STILL APPEAR

The guard for what v13.01 caught by hand. Nothing in this build changes the game; it
gives the harness one thing it did not have, which is the ability to see his edits.

THE PROBLEM IT CLOSES. His wording is applied by exact match on a whole rendered
sentence. Reword that sentence anywhere and his version stops appearing, silently: no
error, no log line, nothing red. v12.98 did it to the bar blurb and only a hand sweep
found it at v13.01. There was nothing standing between the next reworded line and the
same silent loss.

WHY A SOURCE SEARCH CANNOT DO THIS JOB. Most of these lines are assembled at run time
out of pieces, so the finished sentence exists only once a panel has been drawn. Two
attempts at a source sweep during this investigation gave 4 false hits and then 40,
because the search either found the map's own keys or could not find a sentence that
does not exist until it is built. The only honest instrument is to draw the panels and
read them.

WHAT THE HARNESS NEEDED. Opening a panel means finding its element, adding the class
and calling its own renderer, and the renderers live inside the closure where a check
can reach them and nothing else can. So the build adds one table naming the panels
that can be drawn without a raid and the renderer for each. The table draws nothing by
itself.

THE RULE THE CHECK ASSERTS NAMES NO LINE. If his edit is landing, the ORIGINAL
sentence can never be on screen, because his replacement is there instead. So any raw
key visible on a drawn panel, with his replacement absent from that panel, is an edit
that has stopped working. That holds for every line he has ever edited and every line
he edits next, without the check knowing any of them, so it cannot go stale.

HIS REPLACEMENT MUST BE ABSENT TOO, AND THAT IS NOT A DETAIL. Several of his edits
keep the original sentence and add to it, so the raw text is a prefix of his own
replacement and a naive search would flag those every run. The pair is the test:
original present AND replacement missing.

TWO CONTROLS, because this is a sweep and a sweep that sees nothing reports nothing.
At least one panel must have drawn text, and at least one of his replacements must
have been found across those panels. Without both, its silence would mean the same on
a build that had lost every edit he ever made.

MEASURED. Six panels drawn: the bar, Settings, the shop, the terms, the carry screen
and Wirt. No raw key visible on any of them, and his wording found. On the v13.01
fixture the check fails outright, because the table it walks does not exist there.

Not verified: six panels is not every screen. The Undercroft floor, the raid HUD, the
outcome card and the Mainframe are not in the table, so an edit of his on those is
still unguarded; the table is the place to add them and each one needs a way to be
drawn without a live raid.
