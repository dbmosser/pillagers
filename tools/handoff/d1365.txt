## v13.65 - A MEDKIT ON ITS OWN BELT KEY IS NOT REFUSED AS ALREADY HEALING

The in-raid audit of 2026-09-14, finding 4. Since v12.04 useMedical has measured what he can
reach as the lower of health plus the queue and the running heal's ceiling. So two Bandages
queued at 50 health, which stop at 85, still leave room for a Medkit. The named-slot path in
useHot, which a Medkit dragged onto its own belt key takes, tested health plus queue alone
against full health. The same Medkit on its own key was refused as Already healing while the
Medical cell beside it used it.

THE BUILD. The named-slot path uses the same reach as useMedical. Two ways in, one rule.

MEASURED. Check 13.65 binds a Medkit to the last belt key, queues 60 of Bandage heal at 50
health under an 85 ceiling, and requires the key to use the Medkit. At 50 health with nothing
running, the same key must use it (the control). It fails on v13.64.

Not verified: the belt cell's own label while the refusal was showing.
