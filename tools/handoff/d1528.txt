## v15.28 - A GRENADE A PILLAGER THROWS SOUNDS WHERE IT LANDS

Sound audit finding (HIGH). A pillager carrying a Frag Charge throws it from 242 to 520 units away (at the default blast
radius of 190). His charge does not fly: raiderThrow puts it straight into the frag list within 26 units of his target, with
the 1.1 second fuse. The only sound was sfx('clank') at his own hand, a quiet triangle blip (gain 0.03) that at those distances plays
at about three quarters down to two fifths of that, and the same clank every bullet makes hitting a wall, plus smoke, decoys and
dropped items. Nothing sounded where the charge lay until the boom, and on screen it is a small blinking dot. In a firefight
he could not hear a grenade land beside him or tell it from bullets on the walls.

THE BUILD. The clank at his hand stays, and the rising charge warning now plays at the charge itself. It is the sound the game
already uses for "about to hit you here": a machine winds up with it and lightning marks its strike with it. It plays at the
exact spot the charge lies, not at the target, which can be up to about 37 units away. The two scatter draws are taken in the
same order as before (x, then y), so the seeded stream does not move, and sfx draws nothing from it (blip uses Math.random).
It stays out of the sim like every other sound. As a positioned sound it also leaves the usual noise ring when the charge lands
somewhere he cannot see, behind him or past a wall. No dial, number or damage changes.

MEASURED. Check 15.28 deploys a raid, hands a healthy long-armed pillager one charge, stands him 342 units east of the player
(the blast radius plus 152) and pins the two scatter draws to 0.125 then 0.875, so the charge must land 19.5 west and 19.5
south of the player. Controls: he throws exactly one live charge of his own, the pinned draws are taken, and the sound recorder
hears the clank at his hand. Then the charge must land at exactly that spot (two draws, in order) and a charge warning must
play within half a unit of it. On v15.27 it fails: the charge lands 27.6 units away with a 1.1 second fuse and the only sound is
the clank 342 units away. A JScript run of the check against the old and new raiderThrow with a stand-in raid gave that failure
on v15.27 and a pass on the build; a warning at the target instead of the charge fails (27.6 units off), and a swapped draw order
fails. Every change it makes (the draw and sound stubs, the pillager, the new charge, the raid) is put back in finally. Dry run
on scratch copies: p1528 and f1528 applied after p1527 and f1527, every anchor a whole line matching once.

Not verified: a live raid by ear, a pillager throwing a charge at the player in a firefight.
