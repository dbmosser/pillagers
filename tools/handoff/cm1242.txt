v12.42: a gun you stow keeps its rounds

Loaded rounds live in exactly two places, the gun in your hands and the gun on
your back, and the backpack holds a bare key string with nowhere for a number.
So a gun that went into the backpack left its magazine nowhere at all: the
loaded rounds simply stopped existing. Taking it back out handed it half a
magazine conjured from nothing, which no reserve paid for. The same trip is a
loss one way and a gift the other, and it repeats as often as you like.

Three doors stow a gun and all three did it: dragging one off the belt,
equipping a second so the first is displaced, and picking a gun up in the
field.

The rounds now ride with the gun in a queue kept per gun id, which is exactly
as precise as the backpack itself, since it holds keys and not instances. A
gun found in the field has no entry and still comes back on half a magazine.
That is his rule and it does not move.

This one is not free: it closes a faucet and it stops a loss. Both are
corrections of a conservation error rather than a dial, no settings number
changes, and the bot never bags a gun.

Check 12.42 measures loaded plus reserve as one conserved number across a stow
and an equip, staging 47 rounds in a 60 round magazine with an empty reserve,
runs an empty gun as the faucet arm, and controls that a gun never stowed still
arrives on half a magazine; fails on v12.41.
