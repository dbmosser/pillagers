## v12.64 - THE BUY BUTTON SAYS HOW SHORT YOU ARE

From the 2026-09-08 read-only audit, confirmed by a skeptic against the source.
This is my own v8.18 build, and my own check chose the case that works. Verified
by reading at v12.63.

WHAT IT WAS. The buy button on the counter is supposed to say how short you are.
The branch that writes that sentence is gated on the row behind the panel being
ENABLED, and that row disables itself the moment you cannot afford one unit. So
the sentence could only ever appear when you could already afford some of the
order and not all of it.

The commonest refusal at that counter, by a long way, is wanting one thing you
cannot afford at all. In that case the panel shows a greyed BUY with the price
above it and no reason anywhere, while the sentence that explains it sits one
line below in the source, unreachable.

That is exactly the fault the crafting bench was rewritten for at v12.32, on his
own note about the bench doing nothing and saying nothing.

MY v8.18 ENTRY CLAIMS THE BUTTON "SAYS EXACTLY HOW SHORT HE IS". Its own
measurement is a Bandage at 660 with 2,100 in hand: enough for three, so the row
was enabled and the branch did fire. The case that happens most was never
measured, and the entry has read as true ever since.

THE BUILD. Money is the reason unless something else is. A row disabled while
you can afford one unit is locked for its own reason and keeps its own words; a
row you are simply short of the price for now says how short.

IT COSTS NOTHING ANYWHERE IT CAN BE MEASURED. It is a label. Nothing is bought
that could not be bought before, no dial moves, and the bot never opens a
counter.

MEASURED. Check 12.64 reads the DRAWN panel rather than the source, because the
whole finding is that a sentence exists in the source and cannot be reached. The
finding arm asks for one unit with a penny less than the price and requires the
shortfall to be named. The first control is the case v8.18 measured, partly
affordable, which must still say it. The second requires an affordable order not
to be told it is short. The third takes a row locked for want of XP with money in
hand and requires it to keep its own words, so the money line has not been made
to answer for everything.

Not verified: it reads the panel text rather than the pixels, so it cannot say
the label is legible against the disabled colour; it does not click anything;
and it does not cover the pack rows, which have their own tier rule and their
own words.
