## v15.25 - KEY 1 NEVER GOES BLACK WHILE HE HOLDS A GUN

His note, verbatim: "when i pick up a gun from the hotbar in game, the other gun in spot 1 goes black for some reason -- is this
intended behavior". It was not. hotbarSlots builds cells 1 and 2 from the two guns in his hands, and a gun bound to a belt key
that he is holding is drawn on that key as the equipped gun (v6.70). A second pass (v6.70) then blanked any
derived cell showing the same thing as a bound key, and for guns that meant the gun cell showing that gun. Press a key holding a
gun from the backpack with two guns carried: equipFromBag puts it in his hands in place of the gun on cell 1, cell 1 now shows
it, the key shows it too, and the pass turned cell 1 into an Empty slot while he held the gun. With one gun carried the new gun
goes to the empty second slot and comes up, and cell 2 went black instead. A stowed gun bound to a key blanked its cell the same
way, so key 2 answered Empty slot for a gun he was carrying.

THE BUILD. The gun branch of the dedupe pass is removed; heals, plates and throwables keep theirs. Cells 1 and 2 always show the
two guns in his hands. A key he bound to a gun he holds keeps drawing it, so that gun shows twice, which matches the rule that
keys he set never move.

WHAT ELSE LEANED ON THE BLANK CELL, AND IS KEPT. Two loops found the gun in his hands as the first gun cell marked in hand, and
with the derived cell blanked that was always the key he bound:
  gunCell (v12.31, v13.42): the raid starts on the gun cell, and every empty cell says Press N for your gun. With the gun on key 5
  it said Press 5 and the raid started on key 5; without a change it would have become cell 1.
  swapGuns (v8.31): the highlight follows the gun. Pressing key 5 to bring up a stowed gun bound there kept the highlight on key 5;
  without a change it would have jumped to cell 2.
Both now go through heldGunCell, which takes a key he bound holding the gun in his hands first and the first cell holding it
second, so both answer exactly what they answered while the cell was blank. swapGuns also leaves the highlight on the pressed
cell when that cell holds the gun that came up, which is what makes key 2, newly usable for a bound stowed gun, keep its own
highlight. Nothing else reads the blank: the drag code tells the two gun cells apart by gunA and gunB and treats a bound key as
an ordinary belt slot either way (v8.14, v11.97), and the belt draw and the trigger read the cell kind, which is now gun. A drop
of a gun from the backpack onto cell 1 was, while cell 1 was blank, an ordinary binding over the gun slot; it is the weapon slot
drop again (v3.21). Two comments that said the dedupe blanks a gun cell (setHot, autoBelt) are annotated.

TWO CHECKS ASSERTED THE BLANK AND ARE REPAIRED. Check 12.31 required cell 1 to be Empty with the held gun on key 5 as its premise,
and its ARM A clicked the blank cell 1 and required no shot and Press 5. The premise now requires cell 1 to show the gun in his
hands, and ARM A requires a click on cell 1 to fire and keep cell 1 (the same staging as check 13.42 ARM D). Its ARMS B and C are
unchanged and still require Press 5, the key he bound. Check 13.42 ARM E required cell 1 to be Empty as staging; it now requires
key 5 to hold the gun in his hands, and still requires the raid to start on key 5.

MEASURED. Check 15.25 deploys at seed 4242 with a pistol in his hands. ONE: a carbine stowed and the SMG in the backpack on key 3
(controls: cell 1 is the pistol and key 3 the backpack SMG), equipped the way the key does it with equipFromBag (controls: the
SMG is in his hands and key 3 shows it); cell 1 must show the SMG. TWO: one gun carried, key 3 pressed through setHot (control:
the SMG is up and the pistol stowed); cell 2 must show the SMG and the highlight stay on key 3. THREE: the carbine stowed on key 5
(control: key 5 shows it); cell 2 must show the carbine, and key 5 must bring it up (control) with the highlight left on key 5.
FOUR: the same room, and key 2 brings the carbine up with the highlight left on key 2. A control that cannot run after a failure
keeps the failure. It fails on v15.24, where cells 1 and 2 are Empty slots.

Not verified: by eye in a raid; a controller walking the belt with the bumpers; the Undercroft belt, which builds the same cells
