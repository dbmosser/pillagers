## v11.92 - FIRST TEN MINUTES: THE NEW IN CARD FITS, AND A FIRST LAUNCH NEVER SEES IT

From the read-only audit of a brand-new player's first ten minutes
(2026-09-06, eight readers, one screen each). Two of them found this one
independently.

THE FINDING. The NEW IN card on the Undercroft floor carries a comment from
v2.79 that says it is for returning players only and that a profile with no
runs stamps itself current and never sees it. No line ever did that: the
only gate was the once-per-load latch, so a friend arriving cold from the
link pressed ENTER on the title and landed under a list of changes to
controls he had never used. Worse, the list is 137 entries long and grows
every build. The card's two-pass sizing drops one text size when the block
will not fit and then draws all of it anyway, so at 1080p the card was near
five screens tall: its heading and its only dismiss line ("press ENTER or
walk to dismiss") were both thousands of pixels off the canvas, and what
was on screen was an opaque black slab over the whole floor with a middle
slice of patch notes in it. He has been walking through it for weeks
without seeing it for what it was, because the first thing anyone does on
the floor is walk, and walking dismisses it.

THE BUILD. Two lines. A profile with no runs stamps the latch and the card
is never drawn, which is what the comment promised. When the block does not
fit the screen, what does not fit is cut: the list is newest first, so the
cut takes the oldest, and it falls between entries, never inside one, so
the heading, the entries that fit and the dismiss line are always on the
canvas.

MEASURED. Check 11.92 enters the floor at 1920x1080 with the canvas text
call recorded. With runs at 0 it draws two frames and requires no NEW IN
heading and the latch stamped; with runs on the profile it draws one frame
and requires the heading and the dismiss line both inside the canvas and
the newest entry on the card. On the v11.91 fixture the first arm draws the
card and the second arm's heading lands at a negative y.

Not verified: by eye at 4K, where the card is smaller than the screen and
the cut never runs; a player who has runs but had never seen the card
before, who now sees only the newest entries and not the whole history.
