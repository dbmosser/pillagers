## v13.08 - YOU ARE NOT CARRYING A GUN AROUND THE UNDERCROFT

HIS NOTES, 2026-09-12, from live play, two of three:

  "player shouldn't have gun visible in his hand when he's in the undercroft"
  "in undercroft it says 'Compact SMG' FIRE use V signal -- all this text is useless"

ONE CAUSE, TWO SYMPTOMS. The floor reuses the raid's own drawing: the same operator
routine, the same belt strip. Both were handed the raid's arguments down there, so he
walks around his own home holding a gun, under a line offering two actions that do not
exist in the Undercroft. You cannot fire down there and there is nobody to signal.

THE LINE IS KEPT IN A RAID, WHERE IT IS TRUE. He called the text useless where he read
it, and where he read it, it is. Up top those are real actions on a real belt, and
deleting it there would take something he uses.

THE GUN GOES OUT OF HIS HANDS, NOT OUT OF HIS ARMOURY. Nothing about what he owns or
what he goes up with changes. This is only what the figure is holding while he walks
around downstairs, which is nothing. The roll pose is untouched.

MEASURED. Check 13.08 wraps the one routine that draws an operator and records the
weapon and the stance it is handed, because the gun is a few pixels in a hand and
reading the canvas for it would be measuring the art rather than the decision. It then
reads the caption off the frame. The raid arm is the control and it is not decoration:
both of these could have been fixed by deleting them everywhere, which would pass any
check that only looked at the floor and would quietly take two things he uses in a
raid. Three arms fail on the v13.06 fixture.

THE CHECK'S FIRST INSTRUMENT WAS WRONG AND IT IS WORTH RECORDING. It drew the floor
with the world draw alone, which never paints the belt: the belt and the bag are on the
HUD canvas and only the loop puts them there. Measured on the v13.06 fixture: 36
strings and no caption from the world draw, 165 strings and the caption from three loop
frames. The caption arm was asserting nothing until it ran real frames.

Not verified: it reads what the drawing routine is handed rather than the pixels it
produces, so it proves no weapon is passed and not that no weapon-shaped thing is
drawn; and it does not check the character screen, where a gun in hand is correct.
