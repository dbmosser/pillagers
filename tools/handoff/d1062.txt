## v10.62 - THE MACHINES: EACH KIND DIES IN ITS OWN WAY

His note, 2026-09-03 about 19:40: **"guns, footsteps, robots, etc --
everything should sound unique and crisp like a triple-A game"**. The third
family.

### What it was

Nothing on the map died out loud. A kill played a clank, and only when the
body fell more than 620 units away; up close, the most decisive moment in a
fight was silent. A round landing on a machine played the same hit as a
round landing on you, so dealing and taking damage were one sound. The
Bulwark, the armoured heavy, had no entry in the voice table and was silent
apart from its footsteps. And every idle voice, the servo sweep, the drone,
the hydraulics, the dish creak, replayed the same frequencies every time,
so a room of machines was a loop.

### Now

Each kind dies in its own way: a man grunts hard and falls; a crawler
squeals, cut short, and its legs crackle out; a sentry's servo winds down
and it settles with a clank; a snitch drops out of the air in pitch and
pops; a warden or a bulwark lets its hydraulics go and hits the ground
heavily; a listener's dish spins down to a stop. A round landing on plate
ticks bright with a short ping; a round landing on a man is a dull thud
with nothing bright in it. The Bulwark grinds, plate on plate, slow and low
with a click when it stops, faster and twice when it is hunting. And every
machine voice takes six percent of jitter per call, so no two sweeps, drones
or creaks are the same.

### Measured

The check installs a recording audio context, asks the synth for each of
the seven deaths and requires each to schedule something and no two alike
(the warden and the bulwark share one on purpose), requires the plate hit
and the man hit to differ, the Bulwark to have its voice with the hunting
form different from the idle, and two servo sweeps and two dish creaks in a
row to differ. Then, on the play path, it starts a raid at seed 4242, puts
the first crawler or sentry at zero hit points, runs three frames of the
live loop, and requires the frame that culls it to ask for the death sound
of its kind. On v10.57 every death schedules nothing, the two hits are one
sound, the Bulwark has no voice and the sweeps repeat, which is the
finding; on this build the check returns null.

Not verified: how any of it sounds, which is his; the raider going down
rather than dying, which is a separate path with the hit thud and no death
sound, by design since a downed man is not dead; and the mix when several
machines die at once, which the voice budget does not cover because deaths
go through sfx, not the budgeted idle voices.
