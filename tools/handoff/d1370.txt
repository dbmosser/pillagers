## v13.70 - SELL BACKPACK LEAVES YOUR BELT ALONE

The hire and peddler audit of 2026-09-14, finding 3. His v8.78 rule is that an item is on the
tactical belt or in the backpack, never both, and the backpack grid hides the copies the belt
claims. The stall ignored that. SELL BACKPACK counted and sold everything in G.bag, so:

- A Medkit bound to a belt key went for 55 percent, and the key then said No Medkit left.
- A gun bought from him at 190 percent, which the stall puts straight on the belt, went back
  to him at 55 on the next press of 1, and its belt key then did nothing.

THE BUILD. pedBeltClaims counts the belt's copies per key, the same way bagStacks does. The
sale and the SELL BACKPACK count on the stall both leave those copies out, so the number on
the stall and what the press sells always agree.

MEASURED. Check 13.70 puts a Medkit on belt key 3 and one plain item in the backpack, presses
the sale, and requires the Medkit kept and exactly the plain item's price carried. The plain
item being sold is the control. It fails on v13.69.

Not verified: the drawn count on the stall panel, which uses the same claims but was not read
off the canvas.
