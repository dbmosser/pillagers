v13.82: a burst stops when its gun leaves your hand

The later rounds of a burst took ammo from whatever gun was in hand, so a swap
mid-burst charged the new gun for the old one's rounds. They now fire only while
the gun that fired them is still in hand.

Check 13.82 fires a burst and swaps at once. Fails on v13.81.
