## v14.26 - THE TITLE SCREEN WORKS ON A PAD

Controller audit finding 7 (LOW). The title screen is a .screen, not a .modal. The pad finds a panel to drive by looking
for an open window, then the pause box (v14.23), then the stash screen, so on the title it found nothing. The floor is
frozen under the title and the floor's pad taps return there. Booted with only a controller, nothing on the title could be
pressed, ENTER THE UNDERCROFT included. Had the title been found, B's usual way out would have been worse: it finds no
Leave or Close button there and strips the title's .on without running what ENTER THE UNDERCROFT runs, leaving a blank
screen.

THE BUILD. An open title screen is a pad panel, after any window and the pause box. The focus starts on ENTER THE
UNDERCROFT rather than GO FULLSCREEN above it; the D-pad moves to the saves, the name field and CREATE A NEW SAVE; A
presses. B does nothing on the title.

MEASURED. Check 14.26 shows the title with a faked pad (control: ENTER THE UNDERCROFT is laid out as a reachable control),
polls (the focus must be on ENTER THE UNDERCROFT), then presses B (the title must stay). It fails on v14.25.

Not verified: a boot with only a real controller connected.
