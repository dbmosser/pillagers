v10.74: the way out of the run report was below the fold

Not his note. Found by measuring the run report the way v10.73 measured the
title screen.

Reproduced at 1920x1080 with a realistic full bag, ten items packed and
eight picked up, dying with both welcome pack guns. The card holds 945
pixels of content in an 826 pixel box, and what is in the 119 that overflow
is the button row: Log run and return at y1100, and Copy report beside it,
in a card that ends at 1079.

So a friend who dies with a full bag cannot see the button that leaves the
card, or the one that sends me the report, without scrolling a panel he has
no reason to believe scrolls. On the alpha that hides the whole feedback
path.

Widening does not fix it and I measured that before reaching for it: at
1,430 and at 1,625 wide the content is still 945 tall, because the ledger is
one line per item however wide the box gets. The 86vh cap is right. What was
wrong is which part scrolled away.

The action row is pinned to the foot of the card now, with a fade so the
ledger passing under it stays readable. A card too short to scroll is
unchanged.

The check drives a full bag and a one item card, and fails on a v10.73
fixture.
