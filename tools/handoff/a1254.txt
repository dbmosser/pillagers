The size of the siege a call buys is read from the bag being carried, once, at the moment of the call; the comment beside the reader says exactly that and the player's own call clears the stored figure so it is read fresh. A pillager's call does not, though it has carried the same per-zone consequences since v3.58 and the design note says in as many words that you can ride out on his call: it writes every other field on that ring and leaves this one alone. So the first call on a ring froze that ring's figure for the rest of the raid unless the PLAYER called it again: call a point with a heavy bag, miss the extraction, sell the bag, then ride out on his call at the same point and the siege is sized on a bag from half an hour ago; the other order under-sizes it just as wrongly. One field added to his call so both calls read the same way; the figure, how it is worked out and everything the siege does with it are untouched. The bot never rides out on a pillager's call, so no paired number moves. Check 12.54 leaves a stale figure of 7777 on a ring, which no bag can produce, empties the bag, and lets a pillager make the call himself through the real state machine, then requires the stale figure gone and, once the ticker has read it again, equal to what the bag in hand is worth; the control is the same ring with the point closed so no call happens and requires the figure to SURVIVE; fails on the v12.53 fixture.
