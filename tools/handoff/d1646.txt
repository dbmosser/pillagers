## v16.46 - PING ON D-PAD UP, SHARED MAP MARKERS, DANGER PINGS

His orders of 2026-09-27: ping on d-pad up; if a player sets a marker on the map, say how it meets the ping, and the other
player should see it; read up on the Fortnite ping and map practice.

Fortnite practice (researched): one mark button; a ping on a spot or item shows to the squad for about 10 seconds; pressed
twice on an enemy it marks danger; a marker placed on the map is shared with the squad and stays until removed; pinging while
the map is open places that marker. This build follows it:
- Controller: tap D-UP pings (in solo, with nobody to ping for, the tap opens and shuts the map as before); hold D-UP 0.4 s to
  open or shut the map; a tap with the map open shuts it. Both bumpers no longer ping. Keyboard: N or the middle mouse button.
- Ping: 10 seconds (was 8), shown in the world and now on the map. Pressed twice within 0.4 s it becomes DANGER, in red.
- Map marker: a click on the map (right click clears), or a ping while the map is open, sets your marker. It goes to the party
  and shows on their map and in the world with your name and distance, pulled to the screen edge, until you clear it or place
  another. One marker per player. Your own marker still leads your compass as before.
