## v14.21 - AN A THAT CLICKS A MENU BUTTON DOES NOT FIRE ON ARRIVAL

Controller audit finding 3 (MEDIUM). On a pad, A clicks the focused menu button on the press. At the lift that button is
ASCEND, ASCEND TO THIS SECTOR, MY LOADOUT or FREEBIE KIT, and each starts the raid on the spot. On the next poll no menu
was open, so the same held A went to the raid branch, where A is the trigger, and nothing delays input on arrival. Every
pad ascent fired a shot at the spawn point. A mouse click on the same button never did, because the fire listener is on
the canvas, not on the page.

THE BUILD. An A that clicks a menu button is marked spent. The raid branch ignores A while it is spent and clears the mark
on the first poll with A up, so the next press fires as normal. Menus, the stall rows and the keyboard are unchanged.

MEASURED. Check 14.21 opens a probe window with one button, fakes a pad and presses A (the button must be clicked, or the
check skips), starts a raid with A still held and polls twice (the trigger must stay off), then lets A go and presses it
again (control: the trigger is on). It fails on v14.20.

Not verified: a real controller pressing ASCEND.
