v12.34: the Peddler stall is not a pause

The trade window returns out of the player update above the only real call to
the extraction tick, so opening the Peddler stopped every extraction clock:
the inbound countdown, the thirty second boarding window, the siege the call
bought, the re-ping, the mirrors the HUD reads, the closing of a point whose
time has come, and the raider waves. Nothing else stopped, because the frame
loop gates on none of it and kept the raid clock and the machines running in
the same frames. The downed branch and the roll branch already carry this
call past their own early return; the stall takes the roll form, with no pull
passed in, so the clocks run while E belongs to the panel.

Said plainly: this has an in-raid effect. The stall was a free pause on
extraction pressure and is not one any more. No dial moves.

Check 12.34 opens the stall through the real loop with a real key press, then
drives sixty frames three times: the inbound countdown must fall with the
raid clock, a landed boarding window must spend, and a point past its closing
time must shut; the first two are ratios against the raid clock measured in
the same frames and the third is a threshold on the open flag, with two
controls;
fails on v12.33.
