1. In the Claude app on your main PC, click your name at the bottom left, then Settings, then Claude Code, and turn on the switch that allows bypass permissions mode.
2. On your desktop, double-click "Pillagers helper sign-in". A web page opens: pick your personal Claude account and click Authorize. The window says "All set" when it worked; press Enter to close it.
3. On your desktop, double-click "Pillagers keys". Paste your Console key and press Enter, then your itch key and press Enter; the keys stay hidden. Press Enter to close it.
4. In the Claude app, click Code, then New session, choose the folder C:\claudecode\dark raiders, and set its mode to bypass permissions.
5. On your desktop, open "Pillagers first message.txt", press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C, click into the new chat, press Ctrl+V, then Enter. That is all; it takes over from there.
