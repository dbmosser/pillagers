## v10.74 - THE WAY OUT OF THE RUN REPORT WAS BELOW THE FOLD

Not one of his notes. Found by measuring the run report the same way v10.73
measured the title screen, and this one is worse than a layout complaint.

### What it was

Reproduced on v10.73 at 1920x1080 with a realistic full bag: ten items
packed, eight more picked up, dying with both of the guns the welcome pack
gives every new character. The card comes out 890 pixels wide, 46 percent of
the screen, and 1,078 tall, which is all of it. Its content is 945 pixels in
an 826 pixel box, so 119 pixels sit below the fold.

What is down there is the button row.

    LOG RUN AND RETURN     y 1100 to 1144
    COPY REPORT            beside it
    the card ends at       y 1079

So a friend who dies with a full bag cannot see the button that leaves the
card, or the one that sends me the report, without scrolling a panel he has
no reason to believe scrolls. On the alpha that is the whole feedback path
hidden behind a gesture nobody will make.

### What did not fix it

Widening the card, which was my first instinct after v10.73, and I measured
it before reaching for it. At 1,430 pixels wide the content is still 945
tall. At 1,625 it is still 945. The ledger is one line per item however wide
the box gets, so the width buys nothing here. The card is capped at 86vh on
purpose and it is right to scroll on a long report. What is wrong is WHICH
PART scrolls away.

### Now

The action row is pinned to the foot of the card, with a fade so the ledger
passing under it stays readable. Measured with it pinned and the card
scrolled to the top, the buttons sit at 993 to 1038 inside a card ending at
1,079, where before they were at 1,100 to 1,144 and off the end.

On a card short enough not to scroll nothing changes: the row sits where it
always did and the fade ends in the card's own colour.

### Measured

The check drives two real deaths. The first is the case that broke, a full
bag with two of his own guns, and it requires the card to actually scroll,
which is a control that the setup still produces the situation, then requires
both buttons to be inside the card scrolled to the top AND scrolled to the
bottom, so pinning them cannot have pushed them past the other end. The
second is a one item card, which must not scroll and must still show both
buttons, so the short case is not quietly broken by the fix. On a v10.73
fixture the first arm fails naming the button and how many pixels below the
card it sits.

Not verified: the same card at 1440p and 4K, where the pane cannot be made
big enough to measure and 86vh is a taller box, so the fold falls somewhere
else; his text size setting turned up, which makes the content taller against
the same 86vh; and the pause box and the ascent check, which are also capped
panels with buttons at the bottom and were not measured here.
