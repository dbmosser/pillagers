v12.88: a friend you import carries the gun they actually carried

Finding 3 of the 2026-09-11 audit, and the only one in that file where the game
states a fact about a real person and the fact is false.

A friend sends their run report. The game says they favour the Auto Rifle. Their
report said Scav Pistol on every line. The export writes the DISPLAY name, and the
capture group stopped at the first space, so "Scav Pistol" became "Scav", the
lookup missed, and favKey sat on its initialiser, whose name is Auto Rifle.

Nine of sixteen guns have a space in the name. The four issued starters do not,
which is what a fresh alpha friend usually carries, which is why this survived.

Both halves are fixed. The parse stops at the bracket or the next field, not the
first space. A lookup that still fails claims no favourite at all rather than a
confident wrong one; the ghost is armed with the same default either way, so
nothing about the raid moves and only the claim goes away.

Check 12.88 builds a report in the export's own format with names taken off the
weapon table, and requires a two-word gun to come back as itself, with and without
the source bracket. Fails on v12.87.
