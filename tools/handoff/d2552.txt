## v25.52 - GOOD RELOAD NOISES

Co-op hunt while he was away. His order of 2026-10-12: add good reload noises. Every gun made the same quarter-second click at the start of a reload that lasts 1.3 to 4 seconds, then nothing until a second click when the rounds went in, so the Support MG went silent for three seconds and a shotgun sounded like a magazine. Now every reload plays recorded sounds in steps, timed to the reload of that gun: the magazine comes out the moment you press R, the new one seats three quarters of the way through, and the charging handle, slide or bolt goes home on the frame the rounds go in, but only when the gun was empty: a reload with a round still in the chamber skips it, as in CoD. A shotgun pushes in one shell for every shell it takes and racks the pump if it was empty. The Longshot opens and closes its bolt. The Magnum swings its cylinder out, drops the brass, takes a speedloader and shuts. The Support MG opens its cover, swaps the box, lays the belt and slams the cover. The Meridian Lance vents its cell, seats a new one, whines up and pings when ready. A faster reload (the Forge MK II) plays the same steps sooner, and a reload cut short by a gun swap, a draw or going down plays nothing more. In 2 player each window plays its own reload, and your teammate hears yours where you stand. Pillagers have no magazines and never reload, so they have no reload sound (giving them one would change how they fight). Sound only: nothing in the raid changes.
