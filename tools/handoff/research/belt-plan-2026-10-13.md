## Gun rule mechanics
- "Full" means keys 1-4 all show a real gun: none vacant, none stowed Bare Hands. This is autoBelt's test.
- Rank by (gunTierOf(w), gunStats(w).dps), the stat card's basis. Forge MK is counted. A backpack gun is ranked by gunQPeek(gk), else WEAPONS[gk].
- If the lowest is a backpack gun pinned to key 3 or 4: unpin it (it shows in the grid), then gunQPush the new gun and pin it to that key.
- If the lowest is the stowed hand gun p.sec: bag it with its rounds, quality copy and armoury splice (the bagHeldGun body, without its line). The new gun takes p.sec (half a magazine) and the key. This generalises the autoEquip _secFree branch.
- Words: the new gun's pickup row is tagged SLOT n; the old gun's row is tagged BACKPACK; G.gunCard route is SLOT n.
- If the backpack is over the cap afterwards (bagWeight above PACKCAP[P.pack]): dropItem the displaced gun at his feet, and show a red BACKPACK FULL row (key 'bagfull', danger) naming the gun.
- Sites: autoBelt's gun branch (it replaces GUN SLOTS FULL), and grantLoot's gun block for the p.sec case. An automatic item pin on a gun key yields to a gun.
