# How professional studios use generative AI in 2D art pipelines (2025-2026)

Scope note: research run 2026-10-20. Items dated before 2025 are marked BACKGROUND. Sources are linked inline after each claim. Where outlets disagree, both are given. Format note: this project bans em and en dashes, so each finding ends with its source link instead of a dash.

## 1. What surveys and reports say (numbers and dates)

### Takeaway
Use of AI is high and still rising, but developer and player sentiment has turned sharply negative, and artists are the most negative group of all. Most use is text and LLM work (research, brainstorming, code). Generating shipped art is the least accepted use, and in surveys it is mostly the employer pushing it, not the artists.

### Cited Findings
**GDC State of the Game Industry 2026 (released late January 2026, more than 2,300 respondents, mostly US, North America and Western Europe)**
- 36% of game industry professionals use generative AI tools in their job. At game studios the figure is 30%, against 58% at publishers, support teams and marketing or PR firms. [GDC article](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)
- 52% think generative AI is having a negative effect on the industry, up from 30% in 2025 and 18% the year before. Only 7% see a positive effect, down from 13% in 2025. [GDC article](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)
- By discipline, the most negative are visual and technical artists (64%), game design and narrative (63%) and programmers (59%). [GDC article](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)
- Tasks among users: research or brainstorming 81%, daily tasks such as email 47%, code help 47%, prototyping 35%. The top tools are LLMs: ChatGPT 74%, Gemini 37%, Copilot 22%. GDC's summary page gives no figure for asset generation or concept art. [GDC article](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)
- Secondary coverage reports that 78% of respondents work at companies with some internal AI policy, 15% say there is none and 7% are unsure. This figure is not on GDC's summary page. [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/state-of-game-industry-survey-sheds-light-on-layoffs-genai-views-unionization-and-tariffs-gdc-survey/)
- 74% of surveyed students worry about their job prospects, citing laid-off workers and AI displacement. [GDC article](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)

**GDC State of the Game Industry 2025 (released January 2025, more than 3,000 respondents)**
- 52% work at companies that use generative AI tools, up from 31% the year before. 36% use them personally. 30% think it is harming the industry, up 12 points. [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/gdc-2025-state-of-the-game-industry-devs-weigh-in-on-layoffs-ai-and-more); [Business Wire release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250121745145/en/The-2025-Game-Industry-Survey-Reveals-Increasing-Impact-Of-Layoffs-Concerns-With-The-Usage-Of-Generative-AI-Funding-Challenges-and-More)
- Some secondary summaries misreport the 52% as personal use. GDC's own wording is company-level use. [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gdc-the-state-of-the-game-industry)

**Unity 2025 Gaming Report (around March 2025, vendor survey)**
- 96% of studios say they use AI tools in some workflows, and 79% of respondents feel positive about AI tools. [Unity blog](https://unity.com/blog/2025-unity-gaming-report-launch); [Unity GDC 2025 announcement](https://unity.com/en/news/unity-announces-enhanced-engine-performance-gdc-2025)
- Unity sells AI tools, so its sample and framing likely lean positive. Its 79% positive figure sits far from GDC's 7% positive. [GameDev Reports summary of Unity](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/unity-the-gaming-industry-in-2025)

**Google Cloud and The Harris Poll (published 18 August 2025, 615 developers in the US, South Korea, Norway, Finland and Sweden, fielded late June and early July 2025)**
- Google's headline says 90% of developers already use AI in workflows. Reuters reported 87% use AI agents. These measure slightly different things. [Google Cloud press release](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2025-08-18-90-of-Games-Developers-Already-Using-AI-in-Workflows,-According-to-New-Google-Cloud-Research); [Yahoo/Reuters](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/nearly-90-videogame-developers-ai-135408103.html)

**a16z Games (BACKGROUND, 2023 and 2024)**

**Japan**
- CESA 2025 (54 member companies, surveyed June to July 2025): about 51% use generative AI in development. The most common use is **visual assets and images such as characters**, then story and text, then programming help. 32% use AI to help build in-house engines. [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/over-50-of-japanese-game-companies-use-ai-in-development-according-to-tokyo-game-show-organizer); [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/news/cesa-japanese-game-companies-using-ai)
- CESA 2026 preview (Tokyo Game Show 2026, 1,349 individual developers, online May to August 2026): 85.8% use generative AI at work, 63.0% routinely and 22.8% occasionally. This is a different survey from the 2025 one (individuals, not companies), so "51% to 85.8%" is not a like-for-like rise. [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/cesa-survey-japanese-game-developers-generative-ai/); [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113639/almost-86-percent-of-japanese-game-developers-now-use-generative-ai/index.html)
- Square Enix (November 2025 earnings) aims for generative AI to handle 70% of QA and debugging by the end of 2027, through a joint project with the University of Tokyo. This is QA, not art. [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/11/square-enix-wants-70percent-of-its-qa-and-debugging-work-done-by-gen-ai); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/square-enix-wants-ai-to-handle-70-of-qa-within-two-years/)

**Korea**
- Krafton calls itself an "AI first" company. Reports put its agentic AI spend at either $70 million or over 130 billion won (about $88 million) from 2026, and it later opened a voluntary resignation programme. Nexon's CEO said it is important to assume every game company now uses AI. [The Outpost](https://theoutpost.ai/news-story/krafton-launches-voluntary-resignation-program-following-ai-first-company-transformation-21704/); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/345847/gaming-giants-electronic-arts-krafton-going-big-generative-ai)
- PC Gamer, as relayed by an aggregator, notes that only 16% of South Koreans are "more concerned than excited" about AI, far lower than in the West. [Everything Edinburgh, summarising PC Gamer](https://everythingedinburgh.com/games/news/vapor-world-steam-ai-cutscene-backlash/)

**China (BACKGROUND, April 2023)**
- Tencent and indie studios alike started using Midjourney and Stable Diffusion for characters, backdrops and promo art. One studio cut 5 of its 15 character illustrators. Freelancers said clients now paid them only to fix AI images, at about a tenth of the old rate. A recruiter estimated illustrator job posts fell nearly 70% in a year. [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2023/ai-image-china-video-game-layoffs/); [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/04/12/game-artist-jobs-china-down-70-percent-gen-ai-adoption)
- In March 2023 NetEase's Naraka: Bladepoint briefly let players make skins with the company's in-house AI. [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2023/ai-china-video-game-layoffs-illustrators/)

**Players**
- Quantic Foundry (opt-in survey, N=1,799, October to December 2025): 85% of gamers are negative about generative AI in games, and 63% picked the most negative option. Boing Boing reports the split as 62% "very negative" and 7.6% approving. Younger, female and non-binary gamers are more negative. The researchers called a skew this strong rare in their years of surveys. [Games Market](https://www.gamesmarket.global/quantic-foundry-most-gamers-are-negative-about-the-use-of-gen-ai-in-video-games); [Boing Boing](https://boingboing.net/2025/12/20/survey-finds-very-negative-attitude-toward-gen-ai-in-games.html)

**Artists**
- This Week in Videogames interviewed about a dozen working game concept artists. Summaries say most, or all, said AI image tools made their jobs harder (one summary says zero found it easier, another says one did). [TWIV on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/thisweekinvideogames.com/post/3mac3dznt6g2j)
- A CHI EA 2026 study of 378 verified professional visual artists found most strongly opposed to generative AI. They reported mainly negative workplace effects: more stress and fewer job openings. [arXiv 2603.04537](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.04537v2)

### Inferences
- The numbers agree on one split. Studios report high "AI use", but most of it is LLM text and code work. Developers and players both reject shipped generative art, and sentiment is getting worse year on year (GDC: 18% to 30% to 52% negative).
- Vendor surveys (Unity, Google) read far more positive than independent ones (GDC, Quantic Foundry). A report should weight GDC and Quantic Foundry more for questions about sentiment.
- Japan and Korea are much more accepting than the West, and Japanese companies name character and visual assets as their top AI use. PILLAGERS launches to a mostly Western itch.io audience, where sentiment is at its most negative.

### Gaps
- GDC 2026's summary page gives no share of respondents who use AI specifically for art or asset generation. The full PDF may have it, but it was not checked.
- No 2025 or 2026 data found for China, only the 2023 reporting.
- No Konvoy report on AI in game art was found.
- A late-2025 claim of over 10,000 Steam AI disclosures (about 8%) could not be traced to a primary source.

## 2. Named case studies: shipped AI 2D art, policies for and against, public backlash

### Takeaway
Almost every public scandal follows one of four patterns: a placeholder that shipped, store or trailer art, AI that was hidden and then found, or AI on a visible character with obvious inconsistencies. Studios that kept AI in-house, trained it on their own art, limited it to backgrounds, and had artists finish every image (Rovio) report big gains without backlash. Even big studios such as Larian have pulled AI out of concept art just to end arguments about where the art came from.

### Cited Findings
**Pipelines that shipped AI-assisted 2D art**
- **Rovio, "Beacon Picasso" (Angry Birds)**: in-house diffusion models fine-tuned only on Rovio's own Angry Birds art, about 20 to 30 hand-picked images per model, with datasets assembled and captioned by artists, not ML engineers. Uses: season pass backgrounds, environments and secondary characters. Main brand characters were rejected as below quality. Season pass background time fell from 20 days to 4 (about 80%), and some teams doubled output. The source is a ZenML summary of a Rovio and AWS talk, dated 2025 on one copy and 2024 on another. [ZenML LLMOps database](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
- GDC 2026 had a sponsored session, "Angry Birds Meets GenAI", on the same pipeline for Angry Birds Dream Blast. The listing stresses keeping art direction and brand consistency. [GDC schedule](https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/angry-birds-meets-genai-transforming-rovios-asset-creation-with-aws-presented-by-amazon-web-services/917278)
- **EA and Stability AI (announced 23 October 2025)**: co-developing models and workflows. The first projects include generating PBR materials, among them 2D textures, and previsualising 3D environments from prompts, with artists keeping creative control. [EA news](https://www.ea.com/news/ea-partners-with-stability-ai); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/345847/gaming-giants-electronic-arts-krafton-going-big-generative-ai)
- **Level-5**: CEO Akihiro Hino says AI helps with production, for example turning artwork into polygons, toward cutting major-release development from five years to two. He says scenarios, character designs and basic settings are made by hand. He apologised after viewers spotted AI-generated footage in a 2026 showcase (Professor Layton, Yo-kai Watch), which he called an experiment. Earlier (December 2025) he claimed 80 to 90% of Level-5's code is written by AI and then fixed by people. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/level-5-boss-admits-using-ai-for-professor-layton-yo-kai-watch-reveals/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/level-5-ceo-apologises-after-ai-use-in-recent-game-reveals/); [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/level-5-ceo-says-games-are-now-being-made-80-90-by-ai-making-aesthetic-sense-a-must-for-developers/)

**Studio policies against or narrowing AI art**
- **Larian (December 2025 to January 2026)**: a Bloomberg interview revealed generative AI use for placeholder text and concept-art reference. Vincke said Larian was not shipping AI content or replacing artists, and that the studio has 72 artists, 23 of them concept artists. In a January 2026 Reddit AMA, Larian said it would not use generative AI in concept art for Divinity "so that there can be no discussion about the origin of the art". Its writing director said no text generation touches dialogue or journals. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/holy-f-guys-were-not-replacing-artists-larian-boss-responds-to-ai-backlash); [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/news/larian-generative-ai-divinity-reddit); [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/larian-confirms-that-it-will-no-longer-use-generative-ai-for-divinity-concept-art-and-writing-following-backlash-1615314)
- Vincke later clarified that AI images were used only as reference, where artists would otherwise have searched for photos, and not as concept art itself. [The SixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=467109)
- **No Gen AI Seal (February 2025)**: Polygon Treehouse (Röki, Mythwrecked) released a free badge for store pages. Its reason is that generative models were trained on artists' work without permission or pay. Adopters include Mythwrecked: Ambrosia Island and Rosewater. [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/02/indie-devs-have-begun-adding-a-no-generative-ai-stamp-to-their-store-pages)
- **Indie Game Awards**: requires no generative AI in submitted builds, and removing it after launch does not restore eligibility (see Clair Obscur below). [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/indie-game-awards-clair-obscur-ai-controversy/)

**Studio policies for AI**
- Krafton ("AI first"), Nexon (assume every company uses AI), Square Enix (70% of QA by 2027), EA (Stability partnership). See section 1 and above. [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/345847/gaming-giants-electronic-arts-krafton-going-big-generative-ai); [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/11/square-enix-wants-70percent-of-its-qa-and-debugging-work-done-by-gen-ai)

**Backlash cases and their triggers (2025-2026)**
- **Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 (Sandfall), December 2025**: the Indie Game Awards took back its Game of the Year and Debut Game awards. The game had shipped with AI placeholder textures (background images that looked like newspaper clippings), though Sandfall had said at submission that no AI was used. Sandfall patched them out within days of launch, but the IGA said a later fix does not undo it. Reported dates range from 18 to 22 December. [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2025/12/21/indie-game-awards-rescinds-honours-from-clair-obscur-expedition-33-for-genai-use/202718); [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/indie-game-awards-clair-obscur-ai-controversy/)
- **The Alters (11 bit studios), June 2025**: AI-generated placeholder text in a background texture was left in by "internal oversight". The studio also used AI to translate licensed in-game films. Neither was disclosed on Steam beforehand. [The SixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/06/30/11-bit-studios-releases-statement-over-use-of-ai-in-the-alter-development/); [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/the-alters-developers-address-accusations-of-generative-ai-use/)
- **Anno 117: Pax Romana (Ubisoft), November 2025**: an AI loading screen with obvious generation artifacts shipped. Ubisoft said it was a placeholder that "slipped through our review process" and would be replaced in patch 1.3. Its Steam page already disclosed AI use, and Ubisoft says it uses AI for "iterations, prototyping and exploration". [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/ubisoft-says-ai-generated-art-in-anno-117-was-a-placeholder-which-slipped-through-our-review-process); [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/ubisoft-says-ai-generated-loading-screen-was-accidentally-shipped-in-anno-117-pax-romana-1578275)
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 (Activision), November 2025**: within hours of launch, players found campaign calling cards in a Studio Ghibli style typical of AI generators. Activision said it uses AI tools to support teams but that creative work is "led by" its people. The Steam page carries the same general AI disclosure as Black Ops 6. US Congressman Ro Khanna called for regulation. [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/news/activision-responds-to-ai-claims-in-black-ops-7); [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-faces-backlash-over-ai-generated-calling-card-artwork-1576825); [The SixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/11/17/activision-doesnt-deny-black-ops-7-ai-slop-accusations/)
- **Jurassic World Evolution 3 (Frontier), mid-2025**: the Steam disclosure said scientist portraits (background staff only) were AI-generated. After backlash, Frontier removed them before the 21 October 2025 launch, and the Steam disclosure came down with them. [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/jurassic-world-evolution-3-thankfully-wont-feature-ai-generated-character-portraits-following-community-backlash); [Screenhub](https://www.screenhub.com.au/news/news/jurassic-world-evolution-3-genai-2671001/)
- **Vapor World: Over The Mind (Alive Inc, Korea)**: a painterly soulslike with a reported 100,000 wishlists. At early access launch on 19 August, coverage centred on AI-generated cutscenes. The top Steam complaint was that the AI "couldn't even keep the main character's model consistent". The director called it a "hasty judgment" and swapped the cutscenes within days. PC Gamer's headline: "A touch of AI is all it takes to trigger backlash". The year is reported as 2025 by one summary; another outlet's page is dated August 2026. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/steam-week-in-review-a-touch-of-ai-is-all-it-takes-to-trigger-backlash-as-a-promising-new-indie-falls-afoul-of-slop-skeptics/); [Everything Edinburgh](https://everythingedinburgh.com/games/news/vapor-world-steam-ai-cutscene-backlash/)
- **Postal: Bullet Paradise (Running With Scissors and Goonswarm Games), December 2025**: cancelled a day or two after its 3 December reveal, once fans said the trailer art and voices looked AI-made. The developer denied any AI use, but the publisher said its trust was broken, and Goonswarm shut down. An accusation alone was enough. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/postal-game-developer-shuts-down-after-its-project-was-cancelled-due-to-allegations-of-generative-ai-use); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/351204/new-postal-game-canceled-one-day-after-reveal-generative-ai-allegations); [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2025/12/ai-accusations-have-killed-a-ps5-ps4-game-just-two-days-after-announcement)
- **FragPunk (NetEase publisher), March 2025**: criticised for suspected AI art in store assets and skins with no Steam AI disclosure. [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/New-hit-FPS-FragPunk-comes-under-fire-for-using-AI-art-with-no-Steam-disclosure.976414.0.html)

**Platform rules relevant to a 2D indie**
- **Steam**: asked for AI disclosure from January 2024 (BACKGROUND). On 16 January 2026 Valve rewrote the rules. "AI-powered tools" such as code helpers no longer need disclosure. AI that generates in-game content, store page assets or marketing material must be disclosed, as must AI that generates content during play. Coverage says the rule is not enforced by moderation. Tim Sweeney called such labels pointless. [VGC](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/valve-has-significantly-rewritten-steams-rules-for-how-developers-much-disclose-ai-use); [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260120-steam-updates-ai-disclosure-guidelines); [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/valve-has-relaxed-its-rules-for-disclosing-generative-ai-use-in-steam-games-1623888)
- **itch.io (November 2024, BACKGROUND but still current as far as found)**: project pages have a "Generative AI disclosure" field. A yes adds an "AI Generated" tag with sub-tags for graphics, sound, text and dialogue, or **code**. A no adds a "No AI" tag. The field is mandatory and strictly enforced for asset packs (untagged packs can be delisted). Reports differ on games: some say games must answer, others say it is optional for games. [GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2024/11/itchio-store-now-requires-ai-generated-content-disclosures-for-assets); [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/asset-creators-on-itch-io-now-have-to-disclose-the-use-of-generative-ai/); [Games Market](https://www.gamesmarket.global/itchio-now-requires-gen-ai-content-to-be-tagged-in-all-games-and-asset-packs-711e824843d410eab51fcd9436836294/)

### Inferences
- Four of the most-covered 2025 scandals (Clair Obscur, The Alters, Anno 117, Black Ops 7) were not AI-made games. Small AI images slipped into otherwise human-made games. Players punish the discovery more than the use. Studios that disclosed in advance (Ubisoft, Activision) were still criticised, but none lost an award or a whole project, unlike Sandfall.
- The safe lane that big studios defend in public is AI for internal reference and exploration that never ships. Larian shows that even this lane is politically fragile in the West.
- For an itch.io release, the itch field has a "code" sub-tag. PILLAGERS code is written by Claude, so the honest answer to "does the project contain the results of generative AI" may be yes (code), even if no AI art ships. Steam's 2026 rules would not need this, but itch's field asks a different question. The owner should decide this, not a helper.

### Gaps
- No named commercial pixel-art game was found with a documented, well-received AI sprite pipeline. Every documented AI 2D pipeline in production is a large live-service studio (Rovio) or vague publisher statements (EA, Ubisoft, Activision).
- The Roottrees are Dead (see section 4): the exact Steam release date and which AI tool made the jam art were not confirmed.
- The full PC Gamer Vapor World article could not be retrieved (paywall), so its exact date is unconfirmed (2025 vs 2026 conflict).
- Arc Raiders (Embark) AI use is mostly voice and 3D. It was not researched because it is outside 2D scope.

## 3. The accepted professional pattern and how studios keep a consistent style

### Takeaway
The pattern professionals defend is: human art direction and a written style guide first. AI for ideation, reference and moodboards. Only if needed, a model trained on the studio's own art and pointed at low-risk asset classes (backgrounds, variations, secondary content). Batch, curate hard, then a mandatory artist cleanup or paintover of every asset. Hero characters stay human. Placeholders are tracked so they never ship, and use is disclosed.

### Cited Findings
**Where AI sits in the pipeline**
- In GDC 2026, the top uses among AI users are research and brainstorming (81%) and prototyping (35%), well ahead of production work. [GDC article](https://gdconf.com/article/gdc-2026-state-of-the-game-industry-reveals-impact-of-layoffs-generative-ai-and-more/)
- Ubisoft describes its Anno 117 use as "iterations, prototyping and exploration", and its one failure was a placeholder that shipped. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/ubisoft-says-ai-generated-art-in-anno-117-was-a-placeholder-which-slipped-through-our-review-process)
- Larian used AI images as reference, where an artist would otherwise search for photos, not as concept art. It later dropped even that for concept art. [The SixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=467109); [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/news/larian-generative-ai-divinity-reddit)
- Activision's line: AI tools "support" teams while the creative process "continues to be led by" its people. [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/news/activision-responds-to-ai-claims-in-black-ops-7)
- Level-5 draws the line at scenarios, character designs and basic settings being made by people, with AI used for conversion and pipeline steps. [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/level-5-ceo-apologises-after-ai-use-in-recent-game-reveals/)

**Step-by-step production pattern (Rovio, the most detailed public example)**
1. Ground rules: only the studio's own material, data kept on the studio's own servers, and IP respect extended to prompts. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
2. Fine-tune base diffusion models per game and per use case, on about 20 to 30 curated images. Artists build and caption the datasets, and each model gets a unique trigger word. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
3. Artists choose the checkpoint from videos showing output across training steps, because later checkpoints overfit. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
4. Generate batches of dozens to hundreds, lock the seed while changing prompts, and pick the best. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
5. Artists clean artifacts by hand, extend the composition, upscale, build layers and add the brand characters themselves. Artist review is required before anything ships. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
6. Asset scope: backgrounds suit AI because small changes in composition do not break brand consistency. Main characters stay human, because the style guides run to hundreds of pages and "pretty good is not good enough" for brand-critical assets. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)

**Step-by-step pattern for 2D and pixel art (practitioner guide, Relish Games, 21 June 2026)**
- Explore broadly with AI to narrow the brief, then generate "working references" (character sheets, tileset ideas, palette studies), not finals. [Relish Games](https://relishgames.com/journal/generative-ai-for-2d-game-art-tools-and-techniques/)
- For pixel art, a higher-resolution image pixelled down by hand holds up better than asking the model for pixel resolution directly. Keep character art and interactive elements hand-made where consistency matters. Use AI mostly for composition, colour and background elements. [Relish Games](https://relishgames.com/journal/generative-ai-for-2d-game-art-tools-and-techniques/)
- Consistency pass: generate about 20 variants, keep about 3, use those as references for the next batch, then apply the same colour correction, outline weight and palette limit across the whole set. [Relish Games](https://relishgames.com/journal/generative-ai-for-2d-game-art-tools-and-techniques/)
- Every AI asset gets a human pass (transparency, dimensions, tile edges that connect, pixel fixes). Production animation still needs people, and AI animation is for prototypes and placeholders. [Relish Games](https://relishgames.com/journal/generative-ai-for-2d-game-art-tools-and-techniques/)
- "Be transparent with the audience about AI-assisted art production." The guide warns that players who find undisclosed AI art later react badly. [Relish Games](https://relishgames.com/journal/generative-ai-for-2d-game-art-tools-and-techniques/)

**Pixel-specific tools in use (vendor claims, not independently verified)**
- PixelLab: text-to-character on a true pixel grid, skeleton-based animation, 4 or 8 direction rotation, tilesets, inpainting, an Aseprite plugin and an API. Comparison guides call it the most-used dedicated pixel generator. [Cinevva comparison](https://app.cinevva.com/es/guides/ai-pixel-art-generators)
- Retro Diffusion: made by pixel artist Astropulse, with models trained together with experienced pixel artists for grid-aligned, limited-palette art (RD Plus, RD Tile, RD Animation). It is also offered inside Scenario. [Scenario help](https://help.scenario.com/en/articles/retro-diffusion-models-the-essentials/); [Retro Diffusion MCP listing](https://mcpservers.org/ko/servers/retro-diffusion/retro-diffusion-mcp)
- Only PixelLab, Retro Diffusion and Ludo produce animation frame sets, and only PixelLab does so with a poseable skeleton. [Cinevva comparison](https://app.cinevva.com/es/guides/ai-pixel-art-generators)

**Style control methods**
- Artist-curated training sets of the studio's own art, model choice per use case, seed locking, required artist review, and long written style guides (Rovio's run to hundreds of pages). [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
- Vendors market "style training" on a studio's own art (Layer AI, Scenario) as the fix for consistency. [Layer AI](https://www.layer.ai/features/style-training)
- One vendor admits most generators "solve the individual asset problem but not the consistency problem". [Makko blog](https://blog.makko.ai/ai-game-art-generator/)

### Inferences
- The owner's AI-made pixel lineup, used as a style target or moodboard that never ships, sits in the most widely accepted lane (ideation and reference). It matches what Ubisoft, Activision and pre-2026 Larian defend. Once Larian came under pressure, it judged even this lane not worth defending, so the lineup should not appear on the store page, in the trailer or in the game.
- Turning that look into hand-authored rendering code (pixel snapping, fixed palette ramps, dark outlines, 3 to 4 tone shading) follows the professional "human does the final" rule. It also keeps the main characters human-directed and consistent, which is exactly what Rovio found AI could not do well. Procedural outfits and colour swaps are themselves a consistency system that AI sprites struggle to match.
- If AI sprites are ever used, the low-risk class by industry practice is environment variation (grass tufts, rocks, dirt decals) under a locked palette, with a human cleanup pass and disclosure. Pillagers and enemies are the high-risk class.
- Each 2025 placeholder scandal argues for a ship gate that fails any build containing an unapproved or AI placeholder image. That is a cheap check for a single-file game.

### Gaps
- No public GDC Vault talk was found that walks through a full AI-assisted 2D sprite or pixel pipeline from a shipped game. Rovio's is the only detailed one, and it is painterly mobile art, not pixel art.
- Whether studios use LoRAs specifically (rather than full fine-tunes) was not confirmed. Rovio's summary says "fine-tuned" without naming the method.
- No independent quality review of PixelLab or Retro Diffusion output was found, nor their licence terms.

## 4. How small indie and solo pixel-art developers use AI credibly, and how players and press react

### Takeaway
The credible indie pattern on record is AI only for prototypes or jam builds, then all of it replaced by a hired or in-house artist before the commercial release. The clearest example is The Roottrees are Dead, which became a hit after removing AI art. Players and press react harshly to any AI they find in shipped visuals, even small amounts. Indie storefront culture (itch.io tags, the No Gen AI Seal, award rules) treats "no AI" as a selling point.

### Cited Findings
- **The Roottrees are Dead**: the free browser or jam version used AI images. The developers (including Robin Ward) decided it was unethical to sell AI art, and illustrator Henning Ludvigsen redrew it for the commercial Steam release, which The Verge says "became a hit". [The Verge via repost](https://rss.boorghani.com/?p=320625); [Steam forum](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2754380/discussions/0/597389996280810619)
- One reviewer criticised the AI title-screen image in Roottrees for errors the developer and beta testers missed (opinion piece). [wurb.com](https://www.wurb.com/stack/?p=7578)
- An itch.io indie released a "No AI Art" update replacing its AI art, which shows the replace-before-or-after-launch pattern at hobby scale. [itch.io devlog](https://mqsalmon.itch.io/the-road-to-hell/devlog/433295/the-road-to-hell-update-11-the-no-ai-art-update)
- A small studio surveyed its community and found sentiment "largely negative", mainly because people feel AI is built on artists' work without consent. It committed to AI for internal prototyping only and zero AI art in the final game. [Altered Universe Games, Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/AlteredUniverseGames)
- Polygon Treehouse's No Gen AI Seal turns "no AI" into a visible selling point for indies. Steam's own disclosure sits at the bottom of store pages and is easy to miss. [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/02/indie-devs-have-begun-adding-a-no-generative-ai-stamp-to-their-store-pages)
- itch.io tags projects "AI Generated" (with sub-tags) or "No AI", and buyers can see these tags. [GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2024/11/itchio-store-now-requires-ai-generated-content-disclosures-for-assets)
- Player feedback on generic AI-looking indie art: one itch.io commenter called a project's art "a bit too generic" and "AI slop" and urged a custom style. [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/13808819)
- Vapor World (Korean indie, about 100,000 wishlists) had its launch coverage taken over by AI cutscenes and swapped them within days. [Everything Edinburgh](https://everythingedinburgh.com/games/news/vapor-world-steam-ai-cutscene-backlash/)
- Postal: Bullet Paradise was cancelled and its studio closed over AI accusations it denied. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/postal-game-developer-shuts-down-after-its-project-was-cancelled-due-to-allegations-of-generative-ai-use)
- Quantic Foundry: 85% of surveyed gamers are negative about generative AI in games, and younger players are more negative. [Games Market](https://www.gamesmarket.global/quantic-foundry-most-gamers-are-negative-about-the-use-of-gen-ai-in-video-games)
- Pixel-specific AI tools now exist and market themselves to indies (PixelLab, Retro Diffusion), and Retro Diffusion stresses that pixel artists were involved in training. [Scenario help](https://help.scenario.com/en/articles/retro-diffusion-models-the-essentials/); [Cinevva comparison](https://app.cinevva.com/es/guides/ai-pixel-art-generators)
- A vendor blog (SpriteCook, June 2026) argues that AI art is acceptable in indie games. It sells AI sprites, so it is not neutral evidence of player acceptance. [SpriteCook blog](https://www.spritecook.ai/blog/is-ai-art-ok-in-indie-games)

### Inferences
- For a pay-what-you-want itch.io release, goodwill is the business model. The documented risk is not only "AI art looks worse" but "players find AI and the story becomes the AI". The Roottrees pattern (prototype with AI, ship human or hand-authored art) is the one with a clean public record.
- PILLAGERS already follows the credible pattern for art: AI made a reference image, and the shipped characters are drawn by code, not generated. The main exposure is whatever the itch.io disclosure and store-page copy say, and whether any AI image (the reference lineup, a generated cover or a screenshot paintover) leaks into marketing.
- Chunky pixel art with a fixed palette is a style where generated output is easy to spot (doubled outlines, mixed pixel sizes, wandering palettes, per the Relish guide in section 5). Hand-authored or code-rendered pixel art with strict rules is easier to defend if accused.

### Gaps
- No press-reviewed solo pixel-art game was found that openly shipped AI-generated sprites and was well received. This may mean such cases are rare or that developers do not talk about them. It could not be told apart from search results.
- No itch.io-specific data on sales or ratings for AI-tagged vs No AI games was found.
- No figures were found on how PWYW buyers in particular react to AI disclosure.

## 5. Failure modes art directors and artists name

### Takeaway
The named failures are consistency (characters drift between images and frames), defects in gesture, proportion and anatomy, a generic or recognisably "AI" style (such as the Ghibli look), artifacts in small details, and pixel-specific problems (doubled outlines, grid misalignment, mixed pixel density, tiles that do not join). There is also a workflow failure: AI references can slow concept artists down, and stray placeholders ship.

### Cited Findings
- Rovio's early models of Red (the main character) looked acceptable to engineers but artists rejected them for defects in gesture, proportion and style. "Pretty good is not good enough" for brand-critical assets. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
- Other Rovio lessons: brainstorming before generating saved little time, while generating first and then brainstorming worked better. Artists who expected exact results gave up early, and experienced artists wanted more control, not less. Public prompts exposed bias (an "important meeting" prompt gave only white men). GPU cost needed scheduled shutdowns. [ZenML](https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/accelerating-game-asset-creation-with-fine-tuned-diffusion-models)
- Pixel and 2D failure list from a practitioner guide: doubled outlines, misaligned tile grids, mixed pixel density, drifting character proportions, shifting palettes, changing line weight, jumping light direction, upscaling and style-transfer artifacts, weak control of exact dimensions, tile edges that do not join, mismatched animation frames and a heavy cleanup burden. [Relish Games](https://relishgames.com/journal/generative-ai-for-2d-game-art-tools-and-techniques/)
- Character consistency failing in public: the top Vapor World complaint was that the AI could not keep the main character consistent across a cutscene. [Everything Edinburgh](https://everythingedinburgh.com/games/news/vapor-world-steam-ai-cutscene-backlash/)
- A recognisable "AI style": Black Ops 7's calling cards were flagged within hours because the Ghibli-like style matched common AI generator output. [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-faces-backlash-over-ai-generated-calling-card-artwork-1576825)
- Visible artifacts: the Anno 117 loading screen was spotted from "obvious generative artifacts". [Hitmarker](https://hitmarker.net/news/ubisoft-says-ai-generated-loading-screen-was-accidentally-shipped-in-anno-117-pax-romana-1578275)
- Placeholder escape is itself a pipeline failure: Ubisoft (it "slipped through our review process"), 11 bit (an "internal oversight") and Sandfall (patched after launch, award still taken back). [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/ubisoft-says-ai-generated-art-in-anno-117-was-a-placeholder-which-slipped-through-our-review-process); [The SixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/06/30/11-bit-studios-releases-statement-over-use-of-ai-in-the-alter-development/); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2025/12/21/indie-game-awards-rescinds-honours-from-clair-obscur-expedition-33-for-genai-use/202718)
- Concept artists say AI references make their work harder, and commenters argue that relying on generated images weakens a game's design identity. [TWIV on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/thisweekinvideogames.com/post/3mac3dznt6g2j)
- Developers note that separate AI-generated assets (a warrior, a potion, a chest) can look as if they come from "3 different games". This comes from community posts, so treat it as anecdotal. [itch.io post](https://itch.io/post/16647016/view-in-topic)
- Vendors concede that consistency across assets, not single-image quality, is the hard part. [Makko blog](https://blog.makko.ai/ai-character-generator-for-games/)

### Inferences
- For PILLAGERS the relevant risks are those above that land on many sprites at once: palette drift, mixed pixel density and character drift between frames or facing directions. A code-drawn renderer with a fixed palette table and integer pixel snapping avoids these by design. That is a strong argument for keeping the procedural characters and bringing the reference look into code, rather than switching to generated sprite sheets.
- The "AI look" is a style read, not only a defect read. Matching the AI reference too literally (its exact lighting and rendering habits) could itself make the game read as AI-made. Taking the targets as rules (outline weight, a 3 to 4 tone ramp, chibi proportions, ground texture density) and drawing them by hand in code reduces this risk.

### Gaps
- No direct named quotes were found from AAA art directors (on LinkedIn, X or at GDC 2025-2026) listing AI failure modes. The evidence is Rovio's documented lessons, a practitioner guide, artist surveys and the public backlash record.
- The full TWIV article text was not retrieved, only summaries, and they disagree on whether zero or one artist found AI helpful.
