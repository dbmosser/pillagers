# PILLAGERS HUD SPEC (2026-10-15)

This is the layout and messaging spec the HUD rebuild builds toward: one feed, one alert lane, a layout table with reserved zones, and an overlap check. It was written from the code at v27.25, tonight's 1080p and 4K frames, and a research pass over the reference games.

**His words this is answering (2026-10-14):**
- "ui and player messaging just isn't as clean as it should be"
- "the way that different player messages come in at different places is messy, needs to be made consistent, maybe we should just have a persistent feed ala WoW?"
- "gunc ards on the right are causing collisions now"
- "the whole game just needs ui clean up"

**What is binding and what is not:**
- Binding rulings this spec keeps:
  - AAA voice: terse caps callouts.
  - Kill feed shows everything nearby (4th 50, #35).
  - Pickup stack gets more rows (#36). The feed carries the loot lines, at 10 rows.
  - Loot labels at the box stay: image, name and value (#34).
  - Damage numbers are a setting, on by default (#33).
  - Gun card on pickup and in the backpack (2026-10-12 #34).
  - Each window shows only its own belt in 2P (#10).
  - Gun cell shows a magazine bar plus a reserve number (#7).
  - Keep all critical info (#35).
  - The never-bring-back list: no siege count, no backpack weight line, no operator voice.
- Everything else is a proposal. Section 13 lists what to ask him.
- The earlier plan, `ui-design-2026-10-12.md` section 3, is kept where it agrees. Where it disagrees, this spec wins and says why. The main change is that the feed moves from top centre to bottom-left, as built at v27.24.

**Companion file:** `hud-spec-2026-10-15.html` in this folder.
- It draws the target layout at 1080p and in five other cases.
- It runs the same solver and overlap check as Appendix A, live.

## 0. The ten decisions

1. **One feed, bottom-left.**
   - Every raid message is a line in it, including loot, kills, money and team lines.
   - It holds 10 lines, newest at the bottom. Each line is bright for 6 s, then dims and stays (WoW).
   - The top-centre message rows, the right-edge pickup stack and the right-edge co-op kill feed are deleted.
2. **One alert lane, top centre, under the nav line.**
   - One plate at a time, for threats, extraction state changes, the last minute of the clock and team emergencies only.
   - Every alert is also written to the feed.
3. **Halo-style medal row, bottom-left, between the feed and the vitals** (Halo puts medals just above the motion tracker).
   - Emblems are 48 px. Only a small `+$` echo stays at the cursor.
4. **Each corner has one job:**
   - top-left: squad / pillagers
   - top-right: minimap, then OBJECTIVES
   - bottom-left: feed, medals, status, vitals
   - bottom-right: weapon block
   - bottom-centre: belt and the action band
   - top-centre: score, nav, alert, boss
5. **One card slot**, right column, standing on the weapon block.
   - It holds the gun card on pickup, the belt hover card and the lore card, one at a time.
   - This ends the gun-card collisions for good, because nothing else may draw there.
6. **The play space is reserved.**
   - The centre of the screen, from about 24% to 80% of the height, is kept clear.
   - Only world-anchored marks may draw there: prompts, labels, nameplates and ring badges.
   - The only other things there are the cursor layer and the life band above the character.
   - The controls legend, the air strike meter, the power-up chips and the style pops all leave it.
7. **Three text steps plus one display size, at 1080p:**
   - S 18 px (the PC floor in XAG 101)
   - M 24 px
   - L 32 px
   - D 48 px, for the ammo figure, the score and DOWN / YOU DIED only
   - At 4K all sizes double.
8. **One plate and one panel.**
   - LINE PLATE: graphite .78, 4 px corners, 3 px kind rail, ink outline on the words.
   - PANEL: the menu plate, with chamfer and amber tick.
   - Nothing else. Dimmed feed lines and world labels have no plate.
9. **Colour means one thing each.**
   - Red is danger only.
   - Teal is extraction only.
   - Blue is team only.
   - Gold is reward only (medals, money, score).
   - Amber is objective and keycaps.
   - Rarity colours appear only on item names and item tiles.
10. **The layout is a table, and the table is checked.**
    - Every zone is a rectangle computed per frame from the screen size, the safe area, HUD SCALE and the text setting.
    - No two zones may share a pixel. Zones share space only over time, by what they show.
    - The check runs at 31 screen and setting cases. All 31 pass today in the solver (Appendix A).

## 1. Research: how the reference games lay out a HUD

Positions marked UNVERIFIED are the research agent's memory of the game. No source could confirm them, because gameuidatabase.com and several wikis blocked the fetcher. Check them against footage before quoting them to him. Sizes marked (est.) are estimates from remembered screenshots, not measurements.

### 1.1 Per game

**ARC Raiders (2025)**
- Compass top centre, with extractions shown on it within about 400 m. A round countdown clock sits under the compass. [arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Game_UI]
- Health and shield bottom-left, with the squad stacked there too. Weapon, ammo and the last quick-use item bottom-right. [same]
- The reload progress circle is at screen centre. [arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Settings]
- The "Hud Position: Stretched" setting anchors elements to the screen edges, or to a fixed box on ultrawide. [same]
- F10 hides the HUD. [same]
- No kill feed, no enemy HP bars, no damage numbers, by design. Stefan Strandberg: numbers mean "playing the Excel sheet of a game". [gamingbolt.com, ARC Raiders article]
- A downed player fires a flare and Morse audio plays. [vidaextra.com]

**Fortnite BR**
- Minimap top-right. Under it, one row: storm timer, players alive, eliminations. Health and shield bottom-left. Inventory quickbar bottom-right. [gfinityesports.com beginners guide]
- Per-element HUD toggles. [shacknews.com/article/110245] Among them are "Pickup Loot Stream" and "Elimination Feed".
- HUD Scale setting. [gamerevolution]
- Damage numbers can be "Cumulative" or "List". [sportskeeda]
- **Epic's message device is the clearest published queue rule.** [dev.epicgames.com, HUD Message device]
  - Default 5 s on screen.
  - One message per layer, the rest queued.
  - Priority number, lower wins; a higher priority pushes the shown one back into the queue.
  - "Display Immediately" for critical lines.
  - The same message again resets its time by default.
  - Preset spots: Top Center, Bottom Center, Center Right.
- UNVERIFIED: the elimination feed sits left, lower-middle. The loot stream ("+30 LIGHT AMMO") sits right, above the quickbar, and merges counts.

**Call of Duty (MW2019 to BO6)**
- MW2019 official layout [blog.activision.com, MW2019 controls and settings]:
  - Compass top-middle. Weapon, ammo, fire mode, equipment and killstreaks bottom-right.
  - Score and timer bottom-left.
  - Contextual prompts (reload, mount, capture progress) "will appear on the center of your HUD".
  - A "HUD bounds" margin setting.
- Warzone (2021) made the kill feed's time on screen a setting, which can also turn it off. [dexerto.fr]
- BO6 ships 10 HUD presets, with move and resize for the minimap, ammo and equipment. Classic layout: minimap top-left, health bottom-left, ammo and streaks bottom-right. [insider-gaming.com]
- UNVERIFIED: the kill feed sits bottom-left above the score block. "+100" and medal splashes pop near the crosshair.

**Halo Reach / Halo Infinite (medals)**
- Multikill window: 4 s in Reach and earlier, 4.5 s from Halo 4 on. [halopedia.org Triple_Kill_Medal; List_of_Halo_4_medals]
- Reach awards every lower multikill medal along with the top one. [halopedia.org List_of_Halo:_Reach_medals]
- Sprees count in 5s. Reach has 113 medals. Infinite has about 151 in 7 categories and 4 rarity tiers. [halopedia.org]
- After the Halo 5 beta, 343 cut "the frequency and number of medals displayed in the medal feed" and made the announcer call fewer events. [gamerevolution, Halo 5]
- The motion tracker is bottom-left. [halopedia.org Motion_tracker] The "X killed Y" feed scrolls up the lower-left. [forum snippet]
- UNVERIFIED: medals form a horizontal row lower-left, just above the tracker and under the event feed. Each icon is about 3-4% of the height and shows about 2-3 s.
- Infinite was criticised for small, over-styled HUD text. [caniplaythat.com Halo Infinite review]

**Helldivers 1**
- All four players share one screen, locally and online. [co-optimus; Steam discussions]
- Stratagems: hold to open the radio, enter the d-pad code, throw. [helldivers.wiki.gg]
- Identity is in the world: colour-coded markers and tags (UNVERIFIED).

**Zero Sievert**
- The font cannot be resized. [steamdeckhq.com review]
- Extraction points are green circles at the map edges, seen on the full map. [itemlevel.net; godisageek.com]
- HUD positions: not found.

**Enter the Gungeon**
- Co-op: money is shared and health is separate. A dead partner becomes a ghost whose colour shows whether his blank is ready. [enterthegungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Co-op]
- UNVERIFIED: hearts and blanks top-left, gun and clip bottom-right, minimap top-right. Player 2's HUD mirrors on the right. Boss names appear on a letterboxed intro card.

**Accessibility and screen-area standards**
- XAG 101 minimum text body height at 1080p: **18 px on PC**, **26 px on console** (36 / 52 at 4K). Players should be able to scale text to 200%. [devdocs.xbox.com xag-101]
- Game Accessibility Guidelines: 28 px at 1080p as a minimum. [gameaccessibilityguidelines.com]
- Title safe is 90% of the screen: 1720x1000 at 1080p. Action safe is about 93%. [devdocs.xbox.com screen-areas; Netflix]
- Reading speed is about 0.3-0.375 s per word (BBC subtitle rate 160-180 wpm). A 4-word callout needs at least 1.2 s. [clevercast.com, BBC guidelines]

### 1.2 What the games agree on (the rules this spec takes)

1. **Corner roles are stable.**
   - Self status goes bottom-left (ARC, Fortnite, CoD BO6, Halo's tracker).
   - Weapon and ammo go bottom-right (ARC, CoD, Fortnite).
   - Map or compass goes on top.
2. **What you must act on goes where the eye already is.**
   - CoD puts prompts at the centre and ARC puts the reload circle at the centre.
   - In a top-down game the eye is on the cursor and the character, so:
     - hit, reload and heal-wind-up feedback go on the reticle
     - prompts go on the object in the world
     - the player's own life state goes just above the character
3. **The event feed is a side column, never the centre.** Halo and (UNVERIFIED) CoD and Fortnite put it on the left.
4. **One message per layer, with a priority queue.** Run several layers rather than one shared stack: a critical band, a side feed, a reward row. The same message again refreshes, never stacks.
5. **Merge, never spam.** Count repeats (xN), refresh timers, show only the top medal of a chain (Halo 5's cut).
6. **Lifetimes: 4-6 s for feed lines; about 3.5 s for a 2-4 word alert** (reading time 1.2-1.5 s, plus room to look away).
7. **Chain windows of 4-4.5 s.** The game's ARCADE_STREAK_GAP is 4 s, which matches Reach.
8. **Every element can be scaled or hidden.**
   - Fortnite has per-element toggles and HUD Scale.
   - CoD has HUD bounds and presets.
   - ARC has the F10 hide-all.
   - Ship at least: safe area, HUD SCALE, feed lifetime, damage numbers on/off.
9. **Text floor of 18 px at a desk, 26-28 px from a couch.** The pad window in 2P is a couch case.
10. **Shared-screen co-op puts identity in the world. Two-window co-op gives each window its full corner set**, with the partner as a squad card.

## 3. The target layout

### 3.1 Principles

1. **Corners and edges hold instruments; the middle holds the game.**
   - The play space is everything between the left column (ends 472) and the right column (starts 1488 / 1568), from the boss row (y 266) down to the action band (y 880).
   - Only world-anchored marks, the cursor layer and the life band may draw there.
2. **One zone per element. Zones never share pixels.** Two things share a zone only over time, by a stated priority (3.4).
3. **Every zone comes from one table**, computed per frame from W, H, the safe inset, HUD SCALE, the text setting and the minimap size. Nothing places itself by measuring its neighbours.
4. **Every message has exactly one home lane, and possibly the feed as its record.** No line is drawn in two lanes at once, except alert + feed and medal + feed.
5. **The eye is on the cursor** (2.5D rule):
   - hit, reload, low-ammo, heal wind-up and the reward echo go at the cursor
   - verbs go on the object
   - the player's own life state goes over the character

### 3.2 Safe frame and scale

- **u** = `hudRes()`: `clamp(min(W/1920, H/1080), 1, 2.2)`. The code already does this.
  - **Added:** below 900 tall, u falls to `H/1080` (floor 0.67), because a 1080p HUD does not fit in a 720p window.
  - His screens (1080p, 4K, and the P2 popup at about 1728x940) all keep u ≥ 1.
- **Safe inset:** 32 u on each side by default.
  - **TV SAFE AREA** setting: 5% a side (96 / 54 at 1080p, 192 / 108 at 4K), within title-safe.
  - CoD calls this "HUD bounds". It is already in the ui-design plan as `uiSafe()`.
- **HUD SCALE** setting 85-115%. This scales geometry only (Fortnite's HUD Scale).
- **Text setting.**
  - The HUD follows his text-size setting up to **150%**. 175% and 200% go to the menus only.
  - A narrow window caps HUD text at 125%.
  - Inside the solver: text grows zone heights at the full rate and zone widths at half the rate. Lanes show fewer lines rather than overflow.
- **Ultrawide:** zones anchor to corners and edges at u, so they stay at the edges.
  - A later option "HUD BOX 16:9" can clamp L and R to a centred 16:9 box, like ARC's fixed horizontal boundary.

### 3.6 How it scales and degrades (the solver, Appendix A)

The table is produced by `hudLayout(W,H,opts)` (Appendix A, ES3 so it runs under `cscript` on his PC) in this order:

1. **Bottom edge.**
   - The belt slot is `min(80, room)`. The room is the space between the vitals and the weapon block.
   - If a slot would drop under 56, the window is **narrow**. In a narrow window the belt takes the full width and the two corners stand on it.
2. **Then, upward:** action band, status row, medal row.
3. **Top edge:** minimap, score, nav, squad. The alert width is limited by the minimap and, if the squad reaches down that far, by the squad. Then the boss row.
4. **Right column.**
   - OBJECTIVES sits under the minimap and the card stands on the weapon block.
   - If they do not both fit, in this order:
     1. the card drops stat rows, down to name, verdict and 4 rows (240)
     2. OBJECTIVES shows fewer lines, down to 3
     3. the card steps left of the column
5. **Life band:** centred, kept off the card slot.
6. **Feed:** as many of its 10 lines as fit between the squad zone and the medal row, never under 4.
   - The squad first drops to one teammate card, then to its one line.

## 6. The alert lane

**Moves out of the alert lane:**
- DOUBLE KILL / TRIPLE KILL / MULTI KILL / RAMPAGE / UNSTOPPABLE, NEW HIGH SCORE → medal row + feed
- Power-up pickups (said as `ext` today) → action band chip + feed
- SCORE LOST → feed (danger) + a red flash on the score
- STILL PLAYING? and RUN ENDS IN {N} → life band
- PLAYER 2 IS IN A RAID → feed
- {N} MINUTES LEFT for N ≥ 2 → feed (kept)

**Queue** (the v22 rules stand; restated so the rebuild keeps them):
1. **Same text or same key:** refresh in place. No new fade, no new start.
2. **P0:** shows now.
3. **Same or higher rank than the one showing**, once that one has shown 2.0 s: replaces it. An alert replaced with more than 1 s left goes back to the queue with its remaining life.
4. **Otherwise it waits.** The queue holds 3, ranked; overflow drops the lowest. A waiting line drops out after 2.5 s (a displaced one after 5 s, a held one after 8 s). It is already in the feed, so nothing is lost.
5. **Cap:** the plate is at most 640 u wide. Headlines run up to about 28 characters and details up to 32. Longer strings are rewritten, never shrunk.

## Appendix A: the solver and overlap check (ES3, runs under `cscript`)

**How to run it on his PC** (no node needed):
1. Concatenate this code with a small runner that calls `hudLayout(W,H,{...})` and prints `hudOverlaps()`.
2. Run `cscript //nologo run.js`.

The same code drives `hud-spec-2026-10-15.html`.

```js
function hudClamp(v,a,b){ return v<a?a:(v>b?b:v); }
function hudLayout(W,H,o){
  o=o||{};
  var u=Math.min(W/1920,H/1080); if(H>=900) u=Math.max(u,1); u=hudClamp(u,0.67,2.2);
  var tx=hudClamp(o.text||1,1,1.5), hs=hudClamp(o.hs||1,0.85,1.15);
  var g=u*hs, k=u*hs*(1+(tx-1)*0.5), t=u*hs*tx, mmK=o.mm||1, mates=(o.mates===undefined)?1:o.mates;
  var ix=o.tv?Math.round(W*0.05):Math.round(32*u), iy=o.tv?Math.round(H*0.05):Math.round(32*u);
  var L=ix, R=W-ix, T=iy, B=H-iy, CX=W/2, Z=[], byId={};
  function add(id,x,y,w,h,z){ var r={id:id,x:Math.round(x),y:Math.round(y),w:Math.round(w),h:Math.round(h),z:z}; Z.push(r); byId[id]=r; return r; }
  function bot(r){ return r.y+r.h; }
  function rgt(r){ return r.x+r.w; }
  var VW=440*k, WW=400*k, m=16*g, gap=8*g, grp=8*g;
  var halfRoom=Math.min(CX-(L+VW+m),(R-WW-m)-CX);
  var slot=Math.min(80*g,(2*halfRoom-8*gap-2*grp)/9);
  var narrow=slot<56*g, half=(R-L)/2-8*g;
  if(narrow&&tx>1.25){ tx=1.25; k=u*hs*(1+(tx-1)*0.5); t=u*hs*tx; VW=440*k; WW=400*k; }
  var belt, vit, wep, act, sta, med, mm, obj, card, feed, score, nav, alert, boss, life, squad, bw, aw, lw, n, fb, ft, sqH, cw, avail, objH, cardH, fw, medH;
  medH=Math.max(64*g,44*t+8*g);
  if(!narrow){
    bw=9*slot+8*gap+2*grp;
    belt=add('belt',CX-bw/2,B-(slot+8*g),bw,slot+8*g,3);
    vit=add('vitals',L,B-96*g,VW,96*g,3);
    wep=add('weapon',R-WW,B-184*Math.max(g,t*0.85),WW,184*Math.max(g,t*0.85),3);
    aw=Math.min(800*k,2*halfRoom);
    act=add('action',CX-aw/2,belt.y-8*g-72*t,aw,72*t,4);
    sta=add('status',L,vit.y-8*g-36*t,VW,36*t,3);
  } else {
    slot=Math.min(80*g,((R-L)-8*gap-2*grp)/9);
    bw=9*slot+8*gap+2*grp;
    belt=add('belt',CX-bw/2,B-(slot+8*g),bw,slot+8*g,3);
    vit=add('vitals',L,belt.y-8*g-96*g,Math.min(VW,half),96*g,3);
    wep=add('weapon',R-Math.min(WW,half),belt.y-8*g-96*Math.max(g,t*0.85),Math.min(WW,half),96*Math.max(g,t*0.85),3);
    aw=Math.min(800*k,R-L);
    act=add('action',CX-aw/2,Math.min(vit.y,wep.y)-8*g-72*t,aw,72*t,4);
    sta=add('status',L,act.y-8*g-36*t,Math.min(VW,half),36*t,3);
  }
  med=add('medals',L,sta.y-8*g-medH,sta.w,medH,4);
  var mmS=(narrow?160:216)*g*mmK;
  mm=add('minimap',R-mmS,T,mmS,mmS,3);
  var scW=(narrow?240:320)*k, nvW=(narrow?280:400)*k;
  score=add('score',CX-scW/2,T,scW,72*t,4);
  nav=add('nav',CX-nvW/2,bot(score)+6*g,nvW,28*t,4);
  sqH=28*t+56*t*mates; if(o.expand==='squad') sqH=Math.max(sqH,472*t);
  squad=add('squad',L,T,Math.min(360*k,(CX-nvW/2-m)-L),sqH,3);
  aw=Math.min(640*k,2*(mm.x-12*g-CX));
  if(bot(squad)>bot(nav)+12*g) aw=Math.min(aw,2*(CX-rgt(squad)-12*g));
  alert=add('alert',CX-aw/2,bot(nav)+12*g,aw,72*t,6);
  boss=add('boss',CX-Math.min(520*k,aw)/2,bot(alert)+8*g,Math.min(520*k,aw),36*t,4);
  cw=narrow?Math.min(320*k,half):320*k;
  if(narrow){
    obj=add('objectives',R-cw,Math.max(bot(mm),bot(boss))+8*g,cw,32*t,3);
    card=add('card',R-cw,act.y-16*g-316*k,cw,316*k,4);
  } else {
    avail=(wep.y-16*g)-(bot(mm)+12*g);
    objH=(o.expand==='obj')?520*t:260*t; cardH=316*k;
    if(objH+12*g+cardH>avail){ cardH=Math.max(240*k,avail-objH-12*g); }
    if(objH+12*g+cardH>avail){ objH=Math.max(120*t,avail-12*g-cardH); }
    obj=add('objectives',R-cw,bot(mm)+12*g,cw,objH,3);
    if(objH+12*g+cardH>avail) card=add('card',obj.x-16*g-cw,wep.y-16*g-cardH,cw,cardH,4);
    else card=add('card',R-cw,wep.y-16*g-cardH,cw,cardH,4);
  }
  lw=Math.min(800*k,aw*1.25);
  if(narrow) lw=Math.min(lw,(R-L)*0.36);
  if(card.y<bot(boss)+12*g+120*t) lw=Math.min(lw,2*(card.x-16*g-CX));
  life=add('life',CX-lw/2,Math.max(bot(boss)+12*g,narrow?bot(obj)+12*g:0,H*(narrow?0.26:0.28)),lw,120*t,7);
  fw=Math.min(med.w,(life.x-16*g)-L);
  function feedFit(){ fb=med.y-12*g; for(n=10;n>4;n--){ if(fb-(n*28*t+(n-1)*4*t)>=Math.max(bot(squad)+16*g,narrow?bot(boss)+12*g:0)) break; } return n; }
  n=feedFit();
  if(n<6&&mates>1){ squad.h=Math.round(28*t+56*t); n=feedFit(); }
  if(n<4){ squad.h=Math.round(28*t); n=feedFit(); }
  ft=fb-(n*28*t+(n-1)*4*t);
  if(life.y+life.h<ft) fw=med.w;
  feed=add('feed',L,ft,fw,fb-ft,4);
  return {W:W,H:H,u:u,g:g,k:k,t:t,profile:narrow?'narrow':'wide',slot:Math.round(slot),feedN:n,inset:[ix,iy],zones:Z,byId:byId};
}
function hudOverlaps(Lo){
  var Z=Lo.zones, out=[], i, j, a, b, gm=Math.round(4*Lo.u);
  for(i=0;i<Z.length;i++){
    a=Z[i];
    if(a.x<0||a.y<0||a.x+a.w>Lo.W||a.y+a.h>Lo.H||a.w<=0||a.h<=0) out.push(a.id+' off screen');
    for(j=i+1;j<Z.length;j++){
      b=Z[j];
      if(a.x<b.x+b.w+gm&&b.x<a.x+a.w+gm&&a.y<b.y+b.h+gm&&b.y<a.y+a.h+gm) out.push(a.id+' x '+b.id);
    }
  }
  return out;
}
```
