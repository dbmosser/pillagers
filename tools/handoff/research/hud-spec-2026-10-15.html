Pillagers HUD: target layout

From hud-spec-2026-10-15.md. Every rectangle comes from the spec's solver (Appendix A), run live in this page. Pick a case: the zones are re-solved for that screen and checked, and no two zones may touch. Dashed zones show only while something is up in them. The mock content uses the game's real strings.

Text steps at 1080p: S 18, M 24, L 32, D 48 (numerals and DOWN / YOU DIED only). At 4K, double them. Colours: red is danger, teal is extraction, blue is team, gold is reward, amber is objective and keycaps. Rarity colours appear on item names only. Not shown: world marks (prompts, loot labels, nameplates, ring badges), edge chevrons, the map and the backpack.
