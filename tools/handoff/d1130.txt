## v11.30 - A BELT KEY'S HINT WAS OVERWRITTEN BEFORE ANYONE READ IT

The last of the first agent's findings worth a build, and it was deeper than
the agent said. The agent read setHot and reported that a belt key holding a
gun still in the backpack says "TAB to equip it", the wrong key (TAB opens the
backpack; ENTER equips, since v2.94, and the LEGEND row says so). True, and
the sentence was changed. Then the new check pressed the key and read the
message line: "Scav Pistol (field)  x1". Not the sentence at all, old or new.

**THE REAL DEFECT.** setHot says the backpack sentence and then, two lines
later and unconditionally, says the slot label with its count. The message
line shows the last say. So the sentence was dead: no player ever read "TAB
to equip it", wrong key or right key, and a friend who pressed the key for a
gun he could not select saw only its name and "x1" and nothing that told him
what to do. **REPRODUCED on the v11.29 fixture** by pressing 3 with a backpack
pistol bound and an SMG in hand: "Scav Pistol (field)  x1".

**THE FIX.** The sentence names ENTER, and the label yields to the sentence
when there is one, so the line reads "Scav Pistol is in your backpack. TAB,
then ENTER to equip it."

**NEW CHECK v11.30** binds a backpack pistol to key 3 with another gun in
hand, presses 3 through the game's own key handler, and requires the message
line to name ENTER and to be the backpack sentence at all (so key 3 reached
the pistol), and controls that key 1 for the gun in hand prints no such
sentence. On the v11.29 fixture it fails with "Scav Pistol (field)  x1".

Owned: my first cut of this build changed the sentence and shipped a check
that failed at once, which is what the check is for.

Not verified: the pad, whose belt buttons take the same setHot path and were
not pressed.
