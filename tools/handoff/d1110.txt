## v11.10 - THE v11.07 QUESTION ANSWERED: THE BOT CANNOT SEE THAT BUG AT ALL

At v11.07 I fixed the crawler that could not see a hidden man it was touching,
and said plainly that I had not measured what it does to the bot's extract rate.
This is that measurement, and the answer is about the instrument rather than the
game.

**THE PAIRED RUN IS NOT AFFORDABLE HERE.** One paired seed takes about thirty five
seconds in this environment, so the 320 pairs the standard asks for is several
hours, and a single pair outlives the window I can drive a call in. Seven pairs
completed before I stopped it: zero discordant, and neither arm extracted once,
which is a saturated result that says nothing. I am not going to spend more ticks
on a number I cannot finish.

**SO I MEASURED THE THING THAT DECIDES THE ANSWER.** The bug only fires when
concealment falls far enough that a crawler's sight, 100 units times concealment,
drops under its own 36 unit reach. That needs concealment below about **0.36**.

**MEASURED**, three seeded bot raids, 1,647 steps: crawlers were inside biting
distance of the bot for **693 frames**, and **zero** of those frames were in the
blind band. Over 300 frames the bot's own concealment ran **0.40 at its lowest**,
1.00 at its highest, **0.884 on average**, and it crouched in **not one frame**,
because the simCrouch dial is 0 and has been for its whole life.

**THE BOT NEVER GETS THERE.** It cannot exhibit the bug. That is why the seven
pairs showed no difference and why three hundred more would have shown none
either. The bug only ever reached a HUMAN who hides, which is exactly why he hit
it twice and why no number I have ever run showed it.

**NOTHING IN THE GAME CHANGED IN THIS BUILD.** What it adds is the limit, written
where it cannot be forgotten, because the mistake it prevents is one I was a
sentence away from making: quoting a sim number as though it covered how he
actually plays.

**NEW CHECK v11.10** is not shaped like the others and I am not going to pretend
it is. There is no defect here, so there is no control that fails on the previous
build: it passes on v11.09 and on v11.10 alike, and it is written to fail in the
FUTURE. It reads the crouch dial, which needs no raid at all, and fails if the
bot is ever allowed to crouch, because every extract rate in this file would then
describe a different player from the one that produced them. It steps one short
raid and fails if the bot's concealment ever reaches the band, because that would
mean the sim CAN see this class of bug and the reasoning above must be replaced
with a measurement. And where the bot simply meets no crawler, that is recorded
as a gap in the sample rather than counted as a fault in the game.

Not verified: the extract rate effect itself, which remains unmeasured and, on
the evidence above, unmeasurable by this bot. If he wants a real number it needs
either a bot that hides or a person, and the person is him.
