v12.66: the contract board counts contracts

The board's only progress line says "Contracts completed N". N is not a count of
contracts. It is a weighted number: a STANDARD card counts one, a HARD two, an
ELITE three. Claim your first ever HARD card and the board says you have
completed two contracts. The gate under it is wrong from your side for the same
reason: "ELITE work at 6 more" means six more of that weight, not six cards.

The entry for that line is mine. It records a deliberate vocabulary swap with no
look at the number behind it: the line used to say "Contract standing", which
was true, and standing is a banned word, so I renamed it to "completed", which
is false. Renaming is not the same as reading.

The count and the gate are two different numbers, so both are said now, each
called what it is. No arithmetic changes: the credit that opens harder work is
untouched and the thresholds are where they were.

Check 12.66 claims one HARD card, which is the smallest claim whose weight and
count differ, and reads the drawn line; the control requires the credit and the
gate wording to be unchanged; fails on v12.65.
