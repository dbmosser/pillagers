v12.77: dying with the freebie kit gives your tactical belt back too

Both places that hand you the freebie kit keep a snapshot of all three parts of a
loadout: the items, the tactical belt plan and the gun slot. The button that
gives you your own gear back reads all three, which is why it works.

The handoff in the middle took only the items and threw the snapshot away. So the
belt plan and the gun slot were gone the moment you took the kit, and the restore
at the end of a raid had nothing left but a list of items.

A new player takes the welcome pack, drags a Medkit onto key 1 and a Frag onto
key 2, takes the kit, dies, and is told his loadout has been restored. The items
are back. Both keys are blank.

All three travel together now, with the plan filtered to what he still owns, and
the line says how many keys came back.

Check 12.77 presses the real button, runs the real handoff and kills him in a
real raid; controls require a key bound to something he no longer owns to be
dropped, and a clean extraction to restore nothing. Fails on v12.76.
