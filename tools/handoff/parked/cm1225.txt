v12.25: the ring you called keeps the pointer

Standing in a second open ring while the extraction was inbound to another made
the second ring the active one, so the banner and the arrow named a ring no
extraction was coming to, with the called ring's seconds. The ring you stand in
wins the pointer only when it carries the beacon or no ring does, and the
ring you call takes the pointer as the call lands. Check
12.25 calls a beacon at one ring, stands in another, ticks and requires the
called ring to stay active with its clock; with no beacon the ring stood in
wins; fails on v12.24.
