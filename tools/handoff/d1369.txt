## v13.69 - YOUR OWN CHARGE DOES NOT KILL YOUR HIRE

The hire and peddler audit of 2026-09-14, finding 2. Your rounds pass through your hire
(v6.72), your fists do too (v11.38), and the hire screen tells you your shots cannot hurt
him. The frag loop had no such rule. A charge does up to 140 damage, a hire has 78 or less,
and a hire is never downed, so a charge thrown at something near him killed him outright with
byPlayer set. That did four things:

- It put a kill on that identity's record, so he refuses to work for you from then on.
- It ticked a pillager kill contract.
- It billed his death benefit at the extraction.
- A charge that only wounded him set him chasing you.

THE BUILD. When the charge is yours, the frag loop skips your standing hire. An enemy charge
still hurts him.

MEASURED. Check 13.69 bursts your charge 40 units from a 78-health hire and requires his
health, the kill ledger and his state untouched. An enemy charge in the same spot must wound
him (the control). It fails on v13.68.

Not verified: a downed hire in the blast, which the rule leaves to take damage as before.
