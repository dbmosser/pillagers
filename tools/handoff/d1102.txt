## v11.02 - HIS NOTE 8: WHY CHANGING SAVE LEAVES FULLSCREEN

"if i click fullscreen and then click to change the save, it kicks me out of
fullscreen --why??", 2026-09-04.

**THE ANSWER, AND IT IS NOT A BUG I CAN PATCH AWAY.** Clicking another save writes
the slot pointer and calls location.reload, because the profile key is read once
at file scope and the entire game is built from it. Every browser drops
fullscreen on a page load. It cannot be re-entered afterwards on its own, because
requestFullscreen requires a user gesture and a gesture does not survive a
navigation. So there are exactly two honest fixes: switch saves WITHOUT
reloading, which means re-reading the profile in place and re-initialising every
panel built from it, a deep change I am not making 55 hours from an alpha; or
stop it being a mystery and put the way back one click away. This is the second,
and I am saying plainly that it is the second.

**WHAT HE GETS.** A line under the saves, permanently: changing save reloads the
game, which is why it leaves fullscreen. And, if he WAS in fullscreen at the
moment he switched, the title screen tells him so on the way back in, with GO
FULLSCREEN sitting directly above it. The flag lives in its own storage key,
never the profile, because it is about the browser and the tab rather than about
a character, and it has to survive a switch between two saves without belonging
to either. It is read once and cleared once, so the line appears on the reload it
belongs to and never again.

**NEW CHECK v11.02** requires the saves panel to say both words, reload and
fullscreen, where the click is; requires switching while in fullscreen to be
remembered and switching while NOT in fullscreen to be remembered as nothing;
requires the line to appear once, to clear its own flag, and to be gone on the
next boot. One more control: it must not tell him he lost fullscreen while he is
in fullscreen, which is the one case where the line would be noise. Fullscreen
itself cannot be entered by a check, since a browser demands a gesture, so the
check makes the game believe it is in fullscreen and puts the real answer back
afterwards.

Fails on a v11.01 fixture on both halves: no explanation in the panel, and no
memory of having been in fullscreen.

**A CHECK BUG OF MINE, FIXED IN THE SAME BUILD.** The guard for the missing
memory used a return, which threw away the panel finding that had already been
recorded, so the old build reported half the defect. A guard that returns
discards what was already found; it pushes and returns the whole list now.

Not verified: whether he would rather have the deep fix, switching saves without
a reload, which would keep fullscreen and is the only thing that actually answers
his question with a no. That is a real piece of work and it is his call whether
it happens before or after the alpha.
