## v12.43 - AIMING AND WADING NO LONGER LEAVE A SPRINT TRAIL

From the 2026-09-07 read-only audit (P3), specced from the source. Verified by
reading at v12.42.

WHAT IT WAS. The order of three lines. The sprint flag is computed, then the
scent marks are laid, and only THEN is the flag cancelled for aiming and again
for deep water. So holding the sprint key while you aimed, or while you waded,
laid a sprint trail behind a man who was not sprinting. He moved at the aiming
speed or the wading speed, he paid no stamina for it, the readout said AIMING,
and a line of scent went down behind him anyway.

That list is not decoration. Everything on patrol or investigating within 170
units smells it and follows it. So the two moments you are slowest and least
able to leave are the two moments you were quietly marking your own path.

v12.33 stopped drawing your own marks, which is his note about seeing two
trails. This is the half that survives that build, and it is the half that gets
you killed: the marks were still going into the list, and the list is what
hunts you.

THE BUILD. The block moves below both cancels, so it reads the final answer
instead of an early one. Same 34 units of spacing, same ceiling of 40 marks,
same flag on each one. Only the moment the question is asked has moved.

IT CANNOT BE MEASURED BY THE BOT. The sim steps the player through a different
function entirely; this one is never called there. So no paired number moves,
and that is a limit of the instrument rather than a claim of no effect. What
does change for a real player: aiming and wading are now quiet, which they were
always supposed to be. Nothing in the settings moves.

MEASURED. Check 12.43 drives the real keys through the real frame loop, which
is the only driver that moves the player and lays a mark. The control runs
first and on dry land: a plain sprint must cover at least 250 units and lay at
least three marks, or the check skips rather than passing, because without a
trail there is nothing to prove. It then repeats the same run in the same
direction with the aim held and requires zero marks of his own. The wading arm
places him in deep water found by scanning the map, and counts only the frames
he is still actually in the water, so a mark laid after he walked out can never
be read as a mark laid while wading. If the water cannot be staged the whole
check reports as a skip that names what was and was not measured, because half
a check is not a pass.

Not verified: the wading arm places him in the water rather than walking him
into it, which is a placement and not a route; the aim is set on the player
rather than driven through the mouse button; no frame is drawn, so this reads
the scent list itself and not the screen; and the machines that follow the list
are not run, since what is at issue is whether the marks exist at all.
