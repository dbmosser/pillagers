v12.69: the XP a run pays is for what you brought back

The same fault as v12.65, on the other side of the same number.

The XP a run pays for its haul was worked out from the whole backpack, and the
backpack lands already holding whatever you staged. There is no limit on what
you can stage, and every one of those items is handed straight back to the stash
on extraction.

So put a hundred thousand of your own stash in the backpack, ride up, walk to
the nearest extraction point, hold E. That pays about three and a half thousand
haul XP, multiplied up by night and weather and a dose to near six thousand, and
the stash comes back untouched. Repeat. The whole reward track can be walked
with nothing found and nothing lost.

It also poisons my own numbers: the haul on the run record and the career best
both counted value that was never looted, so every haul figure I have quoted off
that log is inflated by whatever you were carrying up.

The run earnings line has always subtracted what the lift carried in. These two
sites never did.

Honestly, this removes an XP faucet, so the track is longer than it was for
anyone who had found that trip. No rate changes, and a haul actually found in
the raid pays exactly what it always did.

Check 12.69 measures the XP as a difference between two records identical but
for where the value came from; fails on v12.68.
