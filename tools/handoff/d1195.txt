## v11.95 - HIS ORDER: GUNS DRAG LIKE ANY OTHER ITEM

His messages of 2026-09-06, about 13:45: "dragging items, especially guns,
in the stash isn't working right"; "GUNS in particular are fucked up, for
some reason you keep treating them like they are special but they need to
just move around like any other inventory item"; "in stash, blue and purple
smgs won't drag onto tac belt".

WHY THEY WERE SPECIAL. A gun you own lives in the armoury (the rack, with
the two figure slots), not in the stash. Every gun that comes home in your
hands is banked into the armoury, so the stash almost never holds a gun
item, and the rack cells were made deliberately not draggable at v8.20b:
an owned gun had no stash item behind it, so planPut had nothing to pack
and every drop target refused. Right-click to equip was the only verb, and
a blue or purple SMG he had carried out could not be put on a belt key at
all.

THE BUILD. Rack cells are drag sources. A drop on a belt key or on the
backpack (on the stash screen or on the ascent check) goes through two new
helpers: rackToStash moves the gun out of the armoury into the stash as its
item form, and rackPut then packs it and binds the key exactly as planPut
does for any item. A gun on the figure (gun 1 or gun 2) is not moved: it
goes up in your hands already, so a drop on a key binds the key alone and a
drop on the backpack says so. In the raid the bound key brings the gun up
the way v11.91 made every belt gun key work; when he extracts, a gun in
his hands is banked into the armoury again, so a rack gun that went up on
a key comes back to the rack.

MEASURED. Check 11.95 enters the floor with a pistol on the figure and an
SMG in the rack, renders the stash screen, requires the SMG rack cell to be
a drag source, drops it on belt key 4 through the real drop handler and
requires it bound, in the stash, packed and gone from the armoury; then
drops the figure pistol on key 6 and requires the key bound with the pistol
still on the figure and not copied into the stash. On the v11.94 fixture
the rack cell is not draggable and the drop is refused as not in the
stash.
