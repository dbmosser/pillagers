v12.76: how far you died from extraction is the nearest way out

The death card says how many metres from extraction you died. That was measured
to the ring the raid nominated, which is a ring the dice picked when the raid was
built and is only re-pointed if you stand in a ring or the one it points at
closes. So a player who dies without ever reaching a ring, which is how a first
raid ends, was measured against a ring chosen at random for them.

It contradicted the compass over your head, which points at the nearest open ring
all raid, and the closest-approach line printed directly underneath it, which is
measured the same way.

The helper already existed: I wrote it at v8.58 for the closest-approach figure
and did not apply it here, twenty feet away in the same file.

Check 12.76 kills him beside one ring with the nomination on a far one and reads
the banked number, asserts its own staging, and controls that a death beside the
nominated ring reports what it always did. Fails on v12.75.
