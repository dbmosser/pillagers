v12.50: breaking a Crier line of sight now actually cancels its alarm

The cancel fired the thing it was cancelling.

Breaking a Crier's line of sight for three seconds is supposed to call the
alarm off, and the branch does the right things: back to patrol, cooldown
cleared, windup cleared to nothing. Nothing stopped the rest of that branch
running in the same frame. Four lines later the windup is decremented, and
nothing minus a number is a negative number, so the very next test saw a windup
at or below zero and fired the whole alarm: the siren, the mark, the counter,
the line saying they are coming to where it last saw you, every machine within
900 units sent to the mark and every Listener sent hunting.

So hiding from a Crier has never once cancelled an alarm. At best it brought it
forward by under a second.

The three seconds were also cumulative rather than continuous, because the
counter is zeroed only when the alarm begins. Two guards on the countdown and
one reset fix both.

Honestly, the halves pull opposite ways: the cancel now works, which favours
you, and the three seconds must be unbroken, which does not. Unbroken is what
the counterplay always claimed to be.

Check 12.50 puts one Crier alone on the map in its alarm with the counter a
hundredth of a second short of three, steps the machines once, and requires no
siren, no mark and no count; the control is the same Crier with you in plain
sight and requires the alarm to fire; fails on v12.49.
