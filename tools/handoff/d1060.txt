## v10.60 - WALLS ARE SOLID, AND NOBODY STANDS INSIDE ONE

His two notes, 2026-09-03 about 21:40 and 21:45, both with pictures:
**"walls don't need to be transparent when player is on the far side of
them, that looks goofy, wall should just be in front of or behind player"**,
and **"this glitch still happening in undercroft where pillager bots
randomly get caught on wlals"**.

They are one fault, and it is mine from earlier tonight.

### What it was

Every wall in this game is painted 26 units above the rectangle it collides
with, 14 for a small one. So the strip of floor just north of any wall is
floor you can stand on and also floor the wall is painted over. Anyone
standing there is swallowed by the wall. Since v9.27 the answer for the
player was a see-through pass: paint him again, faded, on top of whatever
covered him. He has now seen it and does not want it, and he is right that
it looks wrong.

Measured on v10.59 before changing anything, over 1,200 steps of the
Undercroft: bodies stood inside a wall's painted rectangle 1,821 times out
of 19,200 samples, eight of the fifteen people down there, and every single
one of those moments had the wall painting over the body. One counter
holder stood inside the south wall's picture permanently. That is what he
is seeing as people caught on walls.

### Now

In the Undercroft nobody may stand inside a wall's picture. The room keeps
a second list of rectangles, each wall grown upward by the same lift the
renderer draws it with, and the operator, the crowd, the counter holders
and the hired man all collide with those instead of with the smaller
rectangle underneath. The crowd is placed against the pictures when the
room is built, so nobody starts inside one, and a counter holder's home
moves with him. With nobody standing where a wall paints, nothing needs to
be see-through, so the dial that draws a faded copy of you through a wall is
off. A wall is now simply in front of you or behind you.

The pass itself is still in the file behind that dial, in the raid and in
the Undercroft, so it can be turned back on without a build.

### Measured

The check works out each wall's painted rectangle from the collider and the
same lift the renderer uses, so it cannot inherit a mistake from the list
the game builds. It requires the transparency dial to be off out of the
box, then steps the room 1,200 times at a twentieth of a second, a minute
of floor, and after every step tests all fifteen bodies and the operator:
nobody may be inside a picture. On v10.59 it fails at once with the counter
holder standing in the south wall. On this build it returns null.


The dry run and the corpus caught three things before anything shipped. The
room's own walking box reaches into the south wall's picture, so a clamp was
putting bodies straight back inside the wall after the push; the push now
runs after every clamp. The v10.51 check pinned the operator at the plinth's
collider, which is a spot nobody can stand in any more, so it pins him at
the edge of the picture instead and its see-through half is gone. And the
one that mattered most: coming down to the floor REBUILDS the people, and
that second call passed the colliders and skipped the placement pass, so
the fix held on the first visit and was undone on every visit after it.
There is one way to build the crowd now and it places them clear of the
pictures, so a third caller cannot get it wrong either.

The same corpus run also failed the what-is-new card, which is the first
thing a friend reads on the way in: it was written at v10.45 against a
build at v10.60, fifteen builds of news missing, four of them things he
asked for tonight. The card now names the outfits, the gun voices, the
footsteps and the solid walls, and its own version moves with this build.
