## v10.60 - NOBODY IN THE UNDERCROFT WALKS THROUGH A WALL

His note, 2026-09-03 about 21:20: **"pillagers still glitching into walls in
the undercroft"**.

### What it was

The errand walk (v8.07) already called the wall push every step, and the
push did nothing: it keeps a body clear of a wall by the body's radius, and
the loose crowd and the hired man were built without one, so the test was
never true and every walker went straight through the piers, the counters
and the lift housing. The counter staff have a radius and drift home
without any push at all, so a shove from a passer-by could leave one inside
his own counter until he had walked back out of it; and the separation pass
and the hired man's follow move bodies with no push either.

### Now

Every body has a radius, and every move that changes a position is followed
by the same wall push the operator takes: the errand walk, the drift home,
the separation shove and the follow.

### Measured

The check enters the Undercroft, closes any window an earlier check left
open, requires every body to have a radius, then steps the room 1,200 times
at a twentieth of a second, a minute of floor, and after every step tests
each body against every wall: a body is in a wall when the wall's rectangle
comes closer than half the body's radius. It requires zero such moments.
On v10.59 the loose crowd has no radius and the errands carry walkers
through the piers, which is the finding; on this build the check returns
null.

Not verified: the counter staff being shoved by the operator himself, who
takes no part in the separation pass; and bodies that are up top in the
lift, which the check skips because they are not in the room.
