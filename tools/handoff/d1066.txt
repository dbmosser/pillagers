## v10.66 - A NEW CHARACTER ACTUALLY GETS THE BRIEFING

Not one of his notes. This is the alpha: it is the first thing a friend
meets on Saturday, and it was broken.

### What it was

Arriving in the Undercroft on a brand new save opens the primer, FIRST TIME
OUT, "what is worth knowing before you take the lift up; the first three are
the ones that get people killed". Then, on the very next line, it opens the
welcome pack. Opening a window in this game closes every other one, so the
primer was destroyed the instant it appeared and nobody ever read a word of
it.

Measured on v10.65 with a fresh profile. On arrival only the welcome pack is
up; the primer is gone and its seen flag is still 0, so the game still
thinks it owes him the card. It pays that debt only on a SECOND arrival at
the floor with no raid in between, which is not what anybody does: you take
the pack, walk to the lift and go up. And the moment that first raid is
behind him, the primer sees a run on the profile, stamps itself as seen and
never opens again. The one card that teaches the game was the one card a new
player could never read.

### Now

The three first-run cards queue instead of painting over each other. The
welcome pack first, because it is a gift and it is the reason he is standing
there. Then the consent question, when there is somewhere to send reports at
all. Then the primer. Closing one brings up the next, and nothing decides
whether a card is owed except the rule that card already had.

The primer is also owed until it is READ rather than until a raid happens.
v7.88 stamped it away for any profile with a raid behind it so that an old
save would not meet the onboarding card late, which was right, but it also
silenced it for exactly the player it was written for. It now stays owed for
the first three raids, which is where a new player has stopped being new,
and then stamps itself quietly as before.

### Measured

The check drives a brand new save five ways. On arrival, the welcome pack
and exactly one window. Taking the pack brings up FIRST TIME OUT, and only
that, with the pack's guns and items really in the armoury and the stash, so
this is a queue and not a swap. Closing the pack with CLOSE does the same. A
player who takes the pack, goes straight up and comes back from his first
raid still meets the primer. And a player with nine raids behind him does
not, or it is a nag. On v10.65 the second, fourth and fifth of those fail.

Not verified: how the two cards feel one after another to a real person,
which is his to judge; and the consent question's place in the queue, which
cannot fire at all until the public drop is set, so it was reasoned about
rather than driven.
