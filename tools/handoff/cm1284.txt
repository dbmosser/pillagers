v12.84: equipping a gun tells you what happened to the one it replaced

Found by tools/lint.ps1, the same defect as v12.73 in a second place. The lint
named five functions; I read all five and three are false alarms.

Equipping a gun from the backpack says two things into a message line that holds
one string: what happened to the gun it displaced, then what you are holding. The
first is destroyed by the second every time.

The lost line is the one you cannot work out yourself. "Left behind" means a
loaner is gone for good; "it goes back to the armoury" means a gun you own is
waiting at base rather than in your backpack. You were told neither, only the
name of the gun you had just chosen.

Check 12.84 equips three ways and reads the line you are left with; the control
equips over an empty hand and requires no displaced-gun line. Fails on v12.83.
