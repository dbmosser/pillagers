v12.49: a Howler shell no longer reaches through a wall

The Howler blast had three gates and none of them was a wall: how far away you
are, whether you are already down, and whether the shell burst on a roof over
your head. That third gate does a real job, but it is inert whenever the shell
lands in the open, because there is no roof on a street. So the blast reached
ninety units in every direction through anything at all.

The shape is ordinary. You duck into a building. The Howler aimed at where it
last saw you, thirty units outside the door, and the shell was already two
seconds in the air, so it lands where it was aimed. Standing forty units inside
a solid wall you lose about twenty health through masonry, with the hit ring
pointing at a burst you never saw.

The frag blast four lines above tests the line of sight for exactly this. This
is the game agreeing with itself, not a new rule. The roof rule is untouched.

Honestly: buildings now protect you from a Howler the way they always looked as
though they did, so cover is worth more than it was. No dial moves, the damage
and radius are untouched, and the same shell in the open is unchanged.

Check 12.49 finds a row of a real building wall the map's own segments block,
confirms the burst is in the open, runs the open-ground control first, then
requires the man behind the wall to lose nothing and a pillager behind it to
lose nothing while the same pillager in the open is still hit; fails on v12.48.
