v10.85: the hum stops when the raid does

His note about hums that last way too long after you die. Found by counting: the
file starts 40 oscillators and stops 38, and the two never stopped are the
ambient bed's sines, which are meant to be silenced by turning their gain down.

The only thing that turns that gain down is tickAmbience, called from one place
inside the raid loop. End a raid and it is never written again, so the bed
freezes at its last level through the card and the Undercroft. It freezes loud,
because the level follows the nearest hunting body and the last frame of a raid
you lose is the frame something was on top of you.

ambienceOff cuts all three ambient gains from the top of endRaid, cancelling the
in-flight ramp first so it cannot pull them back up. No dial is touched.

The check enforces his rule as well as the fix: cut once per ending, never
during play, and the count of never-stopped oscillators stays at two.
