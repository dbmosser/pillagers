v12.37: a key already down is not a press

The one self-revive is an edge trigger, and the whole file sets its latch in
exactly one place: the line inside the downed branch that reads the F key and
the latch together. The two other lines that name it only clear it. So the
latch is false while you are standing, and a man who already had F down when
the hit landed reached the floor with it false. The very first downed frame
read that held key as a fresh press and spent his one self-revive with no
decision made. F is the melee strike, so a hand resting on it through a fight
is ordinary play, and he paid a second time later with the line telling him a
revive he never chose was already gone.

One assignment at the moment he goes down latches the flag to whatever F is
doing at that instant. The revive now waits for a release and a real press,
which is what the DOWN overlay asks for. With F not held nothing changes at
all: the latch is cleared and the first real press still revives.

Check 12.37 holds F for three real frames, proves the key reached the game by
the strike it swings, puts him down with a real hit from a live crawler and
requires him still on the floor with the revive unspent. Two controls prove a
real press still stands him up with the ledger moving by one, and that a hand
that let go after the down and pressed again still revives; fails on v12.36.
