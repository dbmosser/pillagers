## v12.45 - CUTTING THE SEAL NO LONGER STOPS THE WORLD

From the 2026-09-07 read-only audit (P3), specced from the source. Verified by
reading at v12.44. This is the same fault and the same fix as v12.34, one door
along.

WHAT IT WAS. Cutting the seal returns out of the player update on every frame
of the hold, and two things live below that return. One is the only recovery
tick on the standing path. The other is the only real call to the extraction
clocks.

So for the forty to a hundred and fifteen seconds a cut takes, the health bar
was flat and the clock that decides when you start healing did not even
advance. That is thirteen points of recovery forgone on the shortest cut and
thirty eight on the longest, for a man who has usually just fought his way to
the door.

And every extraction clock was frozen with it. A ship you had already called
stopped coming. The boarding window stopped running. The re-ping stopped. No
pillager wave could arrive, at the exact moment you are making the loudest
noise in the game, standing still, with your back to the room.

The roll branch and the trade window both carry these two calls past their own
returns for exactly this reason, and the comment on the roll has said so since
v8.59. The seal was the last early return that did not.

THE BUILD. The two calls, above the return, in the order the other two branches
use them. No pull of your own: the key belongs to the seal while you are
cutting, exactly as it belongs to the panel while the trade window is open.

WHAT IT COSTS, HONESTLY. The recovery half is a pure repair: a bar that was
supposed to tick now ticks. The wave half is not free. A wave that could never
arrive during a cut can now arrive, so cutting is riskier than it has been.
Nothing in the settings moves and no dial is touched, and it is the world
behaving normally rather than a difficulty being raised, but a player who
learned the old behaviour will feel the difference. Say the word and I will
hold that half.

The bot never cuts a seal, so no paired number can measure any of it.

MEASURED. Check 12.45 holds the real key at the real seal through the real
frame loop for fifteen seconds, with the roster cleared so nothing interrupts
and a ship already inbound so the extraction clock has something to lose. Every
assertion is a RISE against a staged starting value rather than a level: the
cut advances, the recovery clock advances with it, health comes back, and the
inbound ship keeps closing. The control is the same fifteen seconds standing
beside the seal without cutting, which is what the bar is supposed to do; if
that arm cannot see recovery, the check says so rather than passing.

Not verified: it runs one map and one seed; it does not cut a seal to
completion or read the payout; it does not check that a wave actually arrives
during a cut, only that the clock which would produce one is running; and the
bot cannot reach any of it.
