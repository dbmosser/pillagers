The v9.07 noise marks (G.noiseRings, the red #ff5a4a ring for a sound he cannot see) were drawn in the world pass of render2D,
inside wc.save(); wc.scale(Z,Z); wc.translate(-ox,-oy); after the puffs and before the throws. After that pass render2D lays the
darkness sheet (wc.drawImage(litC,0,0); NIGHT_SHEET, about 0.30 by night) and then the fog of war sheet

if(canSee(p.x,p.y,p.face,wx,wy,G.vseg)) return;, so a mark only ever exists where he cannot see, which is ground under the fog
sheet (the small ambient cutout around him aside). Both sheets were laid over every mark: by night a mark reached the screen at
roughly 28 percent of its alpha. This is the fault the v4.05 note names for the pings ("the rings were being buried exactly
where they mattered"), fixed then by the ringsOnTop pass that draws G.pings after the fog; the v9.07 marks came later and went
back under the sheets, and their own comment gives world space as the only reason for the place. Gunshots also get a ping ring
on top, but some sounds have only this mark: the charge warning where a pillager grenade lands (v15.28), the Listener and Crier
windups, and the extraction inbound, landing and last call sounds. By night a grenade landing out of sight behind him showed as
a faint smudge. CFG.noiseSee is 1, so the marks are on. Draw order only, not balance.
