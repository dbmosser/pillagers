## v13.53 - HAZARD PAY IS PAID ONLY ON WHAT THE RUN BROUGHT HOME

The end-of-raid audit of 2026-09-14, finding 1. Hazard pay was the whole backpack value
times the Terms rate. The backpack at extraction includes whatever the lift carried up from
the stash, and all of it goes straight back to the stash. So packing a valuable item,
signing all five Terms and extracting paid up to 140 percent of his own item in credits,
every raid, for loot the run never found.

The same leak was already closed twice: the haul contract at v12.65 and XP at v12.69 both
subtract what the lift carried in (G.carriedIn). Hazard pay did not.

THE BUILD. The bonus is now the Terms rate times the haul minus what came up the lift,
floored at zero. Nothing else about Terms changes: a death under them still pays nothing,
and loot found in the raid pays the full rate.

MEASURED. Check 13.53 stubs the Terms rate at 50 percent and extracts twice with the same
item worth at least 600. Carried up from the stash, the extraction must not pay the hazard
bonus on it. Found in the raid, it must pay the full rate. It fails on v13.52.

Not verified: a raid that carries some items up and finds others, where the subtraction is
by value rather than by item; and the other three audit findings, which are their own
builds.
