## v13.96 - AN ENEMY CHARGE DOES NOT TURN YOUR HIRE ON YOU

The machine and pillager AI audit of 2026-09-14, finding 1. explodeFrag turns every survivor in the
blast into a chaser. Since v13.69 your own charge skips your hire, but an enemy's charge did not. So:

- A hostile pillager's charge went off near your hire and set him to chase.
- Nothing on a hire ever takes him out of chase again.
- The pillager chase branch treats him as a pillager hunting the player. He fired at you every
  cycle (the rounds pass through you since v13.68 and hit whatever is behind), and if his spawn bag
  rolled a Frag Charge he threw it at you, up to 98 damage, killing you under his name.
- On LOOT he stopped looting, and on HOLD he was pulled off his spot.

THE BUILD. A blast never stands your hire up into a chaser, whoever threw the charge. He still
takes the damage.

MEASURED. Check 13.96 puts your hire and an ordinary hostile pillager beside an enemy pillager's
charge. The ordinary pillager must give chase (the control, which proves the blast is live). Your
hire must not. It fails on v13.95.

Not verified: a hire already stuck in chase from an older save state, which this does not reset.
