v10.70: the loadout number counts what is actually going up

The ascent panel reads LOADOUT with "Xc going up" beside it, which is the
number you read before deciding what to risk, and it was counting the
backpack grid rather than the loadout.

The grid is deliberately not the loadout: an item on a tactical belt key is
taken out of it, on your own rule that an item is in one place or the other
and never both. The header borrowed that list and inherited the subtraction.

Reproduced in the Undercroft: a medkit, a plate and a servo read 860c. Move
the medkit onto key 3, which moves nothing out of the loadout, and it reads
650c. The safe pocket was never counted at all, and that is the one thing
that survives your death.

It counts the whole backpack including the copies on keys, plus the safe
pocket. The labels under it are untouched.

The check fails on a v10.69 fixture.
