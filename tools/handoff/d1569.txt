player at hp 100 of 100, so findHeal skips both heals (healReach 100 is at or over the Bandage ceiling of 85 and the Medkit

then P.hotAssign took it and saveProfile ran. Key 6 now held a greyed Bandage on the floor belt and a red 0 on the Stash screen's
tactical belt, and Medical was gone. With one Medkit packed, the Bandage the Undercroft issues kept the pin alive in the raid and
his Medkit had no key. Dragging the Medical cell to another key bound a Bandage he may not have. And a click on any belt key
released a few pixels off the belt unbound it, because the floor had no press point and no distance test. The raid has had the
same-cell rule since v8.14 (if(d.fromHot===HC.i){ dropped=true; break; }) and the 24-unit click threshold since v14.61. Nothing in
the file calls the floor behaviour deliberate, it is not balance, and it touches no player text.
