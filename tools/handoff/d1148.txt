## v11.48 - RIGHT-CLICK "EQUIP AS YOUR GUN" ON A STASH GUN LOST THE GUN

The second P0 data-loss line from the v11.46 read-only audit. The item context
menu, itemMenuRows(key,ctx,count), builds its rows for `key`. The row that turns
a looted gun into one of your own did three things in order: splice the gun out
of P.stash, clearKeysFor(k) so no belt key still pointed at it, then push it to
P.weapons, equip it and save. But `k` is not declared anywhere in that function,
and the whole file runs under 'use strict', so the second step threw
ReferenceError. The splice had already happened; the push, the equip and the
save never did. The gun was in neither the stash nor the armoury: gone, with no
message, and nothing in the report because the throw is the ordinary kind the
crash catcher records after the fact. The other copy of this verb, in the stash
grid, uses a loop-scoped k and was fine, which is why it was not seen sooner.

THE FIX. One token: clearKeysFor(key). The row is built for key; that is the gun.

MEASURED. Check 11.48 drives the REAL rows through a new hook: a gun item the
profile does not own is put in the stash, the equip-as-gun row is taken from
itemMenuRows and its act run. On this build the gun leaves the stash, lands in
P.weapons and is equipped, and nothing throws. On the v11.47 fixture the same
act throws ReferenceError after the splice and the check fails on "in neither
the stash nor the armoury: it was lost". A control requires the gun that
reached the armoury to be the equipped one.

Not verified: his own play; the stash-grid copy of the verb, which was read and
is sound but is not driven by this check; whether any friend has already lost a
gun this way (it would show as a ReferenceError naming clearKeysFor in a run
report's crash list).
