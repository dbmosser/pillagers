v11.10: the bot cannot see the crawler bug at all, measured

At v11.07 I fixed the crawler that could not see a hidden man it was touching and
said I had not measured the effect on the bot's extract rate. This is that
measurement.

The paired run is not affordable here: one paired seed takes about thirty five
seconds, so 320 pairs is several hours and a single pair outlives the window a
call gets. Seven completed, zero discordant, neither arm extracting once, which
is saturated and says nothing.

So I measured what decides the answer. The bug only fires when concealment falls
far enough that a crawler's sight, 100 units times concealment, drops under its
own 36 unit reach, which needs concealment below about 0.36.

Measured over three seeded bot raids, 1,647 steps: crawlers were inside biting
distance for 693 frames and zero of them were in the blind band. Over 300 frames
the bot's concealment ran 0.40 at its lowest, 1.00 at its highest, 0.884 on
average, and it crouched in not one frame, because simCrouch is 0 and always has
been.

The bot never gets there. It cannot exhibit the bug. That is why seven pairs
showed no difference and why three hundred more would have shown none. The bug
only ever reached a human who hides, which is why he hit it twice and no number I
have run ever showed it.

Nothing in the game changed in this build.

New check v11.10 is not shaped like the others and does not pretend to be. There
is no defect, so there is no control that fails on the previous build; it passes
on both and is written to fail in the future. It reads the crouch dial and fails
if the bot is ever allowed to crouch. It steps one short raid and fails if the
bot's concealment ever reaches the band. Where the bot meets no crawler, that is
recorded as a gap in the sample rather than a fault in the game.

Not verified: the extract rate effect itself, which is unmeasurable by this bot.
A real number needs either a bot that hides or a person.
