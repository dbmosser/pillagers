## v12.63 - A CONTRACT THAT PAYS A GUN YOU ALREADY HAVE PAYS ITS VALUE INSTEAD

From the 2026-09-08 read-only audit, confirmed by a skeptic against the source.
Verified by reading at v12.62.

WHAT IT WAS. A contract whose whole physical payout is a gun paid nothing at all
if you had come by that gun since the card was written, and the receipt said it
paid.

The gear is rolled once, at the moment the card is made, and that roll refuses a
gun you already own at that moment. The card then sits on the board for as long
as you like, and the panel says so in its own words: they do not expire while you
are down here. Between the roll and the claim the gun is easy to come by. Extract
carrying one and it banks to the armoury. Buy one at the counter.

Claim it, and the payout adds the gun only if it is new, then returns the same
sentence either way. So the line reads "and Marksman Rifle to the armoury" for a
gun that was already yours, and nothing changed hands. The credits arrive. The
gear does not exist. And you are told it did.

THE BUILD. He is paid what the gun is worth instead, taken from the armoury's own
value for it, and the line says exactly that. Nothing on the receipt is a fiction
any more.

I considered re-rolling the gear at claim time instead, which would keep the
payout physical. I did not, because the card has been sitting on his board naming
a specific gun, and quietly swapping what it pays at the moment he claims it
would be a second lie in place of the first.

IT COSTS NOTHING ANYWHERE IT CAN BE MEASURED. No dial moves, nothing in a raid
changes, and the bot does not claim contracts. What changes is that a payout that
was worth nothing is now worth what it said it was.

MEASURED. Check 12.63 pays the gear through the real payout with the gun already
in the armoury, which is the state the card cannot see because it was written
before he had it. The gun and its value both come off the game's own tables
rather than out of the check. It requires the credits to rise by that value, the
armoury not to change size, and the receipt to name the amount. The control is
the same payout on a gun he does not own: it must still hand over the gun, add no
credits, and say the armoury, so the fix cannot pass by turning every gun payout
into cash.

Not verified: it calls the payout directly rather than claiming a card at the
board, which is the same function one line down; it does not cover the key or
the stash payouts, which have their own branches and were not raised; and it
does not check what the board prints on the card before the claim.
