## v15.07 - A GRENADE A PILLAGER PAYS FOR A REVIVE CAN BE THROWN

Throwables audit finding 5 (MEDIUM). Picking up a downed pillager with E pays you out of his own backpack (the v9.10 revive
reward in updatePlayer): his gun if he carries one, otherwise the most valuable thing he carries. The reward always went into
G.bag. A Frag Charge, Smoke Canister or Decoy Beacon lives in G.pouch everywhere else (a container take, the drop kit and the
free kit all add to G.pouch), and doThrow, startCook and the throw cell on the tactical belt read only G.pouch. Every pillager
spawns with his gun in his backpack, so the first revive pays the gun; but a revived pillager can go down again, and the second
revive pays his best item. Pillager backpacks roll body, locker and safe loot, which hold Frag Charges, and a Frag (180) outranks
bandage 60, ammobox 90, comp 120 and board 150. Steps: pick up a pillager a second time whose most valuable item is a Frag
Charge. The toast says "He puts his frag charge in your hands." and the Frag Charge appears as a tile in the backpack, while the
Frag cell keeps its old count (x0 if you had none) and the Frag key says none is left. Dragging the tile onto a belt key binds a
cell that still reads x0 and hides the tile. The only way to use it was to drop it and search the pile again.

THE BUILD. The revive reward sends a throwable (use 'throw') to G.pouch under its tk key, the same field the container take
uses; everything else still goes to the backpack. The pouch count is guarded the way the drop kit guards it, so an item with no
pouch count still lands in the backpack. The toast and the label are unchanged. No number moves.

MEASURED. Check 15.07 deploys (seed 4242), clears the other entities and stages one downed pillager next to the player, then
runs one updatePlayer frame with E held. Control: carrying only a Medkit, he gets up and the Medkit reaches the backpack, which
proves the revive branch ran. Then the same pillager carrying only a Frag Charge, with 2 Frag carried: his backpack empties (a
second control, he paid the Frag), and the check requires the Frag count to read 3 and no Frag Charge in the backpack. It fails
on v15.06 on both: the count stays 2 and the Frag Charge is in the backpack.

Not verified: a real second revive by hand in a raid; the Smoke and the Decoy, which take the same branch but are not driven; the
belt cell and the throw by eye after the reward.
