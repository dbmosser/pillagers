- 3: no $ bonus for a heir with a worse trait.

- 16: the level bar fills from anything that earns SP; level is tied to SP.

- 24: no extra challenge for high levels; "easier, not easy".
- 27: a small shared boost to the partner when one player levels.
- 32: a roster, but only for pillagers prestiged at level 20 (see notes).
- 41: no cash in raids at all; cash stays on the account "like a credit card".
- 54: nine slots: headgear, amulet, armor, pants, 2 rings, boots, gloves, backpack; no cloak.
- 57: 4 or 5 gear pieces a raid, not 2 or 3.
- 58: no ground glow; all gear comes from containers.
- 62: gear strength follows your current gear, Destiny 2 style, not the district.
- 69: no limit on stash room for gear.
- 76 and 77: no wardrobe at all; a look shows only while you wear that piece; no wearing one piece's look over another.
- 79 and 81: some body choices cost $, and changing hair or face later costs a small $ at the bar.
- 82: dyes for gear, bought, at launch of looks (not later).
- 87: skill prices rise per step inside a branch.
- 89: respec costs $.
- 90: no pillager classes, ever.
- 100: lineage, level and gear built together, not lineage first.
