## v10.82 - A DESTROYED BUILDING DID NOT LOOK DESTROYED

v10.81 shipped the geometry and its own Not verified line named this: the only
thing telling you a building had come down was the absence of some wall.

MEASURED on COLD STORAGE at seed 4242, the building at 2980,1330: the mean
brightness of the floor inside it reads 124.76 with the wrecking dial off and
124.81 with it on. Four hundredths of one percent. The ground under a destroyed
building was the same ground, so at any distance it read as a building somebody
forgot to finish rather than one that fell down, and the feature only existed
for a player who was already close enough to notice a gap.

THE FIX GOES IN THE BAKE, NOT THE FRAME. The ground is a canvas painted once
per raid, so this costs nothing per frame and cannot touch collision, sight or
pathing. It is paint. Four layers, and each one is doing a job:

  the burn      everything inside goes darker and colder, which is the read
                from across the map before a single piece of rubble is legible
  the spill     dust past the line the walls used to hold, because a ruin that
                stops exactly at its own footprint reads as a floor tile
  the rubble    blocks at a density scaled to the floor, each with a shadow,
                because one flat grey on a dark floor disappears at distance
  the scorch    a soft dark centre, so the eye is given a place it came down
                from rather than an even scatter

Every position and size comes from a hash of its own coordinates. No rr() is
drawn, so the seeded stream cannot move behind it, and the same building has
the same rubble every time you walk past it.

MEASURED AFTER, same seed, dial off against dial on:

  the ruined building   99.47 percent of its floor pixels moved
                        mean brightness 124.76 down to 114.30
  the standing building 0.08 percent moved, mean unchanged at 101.89
  entities, containers  85 and 165, identical either way

THE CHECK runs both arms on both maps and asks four things: every destroyed
building moved at least 45 percent of its floor and lost at least 4 points of
brightness, which is half of each measured signal; every standing building
moved under 3 percent, which is far above the 0.08 of existing noise and far
below the signal; open ground well away from any ruin moved under 3 percent, so
this is a mark on a building rather than a wash over the map; and the entity and
container counts are identical, because paint that moves the world is not paint.
Its control is that at least two ruins were found to look at, or it proved
nothing. On a v10.81 fixture the ruined floors move a tenth of a percent and it
fires.

Not verified: what this looks like on the OTHER map's districts. I put the two
crops side by side and the destroyed one is obviously burnt and littered while
the standing one is clean, but that was COLD STORAGE at noon, and the night
palette and the mile's own district colours are only checked by the numbers.
The map screen still paints a destroyed building with the same flat fill as a
standing one, so you cannot pick one out when planning a route; that is its own
build. And nothing here changes the fact that a wrecked building still counts
as indoors for anything overhead.
