v12.40: the man you hired picks up the men shooting at you

The pickup branch asks two questions before one pillager kneels over another:
is this man a pillager, and is he in my crew. Neither asks which side either
man is on. The merc you hire is built by the same maker as every other
pillager and rolls a crew number there, out of two, so he shares a number with
about half the map. Told to loot on his own he reached this branch, and a
downed hostile inside 760 units carrying that number read as one of his own.
He left the job you paid for, knelt over the man who had been shooting at you,
and put him back on his feet on 40 percent health.

One guard between the two existing tests, so it reads as the side question that
has to be answered before the crew question is asked. It is the same test the
engage path already uses, so the pillager he would shoot is the pillager he
will not pick up. A man who has thrown in with you is still picked up, and no
other reviver has his crew rule changed.

This one is not free: it removes revives that used to happen. Nothing in the
settings moves, and the bot never hires anyone, so no paired number can
measure it either way.

Check 12.40 stages a reviver and a downed man twenty units apart on a crew
number no raid can roll, with the player parked a thousand units away, and runs
three arms: the hire and a downed hostile, an ordinary crew picking its own up,
and the hire and a man who has thrown in with you; fails on v12.39.
