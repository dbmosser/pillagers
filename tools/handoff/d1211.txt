## v12.11 - THE MAP SAYS EXTRACT NOW TOO

From the read-only review of this morning's shipped builds, on v11.74.

WHAT IT WAS. v11.74 replaced EXTRACTION IN PROGRESS with EXTRACT NOW and the
seconds left, on his note, in the two places the raid HUD draws the boarding
window: the banner and the off-screen ring label. The sector map keeps a
third copy of that line under each ring, and it still said OPEN TO EXTRACT,
the wording he rejected at v8.68 and again at v11.74. Worse, the map paints
an opaque wash over the whole screen before it draws, so while the map is
open the map's copy is the only boarding line the player can read. The check
for v11.74 read only the HUD function's source, so it could not see the map.

THE BUILD. The map's line says EXTRACT NOW with the seconds left, the banner
words; the ring's letter is already printed on the line above it. The stale
comment over the ring label, which still credited the wording to v8.68,
names the owner.

MEASURED. Check 12.11 deploys, puts a ring into the landed hold with twelve
seconds left, opens the map and draws it with the canvas text call recorded,
and requires the line under the ring to say EXTRACT NOW with 12S LEFT and
not the old words. On the v12.10 fixture it says OPEN TO EXTRACT 12s.

Not verified: his own eye on the map; the CALLED and CLOSED lines, which
were not in his note and stay as they were.
