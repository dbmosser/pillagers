## v14.97 - THE UNDERCROFT BACKPACK SHOWS A PACKED GUN THE RAID SHOWS

Copies audit finding 1 (MEDIUM). A belt key on an item claims one copy of it: that copy is on the belt and not counted in the
backpack. v14.63 fixed the raid's copy of that rule (beltClaimsBag): a key naming the gun in his hands claims no backpack copy,
because the key already has the gun. The Undercroft backpack (renderKitCol) still took one copy away for every key. With the SMG in
gun 1, key 5 on it and a field SMG packed from the stash, the Undercroft read Backpack 0 packed with no SMG in the grid, while the
raid backpack showed the field SMG.

THE BUILD. The Undercroft backpack skips the claim for a key on gun 1 or gun 2, following the raid rule.

MEASURED. Check 14.97 packs a field SMG with key 5 on the SMG: with the pistol in hand the backpack counts 0 (control: the key
claims it), with the SMG in hand it must count 1. It restores the profile through the loader. It fails on v14.96.

Not verified: the same count read in a live raid and on screen.
