## v14.79 - AN EXTRACTION LEAVES BOTH GUN SLOTS ON GUNS HE OWNS

Gun audit finding 2 (MEDIUM). Putting a gun in the backpack from its belt key takes it off the armoury list (v14.03). If that
gun was then dropped or given to the hire and he extracted empty-handed, the extraction branch repaired only a collision
between the two slots, so gun 1 still named a gun he no longer owned. The sector page and the ascent check read the slot raw
and said the gun was going up; the raid handed him a loaner; a gun bought next did not go into his hand, because the slot was
not empty. The same happened to gun 2. The death branch has repaired both slots since v2.82 and v12.39.

THE BUILD. After the collision repair, extraction applies the death branch's two ownership repairs and clears a collision
they create.

MEASURED. Check 14.79 owns only the SMG in gun 1 (control: the raid arms it from the armoury), puts it in the backpack, drops
it (control: neither owned nor carried) and extracts: gun 1 must name a gun he owns or none. It restores the profile through
the loader. It fails on v14.78.

Not verified: the sector page after such a raid by eye.
