v10.94: face marks are painted before the eyes, not over them

His note: "pillagers in the undercroft still have weird eye collissions on trhier
faces in some instances".

"Some instances" cannot be answered by looking at a crowd of eight out of tens of
thousands of possible looks, so it was answered by enumeration. A new fixture
hook draws one figure alone at nine times raid size, and the eye is located from
the drawing itself: the whites are the one flat colour on a lone figure, so their
pixels give two boxes and those are the eyes. Nothing in the check repeats a
coordinate from the source.

Measured on v10.93, percentage of the eye area painted over: plain 0, scar 0,
freckles 0, mud 0, war paint 65.9, shiner 51.4. War paint is two thin bars laid
straight across both eyeballs. The shiner is an ellipse centred on the right eye
and slightly larger than it. Twenty-eight rolls of the crowd's own generator, each
measured against itself with the mark removed, put the worst at 63.9.

The fix is the order, not the artwork. A mark is on the skin and an eye is not
skin, so the face-mark block moves to just before the eyes. Paint still crosses
the face, the shiner still bruises the socket, neither is over the eyeball.
Nothing moved, nothing was recoloured, no rack lost a look. After: 0.0 on all six
and 0.0 across the crowd rolls.

New check v10.94 enumerates the rack and 28 crowd rolls and allows nothing over 8
percent. Controls: a magenta blot painted straight over both eyes must read at
least 80 percent, the rack must yield at least four marks, and the rolls at least
three kinds. Fails on a v10.93 fixture with the numbers above.

Logged and not fixed here, found while proving the instrument: headgear, beards
and tattoos change the hero by 31978, 5221 and 801 pixels and a pillager by zero,
because they sit inside a hero-only branch. No pillager has ever worn a hat. That
is note 17 in AUDIT.md and its own build.
