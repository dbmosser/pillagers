REVIEW OF THE 2026-10-08 NIGHT BUILDS (v20.29-v21.20): 9 confirmed regressions

=== R1 [high] v20.34 (99681dd2) Guest loses an armoury gun on abandon if he drops it and nobody else takes it
SCENARIO: Co-op, player 2 is the guest. He carries an armoury gun up, moves it into the backpack (this splices it out of P.weapons and onto G.spliced), and drops it (Z or the drop control). Then either (a) he leaves it on the ground, or (b) he searches his own pile back up. Then he abandons the raid, or his window closes mid-raid. Before v20.34 the abandon (or the next load, through P.raidSpliced) put the gun back in his armoury, the same as solo does. Now the gun is gone from his save for good, though nobody else ever took it.
EVIDENCE: dropItem, guest branch, lines 14805-14811: `var _gk=(/^gun_/.test(key)&&ITEMS[key]&&ITEMS[key].gk)?ITEMS[key].gk:null, _j; if(_gk){ _j=(G.spliced||[]).indexOf(_gk); if(_j>=0) G.spliced.splice(_j,1); if(P.raidSpliced){ ... P.raidSpliced.splice(_j,1); } saveProfile(); }`. This runs as soon as the 'pile' word is sent, before anyone has taken the gun. Only G.spliced brings an armoury gun back on abandon (endRaid abandon branch, line 22032: `if(G.spliced){ for(...) if(P.weapons.indexOf(G.spliced[i])<0) P.weapons.push(G.spliced[i]); }`), and on load only P.raidSpliced does (line ~3169). If he picks the gun back up, nothing puts it back on the list: openContainer/grantLoot never re-splice it, because the splice sites (15373, 15410, 15540, 15645) only fire when the id is still in P.weapons, and it no longer is. The gift path (~40167) can remove the id at once because a gift always leaves. A drop does not always leave. The host already records who dropped the pile (netPileTake, line 14787: `c.dropBy=peer.seat`), but nothing reads that field. The same code also takes the armoury copy off the list when he drops a field copy of the same gun.
FIX: 1. In dropItem's guest branch (lines 14805-14809), delete the _gk block that splices G.spliced and P.raidSpliced and saves. Keep the issued-Bandage lines and return key, as before v20.34.

2. On the host, when gun items come out of a container with typeof ct.dropBy==='number' and the taker's seat differs from ct.dropBy, tell the dropper's window:

   netSend(netPeerOfSeat(ct.dropBy), {t:'gungone', k:key})

   Do this in two places:
   - netSrchTick, next to both netGunsGone calls (lines 41966 and 41969), with the taker = +s.
   - The host's own staged pull and openContainer on such a pile, with the taker = seat 0.

3. Also gate netGunsGone on (ct.dropBy===undefined || ct.dropBy===0) and on the taker not being the host. Then a guest re-taking his own pile no longer strips the host's matching id (the v17.06 bug).

4. On the guest, add a 'gungone' handler that does what the gift path at line 40167 does:

   j=(G.spliced||[]).indexOf(gk); if(j>=0) G.spliced.splice(j,1);
   if(P.raidSpliced){ j=P.raidSpliced.indexOf(gk); if(j>=0) P.raidSpliced.splice(j,1); }
   saveProfile();

   If his raid has already ended in an abandon, also take the id out of P.weapons and repair P.equipped and P.equippedSec, as netGunsGone's v17.23 branch (lines 41912-41920) does.

5. A dropper who searches his own pile back up gets no word. His id stays on the list, so solo, host and guest behave the same.

The host's kept raid then moves the disc and sends its machines there, and netWorldSend carries the new moves count to player 2. After `var hm=G.hitMark,hk=...` at :27332, add `var _hmz=hudRes();` and use `_hmz` in place of `_rz` on :27335 and :27337. === R8 [low] v21.17 (c355772e) A controller press during the attract clip works the hidden title menu
SCENARIO: A player with a controller (a couch player, or player 2) leaves the title alone for 30 seconds and the attract clip starts. He taps A to stop it. The press goes straight through to the title under the clip: padMenu clicks ENTER THE UNDERCROFT (or whatever control has the pad highlight), so he is moved on from the title without choosing. The clip itself is often not stopped by a quick tap, because the pad is only checked every half second. D-pad presses also move the highlight on the title he cannot see. The commit says 'the press that stops the clip does nothing else'. That is true for keys and the mouse, but not for a controller.
EVIDENCE: dark_raiders.html 33301-33317: the only pad check for the attract is attTick on setInterval(attTick,500). It reads navigator.getGamepads() once every 500 ms and calls attPoke() if a button is down at that moment, so a tap of about 100 ms is usually missed. Only the key, mouse, wheel and touch listeners (33309-33315) swallow the input while ATT.on. Meanwhile pollPad runs every frame (33652) and reaches padMenu (12960). For md.id==='title', padMenu puts the highlight on #titlestart (12729) and on A's rising edge does PAD.focus.click() (12786). Nothing there checks ATT.on, so the click runs under the full-screen #attract overlay (z-index 9000) whether or not attTick also catches the press. In a same-PC window that is not in front, attTick's raw getGamepads read gets no live data (netPadTick passes pad state to that window by hand), so the pad cannot stop the clip at all. Its forwarded A presses still reach padMenu.
FIX: Add this in pollPad just before `if(padMenu(pressed)){ padRelease(); return; }` (line 12960). It uses pollPad's own bt and ax, and also covers the player 2 window, whose pad state comes from netPadTick:

```
if(typeof ATT!=='undefined'&&ATT&&ATT.on){
  var _atb=false, _ati;
  for(_ati=0;_ati<bt.length;_ati++) if(pressed(_ati)) _atb=true;
  for(_ati=0;_ati<ax.length;_ati++) if(Math.abs(ax[_ati]||0)>0.45) _atb=true;
  if(_atb) attPoke();
  for(_ati=0;_ati<bt.length;_ati++) PAD.prev[_ati]=pressed(_ati);
  padRelease(); return;
}
```

The press that stops the clip is used up because PAD.prev is stamped, so tap(0) on the next frame sees the A as already held and clicks nothing until it is released. (Optional, beyond the smallest fix: also set ATT.last=Date.now() when _atb is true while the title is up, so pad use between the 500 ms ticks restarts the 30-second idle timer.)
