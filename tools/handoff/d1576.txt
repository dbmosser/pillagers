  draws them. END THE PARTY on the host closes the channel and clears the mode (netReset calls netSameStop); a player 2 window
  keeps its channel and mode across a netReset, so the host can offer again. A player 2 window that reloads re-pairs on its
  own; a closed one drops off the roster as any lost link does.
