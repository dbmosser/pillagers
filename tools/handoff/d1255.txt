## v12.55 - AN IMPORTED GHOST FIGHTS AT HIS OWN GUN RANGE

From the 2026-09-07 read-only audit (P3), specced from the source. Verified by
reading at v12.54.

WHAT IT WAS. A pillager's gun, his damage and his engagement range are one
thing held in three fields, and the file says so twice. The elite blessing
moves all three together. A man who picks a gun out of a kit moves all three
together. The comment beside one of them spells out the reason: an elite with a
Longshot that kept an SMG's engagement range would read as broken.

The imported ghost is the one re-gun in the game that moved only the gun. So a
ghost whose body rolled an Auto Rifle, and whose report says he favoured the
Riot Scattergun, opened fire at 374 units holding a gun whose rounds die at
260. Every round he fired in that band was thrown away short of the target. The
other way round, a Longshot ghost on a Compact SMG body never engaged past 259
and the cap that exists to stop a man firing from outside your sight never
bound at all.

You only see it if you shoot your own imported friend, because while he is
friendly the fights he picks are governed by a fixed window that never reads
this field. Once you turn him hostile, it is his whole behaviour.

THE BUILD. The same two lines the kit path already uses, with the same cap, so
there is one rule for all three re-gun paths instead of two rules and an
exception.

IT COSTS NOTHING ANYWHERE IT CAN BE MEASURED. The ghost is not built under the
bot at all; the import bails out of a sim raid, so no paired number can move.
No dial changes.

MEASURED. Check 12.55 records what a pillager's body actually rolled, imports a
ghost carrying the Longshot, which is not in the pool a body can roll, and
requires his engagement range and his damage to have moved with the gun. The
control is the same import with the gun that body already carries: nothing may
move, which is what separates carrying the range with the gun from simply
rewriting the range for every ghost. It skips honestly if this seed happens to
build a pillager already holding the Longshot, because then there would be
nothing to measure.

Not verified: it does not watch him fire, only the field the firing branches
read; it runs one map and one seed; and it says nothing about the hostile flip
that makes the mismatch visible, which is its own path.
