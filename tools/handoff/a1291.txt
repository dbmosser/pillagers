| THE LEFT STICK CRAWLS WHEN YOU ARE DOWN | v12.91 | Finding 11 of the 2026-09-11 read-only audit. The downed screen asks him to do one thing and on a controller that one thing is the only thing he cannot do. He is on the pad, and the game itself told him the left stick moves; he is shot to the floor 200 units short of the ring, and the downed overlay says CRAWL TO THE RING, says crawl to an open extraction point if possible, and tells him how far the nearest one is. He pushes the stick and does not move a single unit for the whole bleed-out, then dies where he fell. The downed branch builds its movement vector from keyboard codes alone and then moves the body; the stick lives in PAD.mx and PAD.my, which pollPad refreshes every frame and which every other movement site reads, the standing branch, the roll direction and the Undercroft walk, and this branch is the one place it was never added, with nothing anywhere synthesising a movement key from the pad. The pad is not dead while downed, which makes it worse rather than better: D-DOWN still self-revives and X still extracts, because both go through keys the pad does write, and the left stick still swings the aim cursor, so he turns on the spot while going nowhere and nothing on screen suggests the controller has stopped being read. The fix is the same one-line stick override the other three movement sites use, placed before the magnitude so the flat thirty-units-a-second crawl normalisation is untouched: an input, not a speed. THE FIRST VERSION OF THE CHECK WAS WRONG IN A WAY WORTH RECORDING: setting PAD.mx by hand proves nothing, because pollPad runs every frame before the raid update and with no gamepad present its first act is to set PAD.on false and PAD.mx and PAD.my to zero and return, so anything poked into PAD is gone before updatePlayer reads it, and the check went red on the FIXED build for that reason rather than the build. It now stubs navigator.getGamepads to hand back one connected pad with the axes it wants, so the whole real chain runs: pollPad reads the axes, applies its own deadzone and normalisation, sets PAD, and the crawl reads it. Check 12.91 crawls all four directions twice, once on the keys with a pad connected and the stick at rest and once on the stick; the keyboard arm says whether the way is open at all, so a body against a wall cannot fail this check for the map rather than the build, and it doubles as the control that reading the stick has not taken the keyboard away from a pad player, while the pairing is also the speed control because the stick must cover the same ground and no more; three arms fail on the v12.90 fixture, each reporting 0 units on the stick against 15 or 16 on the keys. NOT MEASURED: the fake pad reports axes and no buttons, so it does not re-test D-DOWN self-revive or X extract, which the audit confirmed already work; and it does not check the analog magnitude, because the crawl is deliberately flat-speed and a half-pushed stick should crawl at the same rate as a full one |
