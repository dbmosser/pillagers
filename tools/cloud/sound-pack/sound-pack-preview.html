Pillagers sound pack

The sounds for tonight's features: raid events, air strike, power-ups, medals, gate, barrels, active reload, LMG spin, finisher, golden enemy and haul reveal. Click a button to hear it. Everything plays through a copy of the game's mix: the world sounds go through its compressor and limiter, and the HUD sounds and stings skip them, so the levels you hear match the game.
