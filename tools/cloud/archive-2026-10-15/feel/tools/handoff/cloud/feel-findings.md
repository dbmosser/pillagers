# Cloud hunt: COMBAT FEEL AND FRAME TIME (2026-10-15, branch claude/cloud-hunt-feel)

Area: builds v26.00 to v26.17, v26.55 to v26.67 and v27.10. Hunted from v27.27 to v27.48 (origin/master moved during the hunt); every draft passed its final gate on v27.48. Six rounds; rounds 5 and 6 found nothing new.
Proofs ran in headless Chromium at 1920x1080 through tools/cloud/ev.sh (the fixture plus one in-scope eval line; see the
script header). Every row below has its proof output or says why it was not drafted.

## Frame time

Headless Chromium here has no GPU: canvas raster runs in software, so absolute frame times are many times what his PC
sees and are NOT evidence of a frame over 16 ms. Only comparisons between builds and between scenes are used.

Scene (same for every build): seed 4242, map 0, 40 extra bodies chasing the player (a third pillagers, the rest
sentries), 8 player rounds every third frame, and on builds that have them 6 lit fires, the ORBITAL DEBRIS and COOLANT
RUPTURE events, 3 power-ups and a smoke cloud. Update = updatePlayer + flowTick + updateEnts + updateBullets +
updateThrowables + msgTick; draw = render2D (which calls drawHUD). Median of 50 frames.

| build | update ms | draw ms | canvas ops in one frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| v26.54 (before the feel builds) | 5.8 | 72.9 | 4,019 (fillRect 2,294, fill 714, drawImage 240) |
| v27.27 (now) | 6.9 | 70.1 | 4,028 (fillRect 2,234, fill 710, drawImage 244) |

Live loop, 6 s profile (tools/cloud/prof.mjs), 3840x2160: v26.54 avg 295 and 246 ms on two runs, v27.27 avg 257 and
260 ms. 1920x1080 v27.27: avg 73 ms, of which JavaScript is under 5 percent of samples (the rest is software raster).
Body count (0, 40, 80, 120 extra) moves update from 4.0 to 9.6 ms and draw within run noise.

Result: the feel builds added about 10 canvas ops a frame and no measurable frame cost at 1080p or 4K. No frame-time
draft. Not verified: real GPU timings, which only his PC (tools/handoff/stress.ps1) can give.
