CAVEATS
- Nobody listened. Picks rest on the authors' descriptions, the pack file names, and measured envelopes
  (foley-tools\aud.ps1 info) and brightness/clickiness (foley-tools\feat.ps1). OwlishMedia's files are only numbered inside each
  category folder, so bandage_tear (Stationery_05: paper/tape) and medkit_case_snap (Plastic_08) were chosen by shape, not by name.
- OGG durations come from the last Ogg page granule position; the WAV durations are exact.
