# What players say made Halo: Reach's earn-cosmetics system work

Research pass, 2026-09-03, for his note: "i want cosmetics to be a major part
of what keeps people coming back for the long haul -- poke around community
forums and read about what people say about Halo Reach because that game had
the perfect 'earn cosmetics' system". Sources: Steam MCC discussions,
Bungie.net archive threads, AnandTech's launch thread, Halopedia, and search
snippets where marked. Reddit, GameFAQs, Waypoint, NeoGAF, ResetEra and
archive.org refused the fetcher.

## The mechanics they praise, and why

- Credits after every game, in every mode (campaign, Firefight, Forge,
  customs, matchmaking). Playing anything advanced you; losing still paid.
- Buy what you want, in any order. Credits were saved and spent by choice;
  no forced path, nothing random. "39% armory completion but everything I
  want."
- Rank is lifetime credits and spending never lowers it. Fifty ranks from
  Recruit to Inheritor, the icon shown beside your name everywhere.
- Rank gates plus price. New pieces "pop up" when you rank up, so both
  counters feel meaningful.
- Four daily challenges and one weekly, a fixed reset, a lump of credits
  each. The reason to log in every day.
- Commendations: 45 tracks in Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Onyx, each tier
  paying credits. They tracked how you play (pistol kills, headshots,
  assists, rounds without dying), so play style became the collection.
- Cosmetics visible everywhere: on your Spartan in matches, in menus, in
  cutscenes. Purely aesthetic, so nobody bought power. Armour Abilities were
  a loadout pick, never an unlock, so the shop never touched balance.
- A prestige capstone: the Haunted helmet cost one credit but needed the top
  rank plus every other helmet owned.

## What they said

1. "I really miss the credits and having that power of choice. The choice of
   getting what you want, not what the game wants you to get." (Stocky, Steam
   MCC, "Reach credit system")
2. "On OG reach I only have 39% armory completion, but I have everything
   that I want." (Xeonzs, Steam MCC)
3. "I had some meaning behind my unlocks because when I ranked up new items
   would pop up"; MCC's list is "like they pulled item names from a hat."
   (Judge Breadd, Steam MCC)
4. "reached the highest rank and unlocked almost everything despite never
   having played multiplayer... you could save your credits for the things
   you actually want." (TheIdiot, Steam MCC)
5. "I really liked earning Credits after most activities and progress on
   commendations." (Puppygirl Layka, Steam MCC, "I miss Halo: Reach credits")
6. "These daily challenges are a great idea. I've been getting really sucked
   into trying to get them done every day." (bucwylde23, AnandTech, Sept 2010)
7. "people really didn't like having all the armour unlocked by default, and
   wanted to work towards armour." (Pyrocitor, Steam MCC moderator)
8. Preferred ranks that "were displayed with you wherever you went."
   (UNKNOWN iXi, Bungie.net)
9. "instead of the near 20K credit jackpot I was looking forward to, I get
   credit capped and pull in a whopping 2K credits." (bsparky16, Bungie.net)
10. "333 days if you reach the cap every single day" / "Thats ridiculous."
    (Atom Alchemist, Spider Wolf 24, Bungie.net)
11. "maxing commendations and hating the grind that followed... getting to
    the rank... was just dreadful." (Xitta, Steam MCC)
12. Halfway through Field Marshal you can buy every piece, so further credits
    "just sit in your account never doing anything." (NeoGAF, search snippet)

## What they hated

- The daily credit cap (60,000 at launch, raised twice). The most hated
  thing in the system: "capping is a sign you play too much."
- The grind at the top: Inheritor took about 333 capped days, roughly 1,500
  hours. "I just want cool armor and play normally."
- Dead money: past about three million credits there was nothing to buy and
  the rank kept climbing for nothing.
- The double gate: farming credits for a piece you also had to rank up for.
- Price spikes on the famous helmets (Pilot 90,000; Mark VI 300,000; the
  effects at 200k, 300k, 1M, 2M).
- The MCC re-do: season points replaced credits, solo modes earned nothing,
  pages unlocked in a fixed order. "Sounds a bit like a downgrade."

## What this means for Pillagers

He has ruled (answers 15 and 16) that cosmetics are earned and never bought,
so the credit shop is out. What survives of Reach's system without it:

1. Every ending pays. Extract, dead, abandon: each moves the counters the
   racks are gated on (runs, extracts, level, Wardens, the board). The runs
   counter already does; the ladder should say so on the outcome card.
2. See what is coming. Every locked piece already prints its requirement on
   the racks; the Depot and the outcome card should name the NEXT piece and
   the distance to it, so a raid always has a piece at the end of it.
3. Free order. No fixed unlock page; the racks already work this way.
4. Play style as collection. The 'how' gates are run counts today; kills by
   weapon, extract streaks, runs without a death, night extracts are the
   commendation tracks players praised, and they are one string each.
5. No cap, no dead money, and a capstone: one piece gated on owning every
   other piece on its rack, the Haunted helmet trick.
6. Daily and weekly challenges were the log-in hook. That is a new system,
   his to decide: "extract twice today", "fifteen kills", "three runs".
